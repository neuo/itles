## P2-老06 · A person who is good at planning — 张伟（Person）  〔pilot · 复用生成〕

> **Cue**: 是谁 / 怎么认识 / 做什么计划 / 你的感受 · 题型 Person
>
> 🔁 **整卡复用自**：张伟池（新07/新25/新16）+ 新12 毕业选行业 + 新18-Q4「计划要不要提前」+ AI 域「think for themselves / a helper not a crutch」。**零新素材**。

### ①P2 拆解（英式中文珠子 → 复用英文）

| 句 | 英式中文珠子 | 复用英文 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 我想聊的那个人 是 我的 一个 老同学，张伟。 | The person I'd like to talk about is one of my old classmates, Zhangwei. |
| 2 | 我们 在 大学 认识，那时候 我们 都 在 那 电脑 社团，一起 写 一些 小 程序。 | We met at university, and back then we were both in the computer club, building little programs together. |
| 3 | 真正 让 他 出众的 是 他 多么 擅长 提前 规划。 | What really makes him stand out is how good he is at planning ahead. |
| 4 | 每次 我们 有 一个 小组 项目，他 会 先 把 整个 东西 铺开——把 它 拆成 步骤、想清楚 什么 可能 出错、还 给 每部分 定 一个 明确的 截止日期。 | Whenever we had a group project, he'd map the whole thing out first — break it into steps, work out what could go wrong, and set a clear deadline for each part. |
| 5 | 我 记得 有一次 我们的 项目 就 一周 后 要 交，而 其他 人 都 在 慌。 | I remember one time our project was due in just a week, and everyone else was panicking. |
| 6 | 张伟 就 保持 冷静、坐下、然后 在 一块 白板上 拟 出 一个 简单的 计划，把 那 活儿 在 我们 之间 分好。 | Zhangwei just kept calm, sat down, and drew up a simple plan on a whiteboard, splitting the work between us. |
| 7 | 因为 那个，我们 按时 完成 了，而 它 说实话 救 了 场。 | Because of that, we finished on time, and it honestly saved the day. |
| 8 | 我 真正 佩服 他 的 是 他 总是 想 前 几步，而不是 脑子一热 冲 进 事情 里。 | What I really admire about him is that he always thinks a few steps ahead, instead of rushing into things. |
| 9 | 他 现在 工作 做 得 很好，而 我 觉得 那 主要 归结到 他 怎么 规划。 | He's doing really well in his job now, and I think it mainly comes down to how he plans. |
| 10 | 说实话，那 是 一个 我 很想 跟 他 学 的 东西。 | To be honest, that's something I'd love to learn from him. |

### ②P2 整段（shadow）

> The person I'd like to talk about is one of my old classmates, Zhangwei. We met at university, and back then we were both in the computer club, building little programs together. What really makes him stand out is how good he is at planning ahead. Whenever we had a group project, he'd map the whole thing out first — break it into steps, work out what could go wrong, and set a clear deadline for each part. I remember one time our project was due in just a week, and everyone else was panicking. Zhangwei just kept calm, sat down, and drew up a simple plan on a whiteboard, splitting the work between us. Because of that, we finished on time, and it honestly saved the day. What I really admire about him is that he always thinks a few steps ahead, instead of rushing into things. He's doing really well in his job now, and I think it mainly comes down to how he plans. To be honest, that's something I'd love to learn from him.

> 🔁 **复用**：句1/2/5/6/9/10 = 张伟池（新07/新25）几乎逐字；句3/8 = `stand out` + `What I really admire about` 句型换填「规划」；句7 = `saved the day`；句8「rushing into things」= 新12。句4「map out / break into steps / what could go wrong」是唯一为"规划"新搭的动词块，但都是大白话。

### ①P3 拆解 + ②整段

**Q1. Do you think it's important to plan ahead?**  〔≈ 你 新18-Q4 练过，几乎直接搬〕

| 句 | 英式中文珠子 | 复用英文 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 绝对，是的——至少 那些 大事。 | Definitely, yes — at least the big stuff. |
| 2 | 即使 是 小 东西，值得 有 一个 大致的 计划，这样 你 不 会 最后 被 困住。 | Even for something small, it's worth having a rough plan, so you don't end up stuck. |
| 3 | 否则 你 浪费 时间 和 钱——比如 脑子一热 冲 进 某个 东西、事后 后悔。 | Otherwise you waste time and money — like rushing into something and regretting it later. |
| 4 | 所以 我 会 总是 提前 搞定 那些 大事，即使 我 让 其余 保持 灵活。 | So I'd always sort out the big stuff in advance, even if I keep the rest flexible. |

> Definitely, yes — at least the big stuff. Even for something small, it's worth having a rough plan, so you don't end up stuck. Otherwise you waste time and money — like rushing into something and regretting it later. So I'd always sort out the big stuff in advance, even if I keep the rest flexible.

> 🔁 **复用**：句1/2/4 = 新18-Q4 逐字（`worth having a rough plan` / `end up stuck` / `sort out the big stuff in advance, keep the rest flexible`）；句3「rushing into」= 新12。
