### ②P2 整段（shadow）

> The building I'd like to talk about is IFS, a tall tower right in the middle of Chengdu, where I live. ### ②P2 整段（shadow）

> The person I'd like to talk about is one of my old classmates, Zhangwei. We met at university, and back then we were both in the computer club, building little programs together. What really makes him stand out is how good he is at planning ahead. Whenever we had a group project, he'd map the whole thing out first — break it into steps and set a clear deadline for each part. I remember one time our project was due in a week, and everyone else was panicking. Zhangwei just kept calm, sat down, and drew up a simple plan, splitting the work between us. Because of that, we finished on time, and it honestly saved the day. What I really admire about him is that he always thinks a few steps ahead, instead of rushing into things. He's doing really well now, and it mainly comes down to how he plans. To be honest, that's something I'd love to learn from him.

> 🔁 **复用**：句1/2/5/6/9/10 = 张伟池（新07/新25）几乎逐字；句3/8 = `stand out` + `What I really admire about` 句型换填「规划」；句7 = `saved the day`；句8「rushing into things」= 新12。句4「map out / break into steps / set a deadline」是唯一为"规划"新搭的动词块，但都是大白话。

### ①P3 拆解 + ②整段

**Q1. Do you think it's important to plan ahead?**  〔≈ 你 新18-Q4 练过，几乎直接搬〕

| 句 | 英式中文珠子 | 复用英文 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 绝对，是的——至少 那些 大事。 | Definitely, yes — at least the big stuff. |
| 2 | 即使 是 小 东西，值得 有 一个 大致的 计划，这样 你 不 会 最后 被 困住。 | Even for something small, it's worth having a rough plan, so you don't end up stuck. |
| 3 | 否则 你 浪费 时间 和 钱——比如 脑子一热 冲 进 某个 东西、事后 后悔。 | Otherwise you waste time and money — like rushing into something and regretting it later. |
| 4 | 所以 我 会 总是 提前 搞定 那些 大事，即使 我 让 其余 保持 灵活。 | So I'd always sort out the big stuff in advance, even if I keep the rest flexible. |

> Definitely, yes — at least the big stuff. Even for something small, it's worth having a rough plan, so you don't end up stuck. Otherwise you waste time and money — like rushing into something and regretting it later. So I'd always sort out the big stuff in advance, even if I keep the rest flexible.

> 🔁 **复用**：句1/2/4 = 新18-Q4 逐字（`worth having a rough plan` / `end up stuck` / `sort out the big stuff in advance, keep the rest flexible`）；句3「rushing into」= 新12。
