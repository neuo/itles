## 0. 开场抽查（active recall，换内容造句）

**① 介词串（稳定弱点）**
- 原句：*whether the museum is accessible to the public general depends on how much the ticket is.*
- ✅ `accessible to` ✓ + `depends on` ✓（两个介词全对，稳定弱点这次稳）；主谓 `whether…depends` 单数 -s 对。
- 🔴 `the public general` → **`the general public`**（语序：形容词在前）
- 🟡 `how much the ticket is` → `how much the ticket costs`
- 精修：*whether the museum is accessible to **the general public** depends on how much the ticket costs.*

**② 论点连接变体组**
- 原句：*one reason is a lack of geographic advantages. On top of that, local residents don't have sufficient funds.*
- ✅ `One reason is …` → `On top of that, …` 双落地；`a lack of` 冠词对，`residents don't` 复数对。**基本零错**。
- 🟡 `don't have sufficient funds` → `lack sufficient funding`（更紧，可选）

## 1. P1 — Tidiness（cold，连环快问快答）

**suzy 逐字**：
> 1. Definitely, keeping everything tidy makes me feel comfortable.
> 2. Yes. My room is as tidy as it was when I was a child.
> 3. I will make sure books or tools back to their original places after I use them.
> 4. Yes. It's important, especially for me.

**诊断**：
- Q1 ✅ `keeping everything tidy **makes** me feel comfortable` — 动名词主语 + 单数 -s 全对（焦点命中）。升级 `makes me feel comfortable`→`helps me relax`。
- Q2 语法干净（as…as + is/was 时态对），但**稍偏题**（问的是小时候整不整齐）→ *Yes, I did. I've pretty much always kept my room tidy, even as a kid.*
- Q3 🔴 **两个硬错**：① 缺动词 `make sure books back` → `make sure **I put** books back`；② 时态习惯用现在式 `I will make sure` → `I **make sure**`。精修：*I make sure I put books and tools back where they belong after I use them.*
- Q4 对但短 → *A tidy space helps me focus and think more clearly.*

**📄 整套 clean 范文（可 shadow）**：
> 1. Definitely. I feel a lot more relaxed when everything's in its place — **a** cluttered desk really distracts me.
> 2. Yes, I did. I've pretty much always kept my room tidy, even as a kid.
> 3. I make sure I put books and tools back where they belong as soon as I'm done with them, so things don't pile up.
> 4. Yes, at least for me. **A** tidy space helps me focus and think more clearly.

**🎓 教**：`make sure (that) + 主语 + 动词` —— "确保某事" 后必须跟完整小句，不能光跟名词（make sure **the door is** closed，不是 make sure the door closed）。

## 2. P2-新07 发小 → suzy 用 **Zhangwei**（改造成小学同学）· 阶段 B cold·untimed

**Cue**: Describe a friend from your childhood.

**suzy 逐字**（typo 已标）：
> The person I'd like to talk about is one of my childhood friends, Zhangwei. We met in primary school when we were around twelve years old, and we ended up being classmates for years right up [until] we finished high school. He is easily the most helpful and patient person I know.
> Back then, we [spent] a lot of time together, especially in the school's computer club. What really made him stand out was how he solved problems. I remember one time we were working on a group project for a science class, and our program suddenly crashed right before the presentation. While the rest of us were panicking, Zhangwei calmly sat down, started checking the lines of code one by one, and fixed the bug within twenty minutes. He basically saved the day.
> The interesting thing is why he loves to help. He once told me that [explaining] things to others actually helped him understand them better. So for him, helping wasn't a chore, but a way of learning.
> I really [admire] him for his calm personality under pressure and the way he helps [others], and honestly, everyone needs a friend like Zhangwei.

### 📊 Scorecard
| 维度 | 分 | 说明 |
|------|----|------|
| 长度 | ✅ ~200 词 / ~2min | 区间内 |
| Bullets | 4/4 | who ✓ where-how met ✓ what did together 🟡薄 what made you like ✓ |
| FC | **7** | 叙事流畅有画面 |
| LR | **7** | stand out / saved the day / wasn't a chore but a way of learning / right up until / under pressure |
| GRA | **7** | cleft `What really made him stand out was how…` + while 状语 + 并列 sat/started/fixed + 报告语 + 近 error-free |

**净 ~7。阶段 B 又一篇过。**

**🎉 焦点全守住**：-s 全对（loves / helped him / made him stand out）；冠词零漏（a group project / a science class / a chore / a way / a friend like Zhangwei）；主谓 attraction 对（the rest of us **were** panicking）。

**🔴 唯一真错**：`the way he helps **other**` → **`others`**（别人作代词/宾语必复数）。

**🟡 改进**：bullet「what you often did together」太薄（说了 computer club 直接跳救场故事）。

**🎓 教**：`we'd + 动词`（过去常做的事）补薄 bullet → *especially in the school's computer club — **we'd mess around building little programs** after class.*（would 比 did 有质感）

**💎 可偷 chunk**：right up until / save the day / wasn't a chore but a way of learning / stand out / under pressure。

## 3. P3-Q1 — How important is childhood friendship to children?

> suzy 一开始**完全没头绪（中文都没）**。教「它给了什么 / 没有它会怎样」抓手 + 打样 → 她用框架自己重产。

**suzy 逐字（框架自产）**：
> Honestly, I think it's really important, maybe more than people realize. The primary reason is that childhood is when kids first learn how to get along with others, sharing toys, taking turns, that kind of thing. These are social skills that kids can't pick up from adults. On top of that, having a close friend gives a child a sense of belonging, so they don't feel alone. I'd say a kid with no friends at all often grows up more withdrawn. So, yeah, childhood friendship plays a big role.

**诊断**：🎉 **零错**。-s 全对（learn / `gives a child` / `grows up` / `plays a big role`）；冠词零漏（a close friend / a sense of belonging / a child / a kid / a big role）；**主动用 3 chunk**：`The primary reason is`（升级 One reason is）+ `On top of that` + `that kind of thing`（毕业 chunk 自然冒出）。

**🧭 抓手（记住）**：「How important / Why」别想抽象的"重要" → 转 **正面它给了啥 + 反面没它会咋样**（反面最好生成内容）。

## 4. P3-Q2 — Difference between younger and older friends ⭐⭐ 突破点

> suzy 先用**写作模式**写出一版（漂亮但 written register），并坦白「**口语根本说不出来**，写作能改、口语没法想也没法回头改」。→ **这是核心 output gap 现形**。

**关键诊断（方法论突破）**：她说不出，**不是口语差，是瞄准了「书面英语」目标**（lies in / valuable advice / fresh perspectives / stay open-minded and connected to the younger generation = essay 词，没人这么说话）。**口语 Band 7 ≠ 写作 Band 7**：口语要短、碎、大白话、允许自我纠正。

**给降级版示范**（spoken register）：
> I'd say the main difference is what you get from them. Older friends are kind of like mentors — they've been through more, so they can give you good advice when you're stuck, like with your career or whatever. Younger friends are different — they bring a lot of energy, and they're more into the latest stuff, like new trends and pop culture. So I guess older friends make you wiser, and younger ones keep you young.

**方法 = 倒珠子，不造句**：脑子只存 4 颗珠子（what you get → older=mentors/advice when stuck → younger=energy/latest stuff → 收尾 wiser/young），现场用最简单词串，错了 `...I mean...` 接着说。

**suzy cold 重产（突破！从"说不出"到说出）**：
> Well, I think the main difference is what you can learn from them. Having older friends is like having [mentors]. They always have more life experience, so they can give you some valuable advice when you're stuck in career problems. Younger friends are different. They bring a lot of energy, and they are the kind of guys that are into the latest trends. So I guess older friends may give you wisdom, and the younger ones make you energetic.

**诊断**：near-clean。降级成功（is / like having mentors / into the latest trends / I guess）；-s 冠词全守。
- 🟡 唯一搭配：`stuck in career problems` → `stuck **with a career decision**` / `stuck **on something at work**`（stuck + with/on）。

## 5. P3 — Why do many countries try to attract people to visit?（巩固倒珠子，换域 economy）

> 抓手 = 它带来什么好处；珠子 money → jobs → image。

**suzy 逐字**：
> The main reason is... tourism is a good way of making money. Foreigners come here to travel. They need cost a lot in various aspects, like hotels, restaurants, or other spots. On top of that, countries can enhance their images by leaving tourists a good impression.

**准确性硬错（列全）**：
1. 🔴 `the tourism` → **`tourism`**（抽象/不可数零冠词）。⚠️ **写作孪生**：写作常**多加** the（the environment），口语常**漏** a/an，这次反过来**多加** —— 同根：冠词未自动化。
2. 🔴 `They need cost a lot` → **`They spend a lot`**（need+裸动词断裂；本意 spend）
3. 🟡 `spend in various aspects` → `spend **on** various things`
4. 🟡 `leaving tourists a good impression` → `**making** a good impression **on** tourists`

**📄 精修**：*The main reason is that tourism is a good way to make money. Tourists come here to travel, and they spend a lot — on hotels, restaurants, and so on. On top of that, countries can improve their image by making a good impression on visitors.*

**🎓 教**：抽象/不可数零冠词 — tourism / money / advice / information / research / traffic / pollution（不加 a/the，除非特指）。
