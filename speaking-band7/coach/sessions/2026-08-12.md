## 三、她今天真正错的（全部进复习清单）

```
❌ for the same issue        → ON the same issue（昨天纠过 question→issue，把 for 留下了＝局部修正残留）
❌ ended up queuing          → end up（"每次去"是习惯，用现在时）
❌ every time.               → every time I GO（连词后面要完整从句）
❌ you feel the energy       → you ONLY get that energy（"只有"丢了，今天的靶心）
❌ new staffs                → staff（集合名词无复数）
❌ jobs are not enough       → There aren't ENOUGH JOBS to go around（enough 要贴着名词）
❌ but I don't go too far    → I WOULDN'T go THAT far（昨天已把 too 改成 that，今天回潮）
❌ i get stuck               → get stuck WITH HIM（丢了 with him，意思整个变了）
❌ everything is ok          → ANYTHING's fine（随便挑一个用 any-，不是 every-）
❌ you make it               → You decide. ／ It's up to you.
❌ it's not such simple      → that simple（such 配名词，that/so 配形容词）
❌ it's parts that…          → the ONLY part that…（唯一丢了，且要单数）
❌ he watch ／ If he come ／ belong ／ he cook   → 全部要加 s（主谓一致 ×4）
❌ speaks that joke          → TELLS that joke
❌ today morning             → this morning
❌ a external ／ an challenge → an external ／ a challenge（a/an 看读音，同一篇两个方向都错）
❌ every students ／ a groups → every student ／ a group
❌ as simple as it got       → as simple as possible
❌ i'd really love           → I really love ／ I've always loved
❌ restore the cube          → solve the cube ｜ computation ability → processing power
📖 但日常应付得来（but I get by）—— 连续两天 📖，没记住
📖 我得去看医生（I need to go to the doctor）—— 漏答
```

## 四、她问出来的（都属于"不会"，已全部入库）

```
can vs be able to      can 才是默认；只有完成时/不定式/情态后面、或"过去单次做成了"才必须换 ★B217
go too far 的 go       go ＝ 在【程度轴】上移动，不是走路（take it too far／draw the line 同族）★B218
a bit 能不能连用       一句里别放两个软化词；递进句第二边要给足，别缩回去
the screen 还是 screens 日常设备泛指惯用 the（the phone／the TV／the internet／the screen）
You decide 空荡荡       decide 可以不及物；★ 这是被 B41 纠正后的【过度泛化】★B221
社会没翻出来            中文的"社会/大家/人们"泛主语，英语用 there is 或被动吃掉 ★B222
know 能不能换 get       不能。know＝知道 · tell＝分辨得出 · get＝听懂（只能 I get it）★B224
a couple 能不能指 3     非正式口语可以；考试默认 a few 更保险
```
