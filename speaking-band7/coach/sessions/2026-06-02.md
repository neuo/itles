### 自产 v1（cued,~200 词 / ~2min）— 逐字
> The place I'd like to talk about is Kyoto. I'd been there three times, and the one that really stuck with me was the first —— I went with my wife and we stayed for nearly one week. We based ourselves almost in the northeast part of the city and roamed around the neighborhoods on foot. We hit the famous spots, like qingshui and nanchan temple —— I'm not sure about the spelling..., but what I really enjoyed more were the tiny, unknown temples and shrines tucked behind the streets. At first I couldn't understand why such a small river was so famous, but I just completely immersed myself in how peaceful it was. What makes Kyoto stand out is how it blends the traditional and the modern —— you can buy the latest iPhone in a centuries-old structure. The local residents were incredibly polite, too. And by chance, there'd just been an earthquake in Kansai, so the airport had closed and there were barely any tourists. It felt a bit selfish, but we felt like that the whole city belonged to ourselves.

### 逐条诊断
| 原 | 对 | 类别 |
|----|----|------|
| **I'd been** there three times | **I've been**（现在完成时=人生经历至今;I'd=had been 需更早过去锚点）| 🔴 时态 |
| what I enjoyed more **were** | **was**（what 引导主语=单数,被 temples 带偏）| 🔴 主谓 attraction(=写作 the power…**are** 同根)|
| felt like **that** … belonged to **ourselves** | felt like … belonged to **us** | 🔴 ① like 后不加 that ② us 非 ourselves(反身误用)|
| based ourselves **almost in** | **mostly based ourselves in** | 🔴 副词位置 |
| nearly **one** week | **a** week | 🟡 |
| tucked **behind** the streets | tucked away **down the back** streets | 🟡 搭配 |
| **format** names | **formal** names | 🟡 近形词(format/formal)|
| so **seamless** | so **seamlessly** | 🟡 副词 -ly 漏 |
| centuries-old **structure** | **building / temple** | 🟡 词选 |

### suzy 的元问题（都问得好,逐一答）
- **"想不到 enjoy 一个动作 wandering"** → 教 `what I enjoyed was + V-ing`(享受一个动作,不只接名词)。
- **"stuck 为啥过去式"** → 指过去那次行程,过去时自然;`stays with me`(现在,强调至今)也对。**但 I'd been 是真错**。
- **"see vs understand"** → `couldn't see why` 更地道(see=想通),用 see。
- **"`at dawn, with hardly anyone around` 我常 miss"** → 这是"副词太少"的根 → **每答强制加 1 个场景状语** `at dawn, with + 名词 + 状态`(6.5→7 texture)。
- **"so seamless 漏了程度"** → seamless→**seamlessly**(同根:副词 -ly 总掉)。

### 📄 整篇修复版（clean,可朗读/shadow）
> The place I'd like to talk about is Kyoto. I've been there three times, but the one that really stuck with me was the first — I went with my wife and we stayed for nearly a week. We mostly based ourselves in the northeastern part of the city and roamed around the neighbourhoods on foot. We hit the famous spots, like Kiyomizu-dera and Nanzen-ji, but what I really enjoyed more was wandering into the tiny, unknown temples and shrines tucked away down the back streets. One moment that stays with me was walking along the Kamo River early in the morning. At first I couldn't see why such a small river was so famous, but at dawn, with hardly anyone around, I just completely immersed myself in how peaceful it was. What makes Kyoto stand out is how it blends the traditional and the modern so seamlessly — you can buy the latest iPhone, then turn a corner into a centuries-old temple. The local people were incredibly polite, too. And by chance, there'd just been an earthquake in Kansai, so the airport had closed and there were barely any tourists. It felt a bit selfish, but we had the whole city almost to ourselves.

### 教的串句招（她当场做到了 ✓）
- **选择+对比**:`…three times, but the one (that) … was the first —`（"做过很多次,但其中一次特别”高迁移框架）
- **破折号挂细节**:`the first — I went with my wife and stayed a week`
- **对比层次**:`famous spots, but what I enjoyed more was…`
- 口诀:**1 主句 + but/that/which/— 把细节挂上去,别每条信息新起一句**。

## P3 配对（6 题；P3-1/2 cold 自产,P3-3~6 范文供学习）

### P3-1｜What kinds of facilities do big cities have?（cold）
**suzy 原**:"The amenities that would take plenty of money may only exist in megacities. Like, airports, highly rail-way stations, that kind of thing. Big cities house a large number of people, and own various industries or services. At the same time, the financial income is enough for big city to construct major infrastructure projects."
**诊断**:🔴 highly rail-way→**high-speed rail** / own various industries→**have / are home to** / for **big city**→a big city(冠词) | 🟡 take plenty of money→**cost a lot (to build)** / financial income→**tax revenue**
**深层问题(suzy 自觉"不咋地"真因)**:4 句散着摆,**没用因果连**。修法=`because…, they… to fund…` 串成一句(同 P2 串句招)。
**范文**:*Well, big cities tend to have facilities that smaller places simply can't afford — things like international airports, high-speed rail stations, big hospitals, that kind of thing. And it makes sense: because they're home to so many people, they generate enough tax revenue to fund these huge infrastructure projects.*

### P3-2｜Do you think modern cities are suitable for young people or old people?（cold）
**suzy 原**:"I'd say it depends on what individuals want. Big cities have a variety of facilities for people to live better, a lot of jobs for the young, and better medical treatment for the old. Have said that, pool air and terrible traffic in megacities are put people away. Some people prefer living in places with a good environment, like small cities or countrysides."
**诊断**:🔴 **Have said that→Having said that**(固定分词,永远 -ing) / pool→**poor** air(→poor air quality) / **are put people away→put people off**(习语 put sb off=让人却步;一般现在主动) / **countrysides→the countryside**(不可数+the) | 🟡 medical treatment for the old→**healthcare for the elderly**
**亮点**:`I'd say` ✓、结构漂亮(表态→三类好处→让步→对比例子)、长度够。
**范文**:*I'd say it depends on what each person wants. Big cities have all kinds of facilities that make life easier, plenty of jobs for young people, and better healthcare for the elderly. Having said that, the poor air quality and terrible traffic in megacities put a lot of people off. Some prefer living somewhere with a cleaner environment, like a small town or the countryside.*

### P3-3｜Before you travel to a city, what factors would you consider?（范文,供学习）
> Well, the first thing I'd look at is probably safety — whether it's somewhere I can walk around at night without worrying. On top of that, I'd think about the food and the local culture, since for me that's half the reason to travel. And another thing is the cost — flights and hotels can vary massively from one city to another. So I'd say it comes down to safety, what there is to see and do, and how much it'll set me back.

### P3-4｜What are the disadvantages of living in a very famous city?（范文）
> The biggest downside is probably the cost of living — famous cities like London or Tokyo are notoriously expensive, so you end up paying a fortune for a tiny flat. On top of that, they tend to be packed with tourists, which makes everyday things like getting around a real hassle. And honestly, that kind of fame often comes with crowds and noise, so it's hard to find any peace and quiet. So yeah, you pay a premium — in both money and stress — just to live there.

### P3-5｜Do you prefer to visit well-developed cities or cities with a long history?（范文）
> I'd say I lean towards cities with a long history. What I like about places like that is the sense that every street has a story behind it — you're not just looking at glass towers. Take Kyoto, for example: walking past centuries-old temples, you really feel connected to the past in a way you just don't in a brand-new city. Having said that, I do appreciate the convenience of modern cities — but if I'm travelling, history wins every time.

### P3-6｜For those who live in cities, is it because they want to or have to?（范文）
> Honestly, I'd say it's more 'have to' than 'want to' for a lot of people. The main reason is work — most of the well-paid jobs are concentrated in big cities, so people move there out of necessity rather than choice. For instance, plenty of my friends would love somewhere quieter, but their careers basically tie them to the city. That said, some genuinely love the buzz and convenience — so it really cuts both ways.

**P3 公式(反复套)**:`起手表态 → because/on top of that 加点 → like(例子) → so yeah/by contrast 收尾`。长度只需 4-6 句 / 30-45s,不是 2min。**扩 P3 = 加"例子 + 对比",不是硬凑词。**
