A customer needed a feature bulit on a really tight deadline, and I manaer to deliver it on time without cutting corner cases. **诊断：**

2. 🔴 "without cutting corner cases" → **"without cutting corners"** — 固定搭配被编程术语 "corner cases" 污染。inventory 近失，count 仍为 0
3. 🔴 "That kind of moment feel" → **"feels"** — 第三人称单数 -s 漏。Pattern 1 再次触发（上次：barely sit / there was three figures）

**亮点：**

- ✅ "on a really tight deadline" — 精准口语
- ✅ "knowing I can handle the pressure" — dangling participle 用对了，叙事自然
- ✅ P9/P13 双零

**精修版：**
> Just a couple of weeks ago, I got some great feedback from my department manager. A customer needed a feature built on a really tight deadline, and I managed to deliver it on time without cutting corners. That kind of moment *feels* really good — knowing I can handle the pressure and still do quality work.

**范文（Band 7）：**
> Just a couple of weeks ago. My manager came to me after a sprint — a customer had a feature request with an almost impossible deadline. I managed to get it shipped on time without cutting corners, and she specifically called it out in the team meeting. That kind of recognition doesn't happen often, but when it does, it makes the long hours worth it.

**教的表达：**
- `X doesn't happen often, but when it does, it makes Y worth it`（收尾：稀少但珍贵的时刻）
