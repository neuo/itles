## 三、🔴 本日四条机制层结论

### ① 元规则：每个语法标记只能标一次，而且必须标一次

```
时态   did/will/should ＋ 原形    didn't FIND    不是 didn't found
否定   一个否定词就够            He's NEVER been  不是 hasn't never
比较   要么 -er 要么 more        EASIER          不是 more easier
人称   主语单三才加 s，只加一次   he makeS / I make

她三个方向都错过：
  多标一次   didn't FOUND · hasn't NEVER been · neither of us DIDN'T · will LOST
  漏标一次   everyone NEED · the roads WAS
  标错位置   I FEEL like every basket FELLED me in   ← 过去式跑到隔壁动词上了
⇒ 根因：**标记不是按规则放的，是凭手感放的**（与 08-15 的结论一致）
```

## 五、她今天真正错的（全部已落号，进复习清单）

```
❌ 冠词/限定词（当天 4 次）：when I was A kid · bring AN umbrella · one of MY classmates · watching IT
❌ all THE night → all night ｜ this morning → all morning（**第三天**）
❌ I've never got up early → been ABLE TO（第三次；理由首次给出：can 没有完成时形式）

❌ you should ___ one eye on → KEEP one eye on（纠正只删掉了错的，没安装对的）
❌ it takes two hours to do ___ → to do IT ｜ more convenient to find → EASIER
❌ he hasn't NEVER been → He's NEVER been ｜ neither of us DIDN'T follow → FOLLOWED
❌ the roads WAS → WERE ｜ I use → USED ｜ children will LOST → will LOSE
❌ There isn't a charger → I DIDN'T HAVE a charger（问：是谁没有？）
❌ he's not BEEN familiar → isn't familiar（状态用简单时，变化用完成时）
❌ high expectation → expectationS ｜ 丢"太"→ too high
❌ he spoke no words → He didn't SAY A WORD ｜ figure/find out → TELL（凭观察辨认）
❌ we, five persons → THE FIVE OF US ｜ spelt out OF → spelt out IN
❌ on THE TV → on TV ｜ stood ON their feet → WERE on their feet ｜ felt like THAT → felt like
❌ even the host team lost → even THOUGH the HOME team ｜ hadn't MET for long → hadn't SEEN each other
❌ a place IN DISTANCE → far away ｜ ONE day or two → A day or two ｜ it's not A HURRY → There's no rush
📖 "愣住了"（08-15 给过又丢）→ I just stood there ｜ "打卡"→ tick things off a list
📖 "你愿意做到什么程度" → How far are you willing to go?
```
