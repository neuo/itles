## P2 阶段 B — 爱种菜的人(P2-新05,persona 外公)· cold·untimed

### suzy cold 逐字
> I'd like to talk about my grandfather, who is in his mid-80s. He is very special to me because he actually raised me during my childhood.
> After retiring, gardening became his favorite hobby. He turned his balcony into a mini garden and also manages a small plot of land nearby. He mostly grows vegetables we eat daily, like tomatoes, chili peppers, and green onions, along with a few pots of flowers.
> He takes care of them with incredible patience. Every day, as soon as the sun comes up, he goes out to water them. He even makes his own compost using kitchen waste like fruit peels.
> I still clearly remember one afternoon when he spent hours teaching me how to plant a flower. I was impatient, but he guided me gently, showing me how to handle the roots with care.
> I think he loves gardening because he treats every plant like a family member. It gives him a true sense of peace and purpose in his retirement.

### 🎉 两个老大难全守住（focus 见效）
- **-s 长句不掉**:`as soon as the sun **comes** up, he **goes** out to water them`(一个长句两个单数 +s 全对 —— 正是 zhangwei 那篇掉的位置);grows/takes/makes/loves/treats/gives 全对。
- **冠词全对**:a mini garden / a small plot / a few pots / a flower / a family member / a true sense(零漏 —— 写作最大拖累,这篇口语零漏)。
- 0 comma splice。

### 🟡 仅 2 个小 polish（非硬伤）
- `After retiring, gardening became…` → `After **he** retired,…`(悬垂修饰:retiring 逻辑主语应是他;=写作 W2-12 同款,跨技能)
- `He **turned**… and also **manages**…` → 时态统一 `He's turned… and looks after…`

### 可偷 chunk
a true sense of peace and purpose / handle the roots with care / makes his own compost / as soon as the sun comes up / treats every plant like a family member。
