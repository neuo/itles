Like, a while back a developer quietly took over a strip of a public park to extend their site, and she was the one who caught it, issued a penalty, and got the land returned within a couple of months.

And honestly, she cares about it beyond work — even on a weekend walk, she's checking whether the flowerbeds are being looked after properly. So yeah, that's my wife.

## 第三人称 -s 限时专项（10 句判断加不加 -s）— 9/10,判断零失误

looks ✅ / has ✅ / enjoy ✅(they 不加) / does ✅ / [跳过#5 company] / go ✅(I 不加) / draws ✅ / means ✅ / live ✅(复数不加) / makes ✅(this kind=单数)

**结论**:-s 规则**完全懂**(低压 9/9,该不加的也没乱加)。P2 里 `she have` 是**自动化漏**(带宽不够),非知识缺口 → 修法=多 cold 反复 + 产出后 1 秒自查"主语单数?加 s 了吗"。
**唯一缺口**:跳过 #5 `The company (try)→tries` —— **集合名词当单数 + y→ies**(company/team/family/this kind of X = 单数)。写作也踩过(creative industry 类),算重点。
