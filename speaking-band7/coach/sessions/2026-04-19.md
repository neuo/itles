## Q1 (Q17): Do you like getting up early in the morning?

**suzy 原句：**
not really. To be honest, i'm not a morning person. When i was younger back in college, i was not able to get up until after three alarms went off. good times, though. I have to get up early on weekdays owing to plenty of routines, the kindergarten run, going to work, that kind of thing. It's just a working dad lifestyle, I guess.

**诊断：**
1. 🔴 "owing to" → **because of / with** — Pattern 9（书面语）。"owing to" 是书面英语，口语没人这么说。改成 because of 或重组："I've got a lot to do in the morning — the kindergarten run, work, that kind of thing."
2. 🟡 "plenty of routines" → **a lot to do / a packed morning** — "routines" 不对。routine = 固定流程（morning routine），不是 "很多事"。她想说的是有很多事要做。
3. 🟡 "I was not able to get up until after three alarms went off" — 语法没错但太重。口语版：**"I couldn't get up without hitting snooze like three times"** 或 **"it took three alarms to drag me out of bed"**

**亮点：**
- ✅ **"Good times, though."** — 复数 s 终于落地！count 0→1
- ✅ **"that kind of thing"** — 自然使用，无 s。count 2→3 → **毕业** 🎓
- ✅ **"just"** 功能用法持续稳定（已毕业，保持一致）
- 叙事结构完整：直答 → 过去回忆 → 现在对比 → 收尾

**精修版：**
> Not really. To be honest, I'm not a morning person. Back in college, I couldn't get up without three alarms going off. Good times, though. Now I have to get up early on weekdays — there's just a lot to do. The kindergarten run, getting to work, that kind of thing. It's just the working-dad life, I guess.

**范文（Band 7）：**
> Not really — I'm not a morning person at all. Back in college I could barely drag myself out of bed, even with three alarms. Good times, though. These days I don't really have a choice — I've got the kindergarten drop-off by seven, then straight to work, so my mornings are pretty packed. It's just dad life, I guess. On weekends, though, I guard my sleep-in like it's sacred.

**教的表达：** drag oneself out of bed（挣扎着起床）

## Q2 (Q2): What kind of food did you like when you were young?

**suzy 原句：**
It was definitely noodles, a variety of noodles. Growing up in Yibin, a small city in the south of Sichuan, It is famous for noodles, especially 'ranmian', which is a noodle being able to burned by fire. The residents living in Yibin were used to eating noodles as breakfast, I was no exception. Actually back in primary school, I could even eat noodles three times a day.

**诊断：**
1. 🔴 "a noodle being able to burned by fire" — 语法崩溃 + Pattern 13 L1 直译。多重错误：being able to + 原形（不是 burned）；整个描述是 "燃面" 的逐字翻译。修成：**"the name literally means 'burning noodles' — there's so much chili oil it could catch fire"** 或简单版 **"a spicy noodle dish tossed in chili oil"**
2. 🔴 "The residents living in Yibin" → **People there / Everyone there** — Pattern 9（书面语）。"The residents living in" 是报纸句式，口语说 "people there"。
3. 🟡 "as breakfast" → **for breakfast** — Pattern 12（介词）。作为早餐 → "as" 从中文逻辑来，但英语搭配是 **for** breakfast/lunch/dinner。

**亮点：**
- ✅ **"were used to eating"** — inventory 表达首次冷启动产出！count 0→1
- ✅ **"actually"** 功能用法正确（引入惊人事实 "三顿都吃面"）
- 结构清晰：直答 → 地域文化背景 → 个人细节 → 趣味收尾
- "I was no exception" 意思到位，稍偏书面（口语版：same for me / me included）

**精修版：**
> It was definitely noodles, a variety of noodles. Growing up in Yibin, a small city in southern Sichuan — it's famous for noodles, especially ranmian, a spicy noodle dish tossed in chili oil. People there are used to eating noodles for breakfast, and I was no different. Actually, back in primary school, I could eat noodles three times a day.

**范文（Band 7）：**
> Oh, definitely noodles. I grew up in Yibin, a small city in southern Sichuan, and it's basically the noodle capital. There's this local dish called ranmian — it's tossed in so much chili oil the name literally means "burning noodles." Everyone there eats noodles for breakfast, and I was the same. I could actually have noodles three meals a day back in primary school and not get tired of it.

**教的表达：** not get tired of it（吃不腻）

## Q3 (Q22): Have you ever sent handmade gifts to others?

**suzy 原句：**
Yes, I've done that, but only once in a while. The last time I did this was a year ago, when I was preparing a birthday present for my wife. I made a small photo album by hand, printed pictures of our family trips, stuck them on a booklet, and wrote something about photos underneath. My handwriting was really embarrassedly bad, and the final work looked pretty rough. But my wife said it was one of the most precious gifts she had received.

**诊断：**
1. 🔴 "embarrassedly bad" → **embarrassingly bad** — Pattern 8（词形）。embarrassingly = 令人尴尬地（修饰 bad 的程度）。embarrassedly = 以尴尬的方式（几乎不存在的词）。
2. 🟡 "wrote something about photos underneath" → **wrote little notes under each one** — 缺 the（photos 前），且 "something about photos" 太模糊。"little notes under each one" 更生动自然。
3. 🟡 "the final work" → **the whole thing** — Pattern 9（书面语）。"The final work" 像提交艺术项目，口语说 "the whole thing" 或直接 "it"。

**亮点：**
- 动词多样性极好：made, printed, stuck, wrote — 没有一个泛化动词。Anti-Pattern 2，真正的进步
- **"pretty rough"** — 自然口语 ✅
- 叙事结构 Band 7 级别：话题 → 具体记忆 → 细节铺开 → 自嘲转折 → 暖心收尾
- 收尾 punchline（"But my wife said..."）落点很好

**精修版：**
> Yes, but only once in a while. The last time was about a year ago, when I was preparing a birthday present for my wife. I made a small photo album by hand — printed pictures from our family trips, stuck them in a little booklet, and wrote little notes under each one. My handwriting was embarrassingly bad, and the whole thing looked pretty rough. But my wife said it was one of the most precious gifts she'd ever received.

**范文（Band 7）：**
> Yeah, but hardly ever — I'm not really the crafty type. The last time was maybe a year ago for my wife's birthday. I printed out a bunch of photos from our family trips, stuck them into a little booklet, and wrote something under each one. My handwriting is terrible, so the whole thing looked pretty rough, honestly. But she loved it — said it was one of the best gifts I'd ever given her. So I guess it's the thought that counts.

**教的表达：** it's the thought that counts（心意最重要）

### 核心发现
- **Pattern 9（书面→口语）依然是最大瓶颈**，三题都出现：owing to / The residents living in / the final work / I was no exception
- **叙事能力显著提升**：三个回答都有完整的故事弧线（开头→细节→收尾），Q3 的 punchline 尤其自然
- **动词泛化问题（Pattern 2）本次未出现** — Q3 的 made/printed/stuck/wrote 全是精准动词，标志性进步
- **语法压力下的形态错误减少**：没有出现 Pattern 11（过去分词丢失）、Pattern 4（时态简化）

### 进步
- **"that kind of thing" 毕业** 🎓（Q1 第 3 次自然正确使用）
- **"Good times, though." 复数 s 首次落地**（count 0→1）

- **"actually" 功能用法持续正确**（已毕业，保持一致）
- **动词多样性**：Q3 四个精准动词连用，Pattern 2 未触发
