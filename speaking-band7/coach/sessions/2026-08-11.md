## 四、P2 自由产出（cold，173 词）

**她的原话**见 fluency_lab.md。**教练版（201 词，只在第 1 个 bullet 加画面）**：

```
The one thing I'd really like to own is a 3D printer. It's basically a small machine that
sits on your desk and builds a plastic model layer by layer — you draw something on the
screen, hit print, and an hour later you're holding it.

Entry-level ones cost about two thousand yuan, and the advanced ones can go over ten thousand.

I got to know about it through one of my colleagues. He's a real enthusiast — he even built
his own machine from spare parts. He showed me what his could do, and I was hooked straightaway.

There are a few reasons why I want one. What makes it stand out is that it can print all
sorts of toys — colourful dinosaurs, Lego blocks, that kind of thing. My son would definitely
love it. On top of that, it's a sneaky way to get him interested in engineering — he can
watch how things on the screen turn into real objects he can hold.

More importantly, on weekends he sits right next to me, we design something together, and
watch the machine build it layer by layer. It's less about the machine itself and more about
the time we spend together.
```

**她这段最强的一层是衔接**：`What makes it stand out is that…` / `On top of that` /
`More importantly` / `It's less about X and more about Y` —— 四个衔接块全对且零重复。
**最好的一句**：`things on the screen turn into a real object he can hold`（长，但全是画面）。
