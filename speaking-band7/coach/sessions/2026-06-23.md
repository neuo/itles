## P2-新25 遇困难终成功的人（同学 Zhangwei · 高压现场救火）· cold·untimed —— ⭐⭐ 自抓加长偏差 + 全自驱

> 人物 + 克服困难弧。真 cold。进场"卡了"→ 接龙 HOOK 1 拍后**她自己 pivot 出整个故事**（计算机社团 → 高压解决问题 → 小组项目演示前崩了 → 沉住气救火），我给的 glue 她有方向就直接覆盖。**注意：她讲的故事 = "高压沉得住气"，不是范文的"自学转AI"——persona 自主改写。**

### suzy 原始 cold 逐字（[接龙]=给骨架 / 其余自驱）
1. [HOOK·接龙glue·appositive] *The person I'd like to talk about is one of my classmates at university, Zhangwei. We **have known** each other for over ten years.* ✓（appositive 补充招；have known 对）
2. [background·自驱] *Back then, **what usually we did was hang out** in the school computer club, and we would mess around building little programs.*（🔴 语序 what usually we did→**what we usually did**；⭐ cleft 后 **hang**[原形] 对，Pattern 23 守住）
3. [CORE·自驱] *What makes him stand out is how he solves problems under pressure.* ✓（makes/solves -s 守住）
4. [NARRATE setup·自驱] *I remember one time, on a group project, the program crashed before the presentation.* ✓
5. [NARRATE 高潮·自驱] *While the rest of us were panicking, he just stayed calm, sat down and went through the code and logs line by line. Finally, in about twenty minutes, he fixed it — he basically saved the day.* ✓⭐（固定短语全对：stayed calm / went through / line by line / saved the day）
6. [EXPLAIN=LAND·自驱] *These days he's doing really well. He's an excellent developer, and I think it's because he **could always stay calm** under pressure. That's also something I really **admire him**.*（🔴 could→**can**[现在特质] / admire him→**admire about him**[admire sth about sb，缺介词，主错区]）

### 阶段 2：加长（出现偏差 → 她自抓 → 重整）
- ❌ **教练首推偏差拍**："he stuck to learning from scratch / stuck with it"（自学毅力）——**与她讲的"高压沉得住气"故事不是一条线**，嫁接拧了。
- ⭐⭐ **suzy 自己喊出**："我觉得这个加长不好，和例子有偏差，需要重新整合下" → **结构层自我把关**（Pattern 17 放大版：能接上但意义对不上）。
- ✅ **重整 = 砍掉偏差拍，顺"沉得住气"这条线加**：*And it's not just that one time — whenever something breaks, he's the kind of person everyone turns to, because he **keeps his cool and is always happy to help**.*（🔴 原 stay calm and **helpful**→keeps his cool…happy to help[stay+helpful 别扭 + 顺手去重，避开结尾 stay calm 撞词]；turns to / the kind of person who… 地道）

### 📄 整合终版（clean，一条线，~135 词，可 shadow）
> The person I'd like to talk about is one of my classmates at university, Zhangwei. We've known each other for over ten years. Back then, what we usually did was hang out in the school computer club, and we'd mess around building little programs.
> What makes him stand out is how he solves problems under pressure. I remember one time, on a group project, the program crashed right before the presentation. While the rest of us were panicking, he just stayed calm, sat down, and went through the code and logs line by line. In about twenty minutes, he fixed it — he basically saved the day.
> And it's not just that one time — whenever something breaks, he's the kind of person everyone turns to, because he keeps his cool and is always happy to help. These days he's doing really well — he's an excellent developer, and I think it's because he can always stay calm under pressure. That's something I really admire about him.

### 🔍 答后扫
**A. 固定短语（主扫）→ ⭐ 干净**：stand out / went through / line by line / saved the day / turns to / mess around / from scratch 全用对。唯二小错已点：admire him→**admire about him** / stay helpful→**keeps his cool**。
**B. -s + Pattern 23 → 全守住**：makes/solves/turns/keeps 对；⭐ cleft `what we usually did was hang`（原形）守住。
**C. 时态**：could always→**can**（现在特质）/ stick to→**stuck to**（她自己改对）。
**D. 冠词/碎句 → 0**：a group project / the presentation / an excellent developer 全对，碎句 0。

## P2-新12 重要决定（毕业选 field=进技术行业）· cold·untimed —— EXPLAIN/说理卡（她主动点 A 练散句）· ⭐⭐ 结构自抓第2次 + 发现"散句不垮垮的是词"

> 说理卡（中间两 bullet=怎么决定/为啥重要=JUDGE/EXPLAIN，非故事）。她**主动选这张专练散句弱项**。进场"卡了"→ 接龙（说理卡需更多脚手架，接龙拍数比 NARRATE 多）。⭐⭐ 中途她**第 2 次结构自抓**："是不是先说决定是什么，再说干了啥"——发现漏了"具体选了啥+干了啥"那拍。

### suzy 原始 cold 逐字（[接龙]=给骨架 / 含中途结构调整）
1. [HOOK·接龙glue] *One important decision I made was **choosing which field I worked in when I just graduated**.*（🔴 which field I worked in→**which field to go into**[决定当下还没工作过] / when I just graduated→**just after I graduated**）
2. [how·by+ing] *I made the decision by **weighing a few things — individual interest and industry prospect**.*（✅ by weighing a few things 用对；🔴 降级 individual interest→**my own interests** / industry prospect→**the job prospects**[复数+大白话，理科腔]）
3. ⭐⭐ **她中途喊结构**："我觉得是不是先说我的决定是什么，然后干了什么" → 自抓漏了"具体选择+行动"拍。教练支持重整：HOOK→**插具体选择+行动**→how→结果→why。
4. [插·choice+action] *In the end, I **chose as a software engineer**, and worked at **a internet company**.*（🔴 chose as→**chose to become**[choose to do，缺动词] / a internet→**an internet**[元音前 an]）
5. [results] *As it turned out, my decision worked out and I found a stable job.* ✓（worked out 用对）
6. [EXPLAIN=LAND·because→so] *It was important because the decision completely shaped my career. Sometimes I imagine **if I went to another field**, it would be totally different.*（🔴 if I went to→**if I'd gone into**[过去虚拟 had gone + go into a field]；shaped my career ✓）

### 阶段 2：加长（~75→~135 词，顺"职业决定"线，吸取新25 教训不嫁接）
7. [背景·同线] *At the time, **I had no idea what I could do**, so it felt like a big decision.* ✓⭐（had no idea 单数守住=老18 no ideas 错没再犯；so-链）
8. [扩 how·同线·make up my mind] *Before I made up my mind, I also **asked some people working in this field for their experience**.*（✅ made up my mind 用对；🔴 ask sb **for** experience→**about** their experience[ask for=要建议/ask about=打听]）
9. [收尾反思·同线] *Looking back, I'm really glad I spent time thinking about it, instead of **deciding freely**.*（🔴 deciding freely→**rushing into it**[随便决定≠freely自由地；要"脑子一热就冲"=rush into/snap decision，Pattern 13 L1直译]）

### 📄 整合终版（clean，~135 词，可 shadow）
> One important decision I made was choosing which field to go into, just after I graduated. At the time, I had no idea what I could do, so it felt like a big decision. In the end, I chose to become a software engineer, and worked at an internet company.
> Before I made up my mind, I weighed a few things — my own interests and the job prospects — and I also asked some people working in the field about their experience. As it turned out, it worked out well, and I found a stable job.
> It was important because the decision completely shaped my career. Sometimes I imagine, if I'd gone into another field, my life would be totally different. Looking back, I'm really glad I spent time thinking about it, instead of rushing into it.

### 🔍 答后扫
**A. 固定短语/降级（主扫）→ 这张漏最多（说理卡生词密）**：✅ by weighing a few things / make up my mind / worked out / had no idea / shaped my career。🔴 individual interest→my own interests / industry prospect→the job prospects / chose as→chose to become / went to→gone into / for their experience→about / deciding freely→rushing into it。
**B. 冠词**：a internet→**an internet**（a/an 元音前，今天第 2 次=老11 a old watch）。
**C. 条件句**：if I went→**if I'd gone**（对过去虚拟）。
**D. ⭐ -s 守住 + 碎句≈0**：shaped/worked/found 对。
**E. ⭐⭐ 结构自抓第 2 次**：中途喊"先说决定再说干了啥"=结构层自我把关再现（新25 之后）。

### 教的点 / 进度
1. ⭐⭐ **关键发现：她的"散句弱点"在说理卡上 = 散句结构没垮，垮的是【词/搭配】**。because/so/by+ing 连接全对、逻辑顺、结构自抓——说理框架站得住；漏的全是抽象决定类词（individual interest/industry prospect/deciding freely）理科腔降级跟不上。**与老11 情感题"生词多→固定短语高发"同一根**：题型生词密度↑→主错区（固定短语/降级）压力↑，不是散句结构问题。→ 训练含义：说理/情感卡重点不是"练连接"（已会），是**抽象词的口语降级反射**（my own interests 不用 individual interest）。呼应 priority 记忆（结构>词汇；抽象词真不会的部分别硬塞）。
2. ⭐⭐ **结构层自我把关连续两张**（新25 自抓加长偏差 + 新12 自抓漏拍）→ 元认知稳定成型，不是偶发。
3. **说理卡接龙拍数 > NARRATE**：EXPLAIN 确实是更弱项，需要更多一拍一拍脚手架（但顺下来零死机）。
4. 守住项：had no idea 单数（老18 错没复发）/ -s / so-链 / by+ing 方式。

## Summary（新12）
- ⭐⭐ **发现"散句不垮、垮的是词"**：说理卡上连接/结构/逻辑全对，漏的是抽象词理科腔降级（individual interest→my own interests 类）。与老11 情感题同根=题型生词密度→主错区压力。训练转向：说理/情感卡练抽象词口语降级反射，非练连接。
- ⭐⭐ 结构层自我把关**连续两张**（新25+新12）=元认知稳定。
- 说理卡接龙拍多（EXPLAIN 更弱）但零死机顺下来。净 ~6-6.5。
- 全 session cold 累计 12 张（6/22 ×5 + 6/23 新25/新12）。
- 🔴 入清单：choose to do（非 chose as）/ go into a field / ask sb about（打听）/ rush into（随便决定）/ a→an 元音前 / if I'd gone（过去虚拟）。
