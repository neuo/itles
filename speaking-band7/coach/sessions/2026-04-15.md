## Q1: Do you walk a lot?

**suzy 原句：**
Not really. After 14 years of coding, I've been just used to work at the desk all day. The kindergarten run is my only exercise in the morning. Maybe it's actually a working dad lifestyle.

**诊断：**
1. 🔴 "I've been just used to work" → **I'm just used to sitting** — be used to + doing；不需要 been
2. 🟡 最后一句 "a working dad lifestyle" 悬空 → **That's just the working dad lifestyle, I guess**

**范文（Band 7）：**
> Not really. After 14 years of coding, I'm just used to sitting at a desk all day. The kindergarten run in the morning is pretty much my only exercise. That's just the working dad lifestyle, I guess. On weekends, though, we do walk quite a bit — parks, playgrounds, that kind of thing.

**教的表达：** be used to doing（习惯做某事）— to 后面跟 -ing 不是原形

**亮点：** the kindergarten run — 上次漏 the，这次用对了 ✅

## Q2: Do you like reading?

**suzy 原句：**
Yeah, without a doubt. Actually, reading science fiction is my main escape. I'm recently revisiting Asimov's Foundation series. Before bed, I always read a chapter of whatever science fiction I'm into, which pretty unwind my wind from the day.

**诊断：**
1. 🔴 "pretty unwind my wind" → **helps me unwind** — unwind 是动词（放松），不需要再加 wind
2. 🟡 "I'm recently revisiting" → **I've recently been revisiting** — recently + 持续动作 = 现在完成进行时（error log Pattern 4 复发）
3. 🟡 "science fiction" → **sci-fi** — 口语更短更自然

**范文（Band 7）：**
> Yeah, without a doubt. Reading sci-fi is kind of my main escape. I've recently been revisiting Asimov's Foundation series — it holds up really well. Before bed, I always read a chapter of whatever I'm into. It really helps me unwind after a long day.

**教的表达：** unwind（放松、解压）— 动词，不加 wind。"Reading helps me unwind."

**亮点：** actually 功能用对了 — 引入可能出乎预料的信息 ✅

## Q3: Do you prefer typing or handwriting?

**suzy 原句：**
Typing, definitely. As a programmer for 14 years, typing is just my second nature. I always press the buttons on the keyboard for everything, coding, documentation writing or something else. On the other hand, my handwriting is pretty bad. I have forgot how to hold a pen, I guess.

**诊断：**
1. 🔴 "I have forgot" → **I've forgotten** — forget 过去分词是 forgotten
2. 🟡 "press the buttons on the keyboard" → **type everything out** — 太具体了，直接 type
3. 🟡 "documentation writing or something else" → **docs, messages, you name it** — 书面→口语 + 列举收尾

**范文（Band 7）：**
> Typing, no question. After 14 years of coding, it's just second nature. I type everything — code, docs, messages, you name it. On the other hand, my handwriting is embarrassingly bad. I've basically forgotten how to hold a pen, I guess.

**教的表达：** you name it（什么都行 / 列举收尾）— "Therapy, medication, lifestyle changes, you name it."

**亮点：** just 自然用了 ✅ / I guess 句尾缓冲 ✅ / on the other hand 转折 ✅

## Round 2 — Scenery / Building / Childhood

## Q4: Do you prefer the mountains or the sea?

**suzy 原句：**
Mountains, without question. I love mountains because I grew up in a small city surround by mountains. I enjoy the valley blanket in mist and the sunrise watching from mountains top.

**诊断：**
1. 🔴 "surround by" → **surrounded by** — 需要过去分词
2. 🔴 "valley blanket in mist" → **valleys blanketed in mist** — 过去分词 + 复数
3. 🟡 "the sunrise watching from mountains top" → **watching the sunrise from a mountaintop** — L1 语序直译

**范文（Band 7）：**
> Mountains, no question. I grew up in Yibin, a small city surrounded by mountains, so they just feel like home. I love that feeling of valleys blanketed in mist in the early morning, or watching the sunrise from a mountaintop. There's a kind of deep peacefulness you don't really get by the sea.

**教的表达：** blanketed in（被...覆盖）— 过去分词当形容词。"Fields blanketed in snow."

## Q5: Do you want to live in a tall building?

**suzy 原句：**
Not really, if I'm being honest. I'm now live on the 18th floor. While there are pretty many advantages, lots of lighting, good views and so forth, it's pretty boring to wait an elevator in the morning when everyone goes out.

**诊断：**
1. 🔴 "I'm now live" → **I now live** — 两个动词撞了
2. 🔴 "wait an elevator" → **wait for the lift** — wait 不及物，必须加 for
3. 🟡 "pretty many" → **quite a few** — pretty 不修饰 many
4. 🟡 "lighting" → **natural light** — lighting 是照明设备，light 是光线

**范文（Band 7）：**
> Not really, if I'm being honest. I live on the 18th floor right now. There are quite a few advantages — lots of natural light, good views, that kind of thing. But waiting for the lift every morning when everyone's heading out is just painful. I'd rather live somewhere lower, to be honest.

**教的表达：** quite a few（相当多）— 不是"很少"。"Quite a few people showed up."

**亮点：** "if I'm being honest" 很自然 ✅

## Q6: What were your favourite activities when you were a child?

**suzy 原句：**
Football. When I was young back in primary school, I played football after school every day. I even played goalkeeper once and sprained my wrist, which making my grandma pretty furious. But that was years ago. These days I'm stuck at a desk, so football isn't really part of my life anymore.

**诊断：**
1. 🔴 "which making" → **which made** — which 从句需要谓语动词，不是现在分词
2. 🟡 缺转折词连接过去和现在 → 加 **Good times, though.** 收尾回忆

**范文（Band 7）：**
> Playing football, definitely. Back in Yibin, I played after school every day with my classmates. I even played goalkeeper once and sprained my wrist — my grandma was furious. Good times, though. These days I'm just stuck at a desk, so football isn't really part of my life anymore.

**教的表达：** Good times, though.（那时候真不错）— 回忆过去的万能收尾

**亮点：**
- "isn't really part of my life anymore" — 非常自然，really 功能词正确 ✅
- 整体流畅度接近 Band 7
