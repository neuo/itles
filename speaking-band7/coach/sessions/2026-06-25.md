## 6 个 P3（老08 配套）
> 前半 untimed（Q1-Q2 暴露两种卡）→ Q3 她指出"40s vs 10min"→ **切 fast&rough（8 秒开口）** → 后半 Q4-Q6 秒开口、渐自驱。

### Q1 · COMPARE（SPLIT）· "differences old vs young using apps"
- **卡=没想法（内容）** → 教练喂对比内容（上手快慢轴）+ floor 词 → 她产出：*They're quite different, young people pick it up instantly, whereas older peoples stick with the ones they know. So it comes down to what they grew up with.*
- ⭐ 框架满分（commit→whereas→land）。🔴 `older peoples`→**people**（多加 -s，people 已复数）。📏 略短 → +1 拍加"老人更谨慎"。**4/5**。
- 📌 卡类型 = **没想法**（content-generation）；喂内容→框架+词立刻出。

### Q2 · REASON（SORT）· "why some don't like apps"
- **有想法但结构跑偏**：opener `mainly trust and habit` 对，但中途拐进"apps 其实方便"（=相反点，TWO-SIDED 的活混进 REASON）。两个原因（数据隐私 + 习惯现金）被埋。
- 🔴 `buy anythings`→anything / `they use to pay`→**they're used to paying**（be used to+ing）/ `the app's owners will know`→现在时。
- **📄 clean**：Mainly trust and habit → 数据隐私一句 → 习惯现金一句 → To be fair land。**3.5/5（结构跑偏扣）**。修法=REASON 别中途辩另一面。
- 📌 卡类型 = **有想法、组装跑偏**（≠ Q1 没想法）。

### ⏱️ —— 此处她指出 "P3 只 40s，我想了 10min" → 切 fast&rough 模式 ——
> **新模式规则**：8 秒必开口（哪怕烂/短）/ 内容第一个想到就说（说得通即可，不挑不等完美）/ 教练不喂内容（除非卡死>10s）/ 词错句糙全不管 / 标准 = "8 秒开口 + 无 10 分钟空白"。**糙的 40s > 完美的 10min。** 诊断：那 10 分钟 = 她"物理优化器"在找最优解，考场不允许、frame 正是设计来杀它（STANCE 2 秒接住话头，边说边捞内容）。

### Q3 · FREQUENCY/ENUMERATE（SORT）· "what apps popular + why"
- ⚡ **秒开口**：*The most popular apps are the short video platforms, because the content is interesting and helps kill time.*（🔴 content **is**/**helps** 不可数单数）。
- 她要范文学复用 → 给 SORT 范文（Oh loads → messaging/payment 最大 + do everything in one place → 短视频 + 第二项 → land）。可复用零件：`Oh, loads.` / `do everything in one place` / `it comes down to convenience and killing time`。
- 📌 昨日糊涂边界（判成 STAND）今天没再判错。

### Q4 · OPINION（STAND）· "should parents limit kids' apps/games"
- ⚡ 秒开 `Definitely, yes`，concede→but→so 结构对；**卡在 how**（why and how 的 how）。喂 how floor 块：`set a daily time limit` + `pair screen time with outdoor play`。
- 🔴 `parent should`→parents / `these also be addictive`→**can be** / `visual things`→**screen time/screens**。

### Q5 · OPINION/依赖（STAND）· "young people more reliant?"
- ⚡⚡ **这条最棒**：秒开 `Yes, I'd say` → **自己生成例子**（WeChat 联系/Meituan 外卖，没要喂）→ 自己收 `To be fair` land。
- 🔴 `put attention on`→**pay attention to**（搭配 error zone）/ `spent`→**spend**（习惯现在时）/ `what the world will be`→**would be like**（虚拟）。
- 📌 **内容自驱**——fast 模式见效。

### Q6 · OPINION（STAND）· "countries banning kids from social media"
- ⚡⚡ **全脊最好的一条**：`I largely agree` → concede（helpful）→ but（drawbacks outweigh）→ **两个机制**（伤面对面 + 负面内容辨不了）→ so 收。全自驱。
- 🔴 `cheap`→**shallow**（想说"浅"，cheap 意思错）/ `can't recognize`→**can't handle/tell what's real** / `will hurt`→**can** / `abilities`→ability。
- 📌 leak 还是抽象词那一下（cheap/recognize），但结构全在。

**P2 · Rednote（含补的 LAND）**
> The app I'd like to talk about is Rednote. I use it quite often — I spend an hour or two on it. I'm really hooked, because it gives me so much information I'm interested in, like short travel videos. Sometimes I also search for things on it — for example, if I'm on a business trip, I'll use Rednote to find a nice restaurant for dinner. As for how I found it, it's an interesting story: Rednote was originally designed for women, and my wife loved it. A few years ago, the company wanted to expand its market, so they redesigned it with content for men too — and that's when my wife recommended it to me. So honestly, it's a really useful tool.

**P3-Q1 · COMPARE · old vs young using apps（含 +1 拍）**
> They're quite different. Young people pick new apps up instantly, whereas older people are a bit more cautious and stick with the ones they know. So it really comes down to what they grew up with.

**P3-Q2 · REASON · why some don't like apps（去掉跑偏的干净版）**
> Mainly trust and habit, I'd say. Some people worry the app companies know everything about them — what they like, what they eat — which feels a bit creepy. Others just prefer the old way; my parents still pay with cash because that's what they're used to. To be fair, for them an app feels like more hassle than help.

**P3-Q3 · FREQUENCY/ENUMERATE · what apps popular + why**
> Oh, loads. The biggest ones are messaging and payment apps like WeChat — basically everyone uses them every day, because you can do everything in one place: chat, pay, even book a taxi. Short-video apps are huge too, mainly because they're addictive and give you a quick laugh when you're bored. So really, it comes down to convenience and killing time.

**P3-Q4 · OPINION · should parents limit kids' apps/games（含 how）**
> Definitely, yes — but with a light touch, not a total ban. Sure, some apps help kids learn and games let them relax, but they can also be really addictive. So I'd set a daily time limit, and pair screen time with outdoor play or reading. If you're too strict, they'll just sneak around anyway.

**P3-Q5 · OPINION · young people more reliant?**
> Yes, I'd say so. I spend a lot of time on various apps — for example, WeChat for staying in touch with friends, Meituan for ordering takeout. Honestly, I can't imagine what the world would be like if these apps disappeared. To be fair, sometimes I think I pay too much attention to them and ignore the real world.

**P3-Q6 · OPINION · countries banning kids from social media**
> I largely agree. Sure, social media helps people connect, but for children the drawbacks really outweigh the benefits. Chatting online is shallow, and it can hurt kids' ability to communicate in person. Plus, there's loads of negative content out there that kids can't really handle. So I'd say it's necessary, at least up to a certain age.
