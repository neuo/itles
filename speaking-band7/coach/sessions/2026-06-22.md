## P2-老05 想拥有的科技产品（高配 3D 打印机）· cold·untimed —— ⭐⭐ 首次独立全 cold 里程碑

### suzy 原始 cold 逐字（未接龙、未提示）
> One piece of technology I'd like to own is a high end three d printer. I got on it through my colleague who is a real enthusiast. He even built his own machine from spare parts. He showed me waht his could do, and I I was hooked straight away.
> As I know, our entry level one, uh, cost around four thousand yuan. It's pretty expensive, but I think it would be completely worth.

### 📄 修复版（clean，保留她内容+地板词，只改错，可 shadow）
> One piece of technology I'd like to own is a high-end **3D printer**. I **got into it** through a colleague of mine, **who's really into 3D printing** — he even built his own machine from spare parts. He showed me **what his one could do**, and I was hooked straight away.
> **As far as I know**, an entry-level one **costs** around four thousand yuan. It's pretty expensive, but I think it'd be completely **worth it**.
> There are a couple of reasons why I'd like to own one. First, it can print all sorts of toys, like **colourful dinosaurs**, Lego blocks, **that kind of thing**. On top of that, it's a sneaky way to get my son interested in engineering — he could see how **something** on the screen turns into a real object.
> What makes it stand out is that a 3D printer is really a giant toy for both of us. **On weekends, my son sits right next to me, we design a model together,** and the printer builds it layer by layer. So it'd bring us **much closer**.

### 🔍 答后扫（分组 · 两类老大难）
**A. 第二类=搭配/固定短语（现在主错区）**：`got on it`→**got into it**/heard about it through · `As I know`→**As far as I know** · `worth`→**worth it**(带 it)。
**B. -s（基本守住，只掉1）**：`one cost`→**costs**（the one costs 单数+s）。✅ turns/builds/makes 都对。
**C. 缺词/碎句**：`what his could do`→**what his one could do**(缺 one) · `my son sitting`→**my son sits**(缺动词=碎句)。
**D. 小词🟡**：Colourful Dinosaur→**colourful dinosaurs**(复数) · On a weekend→**On weekends** · We together design→**We design… together**(语序) · all of it→**that kind of thing**(她毕业 tag)。

## P2-新23 久未回复的消息（给老同学发邮件求建议）· cold·untimed —— NARRATE"等"轨 + 加长到 160+

> 事件卡（NARRATE，核心是"等"——时间本身就是剧情）。进场"卡了"→ 一拍接龙(6拍5零错)→ 完整 → 阶段2加 3 拍到 ~175 词。**确立词数硬地板 ≥160（suzy 6/22 定,入 SKILL.md）。**

### 阶段 1：完整篇（一拍接龙，~110 词）
1. [HOOK] *One time that comes to mind is when I emailed an old classmate to ask for some advice.* ✓
2. [what-about·because] *I wanted to ask him about some industry news, because he works in a field I'm interested in.* ✓
3. [NARRATE 起] *But after I sent it, I didn't hear back for a long time.* ✓（didn't hear back 地板词）
4. [NARRATE 等·推时间] *Days went by, then a couple of weeks, and I started to wonder if I'd said something wrong.* ✓（时间轨：days went by → weeks）
5. [结果] *In the end, he did reply — about a month later — and it turned out he'd just been really busy.* ✓（it turned out 地板词）
6. [EXPLAIN=LAND] *Honestly, the waiting part was a bit stressful, because I kept overthinking it. So now I try not to **take it personally** when people are slow to reply.*（🔴 唯一错 `take it easy`→`take it personally`：固定短语,主错区;take it easy=放轻松,意思反了）

### 阶段 2：润色加长（~110→~175 词，加 3 拍全新信息、零复读、零错）
- 加处境：*At the time, I was thinking about moving into his industry, so I thought he'd be the right person to ask.*
- 加发消息场景：*We hadn't talked in years, so it felt a bit awkward to message him **out of the blue**, and I spent ages wording it.*
- 加等时动作：*I checked my phone a few times a day, and I even went back to see if he'd read it.*
