On top of that, it's a sneaky way to get my son interested in engineering — he could see how **something** on the screen turns into a real object.

### 🔍 答后扫（分组 · 两类老大难）

**B. -s（基本守住，只掉1）**：`one cost`→**costs**（the one costs 单数+s）。✅ turns/builds/makes 都对。
