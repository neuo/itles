## P2 · 新15 语言学习（老婆 persona · cold · 强 ~190 词）

**① 她逐字原句（cold 全文）：**
> The person I'd like to talk about is my wife, who is really good at learning language. We met in high school, and we went on to be classmates for years right up until we finished college. Now, we have got married for over ten years.
> Last year, she began to learn japanese. we have been Japan several times, and she really enjoyed it every time. I suppose the main reason why she wants to be fluent in Japanese is that she wants to get more engaged and involved when she travels next time.
> The method she learns Japanese is by following textboos, sometimes she also watchs cartoons and animations. She insists on it, and chips a bit away every single day.
> My wife has talent in languages. She can speak various languages, like, of course Chinese, english and some dialects.
> I really admire her about her perseverance. She never stop studying even if there are plenty on her plate - works, childcare, that kind of thing. It's really a great example for both me and my son.

**② 诊断（🔴🟡 逐条 · 错落在头号区 介词/搭配 > -s > 可数）：**
- 🔴 介词（头号）：`we have been Japan`→**been TO Japan**（直译表①，抽查刚答对 been to 却在 production 掉=自动化缺口）
- 🔴 介词：`admire her about her perseverance`→**admire her FOR** / what I really admire **about** her is…
- 🔴 介词：`has talent in languages`→**a talent FOR languages**
- 🔴 时态/状态：`have got married for over ten years`→**have been married**（got married=瞬间动作配不了 for；持续用 been married）
- 🔴 -s：`she also watchs`→**watches**；`she never stop`→**never stops**
- 🔴 可数：`learning language`→**languages**；`works`（不可数）→**work**
- 🔴 搭法：`The method she learns Japanese is by…`→**The way she learns is by…**（method 搭不上）
- 🟡 流水句：`…textbooks, sometimes she also…`→拆句 `…textbooks. Sometimes…`
- ✅ 用对：补充招 `who's really good at…`(relative) / `right up until` / `chips away` / `plenty on her plate` / `that kind of thing` / `get more engaged and involved`
- ✅ EXPLAIN=LAND 到位：`admire her FOR her perseverance — even when she's got a lot on her plate`（带机制收尾）

**③ 精修版（原句小改 · 只换 🔴）：**
> …who is really good at learning **languages**. … Now, we've **been married** for over ten years. … we've **been to** Japan several times… **The way she learns is by** following textbooks. Sometimes she also **watches** cartoons and animations. … She has **a talent for languages**. … I really admire her **for** her perseverance. She never **stops** studying even when there's a lot on her plate — **work**, childcare, that kind of thing.

**④ 润色范文（降级 Band7 clean · 可 shadow）：**
> The person I'd like to talk about is my wife, who's really good at learning languages. We met back in high school and stayed classmates for years, right up until we finished college — and we've been married for over ten years now. Last year, she started learning Japanese. We've been to Japan a few times and she's loved it every single time, so I think the main reason she wants to get fluent is so she can get more involved next time she travels. The way she learns is by working through textbooks, and sometimes she watches cartoons and anime too. She really sticks at it — she chips away at it a little every single day. She's just got a real talent for languages. She speaks Chinese, English and a couple of dialects, and now she's adding Japanese to the list. What I really admire about her is her perseverance. She never stops learning, even when she's got a lot on her plate — work, childcare, that kind of thing. Honestly, she sets a great example for both me and my son.

📊 **P2 ~6.5**（长度✅ ~190 / FC 6.5-7 / LR 6.5-7 chunk 鲜活 / GRA 6 主错=介词×3+been to+-s）。

## 6 个 P3（教育/学习域 · fast&rough · 每题三样全文）

### Q1 · OPINION/ENUMERATE · "Are there many people who can speak foreign languages in your country?"
**① 原句：** Yeah, quite a few, I'd say. In China, people start learning English in primary school, And it continues until we graduate at university. But to be fair, the majority of us are not able to speak english fluently, because we mostly focus on reading rather speaking and listening.
**② 诊断：** 🔴 `graduate at university`→**graduate FROM** / all the way through；🔴 `focus on reading rather speaking`→**rather THAN**（漏 than）；🟡 `are not able to`→降级 `can't really`。⭐ 自驱把"重读轻说听"点出=她三通路诊断本人 + 用上 But to be fair 让步。
**③ 精修：** …it carries on all the way **through** university. … we mostly focus on reading **rather than** speaking and listening.
**④ 范文：**
> Yeah, quite a few, I'd say. In China, we start learning English in primary school, and it carries on all the way through university. But to be fair, most of us still can't speak it that fluently — we mostly focus on reading rather than speaking and listening, so we can read it fine but freeze up when we have to talk.

📊 ~6.5-7（stance✅ / 机制✅ / 让步✅ / 长度✅ ~50 词）。

### Q2 · OPINION（STAND，圆成 two-sided）· "Does speaking other languages help at work?"
> 上半自驱、下半卡例子（喂内容）+ 让步卡语言（中文→floor），分三轮搭成。
**① 原句（拼三轮）：** It depends on the job. The industries that are related closely with foreigner customers require, of course, the ability to speak foreign languages. … They are loads of jobs that only deal with domestic staff, like a local shop owner or a taxi driver. … [让步她卡，给中文：如果他们会，也有点用，能接待外国游客]
**② 诊断：** 🔴 `foreigner customers`→**foreign customers**（foreigner=外国人[名词]/foreign=外国的[形容词]=今日盯的名词作形容词）；🔴 `They are loads of jobs`→**There are**；🔴 `domestic staff`→**domestic stuff**（staff=员工/stuff=事务，形近义反）；🟡 `related closely with`→**closely related to** / `jobs that deal with clients from abroad`。喂的让步 floor：**come in handy**（派上用场）/ **now and then**（偶尔）。
**③ 精修：** …industries that **deal with foreign customers**… **There are** loads of jobs that only deal with **domestic stuff**…
**④ 范文：**
> It depends on the job, really. For industries that deal with foreign customers, you obviously need to speak the language. But there are loads of jobs that only deal with domestic stuff — like a local shop owner or a taxi driver — where you'd never really need it. That said, if they do happen to speak one, it can still come in handy now and then, like helping out a foreign tourist.

📊 ~6.5-7（立场✅ / 对比✅ / 让步圆场✅ / 长度✅ ~65 词）。卡类型：下半=没想法（喂例子）+ 让步=语言卡（喂 floor）。

### Q3 · OPINION/ENUMERATE · "Do people learn any languages other than English?"
**① 原句：** Yeah. Definitely. Admittedly English is the most universal language in the world, and it's a main course from primary school in China. Someone prefer other languages, like Japanese or Korean rather than English. Because they are into anime or k dramas.
**② 诊断：** 🔴 `a main course`→**a core subject**（course=课程/主菜🍽️；主科=core/compulsory subject）；🔴 `Someone prefer`→**Some people prefer**（someone=某人[单数]/有些人=some people）；✅ `Admittedly` 让步词好 + 用上喂的 anime/K-dramas。
**③ 精修：** …it's **a core subject** right from primary school… **Some people** prefer other languages like Japanese or Korean over English, because they're into anime or K-dramas.
**④ 范文：**
> Yeah, definitely. Admittedly, English is the most universal language, and it's a core subject right from primary school here. But some people prefer picking up other languages — Japanese or Korean, say — over English, mainly because they're really into anime or K-dramas. So it often comes down to what they're personally into.

📊 ~6.5-7（立场✅ / 让步✅ / 机制✅ / 长度✅）。

### Q4 · REASON（抽象 · 练 REASON 壳）· "Why is it easier for children to learn new things than for adults?"
**① 原句：** Mainly habits, I'd say, because adults have seen and experienced a lot of things, and they are used to be what the world are. So they tend to stay here, and don't move forward. While children are more flexible, kids keep thinking and learning about new things about the world.
**② 诊断：** 🔴 `used to be what the world are`→**used to the way the world is**（be used to **+名词/-ing**不接 be；the world **is** 单数；嵌入句语序 how/the way it is）；🔴 `stay here`→**stay stuck in their ways / stay put**（"stay here"=待这[地点]，要"原地固守"）；🟡 `children…kids…`同主语两遍→合并 `Whereas kids are…`。✅ REASON 壳开对（Mainly habit, I'd say + because）+ 机制自驱。
**③ 精修：** Mainly habit, I'd say, because adults … are **used to the way the world is**, so they tend to **stay stuck in their ways**. **Whereas kids** are more flexible — they keep questioning and learning about the world.
**④ 范文（含抽查漏的 Plus + EDU floor）：**
> Mainly habit, I'd say. Adults have already seen and done so much that they're used to the way the world is, so they tend to stay stuck in their ways and stop pushing themselves. Whereas kids are far more flexible — they're like sponges, always questioning and soaking up new things. Plus, they're not afraid of making mistakes, so they just dive in.

💎 EDU 甜区：**like sponges / soaking up new things / not afraid of making mistakes / dive in**。范文用 **Plus** 叠第二因（抽查漏的槽）。
📊 ~6.5-7（壳✅ / 机制✅ / 对比✅ / 长度✅ ~60 词）。

### Q5 · ENUMERATE/how（SORT）· "How do people learn new things?"
**① 原句：** loads of ways, really. Watching tutorials online may be the most convenient and economical way. There are various types of videos that pass on knowledge. on top of that, asking friends or experieced person face-to-face is a greet way too. You can get practical information based on your personal condition. plus working through notebooks is worth a try.
**② 诊断：** 🔴 `experieced person`→**experienced people**（拼写+复数）；🔴 `a greet way`→**a great way**（greet=打招呼/great=很棒）；🔴 `based on your personal condition`→**for your own situation**（condition=身体状况；个人情况=situation，直译④）；🔴 `working through notebooks`→**textbooks/books**（notebook=空白本子）；🟡 `economical`→降级 **cheap**。✅ 漂亮 SORT：3 项列齐 + on top of that/plus 连接全对 + 用上喂的 watch tutorials online。
- **她随后想给"various types"挂例子但卡语言**（中文：大学正式课程/有经验的人传授/各种示例）→ 喂 **from A, to B, to C** 列项壳：`from proper university courses, to someone experienced just walking you through it, to step-by-step how-to demos`。floor：**walk you through it**（手把手）/ **step-by-step how-to demos**。
**③ 精修：** …the easiest and **cheapest** way… asking friends or **experienced people**… is **a great way** too. You get practical tips **for your own situation**. Plus, working through **books** is worth a try.
**④ 范文：**
> Loads of ways, really. Watching tutorials online is probably the easiest and cheapest one — there's a video out there for pretty much anything, from proper university courses, to someone experienced just walking you through it, to step-by-step how-to demos. On top of that, asking friends or someone experienced face-to-face is great too, since you get practical tips that fit your own situation. Plus, good old working through a textbook is always worth a try.

📊 ~6.5-7（列项✅ / 连接✅ / 长度✅）。

### Q6 · OPINION（STAND）· "What is the most important thing for learning a language well?"
**① 原句：** For me it's got to be practical communication, which is main reason we learn foreign languages. Through talking with people, we can learn how to express ourselves and understand others. [她想补"别做什么+land"但卡语言]
**② 诊断：** 🔴 `which is main reason`→**which is THE main reason**（漏 the）；🟡 `practical communication`→降级 `actually using it to communicate`。✅ 立场+机制自驱好。她想加"别做啥+land"（接她三通路诊断）→ 喂 floor：别做啥=**Don't just bury yourself in grammar books and memorize word lists — that's the trap most of us fall into**；land=**at the end of the day, a language is for communicating — you only really learn it by using it**。
**③ 精修：** …which is **the** main reason we learn foreign languages…
**④ 范文：**
> For me, it's got to be actually using it to communicate — that's the whole reason we learn a language in the first place. By talking to real people, you learn how to express yourself and understand others. What you shouldn't do is just bury yourself in grammar books and word lists — honestly, that's the trap most of us fall into. So at the end of the day, a language is a tool for talking to people; you only really learn it by using it.

📊 ~7（立场✅ / 机制✅ / 别做啥对比✅ / land✅；略长，下车前别再加）。
