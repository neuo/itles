## P2 · 新16 科技问题（系统宕机 · speaker 自己/CS · cold · 强 ~230 词）

**① 逐字原句：**
> The problem I'd like to talk about is one time when our business system went down. I'm a software engineer, and at that time, I was responsible for back-end services. The problem was that one of our services didn't response for request. Users couldn't login, or place orders at all. for our business, it meant we were losing money every second. It was about a year ago. One night late. I was at home, and my phone started buzzing with alerts. What made it hard was that the root cause wasn't obvious the system had been fine for months. I tried to stop the bleed By rolling back the deployment that morning. But it didn't work, so I had to work it through step by step. Firstly, I checked the warning and error log, and I found nothing. Then I checked the network traffic. Within about thirty minutes, I found the root cause. Athletian load broke down and our gateways didn't detect it, and continued to route network traffic to it. I fixed it by removing the node from our clusters, and everything came back at once. So, yeah, That's pretty much it.

**② 诊断（错集中搭配/词类 + 可数）：**
- 🔴 词类+介词+可数：`didn't response for request`→**didn't respond to requests**（respond 动词 / to / requests 复数）
- 🔴 可数/冠词：`from our clusters`→**from the cluster**；`warning and error log`→**logs**
- 🔴 词：`couldn't login`→**log in**（动词两词）
- 🔴 流水句：`root cause wasn't obvious the system had been fine`→ 加破折号 `…wasn't obvious **—** the system…`
- 🔴 词序：`One night late`→**Late one night**；一致性：`that morning`（说的是深夜）→**the latest deployment**
- 🔴 不明词：`Athletian load broke down`→（没听懂，按上下文猜）**one of the nodes had failed under load**
- ✅ ⭐ **过去故事时态全程守住**（was/started/tried/checked/found/broke/fixed/came + `had been fine` 过去完成）——上一场 flag 的"中段滑回现在"这次没犯。✅ 换新故事（没复用 Zhangwei crash）。✅ 地道：went down / root cause / stop the bleed / step by step / buzzing with alerts / losing money every second。✅ cleft `What made it hard was that…`。

**③ 精修：** …didn't **respond to requests**… couldn't **log in**… **Late one night**… wasn't obvious **—** the system… rolling back **the latest deployment**… error **logs**… **one of the nodes had failed under load**… from **the cluster**.

**④ 润色范文（降级 clean · 用上 track down · 可 shadow）：**
> The problem I'd like to talk about is one time when our whole business system went down. I'm a software engineer, and back then I was in charge of the back-end services. The problem was that one of our services stopped responding to requests, so users couldn't log in or place orders at all — and for our business, that meant we were losing money every second. It was about a year ago. Late one night, I was at home when my phone started buzzing with alerts. What made it so hard was that the root cause wasn't obvious — the system had been running fine for months. First, I tried to stop the bleeding by rolling back the latest deployment, but that didn't work, so I had to work through it step by step. I checked the warning and error logs and found nothing, then I looked at the network traffic. Within about thirty minutes, I tracked down the cause: one of the nodes had failed under heavy load, but our gateways didn't pick that up, so they kept routing traffic to it. I fixed it by pulling that node out of the cluster, and everything came back up right away. So yeah, that's pretty much it.

📊 **P2 ~6.5-7**（长度✅ / FC 7 / LR 7 / GRA 6.5 ⭐时态守住=进步；主错=respond to requests + 可数）。🔗 桥：`tracked down the cause because a node had failed` = P3 第二拍机制。

## 6 个 P3（AI 域 · fast&rough · 三样全文）

### Q1 · TWO-SIDED（STAND）· "advantages and disadvantages of AI?"
**① 原句（终版，她自纠 help→helps）：** It's a real mixed bag, honestly. AI helps people do something out of their ability, like analyzing a large amount of data or translating languages. It also takes boring chores off our plate, saving loads of time. To be fair, heavily relying on it too much will hurt our ability to study or work. So overall, it comes down how we use it properly.
**② 诊断：** 🔴 `comes down how`→**comes down TO how**（漏 to）；🔴 `heavily relying too much`→留一个（重复）；🔴 `do something out of their ability`→**do things that are beyond us**（超出能力=beyond）；🟡 `will hurt`→**can chip away at our skills**。✅ ⭐ **自纠 `AI help→helps`**（-s 自查）+ `mixed bag` 开口 + `takes chores off our plate` + `To be fair`。
**④ 范文：**
> It's a real mixed bag, honestly. On the plus side, AI helps people do things that are beyond us — like crunching huge amounts of data or translating languages on the spot. It also takes the boring chores off our plate, which saves loads of time. To be fair, though, relying on it too much can chip away at our own skills, whether at study or work. So overall, it comes down to how we use it.

📊 ~6.5-7（两面✅ / 自查 -s✅ / land✅）。

### Q2 · OPINION（STAND）· "should people today learn about AI?"
**① 原句：** Definitely. Yes. AI is the most powerful tool nowadays. So we should keep up with the times or we are far behind. You don't need to be an expert. Just the basics. So I'd say. AI will completely shape our careers or how we study.
**② 诊断：** 🔴 `or we are far behind`→**or we'll fall behind**（条件未来+fall behind）；🟡 `shape our careers or how we study`→**and how we work and study**（or→and）。✅ `Definitely` + `keep up with the times` + `don't need to be an expert, just the basics`（喂的全用上）。
**④ 范文：**
> Definitely, yeah. AI's one of the most powerful tools out there now, so we should keep up with the times, or we'll fall behind. You don't need to be an expert — just the basics will do. Honestly, AI's going to reshape how we work and study, so the sooner we get to grips with it, the better.

📊 ~7（立场✅ / 机制✅ / floor 全用✅）。

### Q3 · OPINION（STAND·让步）· "should children learn to use AI?"
**① 原句：** Yeah. To some extent. AI is actually a powerful and patient teacher. You can ask it absolutely anything and still get a pretty solid answer. But students shouldn't rely on it too early. because AI can make mistakes. So, learning to think for themselves is the first
**② 诊断：** 🔴 **land 没说完** `is the first`→缺名词 → **should come first** / is the priority；🟡 `too early. because`→连 `too early**, because**`。✅ `To some extent` 让步 + 具体机制（ask anything, solid answer）+ `think for themselves`。
**④ 范文：**
> Yeah, to some extent. AI's actually a powerful and patient teacher — you can ask it absolutely anything and still get a pretty solid answer. But kids shouldn't lean on it too early, because AI does make mistakes. So learning to think for themselves has to come first — AI should back that up, not replace it.

📊 ~7（让步✅ / 机制✅ / land 补全✅）。

### Q4 · ENUMERATE/how（SORT）· "how can AI help in our lives?"
**① 原句（终版，她自纠开口）：** loads of ways, really. AI takes care of everyday chores. Like, making plan for travel, collecting news I'm interested in automatically. That kind of thing. AI can also show me the method how to learn a new thing. I think it's more important than just giving me a answer. Sometimes, to be honest, I feel myself useless. because AI always do better than me.
**② 诊断：** 🔴 `a answer`→**an answer**（⚠️ a/an 又冒头，前两场 clean=检索不稳，答后扫带上）；🔴 `AI always do`→**does**（-s）；🔴 `I feel myself useless`→**I feel useless**（feel+形容词不加 myself）；🔴 `making plan`→**planning trips/plans**（可数）；🔴 `show me the method how to learn`→**show me how to learn**（the method how 多余）。✅ ⭐ **自纠开口 `Not always→loads of ways`**（判类自查）+ `takes care of everyday chores` + `that kind of thing` + 真实感。
**④ 范文：**
> Loads of ways, really. AI takes care of everyday chores — like planning trips, or automatically rounding up the news I'm into, that kind of thing. It can also show me how to learn something new, which I think matters more than just handing me an answer. Sometimes, to be honest, it makes me feel a bit useless, because AI always does it better than me.

📊 ~6.5-7（列项✅ / 自查开口✅ / floor✅）。

### Q5 · OPINION（STAND·让步）· "are students overly reliant on AI?"
**① 原句：** a bit, to be honest. It is so convenient for student to do homework with AI. the only thing student do is copy and paste without thinking. But I don't think it's the student's fault. Actually, Nobody can resist the temptation to use AI. So we need to guide students properly on how to use AI, and put extra restrictions in place.
**② 诊断：** 🔴 `for student`→**for students**；🔴 `the only thing student do`→**all they do** / students do（复数+-s）；🟡 `guide students properly on how`→**show students how to use it properly**（properly 修饰 use）。✅ `a bit, to be honest` 让步 + `copy and paste without thinking` + `resist the temptation` + 有层次（不怪学生→没人能抵抗→引导+限制）+ **占位 it `It is so convenient for...to`**。
**④ 范文：**
> A bit, to be honest. It's just so convenient for students to do their homework with AI — all they do is copy and paste without thinking. But I don't think it's really their fault; honestly, nobody can resist the temptation. So what we need to do is show students how to use it properly, and maybe put a few limits in place.

📊 ~7（让步✅ / 层次✅ / 占位 it✅ / floor✅）。

### Q6 · ENUMERATE（SORT）· "what can teachers do to stop over-reliance on AI?"
**① 原句：** A few things. teachers can set some homework AI can't do. For example, a hands on task. on top of that doing works in class under monitor is also good idea. Besides, teacher can use AI to check their work whether it is done by themselves
**② 诊断：** 🔴 `a hands on task`→**hands-on tasks**（连字符+复数）；🔴 `doing works`→**doing work**（不可数）；🔴 `is also good idea`→**a good idea**（漏 a）；🔴 `teacher can use`→**teachers**（泛指复数）；🟡 `under monitor`→**under supervision**；`check their work whether…`→**check whether students did it themselves**。✅ `A few things` 列项 + 3 项 + on top of that/Besides。
**④ 范文：**
> A few things, really. For one, teachers can set homework AI can't do — hands-on tasks, that kind of thing. On top of that, getting students to do the work in class, under supervision, is a good idea too. Besides that, teachers can use AI the other way round — to check whether students actually did the work themselves.

📊 ~6.5-7（列项✅ / 3 项✅ / 连接✅）。
