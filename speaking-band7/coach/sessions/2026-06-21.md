## P2-新09 成功创业的人（前同事 Chen 咖啡馆）· cold·untimed

### suzy cold 逐字（她自己标的 tag）
- [HOOK · appositive] OK so the person I'd like to talk about is someone I used to work with, Chen.
- [CORE] What really stands out about him is he turned something he loves into a real business — he runs his own coffee shop now.
- [NARRATE-lite · so-tag] We used to work at the same company, so that's how I met him, and we stayed in touch after he left.
- [PLOT] He'd always loved coffee, so he wanted a job he actually enjoyed, because he never liked sitting in an office all day.
- [PLOT] So one day he just decided to try it — he left his job and opened a small shop.
- [FACT-DROP] It's a small coffee shop, not big, but it's always full of people, and he's got a lot of people who come back a lot.
- [EXPLAIN=LAND] The main reason it works is he really cares about it, because he remembers everyone's usual order and it feels nice and warm inside.
- [EXPLAIN=LAND · so-tag] So people keep coming back, and for me, he made his hobby into a job and it really worked out.

### 🎉 亮点
- **系统跑起来了**：自己 tag、自己掐结构（HOOK→CORE→…→EXPLAIN=LAND 闭环）。
- **点名 chunk 全用上**：so/because 链（多处）+ appositive（`someone I used to work with, Chen`）。
- ⭐ **-s 零漏**（老大难）：stands/runs/turned/wanted/liked/cares/**remembers**/works/keep 全对；冠词无错。production 下守住 = 进步实锤。
- **没 then-pump 起源**：NARRATE-lite 一拍带过 `that's how I met him`，没拖成故事 = 纪律到位。

### 🟡 诊断
1. **suzy 自我诊断正确："似乎没有 describe"。** `What they do` bullet = SHOW（他平时干啥）被跳过，直接到评价 → 缺 **DESCRIBE-习惯**块，也是偏短主因。
   - 补法（同时修"没describe"+"偏短"→~120s）：`[习惯] What he usually does is he's there pretty much every day — he roasts his own beans, chats with the regulars, and keeps the place really cozy.` 并把 `remembers everyone's usual order` 上移到习惯块，让 EXPLAIN 专讲"为什么"。
2. **标签纠正（学习点）**：她把 2 句标 PLOT，但 **PLOT 只用于媒体卡**。新09 人物卡：`he'd always loved coffee, so…` = EXPLAIN（为什么开店）；`one day he decided… opened a shop` = NARRATE（事件拍）。**讲人的来历=NARRATE；讲作品内容才=PLOT。**
3. polish（非硬伤）：`a lot of people who come back a lot`（重复）→ `a lot of regulars` / `lots of people who keep coming back`。

### 教的点 / 进度
- cue-driven 系统首次 cold 验证：**结构+珠子+补充招在她嘴里跑得动，GRA 守住** → 系统可用。
- 缺口集中在 **SHOW/习惯块漏跑**（她把"什么样/平时干啥"压缩了）+ PLOT 标签误用（刚学完 PLOT 过度套用，人物卡不该用）。
- 下次盯：**每张确认 SHOW 块（平移/习惯）有没有跑**——她倾向直接跳到 EXPLAIN，丢了 describe 那段（也是长度来源）。

## P2-老26 安静的地方（家附近湖边）· cold·untimed —— ⭐⭐ 破死机 + 加长实战

> 进场即**死机**："吐了两句话没了"。教练改为**一拍一拍接龙**（每次只让她填一个珠子/一拍），从死机 → cold 出完整一篇 → 再加长 +50%。这是本 session 最大价值。

### 死机真因 + 解药（当场验证）
- **真因**：想"整段一次成型" → 认知带宽爆 → 死机。
- **解药**：**一次只想一拍**，说完那一拍再想下一拍。考场答案本来就是一拍一拍出的，没人整段成型。拆 6 拍后**零死机**。

### 一拍一拍 cold 出的逐拍（含当场小修）
1. [HOOK] *the quiet place I'd like to talk about is a lake near my home.* ✓
2. [点名·with-tag] *It's a small, quiet spot, with hardly **anyone** around.*（自发用 with-tag；修 `hardly people`→`hardly anyone`）
3. [NARRATE-lite] *I first found it **by chance**, when I was taking a stroll one evening.* ✓（by chance 地板词）
4. [FACT-DROP] *I go there **not that often**, usually once or twice a month, or **sometimes when I feel stressed**.*（修 sometime→sometimes / 加 when / anxious→stressed）
5. [习惯] *What I usually do there is just **sit** by the water and **take it all in**.*（🔴 老大难 `is just sitting`→`is just sit`：**What I do is + 原形**；自发用 take it all in 地板词）
6. [EXPLAIN=LAND] *I like it because it's so quiet and peaceful, which helps me **clear my head** — **so** for me it's **a little escape from the city**.* ✓（so 链 + 两个地板词）

### 加长 +50%（~75s→~115s，靠加拍不升词 = EXPAND DON'T UPGRADE）
- 平移 +2 画面：*you've got **a few chairs, some trees, and the lake itself**.*
- 习惯 +SCENE：*I usually go in the evening, when **there's barely anyone left and it's really calm**.*
- EXPLAIN +对比：*My days are pretty busy and noisy, **so** going there really helps me **wind down**.*

### ⭐ 卡住自救（重要新技能）
她在 *"when the starlight is reflecting off the water surface"* 卡住 = 够了 Band 7+ 诗意句（cold 说不出正常）。教练当场教：**卡住 = 信号你够高了 → 立刻降到能说的画面**（→ `barely anyone left and it's really calm`），别硬憋（硬憋 = 死机来源）。这是地板规则的镜像：既别书面到说不出，也别压成大白话。

| GRA | 🔴 1 硬伤：`What I do is + 原形`（is just **sit** 非 sitting）；🟡 sometimes / by the water 小词 |

**净 ~6.5。从死机 → 完整 +50% 加长，过程价值 > 分数。**

### 教的点 / 进度
1. ⭐ **死机解药 = 一次只想一拍**（已当场验证：6 拍接龙零死机）。她自己练时卡住就默念"下一拍只填一个空"。
2. ⭐ **卡住自救 = 够不着就降到能说的画面**，别硬憋。
3. **加长 = 每拍后加一拍**（平移+画面 / 习惯+SCENE / EXPLAIN+对比），不加新论点、不升词。
4. 🔴 **新错入 error_log**：`What I do is + 原形动词`（is sit/take 非 sitting）—— 下次盯。
5. 地板校准验证成功：她**自发**产出多个地板词 = 校准（不压她会的词）方向对。

## Summary（老26）
- ⭐⭐ **进场死机 → 一拍接龙 cold 出完整篇 → 加长 +50%**。死机解药（一次一拍）+ 卡住自救（降到能说的）当场验证有效。
- 自发用满地板词（take it all in / a little escape / clear my head / wind down / by chance）= 地板校准对。
