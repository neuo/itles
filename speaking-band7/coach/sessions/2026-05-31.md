### P3 配对(Muye 话题底部 6 问,做了 4 题 cold)— 逐题完整记录

#### Q1 — What is the right age for a child to learn drawing?
- **suzy**：未打字（口述试了一遍,框架一上就不卡,跳过书面）
- **教**：三格框架 = 观点 `I'd say/Honestly` → 理由 `because` → 例子 `like`。点子级中文 → 简单英语,一格一格,不同时做"想内容+翻译"
- **结果**：✅ 框架一给就不卡（她确认）

#### Q2 — Why do most children draw more often than adults do?
- **suzy 原句**：
  > "I think there are two causes of that. Firstly, as I has mentioned, children can express what's in their heads through drawing instead of words. Another reason is that adults have plenty of things to do while children can do anything they like at almost any time."
- **诊断**：
  - 🔴 `two causes of that` → `a couple of reasons for this`（reason **for** this,不是 of that;reasons 比 causes 自然）
  - 🔴 `as I has mentioned` → `as I have mentioned` / `as I mentioned earlier`（I **has**→have;earlier = 她想要的"之前"副词）
  - 🟡 理由 1 没展开 → 追问 why（小孩还不会用语言,画画是少数出口）
  - 🟡 理由 2 太干 → 追问 then what（大人忙 → 慢慢丢了习惯）
- **精修版**：
  > "I'd say **there are a couple of reasons for this**. Firstly, **as I mentioned earlier**, children can express what's in their heads through drawing instead of words — **because at that age they can't really put their feelings into words yet, so it's one of the few outlets they've got**. Another reason is that adults **are caught up with work and responsibilities**, so they just **lose the habit over time**, while kids have loads of free time and fewer worries."
- **教的表达**：there are a couple of reasons for this / as I mentioned earlier / get caught up with X / 追问展开(why + then what)

#### Q4 — Do you think galleries and museums should be free of charge?
- **suzy 原句**：
  > "I'd say no. I understand that if museums are free of change they will be more accessible for people. Having said that, where is money for maintaining museums or constructing new one. Maybe somebody say governments should provide funds for that, but it's completely unfair for individuals who rarely visit museums. The key point is that the income form tickets is mainly used for museums themselves."
- **诊断**（错误多但论证高级,见下）：
  - 🔴 `free of change` → `free of **charge**`（charge 收费 vs change 改变,差一字反义）
  - 🔴 `accessible for` → `accessible **to**` ‖ `unfair for` → `unfair **to**`（介词成串）
  - 🔴 `where is money for...` → `where's the money going to come from?`（地道反问）
  - 🔴 `constructing new one` → `building new **ones**`
  - 🔴 `somebody say` → `someone **might** say`
  - 🔴 `income **form** tickets` → `income **from** tickets`
  - 🟡 `used for museums themselves` → `goes back into running the museums`
  - ✅ **论证高级**：站 no + 预判反方(政府出钱)再反驳(对不常去的人不公平)—— Band 7 思路
- **精修版**：
  > "I'd say no. **I get that** free entry would make museums more accessible **to** people. Having said that, **where's the money going to come from** — for maintaining the old ones and building new ones? **Some people would argue** the government should **fund** it, but that's **unfair to** people who rarely visit. The key point is, ticket income **goes back into running the museums** themselves."
- **教的表达**：I get that... / where's the money going to come from?（反问神器）/ Some people would argue..., but...（预判反方）/ fund X / goes back into X

#### Q5 — How do artworks inspire people?
- **suzy 原句**：
  > "Well, a few things come to mind. Artworks help creators to express their feelings in mutiple formats. Like, when we discribe a piece of music peaceful, it's actually what the author want to pass through. On the other hand, artworks play a crucial role to help people unwind. Every time I go back home by subway, I always have earbus on and listen to music to clear my mind after a day of work."
- **诊断**：
  - 🔴 `pass through`（传达）→ `get across` / convey（直译错）
  - 🔴 `describe music **peaceful**` → `describe music **as** peaceful`
  - 🔴 `the author want` → `the **composer wanted**`（音乐用 composer;want→wanted 时态）
  - 🔴 `play a crucial role **to help**` → `play a key role **in helping**`（role **in** doing,介词又踩）
  - 🟡 `On the other hand` → `On top of that`（这不是对比,是加一点）
  - 🟡 拼写 mutiple/discribe/earbus → multiple/describe/earbuds
  - 🟡 相关性偏：题问 inspire **people**(观众),前半答成 help **creators**(创作者)→ 答提问者角度
  - ✅ `…, like` 第 3 次 cold 自然产出 → **🎓 毕业**;✅ `unwind` 主动调出(inventory 老词);✅ 地铁听歌例子具体真实
- **精修版**：
  > "Well, a few things come to mind. For one, art lets people **feel** emotions they might not put into words themselves. **Like**, when we describe a piece of music **as** peaceful, that's actually what the composer **wanted to get across** — and it stirs something in us. **On top of that**, art **plays a key role in helping** people unwind. Every time I head home on the subway, I've got my **earbuds** in, listening to music to clear my mind after work."
- **教的表达**：get across / play a key role in doing / describe X as Y / On top of that
