## 逐题记录

### Q1 · FREQUENCY · "Are there many tall buildings in your country?"
- **bucket**：她判 STAND ❌ → 纠 SORT（数量答案）。
- **原始 cold**：*Yes. A lot. Big cities like Chengdu or Shanghai have plenty of skyscrapers, and **the lumber is growing up**. I think, the main reason is **huge population**. Building higher apartments is a good way to house so many people.*
- 🔴 **`the lumber`→the number**（形近词 Pattern 16，木材≠数量，同 dusk/dust）+ `growing up`→growing/going up（数字用 grow）
- 🔴 `the main reason is huge population`→**the huge population**（漏 the）/ 更顺 `it's mainly because of the huge population`
- 🟡 `building higher apartments`→building taller blocks/upwards。✅ `house so many people` 地道。
- **clean**：*Yeah, loads. Big cities like Chengdu and Shanghai have plenty of skyscrapers, and the number's still going up. It's mainly because of the huge population — building upwards is a good way to house so many people.*
- **📊 5 钩子**：stance ✅ / mechanism ✅ / 例子 ✅ / floor 🟡（lumber 检索抓错词）/ 长度 ✅ → 3.5/4。frame 跑通。

### Q2 · OPINION · "Do you think it's important to plan ahead?" ⭐ 教科书
- **bucket**：STAND ✅
- **原始 cold**：*Definitely. Making plan saves a lot of last-minute stress. Plus, it can boost your chance of success. For example, if I decide to change job, I'll plan what the target company is, how to prepare interviews and what I should learn. To be fair, it's not all things need to plan in advance, sometimes we can take it easy.*
- ⭐ **整条脊全对**：Definitely（表态）→ saves stress（机制）→ Plus 第二点 → For example 具体 → To be fair 让步。**opener+地板chunk+land 全用对，几乎全独立。**
- 🔴 `prepare interviews`→**prepare for interviews**（介词 error zone）/ `it's not all things need to plan`→**not everything needs to be planned**（结构断）
- 🟡 `change job`→change jobs / `boost your chance`→chances / `making plan`→making plans
- **📊 5 钩子全 ✅ → 4/4 满。** 结构满分。

### Q3 · OPINION（DISAGREE 桶）· "Some people think pets should not be kept in cities. What do you think?" ⭐⭐ 散句不垮垮的是词铁证
- **bucket**：STAND ✅
- **进场卡在翻译**（不是结构！她有完整 plan：让步卫生/疾病→转朋友/减孤独→落只要管理好利大于弊）→ 教练**只喂词不喂结构**（地板珠子表）→ 她立刻产出，还自我升级。
- **原始 cold（喂词后）**：*Not really, no. Pets can bring a bit of hygiene issues, like dealing with their wast, and there's always a risk of sickness. But honestly, they are our friends and really help ease our loneliness. At the end of the day, as long as we manager things well, the benefits definitely outweigh the downsides.*
- ⭐⭐ **词一喂上，结构+连接全回来，还自产** `ease our loneliness` / `At the end of the day` / `outweigh the downsides`（地道）。
- 🔴 `we manager things well`→**manage**（manager 名词）/ 🟡 `a bit of hygiene issues`→a few hygiene issues / `wast`→waste
- **clean**：*Not really, no. Pets can bring a few hygiene issues, like dealing with their waste, and there's always a risk of sickness. But honestly, they're our friends and really help ease our loneliness. At the end of the day, as long as we manage things well, the benefits definitely outweigh the downsides.*
- **📊 5 钩子全 ✅ → 4/4**（需喂词，但喂的是词不是结构=正确干预）。

### Q4 · ENUMERATE · "What kinds of jobs need imagination?"
- **bucket**：SORT ✅
- **进场卡（内容+词都卡）**→ 全程一拍接龙。她填：*The obvious ones are creative jobs, like painter and musicians.* + *But even programmers need it, to solve problems then they've never met.*
- 🔴 `solve problems then they've never met`→**solve problems they've never come across before**（① then 去掉 ② meet a problem 中式→come across/run into/face）/ 🟡 painter→painters
- 📌 **ENUMERATE 瓶颈=冷想例子（内容也卡，不只词）**。解药=frame"只命名最明显1个+1个意外的就停，别凑5个"，减负后产出。
- **长度偏短**（她自己问"是不是太短"）→ 触发本 session 给 skill 加**长度检查**。+1 拍示范：加深 programmer 那点 + 一句 So honestly land → ~45 词。
- **📊 5 钩子**：数量commit ✅/例子 ✅/跑完停 ✅/floor 🟡(内容卡)/长度 🔴(偏短)→ 3/5。

### Q5 · REASON · "Why do some people start their own business?"
- **bucket**：SORT ✅（Why=列原因，反直觉边界判对）
- **进场卡翻译**（她有 plan：主因自己老板/决定做啥 + 第二因追求自己目标/提升主动性）→ 喂珠子后她**让教练出范文**（她要 model 学）。
- **📄 范文（floor 级,给她学）**：*Mainly freedom, I'd say. When you run your own business, you get to be your own boss and decide what you work on, instead of taking orders all day. Plus, you're chasing your own goals, which makes you way more driven. To be fair, it's a lot of pressure too — but for a lot of people, that freedom's worth it.*
- 📌 关键降级：**主动性=driven/motivation，别追 initiative/proactivity**。
- **未独立产出**（她要范文）→ 这题算"范文学词"非 cold 产出。

### Q6 · CHANGE · "How have people's shopping habits changed in recent decades?" ⭐ leak 最清标本
- **bucket**：她判 SORT ❌ → 纠 SPLIT（时间对比）。
- **原始 cold**：*Massively, yeah. We used to buy things in physical shops, but now we tend to shop online, because online shopping **breaks geographical limit, enabling people placing orders from anywhere at anytime**. Having said that, the convenience **makes people way easier buy something** they actually don't need.*
- ⭐ **脊全对**：Massively（规模commit）→ We used to X but now Y（骨架满分）→ because → Having said that downside。
- 🔴 **leak 100% 在中段抽象机制**：`breaks geographical limit, enabling people placing orders`=理科腔/essay 词 → 地板 **`you can buy from pretty much anywhere, anytime`**；`makes people way easier buy`=结构断 → **`makes it way too easy to buy stuff you don't really need`**。
- **clean**：*Massively, yeah. We used to buy things in physical shops, but now we tend to shop online — because you can buy from pretty much anywhere, anytime. Having said that, all that convenience makes it way too easy to buy stuff you don't really need.*
- **📊 5 钩子**：stance ✅/mechanism ✅/对比+downside ✅/**floor vocab ✗（中段飘书面）**/长度 ✅ → 4/5。**= 全系统中心论点的最干净标本：脊站得住，漏全在够抽象词那一下。**

## 📊 这组总结（P3 系统首战）
- ⭐⭐ **系统跑通**：6 题每题 STANCE 都 commit、脊都站得住（STANCE→because→e.g./对比→land）。frame 在她嘴里跑得动。
- ⭐⭐ **"散句不垮垮的是词" 反复实证**（Q3/Q5/Q6 最清）：结构+连接 cold 全 own，**漏几乎 100% 在中段抽象词**（geographical limit / 主动性 / hygiene 一够就飘书面或断句）。**喂词（不喂结构）→ 立刻产出甚至自我升级**（Q3）。→ P3 干预 = 预载 floor 抽象名词 + opener glue，**不碰连接词**，方向实锤正确。
- **STAND/SPLIT/SORT**：OPINION/REASON 判对；**FREQUENCY（判成STAND）+ CHANGE（判成SORT）两个 fuzzy 边界判错** → 下次专 drill 这俩。
- **ENUMERATE 最难**：内容（冷想例子）也卡，不只词 → frame 减负（命名1+1意外，别凑5）。
- **长度**：Q4 偏短触发**给 skill 加长度检查**（micro-scorecard 4→5 钩子，太短加一拍展开/land 不加列举项）。
- 🔴 **error zone 确认**（答后扫抓到的）：搭配/介词（prepare **for** / come across a problem / manage≠manager / change jobs）> 冠词（the number / the huge population / a plan）> 形近词（**lumber→number** Pattern 16）。-s 基本守住。

## 💬 Session note · 状态对话 + 计划转向（重要,影响后续 coaching）

> 6 题后 suzy **表达灰心**："花了那么多时间，主动输出还是想不起来用不起来。"举例 `When you run your own business` —— when/run 都不难,但"用于自己的生意"临场拼不出来。并**怀疑自己"天赋失衡"**：高中没辅导几周自学完大学物理+微积分、大学不听课,但英语投入巨量时间仍"听不懂说不出写不出"。

**教练回应（记下来,以后她再灰心同样的话能复用,详见 memory `feedback_morale_automaticity_reframe`）：**
1. **`run a business` 这类失败 = 最不像天赋、最好修的一种**：词都认识、临场拼不出 = **认得出（知识在）≠ 拼得出（automaticity 没到）**。读到立刻懂 → 是产出重复次数不够,不是智商。chunk 训练补的就是这格,是短桥不是从零盖。
2. **不是"天赋失衡",是 ONE 认知风格**：她的超能力=抓底层生成规则、跳过重复、自己推。物理/数学是规则系统 → 这手是超能力。**语言产出没有"生成器"可抓**,靠重复堆到自动 → 同一手砍不动。**让她物理封神的习惯("懂了就不用练")恰是语言产出最坑她的习惯。** 不是英语天赋低,是最锋利的刀对这块料不管用,而她从没被逼用过"靠重复堆自动"那把慢刀。
3. **"听不懂/说不出/写不出"是三套系统**：她**阅读 7-7.5 = 强接收端**。输入>>输出的巨大落差 = 靠阅读/分析建英语者的标准指纹,不是失衡天赋。
4. **诚实**：没捷径、时间不成比例地多（她太习惯顿悟省重复,这里省不掉）。但**几周啃微积分那股纪律 = 堆重复的引擎本身**,从"理解"调头指向"产出重复"照样转。
5. **当场没推 drill**,让她歇。

**进步曲线证据（灰心时拿这个对照）**：6/21 进场死机每拍接龙 → 6/22 老05 首次完全独立 cold → 6/22-24 只接龙开头一句剩下自驱 → 两次结构自我把关 → 今天 P3 喂一个词立刻产出+自我升级。**一周内接龙依赖从"每一拍"退到"只剩开头一句"。**
