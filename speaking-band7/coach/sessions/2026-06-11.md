> So, the piece of technology I'd really love to own is a high-end 3D printer. I actually already have an entry-level one — it cost me around four thousand yuan — but I'd love to upgrade... I got into it through a colleague of mine, who's a real enthusiast — he even builds his own machines from spare parts... There are a couple of reasons I want a better one. The main one is my son... On top of that, it's a sneaky way to get him into engineering: he gets to see how a model on the screen slowly turns into a real object... What makes it stand out, for me, is that it's basically a giant toy for both of us. On a weekend, with my son sitting right next to me, we'll design something together and watch the printer build it up layer by layer. So yeah, it's brought us a lot closer.

## P3 ×6（配对,technology）

### P3-1 differences past vs today — suzy 先 cold 准备(暴露抓手问题)
**suzy 准备版**:"The biggest difference is the development of semiconductors. Based on this, people invented cell phones, computers, and the internet, making it much easier... In the past, otherwise, information... is hard to access for ordinary people."
**诊断**:
- ⭐ **核心卡点=没抓手**:一上来想"semiconductors(抽象根源)"+"20世纪之前(时间太大)"→ 展不开。
- ✅ `making it much easier for…` 展开动作好(写作 texture 自然做到)
- 🔴 `is → was`(讲过去用过去时;同 production 时态滑) / 🔴 `otherwise → by contrast`(otherwise=否则,误用)
**教的"过去 vs 今天 抓手三步"**:① 锚定 ONE 具体日常(拍照/看新闻/购物),别从抽象根源起 ② "过去"=~20-30年前(一代人),不到世纪 ③ then→now 小故事+例子+(可选)第二角度。
**教的换皮总结 frame**:`So the shift is from [旧] to [新]`(逼你换词复述=LR flexibility,接 Daily Paraphrase Drill)。
