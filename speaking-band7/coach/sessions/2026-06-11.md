## P3 ×6（配对,technology）

### P3-1 differences past vs today — suzy 先 cold 准备(暴露抓手问题)
**suzy 准备版**:"The biggest difference is the development of semiconductors. Based on this, people invented cell phones, computers, and the internet, making it much easier... In the past, otherwise, information... is hard to access for ordinary people."
**诊断**:
- ⭐ **核心卡点=没抓手**:一上来想"semiconductors(抽象根源)"+"20世纪之前(时间太大)"→ 展不开。
- ✅ `making it much easier for…` 展开动作好(写作 texture 自然做到)
- 🔴 `is → was`(讲过去用过去时;同 production 时态滑) / 🔴 `otherwise → by contrast`(otherwise=否则,误用)
**教的"过去 vs 今天 抓手三步"**:① 锚定 ONE 具体日常(拍照/看新闻/购物),别从抽象根源起 ② "过去"=~20-30年前(一代人),不到世纪 ③ then→now 小故事+例子+(可选)第二角度。
**教的换皮总结 frame**:`So the shift is from [旧] to [新]`(逼你换词复述=LR flexibility,接 Daily Paraphrase Drill)。

### 📄 P3 完整范文（6 题,tech 域,可朗读/复习/偷 chunk —— 持久化全文）

**P3-1 Differences between the technology of the past and today?**
> Well, the biggest difference is probably how connected everything is now. Whereas in the past, technology was mostly standalone — a phone just made calls, a camera just took photos — today almost every device is smart and linked to the internet. Take something like a watch: it used to just tell the time, but now it tracks your health, answers your messages, all of that. On top of that, technology used to be a luxury, whereas now it's everywhere and pretty affordable. So I'd say the main shift is from simple, separate gadgets to one connected ecosystem.

**P3-2 What technology do young people like to use?**
> Well, I'd say young people these days are really drawn to anything social and instant. The obvious one is short-video apps like TikTok or Douyin — they tend to spend hours scrolling through them. On top of that, mobile gaming is huge; a lot of them play games like Honour of Kings with their friends online. And increasingly, wearables — a smartwatch that tracks their steps, their sleep, that kind of thing. What they're really after is stuff that's quick, social and entertaining, whereas older people tend to use tech more for practical things, like messaging or paying. So young people lean towards fun and connection.

**P3-3 Differences between online and face-to-face communication?**
> Well, the main difference comes down to depth versus convenience. Online, you can reach anyone instantly, no matter where they are — but a lot gets lost. Whereas face-to-face, you pick up on tone, facial expressions, body language — all the things a text message just can't carry. For example, a joke that lands perfectly in person can come across as rude in a chat, because the other person can't hear your tone. So online wins on speed and reach, whereas in person wins on warmth.

**P3-4 Has technology changed the way people communicate?**
> Definitely — there's no question that it's transformed how we communicate, for better and worse. On the plus side, we can now stay in touch with people across the world in real time; my family has a group chat that keeps everyone connected, even when we're far apart. On the other hand, it's made communication a lot more shallow — people text instead of calling, and a lot of conversations happen through screens. So yes, it's definitely changed things, but I'd say it's a double-edged sword.

**P3-5 Negative effects of technology on relationships?**
> Well, the biggest downside is probably that it pulls people apart even when they're in the same room. You see it all the time — a family sitting at dinner, but everyone's glued to their phone instead of talking. On top of that, a lot of relationships now stay shallow, because it's so easy to just like a post instead of actually checking in on someone. There's also the comparison trap — people see everyone's highlight reel online and end up feeling worse about their own lives. So while technology connects us, it can quietly weaken the relationships right in front of us.

**P3-6 Differences between making friends in real life and online?**
> The main difference is how deep the connection tends to go. In real life, friendships usually build slowly through shared experiences — you do things together, and trust grows over time. Whereas online, you can make friends quickly with people who share your interests, even across the world, but those bonds can be a bit fragile. For example, I've got a few online friends I game with, but honestly, if one of them disappeared tomorrow, it wouldn't hit me the way a real-life friend would. So online is wider, whereas real life is deeper.

**P3 公式（贯穿 6 题）**:`直接答/表态 → because/Whereas 对比 → like/Take…例子 → On top of that 第二点 → So I'd say 收尾`。长度 4-6 句/30-45s。
**-s 聚焦**:单数+s(a joke **lands** / online **wins** / my family **has** / trust **grows** / technology **connects** / it **tracks**) vs 复数光杆(people **see** / friendships **build** / conversations **happen**)。
**高迁移 chunk**:be drawn to / what they're after is / lean towards / comes down to X vs Y / come across as / a double-edged sword / pull people apart / glued to phone / highlight reel / check in on / it wouldn't hit me the way… / from X to Y(换皮总结)。
