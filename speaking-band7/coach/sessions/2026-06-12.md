### 🎯 -s 诊断（最有启发）
- ✅ **短句全对**:he **helps** / **deepens** / everyone **needs** / he **solves**
- 🔴 **长/满句掉**:`he **respond**`→responds / `**don't** make`→doesn't / `everyone **were**`→**was**(everyone=单数)
- **规律**:句子一长一满,带宽被内容占满 → -s 掉。**非知识,是顾不上**。解药=长句说完 1 秒回扫主语。

### 🔴 其它
- `The person I'd like to talk about [ ] an old friend` → 漏系动词 **is**
- `...error logs**,** Zhangwei started...` → comma splice → 句号 / `while everyone was…, Zhangwei…`
- 🟡 made→**makes**(描述特质用现在) / no matter **whatever**→what / helps **other**→others
