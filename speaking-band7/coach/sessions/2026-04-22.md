## Q1 (Q119): When was the last time you visited a museum?

**suzy 原句：**
Actually, last weekend. I just went to the natural history museum of chengdu with my son. he is a big fun of dinosaurs. He stood in front of a T-Rex skeleton for nearly 20 minutes in the museum, it was pretty remarkable for a shy boy, who barely sit quietly still. Museums are some of our most peaceful outings, because he is genuinely focused.

**诊断：**
1. 🔴 "a big fun" → **"a big fan"** — 同音词混淆。fun = 乐趣，fan = 粉丝
2. 🔴 "who barely sit quietly still" — 三重问题：第三人称 -s 漏（sit → sits）+ 情态动词缺（can barely sit）+ "quietly still" 语义重叠 → **"who can barely sit still"**
3. 🟡 "Museums are some of our most peaceful outings" → **"Museum trips are..."**（博物馆本身不是 outings）
4. 🟡 "museum of Chengdu" → "museum in Chengdu"（P12：场馆用 in，不用 of）

**亮点：**
- ✅ `Actually` 引入对比事件，功能正确
- ✅ `just` 持续稳定（已毕业）
- ✅ `genuinely focused` 自然口语
- ✅ 叙事弧线完整：时间→场景→细节→评论→收尾
- ✅ 动词无泛化（went / stood）

**精修版：**
> Actually, last weekend. I just went to the Natural History Museum in Chengdu with my son — he's a big fan of dinosaurs. He stood in front of a T-Rex skeleton for nearly 20 minutes, which was pretty remarkable for a shy boy who can barely sit still. Museum trips are some of our most peaceful outings, because he's genuinely focused.

**范文（Band 7）：**
> Actually, just last weekend. I took my son to the Natural History Museum in Chengdu — he's absolutely obsessed with dinosaurs. He just stood there in front of a T-Rex skeleton for a good 20 minutes, completely locked in. Pretty remarkable, though — because at home he can barely sit still for five. Museum trips are actually some of the most peaceful outings we do as a family. He's just so focused, I don't even need to say anything.

**教的表达：**
- `absolutely obsessed with`（替代 big fan of，强度升级）
- `locked in`（专注到入神）
- `Pretty ___, though — because ___`（短评论后接反差解释，句尾 though）

## Q2 (Q123): How do you feel after taking a nap?

**suzy 原句：**
Perfect, I love sleeping. With a full-time job and a five-time-old, I always have rare 'Me' time to do something I'm into, include taking nap. On weekdays, I'v got a kindergarten run by 8am, then straight to work, so my morning is so packed. After lunch, though, I guard my nap time like it's sacred

**诊断：**
1. 🔴 "a five-time-old" → **"a five-year-old"** — compound: year, not time
2. 🔴 "I always have rare 'Me' time" → **"real 'me' time is pretty rare"** — 逻辑矛盾，always + rare 不通
3. 🟡 "include taking nap" → **"including taking a nap"** — gerund（-ing）+ 冠词 a
4. 小项：`my morning is so packed` → `my mornings are packed`（习惯性复数）
5. ⚠️ 未正面回答题目"nap 之后感觉怎样"——描述了何时 nap，但缺感受

**亮点：**
- ✅ **"After lunch, though"** — `though` 对比功能正确自然，**毕业达成** 🎓（count 3/3）
- ✅ **"I guard my nap time like it's sacred"** — 从范文内化后自然产出，吸收成功
- ✅ "straight to work" / "pretty packed" — 口语地道，无书面词
- ✅ P9/P13 本题均未触发

**精修版：**
> Perfect, I love sleeping. With a full-time job and a five-year-old, real "me" time is pretty rare. On weekdays I've got the kindergarten run by 8am, then straight to work, so my mornings are pretty packed. After lunch, though, I guard my nap time like it's sacred. Just 15 minutes and I feel completely reset — sharper, more focused. Accidentally sleep an hour, though — and I'm done for.

**范文（Band 7）：**
> Perfect, I love sleeping. With a full-time job and a five-year-old, real me-time is pretty rare. My mornings are non-stop — kindergarten drop-off by eight, then straight to work. After lunch, though, I guard my nap time like it's sacred. A quick 15 minutes and I feel completely reset. Accidentally sleep for an hour, though, and I'm done for — groggy for the rest of the afternoon.

## Q3 (Q127): Do you have anything to share recently?

**suzy 原句：**
a gift from my son. Last week, he gave me a drawing by himself, where there was three figures. He just said that the tallest one is me, the middle mom, and the shortest himself. The pictures looked pretty rough, honetly. But I love it, it was one of the best presents I'd ever received. It's the thought than counts, I suppose.

**诊断：**
1. 🔴 **"the thought than counts"** → **"the thought *that* counts"** — typo（than vs that）
2. 🔴 **"there was three figures"** → **"there were three figures"** — 主谓一致，三人 = 复数 = were
3. 🟡 **"a drawing by himself"** → **"a drawing he'd made himself"** — "by himself" 有"独自完成"义但结构略歧义；口语更直接说动作

**亮点：**
- ✅ **"It's the thought that counts"** — 冷启动产出，首次成功 ✓（count 0→1）
- ✅ **"one of the best presents I'd ever received"** — past perfect 正确，未简化 ✓
- ✅ "pretty rough, honestly" — 自然口语习惯表达，稳定
- ✅ P9/P13 均未触发
- ✅ 叙事弧线 Band 7：答题→细节→视觉图像→诚实自评→情感收尾

**精修版：**
> A gift from my son. Last week he gave me a drawing he'd made himself, where there were three figures. He just told me: the tallest one was me, the middle one was mum, and the shortest one was himself. It looked pretty rough, honestly. But I loved it — it was one of the best presents I'd ever received. It's the thought that counts, I suppose.

**范文（Band 7）：**
> Oh, a gift from my son, actually. Last week he gave me a drawing he'd done all by himself — three figures. He just pointed and said: the tall one's me, the middle one's mum, and the little one's him. It looked pretty rough, honestly. But I genuinely loved it. Probably one of the best gifts I've ever been given. I guess it's the thought that counts.
