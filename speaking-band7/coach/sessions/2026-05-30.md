**Prompt**: `My grandpa is the most patient person I know — like ___`
**suzy**: "he once spend a whole day to teach me how to plant flowers."
- ✅ 内容对（陪一整天 = 有耐心），单展开式结构对
- 🔴 **时态**：`once spend` → `once **spent**`（once = 过去具体事件）—— **今日盯点**，P6 时态压力下丢（5/28 是反向 drew→draws）
- 🟡 **搭配**：`spend a whole day to teach` → `spent a whole day **teaching**`（spend + 时间 + doing，非 to do）
- 教：撑大动作 = like 引例 + 再加一句"他没生气"细节（1 句→3 句活句）

## P2 (Mode: P2-B 骨架填充)

**题目**：Describe a city you enjoyed visiting.（example p2_old_25，persona = 京都）

**流程**：给 4 段骨架（起手/连接词已填）→ suzy 填自产内容 + 至少 2 处 like 展开。1 min prep。

**suzy 产出**（原文）：
> "So, the city I'd like to talk about is Kyoto, where is located in the western of Japan. I went there 2 years ago, and we stayed for about 1 week. I went with my wife and son. We did loads there — wondering around the neighborhoods without any vehicles, exploring the sites like temples or shrines, and tasting food at some corners of streets. On top of that, we spent a whole morning strolling along the Kamogawa River, fully enjoying the peaceful vibe unique to Kyoto. But what I really loved was how modern lifestyle in Kyoto combines with traditional culture. I mean, you even could shop Apple products in a ancient structure. So yeah, it was well worth the trip."

### P2-B 5 维打分
| 维度 | 结果 |
|------|------|
| 起手（§2）| ✓ "So, the city I'd like to talk about is..." |
| 中段延伸（§3a / like 展开）| ✗ 只用列举式 like（like temples or shrines），无单展开式 like |
| 收尾（§4）| ✓ "So yeah, it was well worth the trip" |
| bullets 触达 | 4/4 ✓ |
| 时长 | ~50s（目标 90s+，差一半）|

### 诊断（top 3）
1. 🎯 **字数不够的真因 = 没用 like 单展开式**（不是副词不够）。列举式 like 只 +3-5 词；单展开式 `— like, [具体小故事]` +15-25 词/次，把 110 → 190。这是"一句话没了"的解药。
2. 🔴 `Kyoto, where is located in the western of Japan` → `Kyoto, in western Japan`（where is 关系代词错 + the western → western，国家不加 the）
3. 🔴 `wondering around` → `wandering around`（wonder 思考 / wander 漫步，同音坑）
4. 🔴 `you even could shop Apple products in a ancient structure` → `you could even buy Apple products inside an ancient building`（语序 even 在情态后 / shop→buy / a→an）

### 范文要点（演示 like 展开撑到 90s+）
3 处 `— like, [小故事]` + 1 处 that kind of thing，每 like +15-20 词 → 110 词撑到 190。范文详见当日对话。

### suzy 自己的元认知问题
"除了不会写长句，写短句时也不太会用副词" —— 回应：副词是调味料（+1 词），撑句长的引擎是 like 展开 + 关系从句 + that kind of thing 收尾，副词排第二。
