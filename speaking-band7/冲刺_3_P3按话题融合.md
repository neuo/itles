🎯 **通用套路归纳**：STANCE菜单：`A few basic ones, really` / `Yeah, for the most part, I'd say` / `Absolutely, I think so` / `Mainly by example, I'd say`。招牌 chunk：`feel like a cage` · `keeps society safe / running smoothly` · `Kids copy what they see` · `because I said so`(反例) · `setting a good example`。万能 LAND：`keeping it simple and fair` / `at the end of the day, rules are there to protect us`。
