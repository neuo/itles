#489 · in no time（很快、没一会儿就…）
  触发原话：The homeowners went to property management as a group, and the problem was fixed in no time(这个词组学下)—there's strength in numbers.
  问题是什么：in no time ＝ 没一会儿就、很快就（强调快得出乎意料），挂在句尾修饰整个动作：
      the problem was fixed in no time · You'll pick it up in no time. · We got there in no time.
