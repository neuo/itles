#### 1 · #26（比较题必须说出另一边）→ ✅
**她的原话**
> The key thing is freedom. Working alone gives individuals the freedom to make decisions quickly, and allows them to maintain complete control over their pace and focus without distraction. On the other hand, working in a group requires more time and energy for communication.

**档位 ✅** —— 考点位置（"另一边"）达成：`On the other hand, working in a group requires…` 把 B 那一边说出来了。
五层：层1 无错 ｜ 层2 individuals/maintain/without distraction 三处书面 ｜ 层3 On the other hand 用对 ｜
层4 freedom → 两个下位（decisions quickly / control over pace）→ 对照，层次完整 ｜ 层5 有"为什么"（freedom）。
理解侧七条：并列两边同形 ✅（gives… and allows…）｜ 其余本层无对象。

① **最小修改版**（无 ❌）＝ 原句逐字。
② **更好版**
> The key thing is freedom. Working alone means **you** can make decisions quickly, and **you stay in full control of** your own pace, **with nothing pulling you away**. Working in a group, on the other hand, **eats up** a lot more time and energy just on communication.

③ **diff**
| 原句 | 改后 | 档 | 理由 |
|---|---|---|---|
| gives individuals the freedom to | means you can | ⚠️ | individuals 是书面词；口语默认第二人称 you |

| without distraction | with nothing pulling you away | ⚠️ | 抽象名词 → 画面（同 #206 书面词降级族） |
| requires more time and energy | eats up a lot more time and energy | ⚠️ | require 书面；eat up 带损耗的画面 |
