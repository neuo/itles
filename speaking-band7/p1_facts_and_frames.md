# P1 Facts & Frames — 考前预载,降 cold 启动

> **用途**：把"考场现场想"搬到"考前预载"。两块——
> ① **人设事实卡**：你真实生活的固定事实,锁一致性(P1 三题连问同一话题,facts 不能自相矛盾)。
> ② **骨架 / 常用句**：你在 Gemini 大盘里反复用的开头/转折/举例句式,预背这些,cold 时先有壳,再往里填事实。
>
> **你的练习循环**（记录在案,别丢）：想素材(不现场想) → 对着中文找不会翻的(重点学) → 不看英文盲录 → 听录音 → 复录到流利。盲录+复录 = 修弱腿(说+听),不是读认。

> **目标不是"整体用英语思考"**（来得很晚,不等它）——是**这些高频场景各自变条件反射**:cue 一来直接触发英文,跳过中文。滚够遍数就到了。

## Part 2 — 骨架 / 常用句（cross-topic,Gemini 措辞保留）

> 这些是你大盘里**反复出现**的句式。背这批,cold 时先出壳,再填 Part 1 的事实。按功能分。

### 开头 / 立场（先出一句,别卡）
- `To be honest, ...` / `Honestly, ...`
- `Yeah, a little bit.` / `Yeah, absolutely.` / `Of course.`
- `Not really, no.` / `No way, ...`
- `Definitely ...` / `I'd definitely go with ...` / `I'd probably go with ...`
- `It depends on ...` / `It's hard to say whether I ___ or not.`

### 喜欢 / 不喜欢
- `I'm actually pretty into X.` / `I'm actually pretty good at X.`
- `I'm not really into X.` / `I'm not really a big fan.`
- `X is definitely one of my absolute favorites.`
- `I kind of like it, but then again, I kind of don't.`（矛盾态度,很好用)

### 给理由
- `mostly because ...` / `part of it is also because ...`
- `For one thing, ...; and for another, ...`
- `Mainly because ...`

### 加信息 / 转折（P1 拉长的关键)
- `Other than that, ...` / `On top of that, ...` / `Plus, ...`
- `That said, ...` / `But then again, ...`
- `By comparison, ...` / `In contrast, ...`
- `More importantly, ...`

### 举例（bare claim 后立刻加一层)
- `like X, Y, and even Z`
- `for example, ...` / `such as ...`

### 过去 / 对比过去
- `Back when I was a kid, ...` / `Back then, ...`
- `I used to ...` / `Growing up in a small town, ...`

### 犹豫 / 拖延（想不出内容时的合法填充)
- `Let me think ...` / `This question is actually a bit tough.`
- `I honestly haven't paid much attention to that.`
- `I'm not really sure ...`

### 家庭视角（你的招牌角度,几乎任何题能拐过去)
- `my wife usually handles ...` / `for my son ...` / `with my kid ...`

### 收尾 / 升一层
- `at least from my own perspective`
- `it just puts me in a great mood` / `it completely changes my vibe for the whole day`
- `I really miss those days.`

## Part 4 — 句型 DRILL 索引（第二根轴：按句型练）

> **来源**：从 [[p1_answers_bank.md]] 154 条答案里,把她**反复用**的句型抽出来,每类配 verbatim 例句(标话题)。
> **练法**：挑一个句型,跨多个话题连说 5 句同结构的 → 把这个"句子形状"练成条件反射。和 Part 2 骨架配合(Part 2 是清单,这里是带真实例句的 drill)。

### opener_stance — 开口先站队/表态（To be honest / Of course / Definitely / No）

- "Absolutely. A good living environment keeps you in a great mood and really boosts your productivity." (Home/Accommodation)
- "To be honest, when I was a kid, I didn't really care much about parks." (Parks & Gardens)
- "Of course I like it. I lived there for 18 years until I went to college." (Hometown)
- "Definitely. The one that left the deepest impression on me was my trip to Jiuzhaigou." (Scenery)
- "No, I don't. Whether it was when I was a kid or even now, I've never really known much about my hometown's culture." (Hometown)
- "I definitely prefer happy music." (Music)

### preference — 表达喜好/偏爱（I'm into X / not really big on X / I prefer）

- "I'm not really into it, to be honest. I rarely shop for anything." (Shopping)
- "I'm not really big on looking in the mirror, but I still do it once or twice a day." (Tidiness/Mirrors)
- "I definitely prefer watching them at home." (Movies/TV/Comedy)
- "I actually really like it. Back when I was in school, I was pretty good at physics and chemistry." (Science)
- "I'd definitely go with happy music. It keeps me in a good mood." (Music)

### reason — 给理由（mostly because / for one thing... for another）

- "Mainly because I really enjoy programming, and also because the salary in the Chinese internet industry is a bit higher." (Work/Study)
- "For one thing, it's cheaper, and for another, there's no time pressure—I can watch whenever I want." (Movies/TV/Comedy)
- "For one, I've managed to save up enough money to get by, and secondly, after working for 14 years, I'm honestly just pretty tired of the daily grind." (Work/Study)
- "For one thing, there are tons of different science museums... For another, these places are great for family outings." (Science)
- "It's mostly because I just can't seem to find any interest in the movies that have come out over the last couple of years." (Movies/TV/Comedy)

### contrast_addition — 转折或追加（Other than that / That said / On top of that / by comparison）

- "Other than that, I don't really have any reason to look in the mirror." (Tidiness/Mirrors)
- "I love programming, but the actual work at a job always gets super repetitive. On top of that, you have to work overtime sometimes." (Work/Study)
- "By comparison, I prefer Japanese cities since they have their own distinct character." (Scenery)
- "Aside from that, we barely know anyone else." (Neighborhood/Neighbors)
- "Other than that, I just hope to have a relatively stable job with no overtime." (Getting Older/Values)

### example — 举例（like X, Y and even Z / for example / for instance）

- "There are a lot more industries these days compared to when I was a kid; for instance, new energy and tourism have been doing really well." (Hometown)
- "Xiaohongshu, Douyin, and WeChat are super popular in China." (Social Media)
- "For example, AirPods are so comfortable for me that I can wear them all day long except when I'm sleeping." (Headphones/Earbuds)
- "which is a really famous city, whether it's for the pandas, the hotpot, or just the relaxed lifestyle." (Your City (Chengdu))
- "super casual, like just a T-shirt and sneakers." (Clothes)

### past_habit — 过去的习惯/状态（I used to / back when I was a kid / growing up）

- "I absolutely love eating them, and I used to have them almost every morning when I was a kid." (Food & Noodles)
- "I grew up in a small county town, living in a tiny apartment unit." (Home/Accommodation)
- "I used to plan on staying here forever." (Home/Accommodation)
- "Back then, high school was rough, and I used to fall asleep in class all the time." (Teachers)
- "I used to be a bit addicted. A year ago, I was pretty hooked on Xiaohongshu." (Social Media)
- "Back when I was in school, I was pretty good at physics and chemistry." (Science)

### hedge_uncertain — 模糊/不确定缓冲（it's hard to say / not really sure / a bit tough）

- "So it's hard to say whether I liked them or not." (Parks & Gardens)
- "Let me think... I know a singer named Chris Lee is from Chengdu... This question is actually a bit tough." (Your City (Chengdu))
- "I'm not really sure, I barely watch any shows, regardless of the type." (Movies/TV/Comedy)
- "It's a tough question to answer because I never drive—never." (Cars & Driving)
- "I haven't really made up my mind yet." (Home/Accommodation)

### family_angle — 家人视角（my wife usually handles / for my son）

- "my wife usually handles all the buying, whether it's for the house or for me personally." (Shopping)
- "My wife is also the one in charge of buying clothes for me." (Clothes)
- "My wife always reminds me to shave, so I check just because of that." (Tidiness/Mirrors)
- "our living room has plenty of space for my wife and me to work and hang out, and for my son to play." (Home/Accommodation)
- "Showing some optical phenomena to my 5-year-old son definitely counts." (Science)

### concession — 让步（I kind of like it but... / X, though / even though）

- "I kind of like it, but then again, I kind of don't." (Work/Study)
- "It's not huge, but it's enough for the three of us." (Home/Accommodation)
- "Even though public parks can get pretty crowded, it's still a rare and great place to unwind." (Parks & Gardens)
- "Even though my current place isn't huge either, at least now I have a bedroom of my own." (Home/Accommodation)
- "I'm still keeping it updated, even though not many people pay attention to it." (Social Media)

### comparative — 比较（I prefer A to B / more X than / compared to）

- "They made me realize that understanding the logic behind things is way more important than just getting a correct answer." (Teachers)
- "multiple screens, my PC tower at home—it's way better than being at the office." (Home/Accommodation)
- "It's a small city compared to Chengdu." (Hometown)
- "the biggest difference compared to my twenties is that I actually know what I want and what truly matters." (Getting Older/Values)
- "Still, there are way fewer job opportunities than in big cities." (Hometown)

### conditional — 视情况而定（it depends on / if... then）

- "I think it depends entirely on the specific brand." (Headphones/Earbuds)
- "I think it all comes down to whether you actually need it. If you can't use your phone, then the use of a watch is pretty obvious." (Watches)
- "That depends on the city you live in." (Science)
- "But if I were to imagine it, I'd probably prefer a private garden because there are fewer people." (Parks & Gardens)
- "It depends on how you look at it." (Getting Older/Values)

### superlative_favorite — 最爱/之最（one of my absolute favorites）

- "I have one absolute favorite. Pretty much all of my tops are T-shirts." (Clothes)
- "Earbuds are my absolute favorite, especially AirPods." (Headphones/Earbuds)
- "The one that left the deepest impression on me was my trip to Jiuzhaigou." (Scenery)

### future_want — 将来的意愿/设想（I'm thinking about getting / if someone gave me one I'd want）

- "No, but if someone were to actually give me one, I'd want an Apple Watch so I could use it to track my sleep." (Watches)
- "Actually, I'm thinking about going back to school." (Work/Study)
- "I know they also have a hearing assist feature, so I'm planning to get a pair for my grandma." (Headphones/Earbuds)
- "Maybe in the future, I can move to a different country or city to make that happen." (Home/Accommodation)
