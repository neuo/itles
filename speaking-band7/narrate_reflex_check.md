## ⭐ 核心发现（这个练习的价值）

**"然后呢"轨道真正当主引擎的只有 ~10/24 张**（真事件 + 人生弧）。其余靠别的动作撑：人物题靠**双倍佩服**、习惯人物靠**习惯描述+1 个嵌入场景**、信念题靠**前后对比**、"没享受/给建议"靠**堆理由**。所以：

1. **NARRATE 要再分 5 个亚型**，每个告诉你**主引擎是什么** —— 否则你对着"习惯人物 / 改变看法"题狂问"然后呢"会**更卡**（轨道返回空）。
2. **3 张错标**（thenCount=0，轨道全空）：`新03 无聊地方` / `老15 没享受的音乐` / `老04 给建议` —— 见下方改动。
3. **90 秒天花板**：循环从 6 颗珠子只到 ~78-95 秒，**最后 30 秒是预载问题不是反射问题**（薄卡加珠子 / 加第二个场景）。

## 5 个亚型（每个的主引擎不同 = 真正的指导性）

- **(a) 单场景事件** —— 主引擎 = **时间轨**。问"然后呢"一路推到底。这是反射最干净的地方（新04/新10/新16/老09/老21）。ZERO-PLOT 煮咖啡 drill 直接练这个。
- **(b) 人生弧** —— 主引擎 = **传记时间线**（高中→外婆病→现在大学）。"然后呢"放大到年的尺度。尾巴必接 2 句性格理由（predictable handoff）。
- **(c) 危机场景人物** —— 主引擎 = **1 个硬场景 + 双倍佩服**。HOOK→ANGLE(佩服)→1 个 THEN 危机→OUTCOME→**再说一遍为什么佩服**。轨道只开一次，admire 才是 load-bearer。**person 题"双倍 take"是设计不是缺陷。**
- **(d) 习惯人物** —— 主引擎 = **习惯描述(DESCRIBE)+ 1 个嵌入场景**。`every day he…` 是习惯小走位（DESCRIBE），不是时间拍。别狂问"然后呢"——先描述习惯，再嵌**一个**"我记得有次"。这亚型其实半个 DESCRIBE。
- **(e) 信念/感受弧** —— 主引擎 = **前/后对比**（旧观点→触发→新观点）。其实更接近 EXPLAIN 的 before/after，不是真事件。"然后呢"是伪的（事件是内部信念位移）。

## 正向演示：从珠子 → 跑循环 → 成文（5 张，零新增观点）

> 每行 = [步] 内心问的问题 → 产出的句子。证明循环是**生成器**不是标签。

### 新07 Zhangwei（c 危机场景人物，~95s）
珠子：发小Zhangwei小学到高中 / 最helpful耐心 / 电脑社搞小程序 / 一次项目崩他冷静一行行查20分钟修好救场 / 他说讲题帮自己理解=helping is learning / 佩服抗压+乐于助人
- [HOOK] 是什么+锚 → *The person I'd like to talk about is one of my childhood friends, Zhangwei — we met in primary school when we were around twelve.*
- [ANGLE] person→为什么佩服(一个take) → *He's easily the most helpful and patient person I know.*
- [START] 哪开始(场景锚) → *Back then we spent a lot of time together, especially in the school's computer club, where we'd mess around building little programs.*
- [THEN] 然后呢(引出那一次) → *Then one time we were working on a science project, and our program suddenly crashed right before the presentation.*
- [I-DID] 他在场做了啥 → *While the rest of us were panicking, Zhangwei just calmly sat down and started checking the code line by line.*
- [OUTCOME] 怎么收场 → *He fixed the bug within twenty minutes — he basically saved the day.*
- [THEN] 再问一拍(免费,引出为何爱帮) → *Afterwards he told me something interesting — explaining things to others actually helps him understand them better.*
- [LAND] 对我意味啥,回扣佩服 → *So helping wasn't a chore for him, it was a way of learning — and that's why I really admire him.*

### 新04 日出（a 单场景事件，~80s）—— 轨道几乎包办
珠子：4:30摸黑爬山看日出 / 跟几个朋友 / 打手电摸黑爬到顶刚好日出前 / 坐着等又冷又困 / 太阳出来满天橙下面是云太值了 / 本来累看完一整天有精神
- [HOOK] 时间+锚 → *One time that comes to mind is when I got up really early to hike up a mountain and watch the sunrise — a trip a few years ago.*
- [ANGLE] event→为什么这次 → *I had to get up at around 4:30 in the dark, and it ended up being completely worth it.*
- [START] 哪开始 → *I went with a couple of friends, and we started climbing up in the dark with our flashlights.*
- [THEN] 然后呢 → *It was a bit scary, but we made it to the top just before the sun came up.*
- [I-DID] 我们做了啥 → *Then we just sat there, freezing and half-asleep, waiting for it.*
- [OUTCOME] payoff → *The moment the sun came up, the whole sky turned orange and you could see the clouds below us — breathtaking.*
- [LAND] 对我意味啥 → *So even though I was cold and tired at first, after that view I felt energized for the whole rest of the day.*

### 新11 软件团队（problem-solved，~82s）—— 轨道锚在"难点"
- [HOOK]→*a time I worked in a group on a software project, four or five of us.* [ANGLE]→*my job was the backend, and what stuck was how tough it got.* [START 难点]→*the deadline was really tight, and the requirements kept changing.* [THEN 应对]→*so we started having short daily meetings to stay on the same page.* [I-DID]→*and we worked overtime for a week or so to push it through.* [OUTCOME]→*in the end we delivered on time.* [LAND]→*a lot of pressure, but it felt great to pull it off together.*

### 新25 Zhangwei转AI（b/climb，~88s）—— 轨道=困难升起再被克服
- [HOOK]→*my friend Zhangwei, who completely switched his career.* [ANGLE]→*what stands out is his persistence — the difficulty was huge.* [START 底]→*his company moved into AI, but he had no ML background at all.* [THEN 放大难]→*so he had to learn a whole field from scratch while still doing his job — a lot of people would've given up.* [I-DID 攀爬]→*but he stuck with it: studied a little every day, online courses, small projects.* [OUTCOME 登顶]→*after about six months he got the hang of it and moved into the new role.* [LAND]→*I really admire him — hard work pays off.*

### 新26 改看法（e 信念弧，~78s）—— 轨道是伪的，本质前后对比
- [HOOK]→*a time I changed an important opinion — about what success means.* [ANGLE=旧观点]→*when I was younger I thought success was all about a top school and a big-name company.* [START]→*that was my view for a long time.* [THEN 转折]→*but after working a few years I slowly changed my mind.* [I-DID=我观察到]→*I saw people who looked successful on paper but weren't happy, and others with simple lives who seemed content.* [OUTCOME=新观点]→*so I started to feel being happy and doing what you enjoy matters way more.* [LAND]→*it made me a lot more relaxed — I stopped comparing myself and felt less anxious.*
> 注:这张 I-DID="我注意到"不是"我做了",循环会弯但不断 → 印证它其实是 EXPLAIN 的前后对比。
