## P2 答案

So, the job I'd like to talk about is a fairly flexible, self-directed role where I'd get to pick the problems I work on and solve them on my own terms, rather than just following someone else's orders all day.

I've known this is what I want for quite a while now. After years working in tech, I noticed I'm always at my best when nobody's micromanaging me — give me a tricky problem and some freedom, and I'll happily lose track of time.

To land a job like that, I'd need to sharpen a couple of things. Mainly the discipline to manage my own time well, plus the confidence to back my own judgement when there's no boss telling me what's right.

The reason it'd be perfect comes down to who I am. I'm curious by nature, and I really value my freedom — but at the same time, that kind of flexibility means I'd still be around for my family. So yeah, that's the job I'd love to have.
