## P2 答案

So, the law I'd like to talk about is a proper ban on single-use plastics — basically, no more plastic bags, straws or throwaway packaging in shops and restaurants across the country.

The reason people should get behind it is pretty simple, really. That kind of plastic gets used for a few minutes but then sticks around for hundreds of years, clogging up rivers and harming wildlife. If everyone just switched to reusable bags and paper straws, we'd cut down a huge amount of waste without much hassle.

Would it be popular? Honestly, I reckon it'd be a bit of a mixed bag at first. Shop owners might grumble about the cost, and people do love the convenience. But my wife works in city management, and she always says folks come round once a rule actually makes their streets cleaner.

So yeah, at the end of the day, I feel strongly about this one. It's a small change on paper, but it'd make a real difference for the planet.
