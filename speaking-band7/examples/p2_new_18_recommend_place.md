## P2 答案

So, the place I'd like to talk about is Kyoto, an old city in Japan that I've been to three times now with my family.

It's down in the western part of the country, not too far from Osaka, and it used to be Japan's capital, so it's absolutely packed with old temples and quiet gardens.

On our trips, we mostly took our son to see the cherry blossoms in spring, and one autumn we caught the maple leaves turning red, which was honestly stunning. We wandered around Kiyomizu Temple, took loads of photos, and spent a whole afternoon in the bamboo groves at Arashiyama. The food there was pretty amazing too.

The reason I'd recommend it is that it really slows you down. Plus, it's got this timeless feel you don't get in big modern cities. So all in all, Kyoto's one of those places I'll always remember, and I'd love to take my family back again sometime.
