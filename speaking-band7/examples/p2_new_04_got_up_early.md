So, the morning I'd like to talk about is one day last winter, when I dragged myself out of bed at around half past five to go running with my wife.

She's really into fitness — she works out three or four times a week — and she'd been trying to get me to join her for ages. That morning, I actually said yes, so we put on our gear and headed to a park nearby while it was still pitch dark outside.

The reason I got up so early was honestly just to keep her company. She'd told me the early air feels fresher, and that starting the day with exercise sets you up nicely. I'm not great at running, so I mostly jogged and gasped behind her.

To be honest, the first ten minutes were brutal, but once the sun came up, I felt surprisingly clear-headed and proud of myself. So yeah, that's the early morning I wanted to talk about — one of those small wins I still remember.
