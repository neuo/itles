So, the food I'd like to talk about is a homemade birthday cake — the one my wife bakes for our son, Muye, every single year.

The special occasion is obviously his birthday. We always throw a small party at home with a few of his little friends, and the cake is honestly the star of the show. Last year she went for a dinosaur-shaped one, since he's mad about dinosaurs.

As for how it's made, she usually whips up a sponge from scratch — eggs, flour, a bit of butter — then bakes it, lets it cool, and covers the whole thing in buttercream. The fun part is the decorating, which can take her a good couple of hours.

I reckon people make cakes like this because it turns an ordinary day into something memorable. You could just buy one, but a homemade cake carries far more love. So yeah, that's the food I wanted to talk about — it's become a little family tradition we all look forward to.
