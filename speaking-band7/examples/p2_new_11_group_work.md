## P2 答案

So, the time I'd like to talk about is a group project at work a couple of years back, when my team and I had to pull together a tricky proposal under a really tight deadline.

My job was basically to keep the pieces moving — I split the work up, chased people for updates, and stitched everything into one document at the end.

I worked with three colleagues, all from different parts of the company, which is exactly where the trouble started. Two of them clashed pretty badly over how to structure the whole thing, and for a couple of days nothing really got done. So I sat them both down, listened to each side, and we found a middle ground that kept everyone on the same page.

The reason I worked in a group, rather than going it alone, is that the task was just too big for one person. Plus, everyone brought a different strength to the table. So yeah, that's the experience I wanted to talk about.
