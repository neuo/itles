## P2 答案

So, the plan I'd like to talk about is a short family trip to Kyoto that we had to scrap at the last minute, only a couple of weeks ago.

We'd been planning it for ages — flights booked, hotel sorted, the whole lot. My wife and I were really looking forward to taking our five-year-old son, Muye, to see the autumn leaves. But just two days before we were due to fly out, Muye came down with a nasty fever, and the doctor told us he wasn't fit to travel.

So we had to rethink everything on the spot. Instead of flying abroad, we ended up staying put and turning our flat into a little holiday. We baked together, built a huge LEGO city on the floor, and watched films under a blanket fort.

Honestly, I was gutted at first, but it grew on me. At the end of the day, what mattered was the time together, not the destination.
