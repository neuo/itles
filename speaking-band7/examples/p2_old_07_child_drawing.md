## P2 答案

So, the child I'd like to talk about is my five-year-old son, Muye. He's really into drawing — it's pretty much his favourite thing to do at home.

I first noticed it about a year ago, when he started filling every blank piece of paper he could find. These days he draws almost every weekend, usually for an hour or two at a stretch.

What he loves most is drawing animals, especially dinosaurs. He's got this notebook full of colourful, slightly wonky T-Rexes, and he'll proudly show each one to us. One time he spent a whole afternoon on a single picture — a dinosaur having a birthday party — and he was so focused he forgot about his snacks.

I think the reason he loves it is that it lets him build his own little world. At five, he can't really put his feelings into words yet, but through drawing he expresses whatever's in his head. So yeah, that's why it means so much to him.
