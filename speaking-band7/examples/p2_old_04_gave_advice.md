## P2 答案

So, the time I'd like to talk about is a few months ago, when my wife was stretched too thin at work and I sat her down for a proper chat.

She's a civil servant in urban management at the city government, and although the job's stable, it's honestly pretty stressful, with overtime piling up. Back then she was thinking about pushing for a bigger role, and she looked exhausted all the time.

So one evening I told her to take a step back and not bite off more than she could chew. I said she didn't have to impress everyone, and that her health and our family mattered way more than a fancier title. She was hesitant at first, but eventually she eased off a bit, and within weeks she seemed much happier.

I gave her that advice because I could see she was about to burn herself out. So yeah, that's pretty much the moment I'm glad I spoke up.
