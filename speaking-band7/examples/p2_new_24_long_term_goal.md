So, the goal I'd like to talk about is finally carving out time to do my own independent research one day, instead of always building things for other people.

I'd say this idea has been quietly sitting at the back of my mind for about five or six years now. It really took shape after over a decade in tech, when I started feeling that I wanted to dig into questions purely out of curiosity, not because a deadline says so.

As for how I'll get there, I'm taking it step by step. I'm setting a bit of money aside each month so I'm not under pressure later, and I'm studying on the side, picking up the maths and theory I never properly learned. It's slow, but I'm chipping away at it.

The reason I set this goal is pretty simple — I want work that feels genuinely mine. At the end of the day, that sense of ownership is what keeps me going.
