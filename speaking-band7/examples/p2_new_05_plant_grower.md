# P2 新题 05 喜欢种菜的人 — grandfather (配角)

> **Cue card**: Describe a person who loves to grow plants (vegetables, flowers) at home or in the garden
> You should say: Who this person is / What plants he/she grows / How he/she grows the plants / And explain why he/she loves growing plants

## P2 答案

So, the person I'd like to talk about is my grandfather, who raised me when I was little. He's in his seventies now, and growing plants has been his passion for as long as I can remember.

He's turned the small balcony of his flat into a tiny green space. He grows all sorts of veggies there — mostly tomatoes, chilli peppers and spring onions — and a few pots of flowers along the railing just for colour.

As for how he does it, he's pretty hands-on about the whole process. He saves kitchen scraps to make his own compost and waters everything at dawn. On top of that, he'll spend ages just checking the leaves for bugs. He treats each plant like it's a member of the family, honestly.

I reckon the reason he loves it so much is that it keeps him active and gives him a real sense of purpose. Watching a seed grow clearly makes him happy. So yeah, that's the person I wanted to talk about.
