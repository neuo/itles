**Q3. Can technology help people solve their problems?**

**Q4. What's the difference between teaching someone and doing something for them?**
There's a huge gap, honestly. Doing it for someone is quicker, but they learn nothing and you're stuck helping forever. Teaching takes more patience, since you've got to slow down and let them stumble a bit. But I'd say it pays off — once they grasp it, they can stand on their own feet.

**Q6. How does helping each other affect relationships between people?**
