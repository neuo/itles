## P2 答案

So, the person I'd like to talk about is an old colleague of mine, Chen — someone who used to write code with me but now runs a little coffee shop that's doing really well.

We got to know each other years ago at the same company, and we'd often grab lunch together. Back then he was always a bit burnt out by the long hours, and one day he just decided he'd had enough. He'd been into making coffee at home for ages, so he quit and opened his own place near a university.

It's a small specialty café — he roasts his own beans in the back and only sells a handful of drinks, but each one's done properly. The atmosphere is really cosy, with soft music and a few plants, and a lot of students treat it as their second home.

I reckon it's successful because he genuinely cares about every cup, and word's spread. So yeah, that's the kind of business I really admire.
