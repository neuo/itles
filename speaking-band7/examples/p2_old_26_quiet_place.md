**Q1. Is it easy to find quiet places in your country? Why?**
Honestly, it's getting harder. In big cities like ours, there's traffic and crowds almost everywhere, so true peace and quiet are pretty rare. But if you know where to look, you'll still find little pockets — tucked-away parks, quiet temples, or spots by a river. They're just not as easy to stumble on as they used to be.

**Q2. How do people spend their leisure time in your country?**
Well, it really varies. Younger folk tend to scroll on their phones, watch shows, or meet up with mates for hotpot. Older people lean towards calmer stuff, like tai chi in the park or playing cards. I'd say eating out is a big one across all ages, since food's a huge part of how we relax and socialise here.

**Q3. How does technology affect the way people spend their leisure time?**
Massively, I reckon. These days people fill almost every spare minute with their phones — streaming, gaming, scrolling endlessly. It's super convenient, but it's also made us a bit lazier and more glued to screens. For instance, plenty of folk would rather binge a series at home than go out for a walk like they once did.

**Q4. Do you think only old people have time for leisure?**
Not at all, I'd say that's a bit of a myth. Sure, retired folk have more free hours, but everyone needs downtime to recharge. Even busy workers carve out evenings or weekends to unwind. To be fair, younger people might have less of it, but they still squeeze in leisure wherever they can — it's a basic human need, really.

**Q5. Why do old people prefer to live in quiet places?**
Well, I think it's mostly about peace of mind. After decades of hustle and bustle, older people crave somewhere calm where they can slow right down. Quiet places are gentler on them too — less noise, less stress, cleaner air. Plus, they often want a quieter pace to enjoy hobbies like gardening or simply sitting with a cup of tea.

**Q6. Why are there more noises made at home now than in the past?**
Mainly because of all the gadgets we own now. Homes are packed with TVs, speakers, washing machines, and kids glued to noisy games. In the past, places were simpler and quieter, with far fewer appliances humming away. Plus, walls in modern flats are often thinner, so you end up hearing your neighbours far more than before.
