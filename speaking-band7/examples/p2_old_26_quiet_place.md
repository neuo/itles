I stumbled on it years ago by chance, when my son Muye was a baby and needed some fresh air. It's nothing like the famous crowded parks, so I keep going back, usually a couple of times a week.

I tend to go early in the morning or around dusk, when it's almost empty. Mostly I just stroll around the lake, or sit on a bench and read for a while. Other times I'll bring Muye along to feed the fish, and we'll listen to the birds together. Basically, it's where I clear my head.
