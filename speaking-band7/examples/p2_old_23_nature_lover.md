## P2 答案

The person I'd like to talk about is a close friend of mine, someone who's genuinely passionate about protecting the outdoors. We've been mates for years now, and looking after the environment is basically his whole thing.

What he does is give up his weekends to look after nature. He joins litter clean-ups along mountain trails and riverbanks, takes part in tree-planting drives, and quietly nudges his friends to cut down on single-use plastic.

The way he goes about it really gets me — he's hands-on and low-key, never preachy. He just shows up, bin bag in hand, and leads by example. He's out there pretty much every other weekend, plus a few organised clean-ups a year.

Honestly, I really admire his dedication, because he genuinely practises what he preaches. And I'll be honest, it's rubbed off on me — I've started being more careful about waste myself. So yeah, that's the sort of friend I'm lucky to have.
