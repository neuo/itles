## P2 答案

So, the problem I'd like to talk about is a tech scare I had when my laptop suddenly died on me, right before a big work deadline.

It happened about two years ago, at home one evening. I'd been finishing off a report due the next morning, and out of nowhere the screen froze, then the whole thing wouldn't even turn back on. To make it worse, my wife also needed that laptop for our family photos.

It was honestly pretty stressful. All my files were on there, and I hadn't backed anything up for weeks, so I was convinced I'd lost the lot. I kept pressing the power button like that would somehow fix it, which of course it didn't.

In the end, I calmed down and looked it up online. I figured out it was just a flat battery playing tricks, so I left it charging overnight and it came back to life. So yeah, that's a tech scare I won't forget in a hurry.
