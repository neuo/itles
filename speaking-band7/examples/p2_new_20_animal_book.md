## P2 答案

So, the book I'd like to talk about is a children's picture book called The Very Hungry Caterpillar, which my five-year-old son Muye absolutely loves.

The main character is a tiny caterpillar, and there are bits and pieces of other animals dotted around the pages too. Basically, the caterpillar hatches from an egg and munches through all sorts of food, until he turns into a beautiful butterfly. The story's simple, but the artwork's colourful and the ending always gets a smile out of Muye.

I read it because it's become a fixed part of our bedtime routine. He's into animals, so anything with creatures in it grabs his attention. Honestly, he'll pick this one off the shelf night after night, and I've pretty much got it memorised by now.

What I love is the way it slips in a little lesson without being preachy — it quietly teaches him about counting and growing up. So all in all, it's one of those books I'll always remember reading with him.
