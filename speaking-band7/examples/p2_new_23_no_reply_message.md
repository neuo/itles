## P3 follow-ups（答案）

**Q1. In what situations do people spend a long time responding to others' messages?**
Well, I'd say it usually happens when people are swamped with work or buried under deadlines, so a reply just slips down their list. Sometimes the message isn't urgent, so they put it off and then forget completely. Honestly, others simply need time to think before they answer properly, especially if it's something a bit sensitive.

**Q2. In what situations do people not respond to messages at all?**
Honestly, I reckon people stay silent when they're annoyed or want to avoid an awkward conversation, like a tricky favour they'd rather dodge. Spam or random sales texts get ignored too, obviously. Plus, sometimes a message just gets lost in a flood of notifications, so it's never deliberate — they genuinely never noticed it landed.

**Q3. What would you do if you did not receive a reply after sending out a message?**
Well, I'd give it a few days first, since most folk are just busy rather than rude. If it's important, I'd send a gentle nudge or maybe ring them instead. But if they keep ignoring me, I'd take the hint and back off. There's no point chasing someone who clearly isn't keen on replying.

**Q4. Why do some people prefer sending a message instead of making a call?**
I'd say it's mainly about convenience — a text lets you reply whenever suits you, without dropping everything for a call. It's also less pressure, since you can word things carefully and dodge that awkward small talk. Plus, messages leave a written record, so you won't forget what was actually agreed on later.

**Q5. How do you show your respect in your messages?**
Mainly by replying promptly, I'd say, because leaving someone hanging feels pretty dismissive. I also try to keep my tone polite and add a proper greeting rather than just firing off one blunt line. With elders or my boss, I'm extra careful with wording, since a sloppy text can come across as rude or careless.

**Q6. Why do some people feel angry when others don't reply to their message?**
To be fair, silence can feel like being ignored, and nobody enjoys that. People often read it as disrespect, like their message wasn't worth a quick reply. It's worse when you can see the text was read, since that stings a bit. Honestly, it just leaves you guessing whether you've upset them somehow.
