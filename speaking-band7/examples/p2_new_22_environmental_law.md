## P2 答案

So, the law I'd like to talk about is the waste-sorting rule we've got in our city — basically, you have to separate your rubbish into different bins, like food waste, recyclables and everything else, instead of just chucking it all together.

I first heard about it from my wife, actually. She works in city management, so she'd been telling me it was coming long before it hit the news. At first I thought it sounded like a hassle, but she walked me through why it matters.

As for who benefits, I'd say pretty much everyone in the long run. The city ends up cleaner, less rubbish goes to landfill, and the stuff that can be recycled actually gets recycled, which is better for the environment we all share.

Honestly, I'm a big fan of it now. It was a bit of a pain to get used to, but at the end of the day, it's the kind of small habit that adds up to something huge.
