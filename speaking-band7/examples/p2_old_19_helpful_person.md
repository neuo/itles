## P2 答案

The person I'd like to talk about is my wife. She works as a civil servant here in Chengdu, but what really defines her is just how kind-hearted she's always been.

She helps people pretty much all the time, but the one that stands out is an elderly lady living downstairs. A few times a week, my wife will go out of her way to check in on her — it's just part of her routine now.

For example, she'll pick up groceries and medicine for her, and she's endlessly patient teaching the old lady how to use her smartphone. Why does she do it? Basically, she can't walk past someone who needs a hand — she was raised to look out for others, without a second thought.

Honestly, I feel really proud of her, and a bit humbled too. She does all this quietly, without expecting anything back. So yeah, her heart's truly in the right place, and that's the kind of person she is.
