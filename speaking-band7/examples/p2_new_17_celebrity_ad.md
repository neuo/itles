## P2 答案

So, the advertisement I'd like to talk about is one starring Liu Xiang, the famous Chinese hurdler who won Olympic gold years ago. He's still pretty much a household name here.

You can see it all over the place — on billboards near our subway station, and it pops up online before videos. It's a commercial for a well-known sportswear brand, the kind that's hard to miss.

The ad itself is fairly simple. Liu Xiang's training in the rain, then he sprints and clears the hurdles in slow motion, and at the end there's just a short line about never giving up. There aren't many words, which I think makes it more powerful.

Honestly, I quite like it. What I find appealing is that it doesn't just sell shoes — it sort of sells a feeling. On top of that, it's genuinely inspiring, even though it's only promoting a product. Even my son recognised him and copied the running pose. So yeah, that's an ad that really stuck with me.
