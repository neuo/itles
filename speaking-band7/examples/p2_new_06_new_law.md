## P2 答案

So, the law I'd like to talk about is one that would protect the green spaces in our cities — basically, a rule that stops developers from building over public parks and old trees just to put up another shopping mall.

What it'd change is pretty simple. Right now, a lot of these green areas can disappear almost overnight, but under this law, you'd need real public approval before touching them. Plus, every new neighbourhood would have to set aside a decent chunk of land for parks.

Honestly, I reckon it'd be fairly popular with most families, though developers wouldn't be thrilled about it. I came up with the idea from my wife, actually — she works in city management, and she's always telling me about how hard it is to balance growth with keeping a city liveable.

So yeah, at the end of the day, I feel pretty strongly about it. Green spaces are the kind of thing you only miss once they're gone.
