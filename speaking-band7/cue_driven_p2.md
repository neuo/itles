# P2 从 cue 入手：模式组合 + 怎么展开（6/20）

> 反套发现"一卡一模式"是错的 —— 真答案是**混合**。真因：**cue 的每个 bullet 各调一个模式，一道 P2 = 模式的组合。** cue card 本身就是结构，不用背 per-card 模版。
> 模式仍是 3 个（NARRATE/DESCRIBE/EXPLAIN，验证确认不加第 4 个）+ 2 个一拍微动作（FACT-DROP / WEIGH）。
> 配套：填空骨架+降级词 [[p2_frames_and_swaps.md]] / 全54张 spec [[p2_card_specs.md]] / 范文珠子 [[ielts_p2_speaking_bank.md]]。（推演过程 templates.md / narrate_reflex_check.md 已移 `_deprecated/`）

---

## 一、核心：读 cue 的 bullet → 每个 snap 一个模式 → 顺着走
`HOOK → CORE(一个角度) → 按 bullet 顺序走，每个 bullet 跑它的模式 → 最后一个 bullet = EXPLAIN(=LAND)`
**你不挑一个全局模式，是每个 bullet 切一次 —— 但切哪个由 bullet 决定（offline 预定），考场零活决策。** 这天然处理混合卡。

## 二、bullet → 模式 速查（看 bullet 措辞就知道跑哪个）
| bullet 长这样 | 模式 | 怎么跑 |
|---|---|---|
| **是什么/谁/哪里**(Who/What/Where it is, What law) | **DESCRIBE-点名**(最轻) | 一句说它是什么 + 1 个锚。别问"然后呢"(地方/身份没时间线,轨道空) |
| **什么样/什么用/种什么/会哪些语言**(What it looks like/is used for) | **DESCRIBE-平移**(主块) | 对准一部分→落 1 个日常细节→平移下一部分→停在签名细节。干了切"我通常在那干嘛" |
| **何时/做了啥/发生了啥/怎么解决/怎么反应/什么问题**(When/What you did/happened) | **NARRATE**(然后→然后) | 锚→然后→再然后(免费)→我做/说了啥→怎么收场。litmus:"first…then…in the end"成立 |
| **怎么认识/怎么知道/怎么开始**(How you met/knew/started) | **NARRATE-lite**(一拍小起源) | 给一个瞬间/渠道就够,别 then-pump→直接交给下一 bullet |
| **多少钱/多久一次/多久/多大**(How much/often/long/big) | **FACT-DROP**(一拍事实) | 一句答完立刻交棒("一个月一两次,我通常在那…")。⚠️是门不是房,别填 |
| **会不会受欢迎/最后回了没**(Whether it'll be popular) | **WEIGH**(一拍两面) | 两面摆一句再落自己边("多数人喜欢,但有些…,不过我觉得值")。="回了没"=一拍 NARRATE 收(一月后回了) |
| **And explain 为什么/感受如何**(why/how you feel) — **永远是最后一个 bullet** | **EXPLAIN(=LAND)** | claim→为什么真(机制非"because good")→so→**routed 到 健康/钱/时间**(人物题→佩服特质)→回扣 CORE 收尾 |

## 三、各题型的典型组合（读 bullet 读出来的）
| 题型 | 典型组合 |
|---|---|
| **人物** | DESCRIBE点名 → NARRATE-lite(怎么认识) → **DESCRIBE-习惯**(他平时干啥) → **嵌 1 个 NARRATE 场景**(我记得有次) → EXPLAIN→佩服。"双倍佩服"(CORE 一次+LAND 一次),别 then-pump 人物 |
| **物品** | DESCRIBE点名 → FACT-DROP(多少钱) → NARRATE-lite(怎么得的) → EXPLAIN(或 TURN:不值钱but珍贵)。展开靠 **ZOOM**(类别词→2-3 具体:磨损表带/划痕/停4:10) |
| **地方** | DESCRIBE → DESCRIBE-平移(主块) → DESCRIBE-习惯(我通常干啥) → EXPLAIN→感受。**几乎纯 DESCRIBE,别 NARRATE**(地方无时间线="然后呢"返回空=新03 陷阱) |
| **事件/场合** | NARRATE(何时) → NARRATE(做了啥=引擎) → FACT-DROP(跟谁) → EXPLAIN→感受。**最纯 NARRATE**,煮咖啡 drill 练这个 |
| **经历(你做的事)** | NARRATE(做了啥/问题) → NARRATE(怎么解决/转折) → EXPLAIN→感受/教训。陷阱:改看法=假NARRATE(前后对比)；给建议=塌缩中段(引用建议+结局,别then-pump) |
| **观点/未来/愿望** | DESCRIBE点名 → NARRATE-lite(怎么知道) → **NARRATE-future**(怎么实现,条件式"I'd…") → EXPLAIN→自由/钱/感受 |
| **法律** | DESCRIBE点名 → EXPLAIN(带来什么变化,because→so→健康) → **WEIGH**(受欢迎吗) → EXPLAIN→立场。EXPLAIN 主导最难,最需预载;THREE-SO drill 练 |
| **媒体/故事** | FACT-DROP(哪看的) → DESCRIBE+小plot-NARRATE(讲什么:2-3 拍剧情) → EXPLAIN-lite(为啥看) → EXPLAIN→感受 |

## 四、展开到 ~2 分钟：加"拍"，不升"词"（你的两个约束）
> **目标 ~240-280 词 / ~2 分钟。** 现 bank ~80-95s 偏短；但别冲过头(验证里 agent 一展开就 430 词=3 分钟,也不对)。

### 原则：EXPAND DON'T UPGRADE（长 ≠ 难）
长 = **更多小拍**（每个小拍 = 一句大白话）。绝不靠更大的词 —— **更大的词正是你"不知道怎么说"卡住的来源。**

### 卡住时：「像 5 岁小孩那样说」降级招
卡住 = 大脑在找"更高级的词"。**2 秒没词就降一级，用最笨最白的说法**，指它**干啥**：

| 别追(会卡) | 改说(一定会) |
|---|---|
| mind-blowing/breathtaking/stunning | amazing / so cool / it looked amazing, I just stared |
| contagious | you can't help smiling too |
| confidential | secret / private |
| immersive | it feels like you're really inside it |
| energized | full of energy / really awake / I felt great |
| laid-back | slow and relaxed / people take it easy |
| disciplined/consistent | she keeps at it every day / she sticks to it |
| weighing the options | I thought about the good and bad sides |
| good prospects | a good future / lots of jobs in it |
| pediatrician | a doctor for kids |
| featuring a celebrity | with a famous person in it |
| frustrating | really annoying / it was a pain |
| genuinely/absolutely | really（"really"永远管用）|

### 加 ~15-20 秒的招（全用简单词）
- **加一个 `like` 小例子**：笼统词后给一个真东西。"not much to do" → "…like, no cinema, no cafe, nothing for young people"
- **加感受**：一个 feeling 词 + 为什么。"I was a bit annoyed" / "honestly I was relieved"
- **加"我通常在那干嘛"小走位**(地方/物/人)：现在时
- **加一个 `but` 小对比**："it's quiet — but at home it's always noisy"
- **故事多一拍 `and then…`**：别一个动作就停
- **"at first vs later"**：自带两拍。"At first I was disappointed, but later it turned out fine"
- **最后一个 bullet(explain why)拉长**——大多数答案就短在这：给理由 → 一个理由的例子 → 再说一遍。这里最该投资。

## 五、边界规则（验证抓出来的坑）
1. **LAND 保护**：中段 bullet **别把最后 EXPLAIN 要用的"健康路线"提前用光**（法律题 新06:"带来什么变化"已 route 到健康→LAND 又 route 一遍=重复）。中段说"是什么变化"(偏 DESCRIBE)，把 because→so→健康 **留给 LAND**。
2. **WEIGH 当结尾**(老14 "跟老师学是不是更容易")：最后一个 bullet 是两面比较不是感受 → 两面摆一句 + 落自己边收尾，别套"佩服/感受"模板。
3. **5-bullet 卡**(老08/老23/老25/新06 等有 5 行 You should say)：其中 2 个通常是 FACT-DROP 或薄身份 bullet，快速带过，别均分时间。
4. **FACT-DROP 要有"胖"接收者**：薄卡(老26 安静地方/老07 多久画一次)若 FACT-DROP 后没料可交→暴露空洞。这种卡 FACT-DROP 后直接上"我通常在那干嘛"习惯走位。
5. **改看法(新26)= 前后对比不是 NARRATE**；**给建议(老04)= 引用建议+结局，中段塌缩别 then-pump**。

## 六、2 个干净 worked（~250 词，严格简单词，可直接 shadow）

### 新18 京都（地方）— 组合 DESCRIBE→DESCRIBE→DESCRIBE习惯→EXPLAIN
- **[HOOK]** The place I'd really recommend is Kyoto, in Japan.
- **[CORE]** I've been there a few times, and I love it every single time.
- **[是什么/哪里 — DESCRIBE点名]** It's an old city in the middle of Japan, and it's famous for its old temples and quiet little streets.
- **[看到/做了啥 — DESCRIBE平移+习惯]** When you walk around, there's just so much to see. You've got these beautiful old wooden temples — like the Golden Pavilion, which sits right next to a pond. The streets are full of small shops selling snacks and tea, and you'll often see people walking around in traditional clothes. What I usually do there is just wander around slowly, try the street food, and take loads of photos. There's no rush at all — you can sit by a temple for ages and just take it in.
- **[为什么推荐 — EXPLAIN=LAND]** The reason I'd recommend it is that it's so calm and full of history. It's not loud and busy like a big city — it kind of makes you slow down. So for me, if someone likes quiet places and old culture, Kyoto is the first place I'd send them.

### 新10 改计划（经历）— 组合 NARRATE→NARRATE→EXPLAIN
- **[HOOK]** I'd like to talk about a time I had to change a plan, pretty recently actually.
- **[CORE]** It was one of those changes that felt bad at first but turned out fine.
- **[原计划 — NARRATE start]** We'd planned a short trip during the National Day holiday — the idea was to drive to another city for a few days and just relax.
- **[什么让你改 — NARRATE then]** But then, the night before we were going to leave, my son Muye came down with a fever. And then it got a bit worse, so we decided it really wasn't a good idea to take him on a long trip like that.
- **[新计划 — NARRATE/I-DID]** So instead, we just stayed around the city and took it easy. We went to a couple of parks, ate at some nice places, and let him rest.
- **[感受 — EXPLAIN=LAND]** At first I was a bit annoyed, to be honest — I'd really been looking forward to it, so it felt like a waste. But then it turned out fine. We still had a relaxing time, and Muye got better quickly. So for me, it was a good reminder that sometimes a change of plan isn't such a bad thing.
