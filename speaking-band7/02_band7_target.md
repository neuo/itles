# 02. Band 7 目标拆解 + 自查清单

> IELTS Speaking 4 维度评分：**FC + LR + GRA + PRO**（每维度 25%）
>
> 总分 = 4 维度平均。任意一维度低于 7 → 总分被拉到 6.5。**所以 4 个维度都要做到 Band 7**。

---

## 1. 四维度官方描述（Band 6 vs Band 7）

### FC — Fluency & Coherence（流畅度与连贯性）

| Band | 描述 |
|------|------|
| Band 6 | Willing to speak at length, though may lose coherence at times. **Some hesitation**, **repetition** or **self-correction**. Uses a range of connectives but not always appropriately. |
| **Band 7** | **Speaks at length without noticeable effort or loss of coherence**. May demonstrate language-related hesitation at times, or some repetition and/or self-correction. **Uses a range of connectives and discourse markers with some flexibility**. |

**关键差别**：
- Band 6 = 愿意说但偶尔卡 / 自我修正
- Band 7 = **能持续说不明显卡顿** / 衔接词灵活

### LR — Lexical Resource（词汇）

| Band | 描述 |
|------|------|
| Band 6 | Has a wide enough vocabulary to discuss topics at length. **Generally paraphrases successfully**. |
| **Band 7** | **Uses vocabulary resource flexibly** to discuss a variety of topics. **Uses some less common and idiomatic vocabulary** and shows some awareness of style and collocation, with some inappropriate choices. **Uses paraphrase effectively**. |

**关键差别**：
- Band 6 = 词够用 + 能 paraphrase
- Band 7 = **less common/idiomatic** 词 + collocation 意识 + paraphrase 灵活

### GRA — Grammatical Range & Accuracy（语法）

| Band | 描述 |
|------|------|
| Band 6 | Uses a mix of simple and complex structures, but with **limited flexibility**. May make frequent mistakes with complex structures but rarely cause comprehension problems. |
| **Band 7** | **Uses a range of complex structures with some flexibility**. **Frequently produces error-free sentences**, though some grammatical mistakes persist. |

**关键差别**：
- Band 6 = 简单 + 复杂句混用 / 复杂句常错
- Band 7 = **复杂句灵活** + **大部分句子无错**

### PRO — Pronunciation（发音）

| Band | 描述 |
|------|------|
| Band 6 | Uses a range of pronunciation features with mixed control. Can generally be understood throughout, though mispronunciation of individual words or sounds reduces clarity at times. |
| **Band 7** | Shows all the positive features of Band 6, **and some, but not all, of the positive features of Band 8**. |

**Band 8 positive features 含**：
- Uses a wide range of pronunciation features
- Sustains flexible use of features, with **only occasional lapses**
- Is **easy to understand throughout**

**关键差别**：
- Band 6 = 能被听懂但偶尔不清
- Band 7 = **稳定易懂**，部分 Band 8 特征

## 2. Band 7 我要做到什么（具体行为）

### FC 行为
1. **持续说 P2 = 2 min** 不大段沉默（短停顿允许，但不超 3 秒）
2. **衔接词多样化**：well / so / like / you know / I mean / actually / honestly / on top of that / having said that / but at the same time
3. **不要 monotone 句式**：5 句话不能全 "I think X / I believe Y / I feel Z"——混用 "From my perspective / In my experience / What I find interesting is..."
4. **自我修正限 1-2 次/P2**：偶尔 "—— well, what I mean is..." 是自然的；超过 3 次拉 Band 6

### LR 行为
1. **用 5+ 个 less common collocation**（每个 P2）：
    - `pick up new habits` / `serve as the cornerstone of` / `exacerbate the sense of` / `pose a threat to` / `be on the same page` / etc.
2. **避免极简词**（continuation of T2 toolkit）：
    - ❌ a lot of → ✅ a great deal of / a lot of（口语 a lot of 也行，但不要全 a lot of）
    - ❌ very good → ✅ pretty solid / really impressive / quite something
    - ❌ thing → ✅ specific noun（avoid placeholder）
    - ❌ stuff → ✅ specific noun
3. **idiomatic touch**：每个 P2 1-2 个 idiomatic phrase（不要堆，stuff 多了 unnatural）
    - "the kind of thing that..." / "that sort of thing" / "to be honest" / "at the end of the day"
4. **Paraphrase**：被问到 something → 不要直接重复 question 的词，用 paraphrase

### GRA 行为
1. **复杂句类型至少用 3 种**（每个 P2 / P3 round）：
    - 状语从句（because / although / while / since / when / if）
    - 关系从句（who / which / that / where）
    - 名词性从句（that-clause / what-clause）
    - 偶尔条件句（If I had to / If I were to）
2. **修 Band 5.5 硬伤**（每篇 P2 / P3 round 清零）：
    - 主谓不一致（people **is** / wife **don't**）
    - 时态错乱（讲过去事用过去时，讲习惯用一般现在时）
    - 冠词漏（**the** environment / **a** specific decision）
    - 不可数当复数（**informations** / **researches**）
3. **不刻意用 Band 8+ 结构**（cleft / inversion / mixed conditional）—— 用错反而拉档
4. **允许 1-2 个 minor slip / 5 min**（Band 7 不要求 0 错，要求 "frequently error-free"）

### PRO 行为
1. **句末降调清楚**（陈述句不要 uptalk）
2. **重音清楚**（content word 比 function word 强）
3. **同长度的句子之间有 prosody 变化**（不要 monotone）
4. **常见误读单词修对**：
    - `comfortable` /ˈkʌmftəbl/（不是 comf-or-ta-ble）
    - `vegetable` /ˈvedʒtəbl/
    - `chocolate` /ˈtʃɒklət/
    - `interesting` /ˈɪntrəstɪŋ/（不是 in-te-resting）
    - 等等（持续累加）

## 3. 14 项 Band 7 自查清单

> P2 / P3 答案自评时打勾。**所有项都 ✅ = Band 7 边缘**

### FC
- [ ] P2 持续说 ~2 min（短停顿不超 3 秒，无大段沉默）
- [ ] 衔接词覆盖 4-6 种功能（well / so / actually / having said that / but / on top of that 等）
- [ ] 句式不 monotone（不是 5 句全 "I think..."）
- [ ] 自我修正 ≤ 2 次

### LR
- [ ] 用了 ≥ 3 个 mid/upper level collocation（如 active phrase 库的）
- [ ] 没有重复用极简词（a lot of / thing / stuff / very good 同 P2 内 ≤ 2 次）
- [ ] 至少 1 个 idiomatic touch（the kind of / to be honest / at the end of the day）
- [ ] Paraphrase 题目用词（不直接重复）

### GRA
- [ ] 用了 ≥ 3 种复杂句类型
- [ ] 主谓代一致 0 错
- [ ] 时态前后一致（讲过去 → 过去时；讲习惯 → 一般现在时）
- [ ] 冠词决策正确（the / a / 零冠词）

### PRO
- [ ] 录音自检不卡（流畅度 OK）
- [ ] 没有 monotone（prosody 有变化）

## 4. 离 Band 7 还有多远 — 阶段评估

| 阶段 | 14 项清单达成 | 等于 |
|------|------------|------|
| 5.5 | < 6 项 | 起点 |
| 6.0 | 6-9 项 | 基础结构有了 |
| **6.5** | **10-12 项** | **接近目标** |
| **7.0** | **13-14 项** | **🎯 目标** |
| 7.5 | 14 项 + 1-2 个 Band 8 markers | 二考冲 7.5 |

---

## 5. 关于"训练就盯这 14 项"

- W1-W2 阶段：每次练习后**自评 14 项打几勾**
- W3-W4 阶段：录音回听 + 自评 + 我给反馈
- 不强求每次 14/14；**周平均提升比单次满分重要**
