# 校准锚 · 真实带分范文与考官评语

> 判分前如果拿不准某一档长什么样，回来读这里。**考官评语比范文本身更有价值** —— 它告诉你什么样的语言换什么分。
> 来源：① Cambridge IELTS 13/14/15 官方 "Sample answers for Writing tasks"（考官评语，有 band） ② IELTS.org 官方 Sample Candidate Writing Scripts ③ ieltsanswers.com（作者 Mike Wattie，前 IELTS 考官，每篇带四维分表 + 逐句批注）。

## ⭐ 0. 两个直接锚 —— 她自己写过的题，有官方带分样卷

这两道题她 2026-06-15 都 cold 写过。**同题对照是最强的校准工具**：让她把自己的版本和官方 5.5 / 7.5 并排读。

### 锚 A · 剑 14 Test 3 —— 音乐连接不同文化和年龄 → 官方 **Band 5.5**
> 题目（与她 `2026-06-15-agd-music.md` 完全同题）："Some people say that music is a good way of bringing people of different cultures and ages together. To what extent do you agree or disagree with this opinion?"

**Cambridge 考官评语（原文）**：
> "The response shows a clear position that is relevant to the task, with examples to support the main ideas. There is a clear overall progression, with effective use of cohesive devices [this | them | it] in spite of some errors [For the example | they patients | Including / In conclusion?]. Paragraphing is adequate. Vocabulary is generally adequate, **but control over spelling and word choice is weak** [anypeople | rhytum | solf/ soft? | anywhere / everywhere? | song for life?], **sometimes causing difficulty for the reader**. There are attempts at complex sentences, **but these usually contain errors** [people who has | Although ... but when], and punctuation is sometimes faulty. **Better control over spelling, grammar and punctuation might improve the overall score.**"

🔑 **读法**：TR ✅ CC ✅ 段落 ✅ —— 全部达标，但**拼写和词选控制弱到"造成读者困难"**，整篇就只有 5.5。
**这正是她的失分结构。** 她的 TR/CC 比这篇还好，但拼写错量（每篇 6–9 个）在同一个量级。差别只在她的错还没到"causing difficulty"，所以停在 6.5 而不是 5.5。

### 锚 B · 剑 14 Test 4 —— 自雇 → 官方 **Band 7.5**
> 题目（与她 `2026-06-15-2pt-cold.md` 完全同题）："Nowadays many people choose to be self-employed, rather than to work for a company or organisation. Why might this be the case? What could be the disadvantages of being self-employed?"

**Cambridge 考官评语（原文）**：
> "The candidate has produced a well-developed response to the task. Further ideas could be included, e.g. wanting to develop own ideas, wanting to work more flexibly than employment allows for. There is a clear progression throughout the response, with information and ideas organised logically. There is a range of cohesive devices [To start with | These | The first ... the second | Apparently | last but not least] and each paragraph has a clear central topic. There is a wide range of vocabulary, including less common items and showing evidence of style and collocation [contemporary | tempting | vast majority | opt for | entrepreneurship | fraudulent | image | luxury | perplex | jack of all trades], with only rare examples of inaccuracy [insensible]. **There is a variety of complex structures, used flexibly and accurately. Grammar and punctuation are well controlled and there are frequent error-free sentences.** A few errors persist [still the vast majority of people opt for (word order) | (the) major drawbacks] **but the message is still clear.**"

🔑 **读法**：7.5 只需要 **"frequent error-free sentences" + 极少数词汇不准**。它并不需要华丽 —— 但它需要**干净**。这就是 6.5 到 7.5 的全部距离。

## 1. Band 5.0

### 5.0 — "免费大学教育"（TR 3-4 / CC 6-7 / **LR 7 / GRA 7** → 总分 **5.0**）
来源：ieltsanswers.com（Mike Wattie，前考官）
> 题："...the government should make it free for all students no matter what financial background they have. To what extent do you agree or disagree?"

考官在文中直接写下：
> "**What you write here in your topic sentence totally contradicts the previous topic sentence that you wrote! This is a terrible mistake to make and leads to a severely lower score for task response.**"

总评：
> "You need to prevent this kind of disaster happening in your real exam. You need to understand that you have not presented a clear opinion and in fact you contradicted your opinion in the topic sentences of the two body paragraphs."

🔑 **这是整个锚库最重要的一篇**：LR 7 + GRA 7 + CC 6-7，就因为 TR 崩到 3-4，总分 **5.0**。
**立场自相矛盾 = 两个整档的代价。** 她 7/9 那篇"专业人士须在受训国工作"就出现过同类问题（Body2 用定向生的例子论证不该限制自由 = 例子反证自己的论点）。

### 5.0 — IELTS.org 官方 Task 2 Script A 评语（原文）
> "The answer is short at just over 200 words and thus loses marks for content. There are some relevant arguments but these are not very well developed and become unclear in places. The organisation of the answer is evident through the use of fairly simple connectives but there are problems for the reader in that **there are many missing words and word order is often incorrect**. The structures are quite ambitious but often faulty and vocabulary is kept quite simple."

## 5. Band 7.0

### 剑 13 Test 2 —— 选择太多（官方 **Band 7.0**）
> "...Organisation is logical and there is clear progression throughout the writing. There is a range of cohesive devices, used appropriately... The range of vocabulary is sufficient to show some flexibility and precision, as well as less common items and an awareness of style and collocation... **There is only one spelling error** [beome], probably a slip of the pen. **Control over grammar and punctuation is generally good and there is a variety of complex structures with frequent error-free sentences.**"

### 剑 14 Test 1 —— 接受现状 vs 改善现状（官方 **Band 7.0**）
> "The candidate has addressed all parts of the task and shows a clear position throughout the response. **Ideas are presented, extended and supported.**... **Occasional errors do not prevent the message from coming through.** There is a range of grammatical structures and these are usually accurate, **although there are a few errors** [some people chooses | might not know what are the consequences | Not all bad situations needs to be ignored]. **The meaning is still clear, however.**"

🔑 **Band 7 允许真实的错误量**（这篇有 3 处主谓/词序错还是 7.0）。区别在于 **"error-free sentences are frequent"** —— 干净句要够多，不是零错。

### 剑 15 Test 3 —— 广告（官方 **Band 7.0**）
> "...Main ideas are presented, extended and supported... There is a range of cohesive devices..., including reference and substitution... **There are only occasional spelling errors** [bandwagen | repetetive]. There is a variety of complex structures and the writer shows good control over grammar and punctuation."

### 7.0 — "广告 vs 真实需求"（四维全 7.0，ieltsanswers）

🔑 **注意这篇 Band 7 全文最"高级"的词是 `pester`。** 没有任何炫词。
**Band 7 的语言是平的。** 她一直担心"用词太 low 影响分" —— 锚库直接否掉了这个担心。

## 7. 跨档规律（30+ 篇考官评语归纳，直接可用作判分先验）

1. **TR 是绝大多数考生的瓶颈** —— 11 篇 6.5 里有 9 篇是 LR/GRA 7-8 而 TR 5-6。
   ⚠️ **但她相反**：她 TR/CC 已在 7.0-7.5，卡在 LR/GRA。**别照搬通用建议给她加结构训练。**
2. **立场自相矛盾代价最大**："an absolute disaster" / "leads to a severely lower score" / "plunges your score"。
3. **HOW vs WHY**：反复出现的 TR 失分点 —— 解释"怎么做"而不是"为什么有效"。
   > "You focus too much on HOW and not enough on WHY."
4. **每个 body 段恰好 2 个主论点并展开**："This is the magic number." / "By including too many main points, your paragraphs lacked development and **are unlikely to reach seven**."
5. **不足 250 词 = TR −1**，且连带压低其它三项。
6. **段落切分失败 = 硬顶 5**。
7. **`-s` 结尾错被点名为 7→8 的阻断点**："frequent errors with 's' endings that prevent an 8"。
8. **复杂 ≠ 高分，复杂会扣分**："unnecessarily complex structures you use" / "reading your writing causes me to feel frustrated" / "In particular your topic sentences need to be easy to read"。
9. **"partly agree" 被反复劝退**："always risky to be in the middle. I recommend totally agree/disagree."
10. **Band 7 的语言是平的。** 全库 7.0–7.5 的样本里没有一个华丽句子。

## 8. ⭐ 同一个意思写三遍：Band 6 / 7 / 8 长什么样

来源：IELTS Advantage — https://www.ieltsadvantage.com/2015/04/18/difference-band-5-and-8-in-ielts-writing-task-2-band-scores/
**全库最好的教学素材** —— 一个论点，三个档位，差别一目了然。

> **Band 6**（有观点，没展开）
> *The main problem causing traffic jams is too many cars. There are lots more cars these days.*
> 考官：想法切题，但**没解释为什么车多会堵，也没举例**。
>
> **Band 7**（展开了，但太笼统）
> *The main problem causing traffic jams is too many cars. In many cities around the world, there are lots of cars, and this causes traffic jams. For example, the number of cars purchased in developing countries is increasing yearly.*
> 考官：立场清楚，但**解释很泛，例子失焦、没紧扣论点**。
>
> **Band 8**（具体 + 聚焦）
> *The main problem causing traffic jams is too many cars. When we have more vehicles than a city's infrastructure was designed for, it leads to congestion. For example, Ho Chi Minh City was designed to cope with around 500,000 cars, and the city now has over 2 million cars, resulting in chronic traffic problems.*
> 考官：**解释了"为什么"，例子具体且直接证明论点**。

🔑 对她的用法：她的 body 段常停在 **Band 7 那一档**（"there is a tendency to over-generalise" 正是官方 Band 7 TR 的原话）。要往上，靠的是**具体数字/地名/机制**，不是靠换词。

### 配套：Chris Pell 的「100% 规则」
> "**Follow the 100% rule — don't try to use any words you are not 100% sure about. It is better to use simple words and make no mistakes than use advanced words, but in error.**"
> "**You should only write sentences that you know are 100% correct. If there is any doubt then you need to think about how you can express your point in a simpler way, that has no mistakes.**"

🔑 **这条对她是直接处方**：她的中式直译块（P3）几乎全部产生于"想说一个自己没把握的意思"。规则 = 没把握就换更简单的说法，别硬翻。

## 9. Task 1 校准（材料状态说明）

⚠️ **诚实标注**：T1 方向的检索完成了，但**逐字样卷文本没有回传到本文件**。以下只记录**已核实的结论**和**可自取的出处**，不编造样卷。

### 已核实的两条结论
1. **T1 的 overview 是 5.0–6.5 段位的主导封顶项。** 而且考官的批评几乎从来不是"没有 overview"，而是**"overview 在数阶段/罗列，而不是在概括"**。这个判词在官方 Band 8.5 的降档理由里和十余条 Cambridge 考官评语里措辞一致 —— **标准不随档位改变，只是阈值上移**。
   → 直接对应她的 **W11**（overview 两根柱子是同一事实的两面 / 漏 odd-one-out）。
2. **同一份卷子，不同官方出版方给的分能差半档。** ielts.org 把两份机考样卷判 Band 6 / Band 4，British Council 对**完全相同的文本**判 Band 5.5 / Band 3.5，而且对"到底有没有 overview"给出**互相矛盾**的理由。

### ⚠️ 由此得出的判分纪律
**单条考官评语不是精密仪器。** 任何一次判分带 **±0.5 的不可消除噪声**。
→ 所以：**用 `scoring.md` 的可数指标决定"在边界的哪一侧"，不要为某个具体数字辩护。**
→ 反过来说，**干净句率和词汇错个数这类可数的东西才是稳的** —— 这也是本套机制以计数为主判据的原因。

### 已知缺口
- **官方网站上不存在带分的地图/平面图样卷**（唯一的带分地图样卷在 Cambridge 印刷版书里）
- **官方 Task 1 没有 6.5 / 7.5 半档样卷**（半档只在 Task 2 发布）
- **官方材料里没有 T1 的四维分项分**（只有总分）

## 10. 两处业界分歧（喂她之前先知道）

1. **例子能不能编？**
   - Chris Pell（IELTS Advantage）："If you can't think of a specific example, **make one up**. The examiners are not interested in how factual your examples are."
   - IELTS Liz：编造研究/统计"**do nothing for your score. The examiner has no interest in statistics or research for task 2.**"
   - **本 skill 采用的中间路线**：例子要**具体**（真实地名、真实机制、可想象的场景），但**用"一般观察"的口吻**说，不要伪造"某某大学研究表明 70%"。她本来就爱用真实经验（张伟/成都/她老婆），这条对她天然合适。

2. **6.5 卡住时先修什么？**
   - Simon："**I don't recommend that you focus on grammar.** Improving your grammatical accuracy is probably going to be a slow and gradual process… the secret is **planning**."
   - 但 60 篇带分样本的分项数据显示：**GRA 6 是把 6.5 压住的最普遍单一原因**。
   - **对她的裁决**：Simon 的建议假设考生 TR/CC 弱。**她 TR/CC 已 7.0–7.5，planning 没有剩余收益。** 她只有 LR/GRA 一根杠杆，且必须按"慢工"预期来推 —— 这正是"同题反复写"的理由：把内容负荷清零，让慢变量能被专注地推。
