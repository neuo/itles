## Part 1: 类别定义与多层分类映射

> **分类原则：** 同一类别内的素材、句型、逻辑模板可以**自由泛化**；跨类别的内容则属于**特化专属**，不可互换。

### 类别 1 — 生活偏好与选择 (Preferences & Habits)

| 子层 | 话题 | 泛化核心 |
|------|------|----------|
| 1A 物品/消费偏好 | Shoes, Going out (Cash), Borrowing/Lending | "我选 X 而不选 Y 的理由" — 消费观、使用习惯 |
| 1B 体验/活动偏好 | Happy things, Taking photos, Museum, Sports team | "我喜欢做某事/去某处的感受" — 情绪体验、兴趣驱动 |
| 1C 空间/环境偏好 | Building, Public places, Sharing | "我对某类空间/社交模式的态度" — 空间审美、共享 vs 私有 |
| 1D 信息/阅读偏好 | Reading | "我获取信息/娱乐的方式" — 内容选择 |

**泛化逻辑：** 所有 1A–1D 话题共享"偏好表态 → 感受理由 → 对比让步 → 重申"的框架。准备一套"我更倾向于 X，因为它让我感到……"的万能模板即可覆盖。

**泛化逻辑：** 共享"过去状态 → 转折点/原因 → 现在状态 → 感受/评价"的时间线框架。

### 话题 A：Taking Photos（类别 1 — 生活偏好）

---

**组装 ①：1A 肯定 + 2A 感受 + 4A 承认 + 5A 重申**

> To be honest, I'm quite into taking photos in my daily life. The main reason is that it gives me a real sense of satisfaction — whenever I capture a nice moment, I feel genuinely happy about it. I mostly just use my phone, because it's always in my pocket and the quality is decent enough. Of course, I can see why some people might prefer professional cameras for better results. But overall, I'd say taking photos is definitely something I enjoy.

---

**组装 ②：1E 过去vs现在 + 2D 习惯 + 4C 分情况 + 5C 未来**

> When I was younger, I never really cared about taking photos — I just lived in the moment. But these days I've gotten into the habit of snapping pictures wherever I go. It's become part of my comfort zone, and I genuinely enjoy looking back at them later. Having said that, it really depends on the situation — if I'm at a gathering with friends, I'll take a few shots, but if I'm just running errands, I won't bother. Looking ahead, I'd love to learn a bit more about composition and lighting to improve my skills.

---

**组装 ③：1C 中立 + 3A 效率 + 4B 排除 + 5B 习惯**

> I have mixed feelings about taking photos, to be honest. On one hand, the convenience factor is huge — with my phone, I can capture the moment anytime, anywhere, and it only takes a few seconds. It's not that I dislike professional photography — I just find using my phone more practical for everyday life. At this point, snapping quick photos has become almost automatic; I don't even think about it.

---

**组装 ④：1D 对比 + 2B 减压 + 4A 承认 + 5D 准则**

> Compared to videos, I'm more of a photo person. I find that taking photos is a great way for me to unwind — I'll go for a walk, find interesting things around me, and just snap a few pictures. It's like a mental escape. I'll admit it's not a serious hobby — I'm not investing in expensive gear or anything. But for me, the bottom line is: if it makes me happy, it's worth doing.

---

**组装 ⑤：1A 肯定 + 3B 社交 + 4C 分情况 + 5A 重申**

> Absolutely, I'd say taking photos is something I really enjoy. It gives me a chance to bond with friends — we often take group photos when we hang out, and it's become kind of a shared ritual. On weekdays I rarely bother, but on weekends or during trips, I'll take loads of pictures. All in all, capturing moments with the people I care about is something I truly value.

### 话题 B：Rules（类别 4 — 人际社会）

---

**组装 ①：1A 肯定 + 3D 实用 + 4A 承认 + 5A 重申**

> Yes, definitely. I think rules are important in any society. From a practical point of view, they help maintain order and make sure things run smoothly — whether it's traffic rules or school policies. I'll admit that some rules can feel a bit outdated or overly strict. But at the end of the day, I believe rules are necessary to keep things fair for everyone.

---

**组装 ②：1C 中立 + 2C 性格 + 4C 分情况 + 5D 准则**

> I wouldn't say I love or hate rules — I'm somewhere in between. As someone who's naturally quite flexible, I don't enjoy rigid systems that leave no room for exceptions. In certain cases, though, I might see strict rules as necessary — like safety regulations, for example. My personal rule is: follow the rules that make sense, and respectfully speak up about the ones that don't.

---

**组装 ③：1E 过去vs现在 + 2A 感受 + 4B 排除 + 5C 未来**

> My attitude towards rules has changed a lot over the years. Back in school, I found most rules annoying and pointless. But now I appreciate the sense of structure they provide — there's something about a well-organised system that makes me feel secure. It's not about blindly obeying — it's simply a matter of understanding the purpose behind them. Going forward, I hope I can pass this mindset on if I ever have kids.

---

**组装 ④：1D 对比 + 3B 社交 + 4A 承认 + 5B 习惯**

> Between written rules and unwritten social norms, I find social norms more interesting. They strengthen relationships — things like being polite, waiting your turn, or showing respect to elders keep society running smoothly. Of course, I understand that formal rules serve a bigger purpose in areas like law and safety. But for me, following basic social etiquette has become second nature at this point.

---

**组装 ⑤：1B 否定 + 2C 性格 + 4A 承认 + 5D 准则**

> Honestly, I'm not that keen on rules that feel too rigid or controlling. I think it suits my character, because I've always been quite independent — I prefer to make my own judgements about what's right. That said, I understand it's not for everyone, and some people feel more comfortable with clear guidelines. Life's too short to force yourself into a system that doesn't fit, but I do respect the rules that genuinely protect people.
