# 03. 五种题型 — 骨架 + T 句模板

> 5 种题型必须 **10 秒判出**——审题花太久就没时间写。
> 每种题型的骨架要背到能闭眼默写。考场没时间现想结构。

---

## 0. 10 秒题型判断流程

读题目→找**问题词**→对照表：

| 题目里出现 | 题型 | 简称 |
|----------|------|------|
| "To what extent do you agree or disagree?" / "Do you agree?" | Agree/Disagree | **A/D** |
| "Discuss both views (and give your own opinion)" / "Discuss both these views" | Discuss Both Views | **DBV** |
| "What problems / What solutions" / "What can be done" / "How can this be addressed" | Problem/Solution | **P/S** |
| "What are the causes / reasons" + "What are the effects / consequences" | Causes/Effects | **C/E** |
| "What are the causes" + "What can be done" | Causes/Solutions | **C/S**（P/S 的变体）|
| 两个独立问题（"Why X? Is this a positive or negative development?"）| Two-Part Question | **2-Pt** |

**判错 = 立场不对 = TR 直接降到 5.5**。

---

## 1. A/D（Agree/Disagree）

**题目特征**：
- "To what extent do you agree or disagree with this statement?"
- "Do you agree?" / "Is this a positive or negative trend?"

**立场选项（3 选 1，必须明确）**：
- `largely agree`（大部分同意）
- `partly agree` / `agree to some extent`（部分同意）
- `largely disagree`（大部分不同意）

**禁用**：`balanced view` / `I see both sides`——这是 5.5 标志，TR 直接降级。

### 骨架（4 段，250-280 词）

```
Intro（50-60 词）
  - Sentence 1: 改写题目（paraphrase，换词换句型）
  - Sentence 2: 简要框架对方观点（"While some argue X..."）
  - Sentence 3: 表立场 + 预告 2 个理由（"...I largely agree because A and B"）

Body 1（80-100 词）—— 理由 1
  T: 第一个理由（最强的理由放这里）
  E: 解释为什么这是个理由（因果链）
  Ex: 具体例子（国家 / 政策 / 个人 / 假设场景）
  L: 回扣 T 和题目

Body 2（80-100 词）—— 理由 2 或 让步+反驳
  T: 第二个理由 OR "Admittedly, ... However, ..."
  E: 解释
  Ex: 具体例子
  L: 回扣

Conclusion（40-50 词）
  - 重申立场（换说法）
  - 综合两个理由
  - 收尾（不加新论点）
```

### T 句模板（按段填）

**Intro 第 1 句**（paraphrase）：
- `It is often argued that [paraphrase].`
- `Many people believe that [paraphrase].`
- `[Topic] has become an increasingly common phenomenon in recent years.`

**Intro 第 3 句**（表立场）：
- `While I understand [opposing view], I [largely agree / partly disagree / strongly disagree] because [reason A] and [reason B].`

**Body 1 T 句**（主理由）：
- `The primary reason for my position is that ___.`
- `The most compelling argument in favour of this view is that ___.`

**Body 2 T 句**（次理由）：
- `Another important reason is that ___.`
- `Furthermore, ___.`

**Body 2 T 句（让步+反驳变体）**：
- `Admittedly, [opposing view] has some merit. However, ___.`
- `Granted, some argue that ___. Nevertheless, ___.`

**Conclusion 开头**：
- `In conclusion, while [opposing view] is understandable, I believe ___.`
- `On balance, the case for [your stance] is stronger because ___.`

### A/D 高频陷阱

| 陷阱 | 后果 |
|------|------|
| 立场摇摆（Intro 写 largely agree，Body 2 转 disagree）| TR 5.5 |
| 写"both sides equally"（这是 DBV 才该做的）| TR 5.5 |
| 只写一个理由（Body 2 也是 Body 1 的延伸）| TR 6.0 上不去 |
| Conclusion 加新论点 | CC 扣分 |

## 2. DBV（Discuss Both Views）

**题目特征**：
- "Discuss both views and give your own opinion."
- "Discuss both these views."

**立场必须有**——题目明说 "give your own opinion"。**没有立场 = 漏题 = TR 5.5**。

**关键判断**：Body 1 写**你不同意的**那边，Body 2 写**你同意的**那边——因为读者最后记得的是 Body 2。

### 骨架（4 段，260-285 词）

```
Intro（55-65 词）
  - Sentence 1: paraphrase 话题 + 提"is debated"
  - Sentence 2: 列两边观点（"Some believe X, while others argue Y"）
  - Sentence 3: 表立场（"While both views have merit, I find Y more convincing"）

Body 1（85-100 词）—— 公正陈述对方
  T: "Supporters of [View A] argue that ___."  / "Those who favour [View A] point out that ___."
  E: 解释他们为什么这么想（中性，不嘲讽）
  Ex: 例子支持他们的观点
  L: "This view therefore reflects a real concern about ___."（中性收尾，留转折空间）

Body 2（90-105 词）—— 我的观点（更强）
  T: "However, I find the case for [View B] more convincing." / "Nevertheless, I lean towards ___ for the following reasons."
  E: 解释你的理由（可以隐含反驳 Body 1）
  Ex: 例子
  L: 强收尾，回扣立场

Conclusion（40-55 词）
  - 承认对方有理（"While [View A] is understandable..."）
  - 重申立场（换说法）
  - 综合
```

### T 句模板

**Intro 第 3 句**（DBV 表立场）：
- `While both views have merit, I find [View B] more convincing.`
- `Although there is something to be said for [View A], I largely side with [View B].`
- `I see value in both, but lean more towards [View B].`

**Body 1 T 句**（公正陈述对方）：
- `Supporters of [View A] argue that ___.`
- `Those who favour [View A] point out that ___.`
- `On the one hand, there is a strong case for ___.`

**Body 2 T 句**（转向我的立场）：
- `However, I find the case for [View B] more convincing.`
- `On the other hand, I lean more towards the view that ___.`
- `Nevertheless, the argument for [View B] is in my view stronger because ___.`

**Body 2 L 句**（强收尾示范）：
- `The strength of this view is therefore that it ___.`
- `This is why, on balance, [View B] offers a more compelling answer to the question.`

### DBV 高频陷阱

| 陷阱 | 后果 |
|------|------|
| 没表立场（只 discuss 不 opinion）| 漏题，TR 5.5 |
| Body 1 嘲讽对方（"Wrongly, supporters claim..."）| CC 扣分 + TR 扣分 |
| 两边写得一样长但立场糊 | TR 6.0 上不去 |
| Intro 用 "This essay will present my own stance"（推迟立场版）| 风险高，Band 7 边缘脆弱 → **现阶段不用** |

## 3. P/S（Problem/Solution）

**题目特征**：
- "What problems does this cause? What solutions can governments implement?"
- "What can be done to solve this issue?"

**结构有两种**：
- **2-Body 版**（Body 1 = 所有 problems；Body 2 = 所有 solutions）—— 更易写，推荐
- **4-Body 版**（Body 1+2 = problem 1, 2；Body 3+4 = solution 1, 2）—— 更详细但容易超字数

**立场不必须**——P/S 没有 "do you agree" 问题。但 solutions 段要表达**你建议**做什么。

### 骨架（2-Body 版，4 段，255-275 词）

```
Intro（45-55 词）
  - Sentence 1: paraphrase 问题
  - Sentence 2: 预告 essay 结构（"This essay will discuss the main problems and propose practical solutions"）

Body 1（90-110 词）—— 问题段（2 个 problems 压在一段）
  T: "One major problem is ___."
  E1+Ex1: 解释 + 例子（问题 1）
  "Another concern is ___." (mini-T for problem 2)
  E2+Ex2: 解释 + 例子（问题 2）
  L: 整段收尾（"Together, these problems undermine ___")

Body 2（90-110 词）—— 方案段（2 个 solutions 压在一段，对应问题）
  T: "To address these issues, ___."
  E1+Ex1: 方案 1 + 例子
  "In addition, ___" (mini-T for solution 2)
  E2+Ex2: 方案 2 + 例子
  L: 整段收尾（"Combined, these measures could ___")

Conclusion（40-50 词）
  - 简短重申问题
  - 简短重申方案
  - 收尾
```

### T 句模板

**Intro 第 1 句**（paraphrase 问题）：
- `[Topic] has become an increasingly pressing issue in recent years.`
- `In many parts of the world, [problem] continues to grow.`
- `The problem of [X] has drawn considerable concern lately.`

**Body 1 T 句**（问题段开头）：
- `One major problem is that ___.`
- `One of the most serious consequences of [X] is ___.`
- `The most pressing problem associated with [X] is ___.`

**Body 2 T 句**（方案段开头）：
- `To address these issues, governments could ___.`
- `Several practical steps can be taken to mitigate these problems.`
- `Tackling these challenges requires action on multiple fronts.`

**多原因/多方案的衔接（段内）**：
- `Another concern is that ___.` / `A further issue is ___.`
- `In addition, ___.` / `Equally important is ___.`

### P/S 高频陷阱

| 陷阱 | 后果 |
|------|------|
| Problem 段和 Solution 段对不上（问题 = 教育不足，方案 = 增税）| TR 扣分 |
| Solutions 太空（"governments should do more"）| TR 6.0 上不去 |
| 段末漏 L（每段至少 1 个 L 收尾）| CC + TR 扣分 |
| 4-body 版字数超 290 | 时间不够 + 偏题风险 |

## 4. C/E（Causes/Effects）和 C/S（Causes/Solutions）

**题目特征**：
- C/E: "What are the causes? What are the effects?"
- C/S: "What are the causes? What solutions can be implemented?"

C/S 本质上是 P/S 的变体（只是把 "problem" 换成 "cause"）。**结构同 P/S**。

C/E 是 C/S 的兄弟——Body 1 写原因，Body 2 写影响。**结构和 P/S 几乎一致**。

### 骨架（C/E，4 段，260-280 词）

```
Intro（45-55 词）
  - paraphrase 现象
  - 预告"This essay will examine the main causes and discuss the resulting effects"

Body 1（90-110 词）—— 原因段（2 个 causes 压在一段）
  T: "The primary cause of [X] is ___."
  E1+Ex1: 原因 1 + 解释 + 例子
  "Another contributing factor is ___."
  E2+Ex2: 原因 2 + 解释 + 例子
  L: 收尾

Body 2（90-110 词）—— 影响段（2 个 effects）
  T: "These causes lead to several serious consequences."  OR  "The most visible effect is ___."
  E1+Ex1: 影响 1
  "In addition, ___."
  E2+Ex2: 影响 2
  L: 收尾

Conclusion（40-50 词）
  - 简短综合原因 + 影响
  - 可选：一句话预测或呼吁
```

### T 句模板

**Body 1 T 句**（原因段）：
- `The primary cause of [X] is ___.`
- `One of the main reasons behind this trend is ___.`
- `A key driver of [X] is ___.`

**Body 2 T 句**（影响段）：
- `These factors have produced several visible consequences.`
- `The most significant effect of [X] is ___.`
- `The impact of this trend has been felt in multiple areas.`

### C/E 高频陷阱

| 陷阱 | 后果 |
|------|------|
| Causes 和 Effects 内容混（在 cause 段讲 effect）| CC 扣分 |
| 只写 1 个 cause 或 1 个 effect | TR 不够 developed |
| 影响段太抽象（"society suffers"）| 例子不够具体，LR + TR 扣分 |

## 5. 2-Pt（Two-Part Question）

**题目特征**：题目里有**两个独立问题**，常见格式：
- "Why is X happening? Is this a positive or negative development?"
- "Why has X changed? What can be done about it?"

**关键**：两个问题**都要答，篇幅大致均衡**。漏答任何一个 = TR 5.5。

### 骨架（4 段，255-280 词）

```
Intro（50-60 词）
  - Sentence 1: paraphrase 话题
  - Sentence 2: 明确提两个问题
  - Sentence 3: 预告（如果第二个问题是 positive/negative，可以这里表态）

Body 1（90-110 词）—— 回答问题 1
  T: 直接回答问题 1
  E: 解释
  Ex: 例子
  L: 回扣

Body 2（90-110 词）—— 回答问题 2
  T: 直接回答问题 2
  E: 解释
  Ex: 例子
  L: 回扣

Conclusion（40-50 词）
  - 综合两个回答
```

### T 句模板

**Intro 第 2-3 句**（明确两个问题）：
- `This essay will explore the reasons behind [Q1] and consider whether [Q2].`
- `[Topic] raises two related questions: why this is happening, and whether it should be welcomed.`

**Body 1 T 句**（回答 Q1）：
- `Several factors explain [Q1].` （如果 Q1 是 "why"）
- `The main reason for [trend] is ___.`

**Body 2 T 句**（回答 Q2）：
- `As for whether this is a positive development, the answer largely depends on ___. In my view, ___.`
- `Turning to [Q2], I believe this trend is overall [positive/negative] because ___.`

### 2-Pt 高频陷阱

| 陷阱 | 后果 |
|------|------|
| 只答一个问题（另一个忘了）| TR 5.5 |
| 两个问题答的比例极不均衡（80/20）| TR 6.0 上不去 |
| Q2 是 "positive or negative" 但你写 "balanced" | 同 A/D 陷阱，TR 5.5 |

## 6. 5 题型对比速查

| 题型 | 段数 | 立场必须 | 关键特征 | 最常见话题 |
|------|-----|---------|---------|----------|
| **A/D** | 4 | ✅ 必须 | 选 1 边，全篇贯穿 | Education / Government / Society |
| **DBV** | 4 | ✅ 必须 | Body 1 对方，Body 2 自己 | Education / Technology / Globalisation |
| **P/S** | 4 | ❌ 不必须 | Problem 段 + Solution 段对应 | Crime / Health / Environment |
| **C/E** | 4 | ❌ 不必须 | Cause 段 + Effect 段分开 | Society / Environment / Health |
| **2-Pt** | 4 | ⚠️ 看 Q2 | 两个问题都答，均衡 | 各话题都可能 |
