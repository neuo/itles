## 1. 衔接词（10 类功能 × 每类 2-3 个）

### 1.1 用这个 / 不要用

| 功能 | ✅ 用这个 | ❌ 5.5 级硬伤 | 备注 |
|------|---------|------------|------|
| **引入第 1 论点** | `The primary reason is that...` / `To begin with,` | Firstly, | 任何题型 Body 1 T 句 |
| **加论点（段间）** | `Furthermore,` / `Moreover,` / `In addition,` | Also, / And, | Body 2 T 句开头可用 |
| **引入第 2 论点（DBV / 2-Pt）** | `Another important point is...` / `Another reason is that...` | Secondly, | DBV Body 2 转向自己立场前的过渡 |
| **转折** | `However,` / `Nevertheless,` | But, | 段首和句首都可 |
| **因果（给原因）** | `Since` / `Because` / `As`（句首子句）| 句首裸 Because 接残句 | 用于子句开头，注意接完整子句 |
| **让步** | `Admittedly,` / `Of course,` | Although...（容易写残成残句）| A/D 让步段 / DBV Body 1 |
| **举例** | `For example,` / `For instance,` | Like,（口语化）| Body 段 Ex 句开头 |
| **表结果** | `As a result,` / `Therefore,` / `Consequently,` | So, | E 句因果链 |
| **强调** | `Importantly,` / `Notably,` | Very important, | L 句强调重点 |
| **结论** | `In conclusion,` / `Overall,` | In a word,（中式）/ Anyway, | Conclusion 第一句 |

**🆕 2026-05-17 校准变更**：
- ❌ 旧版"加论点（段间）"`Another compelling argument is...`（Band 8 academic）→ ✅ 新版 `Another important point is...` / `Another reason is that...`
- ❌ 旧版"因果"`Owing to / Given that` → ✅ 新版 `Since / Because / As`（更高频；`Given that` 偏 Band 7.5）
- ❌ 旧版"让步"`Granted,` → ✅ 新版 `Of course,`（更口语自然，Band 7 安全）
- ❌ 旧版"举例"`A case in point is...` 单独列 → ✅ 新版统一用 `For example,` / `For instance,`（"A case in point" 偏 Band 7.5，移到 §4.2 句式 6 备注里）
- ❌ 旧版"表结果"`Consequently,` 单独列 → ✅ 新版补 `Therefore,`（更高频，Band 7 主推）
- ❌ 旧版"强调"`Crucially,` / `It is worth noting that` → ✅ 新版 `Importantly,` / `Notably,`（`Crucially` 偏 Band 7.5+；`It is worth noting that` 是 Band 7.5）
- ❌ 旧版"结论"`On balance,` → ✅ 新版补 `Overall,`（`On balance,` 保留可选但 `Overall` 更高频）

### 1.2 用法纪律（写作时遵守）

1. **一段最多 1 个显性衔接词**（Body 的 T 句开头算 1 个，段内别再加第 2 个）
2. **同一篇文章不重复用同一个**（已用 `Furthermore`，下一处加论点用 `Moreover`）
3. **4 段开头不要全用衔接词**（Intro 没有 / Body 1 没有 / Body 2 用 `However` / Conclusion 用 `In conclusion` —— 这种节奏才自然）
4. **覆盖 4-6 个不同功能**（不是 `Furthermore` × 4）

## 2. 8 个核心句式（每个会用 = 6.5 起步）

### 2.1 必背 8 句（已校准）

```
1. It is often argued that [paraphrase topic].
   → A/D 开头万能起手 ✓ Band 7

2. While I understand [opposing view], I [position] because [reason A] and [reason B].
   → 表立场带让步（A/D Intro 第 3 句）✓ Band 7

3. While I see value in both, I think [View B] is stronger.
   → DBV Intro 第 3 句（🆕 校准版，见 §2.3）

4. The primary reason for my position is that ___.
   → Body 1 T 句（A/D + DBV）✓ Band 7

5. This is because ___. As a result, ___.
   → E 句因果链（任何 Body 段）✓ Band 7

6. For example, in [country/situation], ___.
   → Ex 句（任何 Body 段）（🆕 校准版，见 §2.3）

7. This shows that ___.
   → L 句回扣（🆕 校准版，见 §2.3）
   备选：This demonstrates that ___. （✓ Band 7，可换用一次避免重复）

8. In conclusion, while [opposing view] has some merit, [restate your position in new words].
   → Conclusion 万能收尾 ✓ Band 7
```

### 2.2 句式覆盖检查

写完一篇文章，**至少用上 5 个**这 8 句中的（不一定原句，是该句式的变体）。**用 < 4 句 = 句式没用熟，回去练**。

### 2.3 🆕 2026-05-17 校准变更（句式 3 / 6 / 7）

#### 句式 3：DBV Intro 第 3 句

| 版本 | 句子 | 评价 |
|------|------|------|
| ❌ 旧版（已弃用）| `While both views have merit, I find [View B] more convincing.` | Band 7.5：`have merit` + `find...more convincing` 都是学术高阶 collocation，5.5-6 学生写不出 |
| ✅ 新版（推荐）| `While I see value in both, I think [View B] is stronger.` | Band 7：`see value in` 是中级 collocation，`is stronger` 简洁直接 |
| ✅ 备用 | `Although both views have something to offer, I largely agree with [View B].` | Band 7：略长但每个词都常见 |

**为什么换**：`merit` 是 Band 7.5 词，`find...more convincing` 是 examiner 看到会判 Band 7.5+ 的 collocation。新版用同样的"承认对方 + 站队"结构，但词汇全部 mid-level。

**对照范文使用**：`examples/01_education_dbv.md` Intro 第 4 句用的就是这个思路（`I largely agree with the second view.` —— 比新版还简洁，因为前面铺了立场）。

#### 句式 6：Ex 句

| 版本 | 句子 | 评价 |
|------|------|------|
| ❌ 旧版（已弃用）| `A case in point is ___, where ___.` | Band 7.5：`A case in point` 是高阶 academic phrase，配 `where` 从句是 Band 7.5 双结构 |
| ✅ 新版（推荐）| `For example, in [country/situation], ___.` | Band 7：最高频的举例方式 |
| ✅ 备用 1 | `For instance, ___.` | Band 7：和 `For example` 互换用 |
| ✅ 备用 2（写多了想换花样时）| `A good example is ___.` | Band 7：仍然简单 |

**为什么换**：`A case in point` 在学术写作里是 Band 7.5 标志（examiner 见到会给 LR 7.5）。普通 Band 7 考生不会主动用，硬背用 1 次就显眼。新版 `For example, in + 国家/情境` 是范文里最自然的 Ex 句开头。

**对照范文使用**：`examples/01_education_dbv.md` Body 1 Ex = `For example, in countries such as Singapore, ___`；Body 2 Ex = `For instance, ___`。

#### 句式 7：L 句

| 版本 | 句子 | 评价 |
|------|------|------|
| ⚠️ 旧版（保留但降级）| `This demonstrates that ___.` | Band 7：`demonstrates` 是 Band 7 但偏正式，**一篇用 1 次为限** |
| ❌ 旧版（已弃用）| `Such evidence shows that ___.` | Band 7.5：`Such evidence` 是 academic register，普通考生写不出 |
| ✅ 新版（主推）| `This shows that ___.` | Band 7：最高频的 L 句开头 |
| ✅ 备用 | `This suggests that ___.` | Band 7：弱一点的语气，适合 Body 2 |

**为什么换**：`Such evidence shows that` 是 Band 7.5 academic 套路。`This demonstrates that` 是 Band 7 但 register 偏正式，一篇用 1 次没问题，2 次就显堆砌。新版 `This shows that` 是 Band 7 最自然的 L 句开头，2 段 Body 都用也不显重复（因为后接的内容不同）。

**对照范文使用**：`examples/01_education_dbv.md` Body 1 L = `This shows why some people feel that ___`；Body 2 L = `This shows that ___`。两段都用 `This shows`，examiner 验证 Band 7 PASS。

## 3. 5 个题型 opener（Intro 第 1 句）

### 3.1 校准后版本（推荐使用）

| 题型 | ✅ 新版 Opener（Band 7） |
|------|----------------------|
| **A/D** | `It is often argued that ___.` |
| **DBV** | `[Topic] is often debated.` 或 `Opinions are divided on [topic].` |
| **P/S** | `[Topic] is a serious problem in many countries.` 或 `In many parts of the world, [problem] is getting worse.` |
| **C/E** | `More and more people now [trend].` 或 `In recent years, [trend] has become common.` |
| **2-Pt** | `There are two questions to consider about [topic]: ___ and ___.` 或 `This raises two questions: ___ and ___.` |

**用法**：每篇文章 Intro 第 1 句直接选对应 opener 改造（替换 [Topic]）。不要每次现想。

### 3.2 🆕 2026-05-17 校准变更（DBV / P/S / C/E / 2-Pt）

#### DBV opener

| 版本 | 句子 | 评价 |
|------|------|------|
| ❌ 旧版（已弃用）| `[Topic] is a subject of considerable debate.` | Band 8：`a subject of considerable debate` 是 examiner 一眼判 Band 8 的高阶 academic collocation（在 §3 markers 清单里明确列出）|
| ✅ 新版主推 | `[Topic] is often debated.` | Band 7：`often debated` 是 mid-level，自然 |
| ✅ 新版备用 | `Opinions are divided on [topic].` | Band 7：`opinions are divided` 是常见 collocation，比旧版降一个档 |

**为什么换**：`of considerable debate` 在 examiner protocol §3 明确列为 Band 8 marker。新版两个版本都是 Band 7 标准。

**对照范文使用**：`examples/01_education_dbv.md` Intro 第 1 句 = `What schools should teach children is often debated.` —— examiner verified Band 7 PASS。

#### P/S opener

| 版本 | 句子 | 评价 |
|------|------|------|
| ❌ 旧版（已弃用）| `[Topic] has become an increasingly pressing issue in recent years.` | Band 7.5：`increasingly pressing issue` 是雕琢搭配（`pressing` + `issue` + `increasingly` 三个升级词堆一起）|
| ✅ 新版主推 | `[Topic] is a serious problem in many countries.` | Band 7：`serious problem` + `in many countries` 全是中级常见 |
| ✅ 新版备用 | `In many parts of the world, [problem] is getting worse.` | Band 7：`is getting worse` 是日常但完全 Band 7 |

**为什么换**：旧版三个升级词堆一起是典型的"学生背模板的痕迹"，examiner 会判 LR 7.5（暴露刻意）。新版用更自然的 mid-level 词。

#### C/E opener

| 版本 | 句子 | 评价 |
|------|------|------|
| ❌ 旧版（已弃用）| `In recent decades, [trend] has [verb], prompting concern.` | Band 8：`prompting concern` 是高阶分词结构 + abstract noun；`In recent decades` 复杂时间状语 |
| ✅ 新版主推 | `More and more people now [trend].` | Band 7：`more and more` 是 Band 7 入门表达，简洁有效 |
| ✅ 新版备用 | `In recent years, [trend] has become common.` | Band 7：`has become common` 是常见现在完成 + 形容词 |

**为什么换**：`prompting concern` 是 Band 8 分词结构（reduced participle phrase 表结果），属于"减得不准会扣分"的高风险结构。新版用现在完成时 + 高频副词。

#### 2-Pt opener

| 版本 | 句子 | 评价 |
|------|------|------|
| ⚠️ 旧版（保留但降级）| `[Topic] raises two related questions: ___ and ___.` | Band 7.5：`raises two related questions` 是学术写作 collocation，偏 Band 7.5 |
| ✅ 新版主推 | `There are two questions to consider about [topic]: ___ and ___.` | Band 7：`there are` + `to consider` 是中级，每个词都常见 |
| ✅ 新版备用 | `This raises two questions: ___ and ___.` | Band 7：去掉 `related` 就降到 Band 7（更直接）|

**为什么换**：`raises... related questions` 三词捆在一起是 Band 7.5 学术 register。新版主推用 `there are ... to consider` 平直结构，备用版去掉 `related` 单词降档。

## 4. 词汇升级 20 组（mid-to-upper vocabulary）

> 这 20 组是"低级 → 中级"的高频升级。**只升一半**——文章里如果全用升级版会显得做作。
> 目标：让阅卷人感觉"这人有意识在选词，但不刻意"。

### 4.1 通用升级（已标 Band 8 风险项）

| 低级（5.5）| 升级（6.5-7）| 备注 |
|----------|------------|------|
| people | individuals / citizens / the public | 看语境 |
| things | aspects / issues / matters | "issues" 偏问题语境 |
| problem | challenge / concern / drawback | "challenge" 偏中性 |
| important | crucial / vital / significant | 三选一；`crucial` 偏 Band 7.5，一篇 1 次 |
| good | beneficial / valuable | `advantageous` 略高阶，能不用就不用 |
| bad | harmful / damaging | `detrimental` 是 Band 7.5，一篇 1 次 |
| a lot of | a great deal of / a large amount of | ⚠️ `considerable` / `substantial` 是 Band 7.5 边界，**一篇最多用 1 次**，多用触发 Band 8 |
| many | a significant number of / numerous | `numerous` 偏 Band 7.5，可选 |
| big | major / large | ⚠️ `substantial` / `considerable` 同上，限 1 次 |
| help | support / contribute to | `facilitate` 偏 Band 7.5，可选 |
| use | use（默认）/ employ（可选）| ⚠️ `utilise` 是 Band 7.5+ formal，**默认用 `use`**；想换花样最多 1 次 `employ` |
| think | believe / argue | `maintain` 偏 Band 7.5，可选 |
| show | show / demonstrate | `illustrate` / `reveal` 偏 Band 7.5，可选 |
| change | shift / change（名词版）| `transformation` 是 Band 7 但戏剧化，**只在真的"大转变"语境用**，一篇 1 次 |
| start | begin / launch | `initiate` 偏 Band 7.5，可选 |
| improve | improve / strengthen / boost | `enhance` 偏 Band 7.5，可选 |
| reduce | reduce / curb | ⚠️ `mitigate` 是 Band 7 但偏正式，**一篇 1 次**；默认 `reduce` |
| because | because / since | ⚠️ `owing to / due to` 接名词不接句子，写错就降；`on account of` 偏 Band 7.5 |
| so | as a result / therefore | `consequently` 也行但偏正式 |
| more and more | increasingly / a growing number of | |

### 4.2 用法纪律（🆕 校准后强化）

1. **每篇文章升级 5-8 个**（不是全部）
2. **同一篇不重复用同一个升级词**（用了 `crucial` 就别再用第二次）
3. **不会用就别用**——`utilise` 用错比 `use` 用对扣分多
4. **collocation 优先于 vocabulary**——"conduct research" 比 "do a research" 重要得多
5. **🆕 Band 7.5 词限量**：表里标 ⚠️ 的词（`considerable` / `substantial` / `crucial` / `mitigate` / `transformation` / `utilise`）**一篇最多 1 次**。用 2 次以上 examiner 就会判 LR 7.5+
6. **🆕 默认降档**：能用 mid-level 就别上 Band 7.5。例：`reduce` > `mitigate`；`use` > `utilise`；`show` > `demonstrate`；`change` > `transformation`

### 4.3 致命的 collocation 错误（必须修）

| ❌ 错 | ✅ 对 |
|------|-----|
| do a research | conduct research / carry out research |
| make a research | conduct research |
| make a homework | do homework |
| informations | information（不可数）|
| advices | advice（不可数）|
| equipments | equipment（不可数）|
| furnitures | furniture（不可数）|
| an useful | a useful（u- 发 /ju/ 用 a）|
| in nowadays | nowadays（直接当时间副词）|
| in the same time | at the same time |

## 5. 复杂句结构（GRA 上 7 必备）

Band 7 要求"**variety of complex structures**"——你**至少要稳定使用 3 种**。

### 5.1 必会的 3 种 + 1 个可选

#### A. 状语从句（最基础，必会）

引导词：`because / although / while / since / when / if / as / unless`

例：
- `Although technology has connected people across distances, it has also created new forms of isolation.`
- `Since governments have limited budgets, they must prioritise essential services.`
- `If this trend continues, social inequality will deepen.`

#### B. 关系从句（必会）

引导词：`who / which / that / where / whose`

例：
- `Students who study subjects they enjoy tend to develop deeper skills.`
- `Cities such as Vienna, which attract millions of tourists annually, benefit from cultural investment.`
- `Countries where education is well-funded show stronger economic growth.`

**注意**：限定性（无逗号）vs 非限定性（有逗号）——别混。

#### C. 名词性从句（that-clause / what-clause，必会）

例：
- `It is often argued **that** education should serve society.`
- `The fact **that** young people prefer digital communication shows a generational shift.`
- `**What** matters most is not how much we earn but how we spend.`

#### D. 条件句（可选，1-2 个用对加分）

- 第 1 类：`If governments invest more in education, students will benefit.`（真实假设）
- 第 2 类：`If schools focused on creativity, students would be more engaged.`（虚拟现在）
- 第 3 类：`If this had been addressed earlier, the crisis could have been avoided.`（虚拟过去）

**第 2 类最常用**——讨论"应该怎么做"时自然。第 3 类容易写错，**没把握就不用**。

### 5.2 不用的 Band 8+ 结构（用了反而扣分）

| 结构 | 例 | 为什么不用 |
|------|---|----------|
| **Cleft** | "It is education that matters most." | 容易写错，且做作 |
| **Inversion** | "Not only does X benefit Y, but Z also..." | 同上 |
| **Mixed conditional** | "If X had been done, Y would be different now" | 时态容易乱 |
| **Reduced relative clause** | "Students struggling to..." (= "who struggle to") | 减得不准会写残 |
| **Passive reporting clause** | "It is widely believed that..." | 常被认定 filler |

## 8. 校准 self-test：用新版工具拼一个 DBV Intro

> **目的**：验证 §4.3 校准后 DBV opener + §4.2 句式 3 校准后版本拼出来的 Intro 是 Band 7（不是 Band 8 漂移，也不是 Band 5.5 极简）。
>
> **方法**：不调 examiner subagent（4 句 Intro 太短无法 examiner），靠对照 §_examiner_protocol.md §3 Band 8 markers 清单 + §4 Band 7 安全区 手动判断。

### 题目（用 examples/01 同题，便于对照）

> Some people think that schools should teach children academic subjects that will be beneficial to their future careers. Others think it is more important for schools to teach a wide range of subjects, including arts and music.
> Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

### Demo Intro（用新版工具）

> **What schools should teach children is often debated.** Some people believe schools should focus on academic subjects that prepare students for their careers. Others think schools should also teach a wide range of subjects such as arts and music. **While I see value in both, I think the second view is stronger.**

（粗体是新版工具：opener = §4.3 DBV 新版；立场句 = §4.2 句式 3 新版）

### Band 8 marker scan

| Marker（来自 _examiner_protocol §3）| 这段 Intro 有吗 |
|---------|--------------|
| "of considerable debate" / "a subject of..." | ❌ 无（用 `is often debated`）|
| "the role of X in doing Y" 抽象名词链 | ❌ 无（用 `What schools should teach children` 主谓直接）|
| "instrumental" / "thrive in" / 高阶 collocation | ❌ 无 |
| `have merit` / `find...more convincing` | ❌ 无（用 `see value in both` + `is stronger`）|
| Cleft / Inversion / Reduced relative / Passive reporting | ❌ 无 |

### Band 7 安全区 scan

| 检查（来自 _examiner_protocol §4）| 这段 Intro 有吗 |
|----------|--------------|
| 立场清晰（Intro 第 4 句明确）| ✅ `I think the second view is stronger` |
| 衔接覆盖（开头 opener + 立场句过渡 `While I see value in both`）| ✅ 有 |
| 复杂句类型（that-clause / who-clause）| ✅ `believe schools should ___` / `subjects that prepare ___` / `subjects that...` |
| 升级词中等数量 | ✅ `debated` / `focus on` / `prepare for` / `value in` |
| 无 §3 Band 8 markers | ✅ 全部清空 |

### 5.5 级硬伤 scan

| 检查 | 结果 |
|------|------|
| `people think` 这种极简表达 | ❌ 无（用 `believe` / `think` 配复杂宾语从句）|
| `things` / `very important` 极简词 | ❌ 无 |
| 主谓 / 冠词 / 时态错误 | ❌ 无 |

### Self-test verdict

**✅ PASS** — Intro 落在 Band 7 安全区：

- 没踩任何 Band 8 marker（参 §3 清单）
- 命中 4 个 Band 7 安全区特征
- 没有 5.5 极简表达（`see value in both` 不是 `like both`；`is stronger` 不是 `is better`）

**结论**：§4.2 句式 3 / §4.3 DBV opener 校准成功，新版工具拼出来的 Intro 落在目标 Band 7 区间。
