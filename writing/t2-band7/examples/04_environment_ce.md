# Example 04 — Environment C/E（Band 7 范文 + 详细标注）

> **第四篇标杆范文**，演示 C/E（Causes + Effects）题型的"原因段 + 后果段"结构。
>
> **目标水平**：Band 7（不是 Band 8、不是 Band 5.5）。仿写时**只能学这个水平**，不要学得太花。
>
> 关键技术点：Effects 段可以 **mixed positive / negative**——不一定全是坏处。Band 7 看的是分析平衡而不是立场偏激。

---

## 题目

> In recent years, more and more people are buying clothes, shoes, and other items online instead of in shops.
>
> **What are the reasons for this trend, and what are the effects on traditional retail stores?**

题型：**C/E** | 结构：Body 1 = 2 个原因 / Body 2 = 2 个后果（混合正负影响）

---

## 范文（280 词）

### Intro

More and more people now buy clothes, shoes and other goods online instead of in shops, and this has raised concerns about the future of high-street stores. This essay will look at the main reasons for this shift and its effects on traditional retailers.

### Body 1 — 原因段（2 个原因压一段）

**[T]** The main reason for the move to online shopping is the combination of convenience and price.
**[E]** In terms of convenience, customers can browse thousands of items at any hour and have them delivered to their door, which suits busy adults. In terms of price, online retailers have lower running costs and can therefore offer discounts and free shipping.
**[Ex]** A case in point is the growth of platforms such as Amazon and Taobao, which have built large customer bases through cheap goods and instant reviews.
**[L]** This shows that the trend is driven by practical advantages that physical shops find hard to match.

### Body 2 — 后果段（2 个后果，混合正负影响）

**[T]** The effects on traditional retailers have been large, and mostly negative for the high street.
**[E]** On the one hand, many small shops have had to close because they cannot match online prices, which has emptied town centres and cost local jobs. On the other hand, shops that survive are changing the in-store experience, with personal styling, cafés and special products that cannot be copied online.
**[Ex]** For instance, several department stores in the United Kingdom have moved toward "experience-led" formats, where shoppers come for the events as much as the goods.
**[L]** This shows that, although online shopping has damaged regular retail, it has also pushed survivors to change.

### Conclusion

In conclusion, online shopping has grown mainly because it is more convenient and cheaper, and its effects on retail have been mixed: many shops have closed, while the rest are finding new ways to attract customers. Overall, the high street will survive, but in a very different form.

## 4 维度评分检查（examiner verified Band 7.0）

### TR（Task Response）= 7.0

| 检查点 | 怎么做到 |
|--------|---------|
| Addresses both parts (causes + effects) | Body 1 给原因 + Body 2 给后果；Intro 第 2 句已经路线图 |
| Causes / effects clearly separated | Body 1 严格只讲 reasons（convenience + price）；Body 2 严格只讲 effects（closures + adaptation）|
| Effects balanced (not all-bad) | "On the one hand...have had to close" + "On the other hand...are changing the in-store experience" → 混合 positive/negative |
| Main ideas extended + supported | 每段完整 TEEL；例子具体（Amazon/Taobao / UK department stores）|

**注意**：没量化（没"X% 实体店关闭"）—— 没到 7.5 fully developed。

### CC（Coherence and Cohesion）= 7.0

| 检查点 | 怎么做到 |
|--------|---------|
| 4 段结构 + clear progression | Intro 框架 → Body 1 原因 → Body 2 后果 → Conclusion 综合 |
| Range of cohesive devices | 用了 **6 个不同功能**：The main reason is / In terms of X...In terms of Y / On the one hand...On the other hand / A case in point is / For instance / This shows that / In conclusion / Overall |
| 每段一个明确主题 | Body 1 T = "combination of convenience and price"；Body 2 T = "large, and mostly negative" |
| 段内 mini-point 衔接清楚 | Body 1 "In terms of convenience...In terms of price"；Body 2 "On the one hand...On the other hand" |

### LR（Lexical Resource）= 7.0

**Mid-level 词汇（用对的 collocation）**：
- "high-street stores" / "traditional retailers" / "running costs"
- "discounts and free shipping" / "large customer bases" / "instant reviews"
- "practical advantages" / "town centres" / "in-store experience"
- "personal styling" / "experience-led formats" / "department stores"
- "regular retail" / "survivors"

**没用的 Band 8 高阶**：
- ❌ "In recent decades, online shopping has expanded rapidly, prompting concern"（改用 "More and more people now buy ... and this has raised concerns"）
- ❌ "primary reason"（改用 "main reason"）
- ❌ "loyal customer bases"（改用 "large customer bases"）
- ❌ "have been significant, and largely negative"（改用 "have been large, and mostly negative"）
- ❌ "have been forced to close"（改用 "have had to close"）
- ❌ "reinventing the in-store experience" / "exclusive products that cannot be replicated"（改用 "changing the in-store experience" / "special products that cannot be copied"）
- ❌ "shifted toward" / "conventional retail" / "evolve"（改用 "moved toward" / "regular retail" / "change"）
- ❌ "On balance" / "reinventing themselves"（改用 "Overall" / "finding new ways to attract customers"）

### GRA（Grammatical Range and Accuracy）= 7.0

**复杂句类型（4 种 = variety）**：

| 类型 | 例 |
|------|---|
| 状语从句（because / although）| `many small shops have had to close **because** they cannot match online prices` / `**although** online shopping has damaged regular retail` |
| 关系从句（非限定 which）| `..., **which** suits busy adults` / `..., **which** has emptied town centres` / `platforms such as Amazon and Taobao, **which** have built...` |
| 关系从句（where）| `"experience-led" formats, **where** shoppers come for the events as much as the goods` |
| 名词性从句 | `This shows **that** the trend is driven by practical advantages...` / `This shows **that**, although...` |

**无 Band 8+ 雷区**：
- ❌ 无 cleft（无 "It is X that Y"）
- ❌ 无 inversion
- ❌ 无 mixed conditional
- ❌ 无 reduced relative clause（用全 relative：`shops that survive` 不是 `shops surviving`）
- ❌ 无 passive reporting clause（无 "It is widely believed that..."）

**5.5 级硬伤清点**：0 个 ✅
- 冠词都对（the move / the high street / the trend / the events / the goods / the rest）
- 可数不可数都对（shopping 不可数 / costs 复数 / shipping 不可数 / experience 不可数）
- 主谓一致都对（platforms...have built / shops have had to / retailers have lower）
- 时态稳定（present perfect 表"已造成的影响" + present simple 描述常态 + will 用于结尾预测）
- 被动用得当（have had to close 用主动 / cannot be copied 用被动）

| §4.1 类别 | 用的衔接词 | 出现位置 |

| 引入第 1 论点 | The main reason is that... | Body 1 T |
| 段内并列对仗 | In terms of convenience / In terms of price | Body 1 E（mini-T）|
| 段内对比 | On the one hand / On the other hand | Body 2 E（mini-T）|
| 举例 | A case in point is / For instance | Body 1 Ex / Body 2 Ex |
| L 句回扣 | This shows that | Body 1 L / Body 2 L |

| §4.2 句式 | 文章里对应 |
|----------|----------|
| 句式 4（The main reason for ... is ...）| Body 1 T |

## 这篇为什么是 Band 7（不是 6.5 也不是 7.5）

| 维度 | 离 6.5 的差距 | 离 7.5 的差距 |
|------|-------------|-------------|
| TR | 原因 / 后果严格分开；后果段做了 positive/negative 平衡处理 → **不是 6.5 的 "after-effects 全是负面 + 偏激"** | 没有量化分析（如"X% 的实体店关闭"）→ 没到 7.5 的 "fully developed" |
| CC | 段内用 "In terms of X / On the one hand" 等对仗清晰 → **不是 6.5 的 "mini-point 混在一起"** | 衔接 "appropriate" 不到 "seamless" → 没到 7.5 |
| LR | mid-level 词 + topic-specific collocation（"running costs" / "experience-led formats" / "town centres"）→ **不是 6.5 的 "limited"** | 没用 academic intensifier / 没精巧 word formation → 没到 7.5 |
| GRA | 4 种复杂句 + 被动用得当 + 0 硬伤 → **不是 6.5 的 "some errors"** | 没玩 inversion / cleft → 没到 7.5 |

**这就是 C/E 题型 Band 7 该长的样子——原因后果分开 + 后果段不偏激 + 段末 L 句承认 trade-off**。

## 怎么用这份材料

### Phase 1 — 看 3 遍画结构（15 min）

#### 第 1 遍（2-3 min）— 读懂
一句话总结："作者认为网购火主要是方便（任意时间浏览 + 送货）+ 便宜（运营成本低、可让利）；对实体店的后果是：很多关门，但活下来的被迫升级体验（experience-led formats），整体看 high street 不会消失但会变形。"

#### 第 2 遍（5-8 min）— 画结构

```
【Intro】
  Sentence 1 paraphrase: ___（趋势描述）
  Sentence 2 路线图: ___（这篇要讲 causes + effects）

【Body 1 = 原因段】
  T = ___（一句话概括所有原因的共性）
  E = ___
    mini 1 (方便侧): ___
    mini 2 (价格侧): ___
  Ex = ___（具体平台）
  L = ___（段末收尾——只 1 个 L 句）

【Body 2 = 后果段（混合 +/-）】
  T = ___（一句话定基调，可以"largely negative"也可"mixed"）
  E = ___
    mini 1 (负面): ___
    mini 2 (正面/适应): ___
  Ex = ___
  L = ___（段末承认 trade-off）

【Conclusion】
  概括原因：___
  概括后果（保留 mixed）：___
  小预测/收尾：___
```

#### 第 3 遍（2-3 min）— 合上原文复述
口头说出骨架（30-45 秒）。

### Phase 2 — 仿写同题（25 min）

**仿写时刻意控制**：
- Body 1 只写**原因**，绝不开始说后果（C/E 题最容易在 Body 1 偷跑去讲后果）
- Body 2 必须有**至少一处正面影响**——纯负面会被批"underdeveloped"
- 段末 L 句要带"承认 trade-off"语气（"although...has damaged..., it has also pushed..."）

### Phase 3 — 对比 + 反馈（10 min）

按本文的"4 维度评分检查"清单逐项打勾。
