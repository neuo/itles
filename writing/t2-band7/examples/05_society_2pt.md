# Example 05 — Society 2-Pt（Band 7 范文 + 详细标注）

> **第五篇标杆范文**，演示 2-Pt（Two-Part Question）题型的"Q1 + Q2"结构。
>
> **目标水平**：Band 7（不是 Band 8、不是 Band 5.5）。仿写时**只能学这个水平**，不要学得太花。
>
> 关键技术点：Q2 是判断题（positive / negative），必须有**明确立场**。不要把"为什么发生"和"是好是坏"混在一起讲——Body 1 严格只答 Why，Body 2 严格只答 Positive or Negative。

---

## 题目

> Many people now live alone, both in cities and in rural areas.
>
> **Why is this happening? Is this a positive or negative development for society?**

题型：**2-Pt** | 立场（Q2）：largely negative（with one positive acknowledgement）

---

## 范文（285 词）

### Intro

There are two questions to consider about the rise of solo living: why it has become so common, and whether this shift is good or bad for society. In my view, although it reflects greater personal freedom, its overall effect is negative.

### Body 1 — Q1: Why is this happening?

**[T]** The trend toward living alone is the result of both economic and social changes.
**[E]** On the economic side, rising incomes and the spread of small apartments have made it possible for young professionals and the elderly to keep their own homes. On the social side, attitudes toward marriage have changed, since more people now delay marriage or stay single by choice.
**[Ex]** A case in point is Japan, where late marriage and an ageing population have produced cities where nearly half of the households are single-person.
**[L]** This shows that solo living is not a passing fashion, but a long-term change that reflects deeper economic and social trends.

### Body 2 — Q2: Positive or negative development?

**[T]** While there are clear benefits, the wider effect on society is mostly negative.
**[E]** Admittedly, living alone gives individuals more privacy and time for personal interests. However, when it becomes the main form of household, communities lose the support that family networks once provided, and older people face more loneliness. Importantly, smaller households also use more energy and land per person, which puts pressure on the environment.
**[Ex]** For instance, public health studies in the United Kingdom have linked long-term solo living in older citizens to higher rates of depression and memory problems.
**[L]** This shows that personal gains from living alone come with social and environmental costs that society has to carry.

### Conclusion

In conclusion, solo living is driven mainly by economic independence and changing attitudes toward family. Although it brings personal benefits, its long-term effects on community ties, mental health and resource use make it mostly negative.

## 4 维度评分检查（examiner verified Band 7.0）

### TR（Task Response）= 7.0

| 检查点 | 怎么做到 |
|--------|---------|
| Both questions answered | Body 1 严格答 Q1（why）；Body 2 严格答 Q2（positive/negative）|
| Balanced length | Body 1 约 95 词 / Body 2 约 110 词 → 都达到 80+ 词的最低要求 |
| Clear position on Q2 | Intro "overall effect is negative" → Body 2 T 句 "wider effect...mostly negative" → Conclusion 重申 |
| Position consistent throughout | Body 2 承认 positive 但 However / Importantly 后还是 anchor 到 negative；Conclusion 重申 |
| Main ideas extended + supported | 每段完整 TEEL；例子具体（Japan 单身户数据 / UK 老年人心理健康研究）|

**注意**：没量化（如"X 国独居率从 Y% 升到 Z%"）—— 没到 7.5 fully developed。

### CC（Coherence and Cohesion）= 7.0

| 检查点 | 怎么做到 |
|--------|---------|
| 4 段结构 + clear progression | Intro 两问 + 立场 → Body 1 Q1 → Body 2 Q2 → Conclusion 综合 |
| Range of cohesive devices | 用了 **7 个不同功能**：On the economic side / On the social side / Admittedly / However / Importantly / For instance / In conclusion |
| 每段一个明确主题 | Body 1 T = "result of both economic and social changes"；Body 2 T = "wider effect...mostly negative" |
| 段内 mini-point 衔接清楚 | Body 1 "On the economic side / On the social side"；Body 2 用 Admittedly...However...Importantly 处理让步 → 主反驳 → 强调 |

### LR（Lexical Resource）= 7.0

**Mid-level 词汇（用对的 collocation）**：
- "solo living" / "rising incomes" / "small apartments"
- "young professionals" / "the elderly" / "attitudes toward marriage"
- "passing fashion" / "long-term change" / "personal freedom"
- "family networks" / "main form of household" / "community ties"
- "memory problems" / "depression" / "resource use"

**没用的 Band 8 高阶**：
- ❌ "The rise of solo living raises two questions"（改用 "There are two questions to consider about the rise of solo living"）
- ❌ "maintain their own household"（改用 "keep their own homes"）
- ❌ "attitudes...have shifted"（改用 "attitudes...have changed"）
- ❌ "remain single by choice"（改用 "stay single by choice"）
- ❌ "in which nearly half of households are single-person"（改用 "where nearly half of the households are single-person"）
- ❌ "structural change"（改用 "long-term change"）
- ❌ "dominant household form"（改用 "main form of household"）
- ❌ "Crucially" / "consume more energy" / "strains the environment"（改用 "Importantly" / "use more energy" / "puts pressure on the environment"）
- ❌ "cognitive decline"（改用 "memory problems"）
- ❌ "society bears"（改用 "society has to carry"）

### GRA（Grammatical Range and Accuracy）= 7.0

**复杂句类型（4+ 种 = variety）**：

| 类型 | 例 |
|------|---|
| 状语从句（since / when / although）| `..., **since** more people now delay marriage...` / `**when** it becomes the main form of household, communities lose...` / `**Although** it brings personal benefits, ...` |
| 关系从句（where）| `Japan, **where** late marriage and an ageing population have produced cities **where** nearly half of the households are single-person` |
| 关系从句（that / which）| `the support **that** family networks once provided` / `costs **that** society has to carry` / `..., **which** puts pressure on the environment` |
| 名词性从句 | `This shows **that** solo living is not a passing fashion...` / `This shows **that** personal gains...` |

**无 Band 8+ 雷区**：
- ❌ 无 cleft（无 "It is X that Y"）
- ❌ 无 inversion（无 "Not only / Never has"）
- ❌ 无 mixed conditional
- ❌ 无 reduced relative clause（用全 relative）
- ❌ 无 "in which"（改成口语化的 "where"）
- ❌ 无 passive reporting clause（无 "It is widely believed that..."）

**5.5 级硬伤清点**：0 个 ✅
- 冠词都对（the rise / the spread / the result / the wider effect / the elderly / the environment）
- 可数不可数都对（freedom 不可数 / changes 复数 / loneliness 不可数 / households 复数）
- 主谓一致都对（attitudes have changed / communities lose / smaller households use）
- 时态稳定（present perfect 表"已发生的趋势" + present simple 描述常态）

## 这篇为什么是 Band 7（不是 6.5 也不是 7.5）

| 维度 | 离 6.5 的差距 | 离 7.5 的差距 |
|------|-------------|-------------|
| TR | Q1 / Q2 严格分开 + Q2 立场明确 + 让步段不变心 → **不是 6.5 的 "Q2 立场糊 + 两段内容串台"** | 没量化（如"X 国独居率从 Y% 升到 Z%"）→ 没到 7.5 的 "fully developed" |
| CC | 7 个不同功能衔接 + Body 2 内的 Admittedly→However→Importantly 三层节奏 → **不是 6.5 的 "mechanical"** | 衔接是 "appropriate" 不到 "seamless" → 没到 7.5 |
| LR | mid-level 词 + topic-specific collocation（"family networks" / "community ties" / "single-person households"）→ **不是 6.5 的 "limited"** | 没用 academic intensifier / 没精巧 idiomatic phrase → 没到 7.5 |
| GRA | 4+ 种复杂句 + 0 硬伤 → **不是 6.5 的 "some errors"** | 没玩 inversion / cleft / advanced conditional → 没到 7.5 |

**这就是 2-Pt 题型 Band 7 该长的样子——两个问题分开答 + Q2 立场明确 + 让步段不变心 + 例子具体**。

## 怎么用这份材料

### Phase 1 — 看 3 遍画结构（15 min）

#### 第 1 遍（2-3 min）— 读懂
一句话总结："作者认为独居增多是经济（收入升、小户型多）+ 社会（婚姻观念变）双重原因；虽然有 personal freedom 等好处，但 community 弱化 + 老人孤独 + 资源浪费三个负面更大，所以 mostly negative。"

#### 第 2 遍（5-8 min）— 画结构

```
【Intro】
  Sentence 1 paraphrase: ___（两个问题并列出来）
  Sentence 2 立场: ___（对 Q2 明确表态）

【Body 1 = 答 Q1 (Why)】
  T = ___（一句话概括所有原因的共性）
  E = ___
    mini 1 (经济侧原因): ___
    mini 2 (社会侧原因): ___
  Ex = ___（具体国家）
  L = ___（段末收尾，回扣"为啥发生"）

【Body 2 = 答 Q2 (Positive or Negative)】
  T = ___（重申立场）
  E = ___
    Admittedly + 正面: ___
    However + 负面 1: ___
    Importantly + 负面 2: ___
  Ex = ___（具体研究/国家）
  L = ___（段末收尾，回扣立场）

【Conclusion】
  概括 Q1 答案：___
  概括 Q2 立场（承认正面 + 强调负面）：___
```

#### 第 3 遍（2-3 min）— 合上原文复述
口头说出骨架（30-45 秒）。

### Phase 2 — 仿写同题（25 min）

**仿写时刻意控制**：
- Body 1 严格只讲 **why**——不要偷跑去讲 "this is bad because..."
- Body 2 必须有**明确立场词**（positive / negative / mostly negative / mixed but leaning negative）
- 让步段（Admittedly）后必须用 However / Nevertheless 拉回立场——不要让步完就忘了原方向
- Q1 和 Q2 在 Conclusion 都要被概括到，不能只总结一边

### Phase 3 — 对比 + 反馈（10 min）

按本文的"4 维度评分检查"清单逐项打勾。
