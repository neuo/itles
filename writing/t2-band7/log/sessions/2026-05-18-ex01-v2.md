## 我的 v2（原版，保留错误）

### Intro
"What is the goal of education is frequently debated. Some people argue that schools should focus on academic subjects to help students find jobs. Others believe teaching a variety of subjects, like arts and music, is also important. I largely agree with the second view."

### Body 1
"Those who support a job-focused curriculum argue that it is the duty of schools to help students find good jobs. As the job market becomes increasingly competitive today, students without practical skills in subjects like computer or math may struggle to find stable jobs. For example, in some countries such as Singapore, schools particularly focus on STEM subjects, and as a result, the majority of students there get on to well-paid jobs in technology and finance. That is why the supporters worry about — schools should not produce students who are well-rounded but can't find jobs."

Subjects like art and music teach students to express their feelings and develop creativity, both of which also play an important role in their life and career. For instance, students who learn music tend to have better memory and concentration, while those who study art tend to show more imaginative when solving problems. These abilities are more valued in creative industries and many academic careers. This demonstrates that a wider curriculum is not just a luxury, but the foundation for both personal growth and long-term career development."

### Conclusion
"In conclusion, while preparing students for jobs is important, focusing on a job-related subject would mean missing the bigger purpose of education. A education system that combines academic subjects with art and music is more likely to produce students who would success both at work and in their wilder lives."

## 修复版（fix 后）

### Intro
What the goal of education is has been frequently debated. Some people argue that schools should focus on academic subjects to help students find jobs. Others believe teaching a variety of subjects, like arts and music, is also important. I largely agree with the second view.

### Body 1
**[T]** Those who support a job-focused curriculum argue that it is the duty of schools to help students find good jobs.
**[E]** As the job market becomes increasingly competitive, students without practical skills in subjects like computing or maths may struggle to find stable jobs.
**[Ex]** For example, in countries such as Singapore, schools particularly focus on STEM subjects, and as a result, the majority of students go on to well-paid jobs in technology and finance.
**[L]** This is what supporters worry about — schools should not produce students who are well-rounded but cannot find jobs.

### Body 2
**[T]** Nevertheless, I think a broader curriculum is more beneficial because education is not only about preparing students for jobs but also about developing them as whole people.
**[E]** Subjects like art and music teach students to express their feelings and develop creativity, both of which play an important role in their life and career.
**[Ex]** For instance, students who learn music tend to have better memory and concentration, while those who study art tend to be more imaginative when solving problems. These abilities are increasingly valued in creative industries and many academic careers.
**[L]** This shows that a wider curriculum is not just a luxury, but the foundation for both personal growth and long-term career development.

### Conclusion
In conclusion, while preparing students for jobs is important, focusing only on career-related subjects would mean missing the bigger purpose of education. An education system that combines academic subjects with art and music is more likely to produce students who can succeed both at work and in their wider lives.

## Drill #2 记录（一并存这里）

### 源句
"学生应该具备适应未来变化的能力。"

### 我的 3 个英文版本
1. `students should have the ability to fill in the changes of future`（🚨 fill in 致命中式直译）
2. `It's necessary for students to have adaptability for future`（✅ 最佳 frame，需修小错）
3. `students should show the abilities to adapt the future`（adapt 漏 to）

### 最佳修复版
**V3 修后**：`Students should develop the ability to adapt to future changes.` ← 进 active_phrases.md Phrase 5

### Drill 学到
- ✅ "It is necessary for X to Y" 是 Band 7 frame
- ✅ `develop the ability to adapt to X` 是地道 collocation
- 🚨 **fill in 不是"适应"**（W2-9 新中式直译）
- 🚨 **adapt 必须接 to**（W2-9 新介词错）
- ⚠️ 跳过中文 3 版本可能漏 frame variety
