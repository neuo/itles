## 我的 v2（清版，0 硬伤）

### Intro
These days, the trend towards purchasing clothes, shoes, and other goods online has grown rapidly, transforming the way people shop. This essay will examine the main reasons for this shift and its effects on traditional retailers.

### Body 1
**[T]** The popularity of online shopping results primarily from the combination of convenience and price.
**[E1]** Regarding convenience, online platforms allow consumers to break geographic barriers, enabling them to browse thousands of goods and place orders through digital devices at any time, from anywhere.
**[E2]** As for price, online retailers often offer competitive pricing because they have lower running costs, especially through saving on rent.
**[Ex]** A case in point is the growth of Amazon, which has built large consumer bases through low-priced goods and their convenient delivery service.
**[L]** Therefore, the practical advantages, which are hard for physical shops to match, make online shopping an irresistible choice for modern consumers.

### Body 2
**[T]** However, the digital shift has brought significant challenges, most of which are negative, for traditional retailers.
**[E1]** The most direct impact is a dramatic decline in foot traffic and sales. As more consumers prefer shopping online, a large number of physical shops struggle to cover their fixed costs.
**[Ex1]** For example, many traditional appliance stores located in city centers in China have had to close down over the last decade.
**[E2]** On the other hand, the pressure also forces surviving shops to adapt by upgrading their in-store services, such as personal styling, cafes and interactive shopping experiences that cannot be replicated online.
**[Ex2]** For instance, many shopping malls in China have moved towards "experience-led" formats, where shoppers come for the events as much as the goods.
**[L]** These examples demonstrate that the growth of online shopping **not only** threatens the survival of physical shops, **but also** drives the remaining ones to reinvent themselves.

### Conclusion
In conclusion, modern consumers have picked up new shopping habits because online shopping is more convenient and cheaper. While this has undoubtedly put immense pressure on traditional shops and caused some of them to close down, it also forces the retail industry to evolve and find new ways to attract consumers.

**字数**：~334 词（略高于 250-285 spec，但 Band 7 范围内）
