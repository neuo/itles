## 题目（same as v1）

> Some people believe that the increasing use of computers and mobile phones for communication has had a negative effect on young people's social skills. **To what extent do you agree or disagree?**

**题型**：A/D | **立场**：largely disagree | **结构**：Option A（让步 + 主反驳）

## 我的 v2（原版，保留错误）

### Intro
"It is often argued that the heavy use of computers and mobile phones is damaging the social abilities of young people. While I understand the concern behind the view, I largely disagree, since digital tools have actually widened rather than narrowed how young people interact."

### Body 1（让步段）
"[T]Admittedly, excessive screen time may, to some extend, weaken certain face-to-face skills. [E]When teenagers put the majority of their attention on screens, the time spent on real-life interaction naturally decreases. In real life, however, a large part of social interaction involves reading body language and listening to voice tones. [EX]For example, many adolescents in China can express themselves fluently in online group chats, but show noticeably social awkward when speaking face-to-face because they don't know how to handle a live conversation. [L]The shows that the concern is not entirely unfounded, but only when youth lack proper guidance on how to use these tools."

### Body 2（主反驳）
"[T]Nevertheless, online communication is not only a part of socialization, but also something that has strengthened young people's abilities to connect with others. [E]Digital devices allow people to break physical barriers, enabling them to share information anytime and anywhere. Furthermore, they lower the threshold for getting to know others, making socialization more accessible. [EX]For instance, friends who live in different cities can easily share their daily lives every week via WeChat. Similarly, people who have never met in person can comfortably discuss their cats' preferences in an online pet community. [L]These examples clearly demonstrate that online interaction and traditional socialization are not mutually exclusive; rather, online communication itself is a vital component of modern social life."

### Conclusion
"In conclusion, although the heavy use of technology may reduce the opportunities for young people to practice real-life social skills, its benefits cannot be ignored. Digital tools break geographic limits and lower social barriers, making communication more convenient than ever. Therefore, online communication should be seen as an extension of social life, rather than a threat to it."
