## 修复版（只改硬伤 + 关键升级）

### Intro
These days, the trend of purchasing clothes, shoes, and other items online has grown rapidly, transforming the way people shop. This essay will examine the main causes of the shift and its effects on traditional retailers.

### Body 1
**[T]** The popularity of online shopping results primarily from the combination of convenience and price.
**[E1]** In terms of **convenience**, online shopping platforms allow consumers to break geographic barriers, enabling them to **browse** thousands of goods and place orders through digital tools **at any time, from anywhere**.
**[E2]** In terms of price, online retailers often offer competitive pricing because they have lower running costs, especially **through savings on rent**.
**[Ex]** A case in point is the growth of Amazon, which **has** built large customer bases through **low-priced** goods and **their** convenient delivery service.
**[L]** Therefore, the practical advantages, which physical shops find hard to match, make online shopping an irresistible choice for modern consumers.

### Body 2
**[T]** This digital shift has brought significant challenges, **most of which are** negative, for traditional retail stores.
**[E1]** The most direct impact is a dramatic decline in foot traffic and sales. As more **consumers** prefer shopping **online**, a large number of physical shops struggle to cover their fixed costs.
**[Ex1]** For example, many traditional appliance stores located in city centers in China have been forced to close down **over** the last decade**.**
**[E2]** On the other hand, the pressure also forces surviving shops to adapt by upgrading **their** in-store services, **such as** personal styling, **cafés** and interactive shopping experiences that **cannot** be copied by websites.
**[Ex2]** For instance, **many** shopping malls in China have moved towards "experience-led" **formats**, where shoppers come for the events as much as the goods.
**[L]** This shows that, although the online trend threatens the survival of traditional shops, it also drives the remaining ones to reinvent themselves.

### Conclusion
In conclusion, modern consumers have picked up new shopping habits because online shopping is more convenient and cheaper. While this has undoubtedly put **immense** pressure on traditional shops and **caused** them to close, it also forces the retail industry to evolve and find new ways to attract consumers.

## 🆕 新错误模式

### W2-18 — 平行结构错（and 两边动词形态不一致）
- **首次**：2026-05-24 Day 8 v1 Conclusion
- **例**：`this has undoubtedly **put** ... **and cause** them to close` → has put 和 [has] caused 要平行，**cause 没变形**
- **修法**：每个 `and` 两边问 "动词形态一致吗"
- 与 Day 7 v2 W2-15（not...but 平行少 by）同源——**平行结构是新瓶颈层**

### W2-19 — 冠词 the 误用（特指/泛指混淆）
- **首次**：2026-05-24 Day 8 v1（共 2 处）
- **例 1**：`upgrading **the** in-store services` → "their in-store services"（泛指各家店的服务，不是特指唯一）
- **例 2**：`**the** shopping malls in China` → "many shopping malls in China"（the 暗示特指中国所有商场 → 不准确）
- **修法**：写 "the + 名词" 时问 "**特指还是泛指**"
  - 特指（前文出现 / 双方共知 / 唯一）→ 用 the
  - 泛指（一般概念 / 一些 / 多种）→ 不加 the / 改 their / many / some
