## 2026-05-17 22:00 Example 01 v1 仿写 — DBV Education

### Intro
- `it's debated that what a curriculum schools should offer` → `What schools should teach children is often debated.` [META/Object frame 冲突]
- `it's debated that **what...**` 模式冲突（that 接陈述句，what 是间接问句）[GRA]
- `a curriculum` → `curriculum`（uncountable）[GRA W2-2]
- `Others argue educators should` → `Others argue **that** educators should` [W2-7 漏 that]
- `S2 写成 View B 而不是 View A`：`schools should cover a variety of subjects` (View B's WHAT) + `to help students find jobs` (View A's WHY) [View 混淆，TR]

### Body 1
- `those who` 句首小写 → `Those who` [GRA 大写]
- `a job-related curriculum argue that it's the duty of schools to help students find **a good job**` → `**good jobs**`（泛指应复数）[W2-2]
- `**work market**` → `**job market**`（固定搭配错）[W2-6]
- `**is more and more competitive**` → `**is becoming more and more competitive**` 或 `**is increasingly competitive**` [LR collocation 优化]
- 🚨 `students **with** practical skills... **are difficult to get** a stable job`（意思反了 + L1 直译）→ `students **without** practical skills may struggle to find stable work` [W2-5 致命逻辑反 + W2-4 L1 直译]
- `**specially** focus on STEM` → `**particularly** focus on STEM`（specially 词义错）[W2-6]
- `as a result the marjority` → `as a result, the majority`（标点 + 拼写 marjority → majority）[GRA + W2-1 拼写]
- `**marjority**` → `**majority**` [W2-1 拼写]
- `prepare student who learn everything` → `produce students who are well-rounded`（动词选择 + 复数）[W2-2]
- `This is what supporters worry` → `This is what supporters worry **about**`（worry about 必须接 about）[GRA]

### Body 2
- `a **broder** curriculum` → `a **broader** curriculum` [W2-1 拼写]
- `the goal of education is **for** not only finding jobs but also preparing students as whole people` → `education is not only about preparing students for jobs, but also about developing them as whole people`（删 is for + 平行结构）[GRA]
- `teach **student**` → `teach **students**` [W2-2]
- `express **feeling**` → `express **their feelings**` [W2-2]
- `students **learning** music` → `students **who learn** music`（reduced relative L1 transfer）[W2-3]
- `**it is common that** students learning music are better at` → `students who learn music **tend to have**`（中式直译）[W2-4]
- `better at memorising **and concentration**`（gerund + noun 不平行）→ `better memory and concentration` 或 `better at memorising and concentrating` [GRA]
- `those **learning** art` → `those **who study** art` [W2-3]
- `address problems with imagination` → `tend to be more imaginative when solving problems`（搭配优化）[LR]
- `**ablities**` → `**abilities**` [W2-1 拼写]
- `**the creative industry**` → `**creative industries**`（泛指应复数）[W2-2]
- `That demonstrates a **variaty** subjects` → `This shows that a **variety of** subjects`（拼写 + 漏 of + that → This）[W2-1 + GRA]
- `career **developmenet**` → `career **development**` [W2-1 拼写]

### Conclusion（自己 copy 范文还抄错 3 处）
- `for work is **import**` → `for work is **important**` [W2-1 拼写]
- `subjects mean missing` → `subjects **would mean** missing`（漏 would）[GRA]
- `in **ther** wider lives` → `in **their** wider lives` [W2-1 拼写]

## 2026-05-18 10:30 Example 01 v2 仿写 — DBV Education（同题二刷）

### Intro
- `What **is** the goal of education **is** frequently debated`（间接问句词序错，同 v1 META frame 问题变体）→ `**The goal of education is** frequently debated.` 或 `What the goal of education **is** has been frequently debated.` [GRA 词序]

### Body 1
- `subjects like **computer or math**` → `subjects like **computing or maths**`（computer 是名词不是科目；math/maths 拼写）[LR collocation]
- `the majority of students there **get on to** well-paid jobs` → `**go on to** well-paid jobs`（get on to ≠ go on to）[W2-9 collocation 新]
- `That is **why** the supporters **worry about** — schools should not produce` → `That is **what** the supporters **worry about** — ___`（why 和 worry about 不能同时）[GRA + LR]
- `the supporters`（generic）→ `supporters`（去 the，泛指）[GRA]
- `can't find jobs` → `cannot find jobs`（学术正式，不用缩写）[LR 风格]
- `**particularly focus on**` → 正确是 `**focus particularly on**`（词序倒了，phrase 3 D+1 半通过）[LR collocation]

### Body 2
- `I **lean towards that** a broader curriculum is more beneficial` → `I **lean towards the view that** a broader curriculum is more beneficial` 或 `I **think** ...`（lean towards 接 noun/gerund，不接 that-clause）[W2-9 collocation 新]
- `**fostering** them as whole people` → `**developing** them as whole people`（foster 通常配 abilities/growth，不直接配 people）[W2-9 collocation 新]
- `tend to **show more imaginative**` → `tend to **be more imaginative**` 或 `tend to **show more imagination**`（imaginative 是 adj，show 接 noun）[GRA + LR]
- `more valued in creative industries` → `**increasingly** valued in creative industries`（更地道，且增强语气）[LR]

### Conclusion
- `**A** education system` → `**An** education system`（vowel sound）[W2-2 article]
- `who would **success** both at work` → `who would **succeed** both at work`（success noun → succeed verb）[W2-1 拼写 + 词形]
- `in their **wilder** lives` → `in their **wider** lives`（wilder = 狂野；wider = 更广阔——拼写差 1 字母意思差万里）[W2-1 拼写]

## 2026-05-18 11:30 Daily Drill #2 — "学生应该具备适应未来变化的能力"

### V1: `students should have the ability to fill in the changes of future`
- `students` 句首 → `Students` [GRA 大写]
- 🚨 `**fill in** the changes` → `**adapt to** the changes` 或 `**deal with** the changes`（fill in = 填表，整句失去意思——中式直译"应对/适应"→"fill in"）[W2-4 + W2-9]
- `of future` → `of **the** future` 或 `**future** changes`（article 漏）[W2-2]

### V2: `It's necessary for students to have adaptability for future`
- `for future` → `for **the** future` 或 `for an uncertain future`（article 漏）[W2-2]
- `It's` 缩写 → `It is`（学术正式）[LR 风格]
- 句末漏 period [GRA]

### V3: `students should show the abilities to adapt the future`
- `students` 句首 → `Students` [GRA 大写]
- `the abilities`（复数）→ `the ability`（单一能力）[W2-2]
- 🚨 `adapt **the** future` → `adapt **to** the future`（adapt 必须接 to）[W2-9 新错——介词缺失]
- `show the ability` → `develop the ability` / `demonstrate the ability`（搭配优化）[LR]
