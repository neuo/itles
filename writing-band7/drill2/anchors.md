# 校准锚 · 真实带分范文与考官评语

> 判分前如果拿不准某一档长什么样，回来读这里。**考官评语比范文本身更有价值** —— 它告诉你什么样的语言换什么分。
> 来源：① Cambridge IELTS 13/14/15 官方 "Sample answers for Writing tasks"（考官评语，有 band） ② IELTS.org 官方 Sample Candidate Writing Scripts ③ ieltsanswers.com（作者 Mike Wattie，前 IELTS 考官，每篇带四维分表 + 逐句批注）。

## ⭐ 0. 两个直接锚 —— 她自己写过的题，有官方带分样卷

这两道题她 2026-06-15 都 cold 写过。**同题对照是最强的校准工具**：让她把自己的版本和官方 5.5 / 7.5 并排读。

### 锚 A · 剑 14 Test 3 —— 音乐连接不同文化和年龄 → 官方 **Band 5.5**
> 题目（与她 `2026-06-15-agd-music.md` 完全同题）："Some people say that music is a good way of bringing people of different cultures and ages together. To what extent do you agree or disagree with this opinion?"

**Cambridge 考官评语（原文）**：
> "The response shows a clear position that is relevant to the task, with examples to support the main ideas. There is a clear overall progression, with effective use of cohesive devices [this | them | it] in spite of some errors [For the example | they patients | Including / In conclusion?]. Paragraphing is adequate. Vocabulary is generally adequate, **but control over spelling and word choice is weak** [anypeople | rhytum | solf/ soft? | anywhere / everywhere? | song for life?], **sometimes causing difficulty for the reader**. There are attempts at complex sentences, **but these usually contain errors** [people who has | Although ... but when], and punctuation is sometimes faulty. **Better control over spelling, grammar and punctuation might improve the overall score.**"

🔑 **读法**：TR ✅ CC ✅ 段落 ✅ —— 全部达标，但**拼写和词选控制弱到"造成读者困难"**，整篇就只有 5.5。
**这正是她的失分结构，而且她的实考分和这篇一模一样 —— 5.5。** 拼写错量在同一量级（她每篇 6–9 个），考官判词里的 `control over spelling and word choice is weak … sometimes causing difficulty for the reader` 就是她的 LR 5.0 的官方措辞。
**同题对照怎么用**：把她 2026-06-15 那篇和这篇官方 5.5 并排读，问一句"考官会不会对我这篇写出同样的评语"。

### 锚 B · 剑 14 Test 4 —— 自雇 → 官方 **Band 7.5**
> 题目（与她 `2026-06-15-2pt-cold.md` 完全同题）："Nowadays many people choose to be self-employed, rather than to work for a company or organisation. Why might this be the case? What could be the disadvantages of being self-employed?"

**Cambridge 考官评语（原文）**：
> "The candidate has produced a well-developed response to the task. Further ideas could be included, e.g. wanting to develop own ideas, wanting to work more flexibly than employment allows for. There is a clear progression throughout the response, with information and ideas organised logically. There is a range of cohesive devices [To start with | These | The first ... the second | Apparently | last but not least] and each paragraph has a clear central topic. There is a wide range of vocabulary, including less common items and showing evidence of style and collocation [contemporary | tempting | vast majority | opt for | entrepreneurship | fraudulent | image | luxury | perplex | jack of all trades], with only rare examples of inaccuracy [insensible]. **There is a variety of complex structures, used flexibly and accurately. Grammar and punctuation are well controlled and there are frequent error-free sentences.** A few errors persist [still the vast majority of people opt for (word order) | (the) major drawbacks] **but the message is still clear.**"

🔑 **读法**：7.5 只需要 **"frequent error-free sentences" + 极少数词汇不准**。它并不需要华丽 —— 但它需要**干净**。这就是 6.5 到 7.5 的全部距离。

## 7. 跨档规律（30+ 篇考官评语归纳，直接可用作判分先验）

1. **TR 是绝大多数考生的瓶颈** —— 11 篇 6.5 里有 9 篇是 LR/GRA 7-8 而 TR 5-6。
   ⚠️ **她的形状不一样**：08-10 那篇 **TR 6.5 / CC 6.0 / LR 6.0 / GRA 6.0** —— 四项挨得很近，
   TR 只领先半档。**别照搬通用建议给她加结构训练**，但也**别把"TR 相对好一点"读成"TR 已经到 7"**。
   ⛔ 七审更正：原写"她 TR/CC 已在 7.0-7.5"。那是虚高期的数，实考 5.5 已经推翻。
2. **立场自相矛盾代价最大**："an absolute disaster" / "leads to a severely lower score" / "plunges your score"。
3. **HOW vs WHY**：反复出现的 TR 失分点 —— 解释"怎么做"而不是"为什么有效"。
   > "You focus too much on HOW and not enough on WHY."
4. **每个 body 段恰好 2 个主论点并展开**："This is the magic number." / "By including too many main points, your paragraphs lacked development and **are unlikely to reach seven**."
5. **不足 250 词 = TR −1**，且连带压低其它三项。
6. **段落切分失败 = 硬顶 5**。
7. **`-s` 结尾错被点名为 7→8 的阻断点**："frequent errors with 's' endings that prevent an 8"。
8. **复杂 ≠ 高分，复杂会扣分**："unnecessarily complex structures you use" / "reading your writing causes me to feel frustrated" / "In particular your topic sentences need to be easy to read"。
9. **"partly agree" 被反复劝退**："always risky to be in the middle. I recommend totally agree/disagree."
10. **Band 7 的语言是平的。** 全库 7.0–7.5 的样本里没有一个华丽句子。

## 8. ⭐ 同一个意思写三遍：Band 6 / 7 / 8 长什么样

来源：IELTS Advantage — https://www.ieltsadvantage.com/2015/04/18/difference-band-5-and-8-in-ielts-writing-task-2-band-scores/
**全库最好的教学素材** —— 一个论点，三个档位，差别一目了然。

> **Band 6**（有观点，没展开）
> *The main problem causing traffic jams is too many cars. There are lots more cars these days.*
> 考官：想法切题，但**没解释为什么车多会堵，也没举例**。
>
> **Band 7**（展开了，但太笼统）
> *The main problem causing traffic jams is too many cars. In many cities around the world, there are lots of cars, and this causes traffic jams. For example, the number of cars purchased in developing countries is increasing yearly.*
> 考官：立场清楚，但**解释很泛，例子失焦、没紧扣论点**。
>
> **Band 8**（具体 + 聚焦）
> *The main problem causing traffic jams is too many cars. When we have more vehicles than a city's infrastructure was designed for, it leads to congestion. For example, Ho Chi Minh City was designed to cope with around 500,000 cars, and the city now has over 2 million cars, resulting in chronic traffic problems.*
> 考官：**解释了"为什么"，例子具体且直接证明论点**。

🔑 对她的用法：她的 body 段常停在 **Band 7 那一档**（"there is a tendency to over-generalise" 正是官方 Band 7 TR 的原话）。要往上，靠的是**具体数字/地名/机制**，不是靠换词。

### 配套：Chris Pell 的「100% 规则」
> "**Follow the 100% rule — don't try to use any words you are not 100% sure about. It is better to use simple words and make no mistakes than use advanced words, but in error.**"
> "**You should only write sentences that you know are 100% correct. If there is any doubt then you need to think about how you can express your point in a simpler way, that has no mistakes.**"

🔑 **这条对她是直接处方**：她的中式直译块（P3）几乎全部产生于"想说一个自己没把握的意思"。规则 = 没把握就换更简单的说法，别硬翻。

## 9. Task 1 校准 —— 官方带分样卷与考官评语

全部逐字取自 IELTS.org 官方三份 PDF（下载并本地抽取，非二手转述）。出处见 §11。

### 9.1 ⭐ 机考版 Band 6 —— **她的考试格式，最贴近的对照物**

> 任务：柱状图，1990 vs 2010 儿童上下学交通方式（car / walking / cycling / walking and bus / bus）

**考生原文（逐字，全文）**：
> "The chart gives you information on how children travelled to and from school in the years 1990 and 2010. The modes of transport were by car, walking, cycling, walking and by bus and by bus only.
> A striking feature in this chart is that the number of children who travelled by car has increased from 1990 to 2010. In 1990 the most number of trips per year by children were by walking. However in 2010 it reduced to up to 6 million trips per year. The total number of trips to school by cycling and walking and bus were approximately 6 million in 1990. However the number of trips to school by cycling reduced to 2 million and the number of trips to school by walking and bus reduced to about 3 million in 2010. There isn't a significant change to the number of trips to school by bus. In 1990 it was about 7 million and in 2010 it came down to approximately 5 million.
> In 1990 the amount of children who travelled to and from school by car was significantly lower than the children who travelled by walking. In contrast in 2010 the number of children who travelled to school by car increased and the number of children walking to school has decreased. In 2010 children travelled to school by bus more than they cycled to school."

**考官评语（逐字）**：
> "The key features which are selected are covered and clearly highlighted, **but reporting is somewhat mechanical and data is provided to support only some of the descriptions**. There is a relevant overview in the final paragraph, with a summary of the main changes. Information and ideas are generally arranged coherently, though there is some repetition [number of trips]. Cohesive devices are used effectively and there is a clear progression overall.
> The range of vocabulary is sufficient to allow some flexibility, with some less common items. **For a higher band, a wider range of vocabulary could be used**, within the scope of the task. There is a mix of simple and complex sentence structures, used fairly accurately, but again, **there is not a wide enough variety of structures to achieve a higher band**."

🔑 **对她的用法（最重要的一条）**：这篇的英语水平和她非常接近 —— `the most number of trips`、`the amount of children`、重复 `the number of trips to school by…` 四次 —— **这样就是 Band 6**。
注意它拿到 6 靠的是：**有 overview（哪怕放在最后一段）+ 数据支撑 + 进展清楚**。压住它不上 7 的是：**机械罗列、数据只支撑了一部分描述、句式和词汇范围窄**。
→ 她的 T1 要过 6，overview 和数据必须齐；要过 7，得摆脱"逐项报数"。

### 9.2 机考版 Band 4（同一任务，反向锚）
> "This response does generally try to address the requirements of the task **but the key features are not adequately covered**. Limited detail is provided, for example, there is no reference to the 'walking and bus' categories. Information is presented, **but it is difficult to identify organisation and there is no clear progression**... The range of vocabulary is not minimally adequate for the task, it is basic and used repetitively. There is limited control over spelling, **and errors cause confusion** [tripe mad/trips made]... errors are frequent and **this does impede meaning**."

🔑 **"errors cause confusion" / "impede meaning" 是 4–5 档的判词。** 她还没到这里，但她的拼写密度（每篇 6–9 个）是同一类风险。

### 9.3 ⭐⭐ Band 6 → Band 7 的分界，官方原话说得最直白

**折线图 Band 6**（广播/电视听众随时段变化）：
> "The answer has an appropriate introduction... There is good coverage of the data and a brief reference to contrasting trends. The answer can be followed although **it is rather repetitive and cohesive devices are overused**. **In order to gain a higher mark for content, the candidate would be expected to summarise the most important features of the graph in an overview, for example, 'audiences for radio peak in the morning, and for TV, in the evening'.** There is some flexibility and precision in the range of vocabulary used. **Sentences are long but do lack complexity.** To score more highly there would be fewer errors in tense, verb form and spelling."

**同一任务 Band 7**：
> "The answer **deals well with both the individual media trends and the overall comparison of these trends**. The opening could be more fully developed... There is a good variety of cohesive devices and the message can be followed quite easily although the expression is sometimes a little clumsy. Structures are complex and vocabulary is varied but there are errors in word forms, tense and voice **though these do not impede communication**."

🔑 **6→7 的两个动作，官方给的就是这两条**：
1. **overview 要"概括最重要的特征"** —— 官方给的范例 `audiences for radio peak in the morning, and for TV, in the evening` 就是**一句话把两条线的对立说清**，不是罗列
2. **要有跨线/整体对比**（Band 7 那篇的第一句评语就是"both the individual trends **and the overall comparison**"）
→ 这正是她的 **W11 + W6**。**她 T1 的全部提分空间就在这两条上。**

### 9.4 折线图 Band 5（无数据支撑）
> "There is a good attempt to describe the overall trends **but the content would have been greatly improved if the candidate had included some reference to the figures given on the graph. Without these, the reader is lacking some important information.** The answer is quite difficult to follow and there are some punctuation errors that cause confusion. The structures are fairly simple and efforts to produce more complex sentences are not successful."

🔑 **不给数字 = Band 5。** 对应官方 Band 5 描述符 "There may be no data to support the description."

### 9.5 Cinema Attendance Band 5.5 —— **和她同一个失分结构**
> "This response addresses the requirements of the task and selects relevant material to describe. **Key features and an overview are presented**, although clearer highlighting, more support and a more comprehensive overview would be needed to reach a higher band. Information is well organised and there is a clear overall progression... The range of vocabulary is not wide, but it is adequate for the task. **Control of word form and spelling is consistently good**...
> The test taker attempts to use a mix of simple and complex sentences, **but control is variable and grammatical errors or omissions are quite intrusive at times. Figures are poorly integrated into sentences**...
> **The weakness in grammatical range and control prevents this response from reaching Band 6.**"

🔑 **TA/CC 都够，overview 也有，就是被 GRA 一项压到 5.5。** 这是她当前的画像（她甚至比这篇差 —— 这篇"拼写和词形控制一贯很好"，她不是）。

### 9.6 流程图三档（同一道砖厂制砖题）

**Band 5**：
> "Although the basic process is accurately described, **this script fails to present an overview** and some of the key features are not adequately covered. The first sentence may have been an attempt at an overview but it simply causes confusion... the overall progression is clear and there is **effective, though mechanical, use of linkers and sequencers**... The vocabulary is minimally adequate... generally the structures are limited and there are frequent errors."

**Band 7**（⚠️ 语言很强却只有 7）：
> "This test taker uses an **inappropriate format** at times (e.g. the letter-style opening and personal comments) and this limits the band for Task Achievement. The process itself is adequately described, although some irrelevant information is included and **there is no clear overview**... **A wide range of sophisticated lexis** is used to convey meaning with precision... **A wide range of structures is also used fluently with only occasional slight error and the majority of sentences are error-free.** In spite of the high level of language proficiency, **the flaws in format and organisation limit the rating for this response to Band 7.**"

🔑 **这篇的 GRA 已经是 8 分水平（"majority of sentences are error-free"），却因为没 overview + 格式不当被压在 7。** 和 T2 那篇 LR7/GRA7 因立场矛盾掉到 5.0 是同一个道理：**TA 是可以单点拖垮全局的。**

### 9.7 🔴 我自己核到的一个事实：同一份卷子，官方两份文件给了不同的分

砖厂题 **Response C**，两份 IELTS.org 官方 PDF：

| 出处 | 判分 | 对 overview 的判词 |
|---|---|---|
| `ielts-scores-guide.pdf` | **Band 9** | "**An excellent overview** is given at the beginning of the response and this skilfully incorporates part of the rubric" |
| `ielts-academic-writing-sample-tasks-2023.pdf` | **Band 8.5** | "There is an overview... indicating that there are 'seven consecutive steps' **however for the highest score, a fuller overview would be needed**, to summarise those key stages" |

**同一段文字，同一个出版方，一个说"excellent"，一个说"不够"，差半档。**
（Response A/B 两份文件完全一致，都是 5 和 7 —— 只有最高那档分歧。）

而且官方自己在样卷首页写了：
> "There are many different ways a candidate may achieve a particular band score. **The candidates' answers that follow should not be regarded as definitive examples of any particular band score.**"

### ⚠️ 由此得出的判分纪律
1. **T1 最主观的判断就是"overview 够不够好"** —— 官方考官之间能在这一点上差半档。
2. **任何单次判分带 ±0.5 噪声。** 用 `scoring.md` 的可数指标决定"在边界的哪一侧"，**不要为某个具体数字辩护**。
3. 反过来说，**干净句率、词汇错个数、数据错个数这类可数的东西才是稳的** —— 这就是本套机制以计数为主判据的原因。

### 9.8 从上面提炼的 T1 可核查测试（判 T1 时逐条走）
| 测试 | 不过 → 封顶 |
|---|---|
| 有没有 overview 段？ | 没有 → **TA ≤ 5** |
| overview 是"概括"还是"罗列/数个数"？ | 罗列 → **TA ≤ 6**（连 Band 8.5 都栽在这） |
| 每个描述有没有真实数字支撑？ | 没有 → **TA ≤ 5** |
| 多线/多图有没有跨线整体对比？ | 没有 → **TA ≤ 6** |
| 是不是逐项报数（mechanical reporting）？ | 是 → **TA ≤ 6** |
| 格式对不对（没有书信腔、没有个人评论、没有图上没有的推测）？ | 不对 → **TA ≤ 6**（★ 2026-08-19 v4 填死：原文只写「TA 封顶」**没有数值**，判分时教练只能自己编一个数，而 skill 又禁编数 ⇒ 怎么选都违规，模拟里同一篇作文因此出现 6.0/6.5 两解。取 6 的依据：本表其余「格式/覆盖面」类不过项一律 ≤6，只有「缺 overview／无数字支撑」这两条结构性缺失才 ≤5；越界推测属格式类，与它们同档） |
| 语法错误会不会让读者困惑？ | 会 → **GRA ≤ 5** |

### 已知缺口（仍然存在）
- **官方没有带分的地图/平面图样卷**（带分地图只在 Cambridge 印刷版书里：剑 17 T1、剑 19 T2）
- **官方 T1 没有 6.5 / 7.5 半档样卷**（半档只在 Task 2 发布）→ 这两档靠 §9.3 的 6/7 分界测试插值
- **官方 T1 没有四维分项分**（只给总分）

## 10. 两处业界分歧（喂她之前先知道）

1. **例子能不能编？**
   - Chris Pell（IELTS Advantage）："If you can't think of a specific example, **make one up**. The examiners are not interested in how factual your examples are."
   - IELTS Liz：编造研究/统计"**do nothing for your score. The examiner has no interest in statistics or research for task 2.**"
   - **本 skill 采用的中间路线**：例子要**具体**（真实地名、真实机制、可想象的场景），但**用"一般观察"的口吻**说，不要伪造"某某大学研究表明 70%"。她本来就爱用真实经验（张伟/成都/她老婆），这条对她天然合适。

2. **官方判分自己就有分歧** —— 见 §9.7，我自己核到的：砖厂 Response C 在两份 IELTS.org 官方 PDF 里分别是 Band 9 和 Band 8.5，对 overview 的评价直接对立。
   > ⚠️ 早前有一条转述说"ielts.org 判 6/4、British Council 对同样文本判 5.5/3.5"——**我核不到**（我手上两份 ielts.org PDF 是不同题目：机考版是上下学交通，scores guide 是 Cinema Attendance）。**该转述不采信**，噪声这一点以 §9.7 我自己核到的证据为准。

3. **6.5 卡住时先修什么？**
   - Simon："**I don't recommend that you focus on grammar.** Improving your grammatical accuracy is probably going to be a slow and gradual process… the secret is **planning**."
   - 但 60 篇带分样本的分项数据显示：**GRA 6 是把 6.5 压住的最普遍单一原因**。
   - **对她的裁决**：Simon 的建议假设考生 TR/CC 明显弱于 LR/GRA。**她不是那个形状** ——
     08-10 四项是 6.5/6.0/6.0/6.0，TR 只领先半档，planning 的剩余收益不大。
     主杠杆在 LR/GRA，且必须按"慢工"预期推 —— 这正是"同题反复写"的理由：
     把内容负荷清零，让慢变量能被专注地推。
     ⛔ 七审更正：原写"她 TR/CC 已 7.0–7.5"。实考 5.5 已推翻，且**本文件不许夹带对她的估分**。
