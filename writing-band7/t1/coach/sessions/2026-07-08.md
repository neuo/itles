# 第二题 — Process 图 乙醇生产循环（她 cold 自写，**首次 Process**）≈6.5

## 她的原文
The flow chart illustrates the multi-stage process of how to produce a biofuel called ethanol, as well as its subsequent use.
Overall, it's a cyclical and repetitive process consisting of several main stages, beginning with the growth of plants and culminating in the release of carbon dioxide.
The process commences when plants and trees utilize energy from sunlight and carbon dioxide to grow. Once fully grown, they are harvested using machinery. Following this, the harvested material undergoes pre-processing, where it is broken into cellulose. The cellulose is then deliveried to a processing facility, where it is convert into sugars.
In the subsequent stages, microbes are added to sugars to produce ethanol. Finally, the biofuel is used to power various vehical, such as cars, trunks, and airphanes. As these vehicles run, they release carbon dioxide into the atmosphere, which is then absorbed by new plants, restarting the entrie circle.

## 判分 ≈ 6.5（方法=7，拼写压到 6.5）

**⭐⭐ 首次 Process，方法一次拿下**：① 现在时 + 被动全程（are harvested/is broken/is delivered/is converted/are added/is used/is absorbed）② 顺序词（commences/Once/Following this/then/In the subsequent stages/Finally/As）③ **overview 教科书级**（cyclical + several stages + beginning…culminating）④ **抓住循环闭合**（CO2 absorbed by new plants, restarting the cycle）⑤ 全阶段覆盖。

**🎓 W15 碎片毕业**：连续第 3 篇无 Regarding 碎片 → 毕业。

**唯一天花板 = 拼写 W13（现头号）**：
| 错 → 对 |
|---|
| deliveried→delivered · is convert→is converted（被动=过去分词，属 GRA）· vehical→vehicles · **trunks→trucks**（词用错）· airphanes→airplanes · entrie→entire · **circle→cycle**（circle=圆圈/cycle=循环）|
小修：how to produce→how ethanol is produced · broken into→broken down into · added to sugars→to **the** sugars。

**📄 纠正版**：
The flow chart illustrates the multi-stage process of how a biofuel called ethanol is produced, as well as its subsequent use. Overall, it is a cyclical and repetitive process consisting of several main stages, beginning with the growth of plants and culminating in the release of carbon dioxide. The process commences when plants and trees utilize energy from sunlight and carbon dioxide to grow. Once fully grown, they are harvested using machinery. Following this, the harvested material undergoes pre-processing, where it is broken down into cellulose. The cellulose is then delivered to a processing facility, where it is converted into sugars. In the subsequent stages, microbes are added to the sugars to produce ethanol. Finally, the biofuel is used to power various vehicles, such as cars, trucks, and airplanes. As these vehicles run, they release carbon dioxide into the atmosphere, which is then absorbed by new plants, restarting the entire cycle.

# 第三题 — Map 图 农场 1950 vs today（她 cold 自写，**首次 Map**）≈7

## 她的原文
The maps illustrate the transformation of Beechwood Farm from 1950 to the present day.
Overall, the farm has shifted from a agricultural site to a commercialized area, combining farm with tourism and green energy, while the river and farmhouse remain unchanged.
In the northern section, the large area designated for sheep has been completely replaced. A comping field and a set of solar panels now have occupied this space. Additionally, two new parking lots have been constructed on the north side of the main road.
Regarding the southern section, agricultural areas have been partially repurposed. The fruit tree zone has been halved to accommodate a new farm shop. To make room for several newly built holiday cottages, the barn has been relocated southwest, positioning it west of farmhouse. Meanwhile, the original zones for soft fruits, vegetables, and chickens are retained.

## 判分 ≈ 7（方法全对，卡老面孔表层错）

**⭐⭐ 首次 Map，方法一次拿下**：① **被动变化语言**贯穿（has been replaced/constructed/halved/relocated/partially repurposed）② **方位词**（In the northern section / on the north side / relocated southwest / west of）③ **时态**（变化=现在完成，现状=现在时）④ **overview 顶级**（shifted from agricultural→commercialized, combining farming+tourism+green energy, 保留 river/farmhouse）⑤ 招1 目的从句（to accommodate / To make room for）⑥ 按区域分组。

**小修（教练抓，她不自标）**：
| 原 → 改 | 类型 |
|---|---|
| a agricultural → **an** agricultural | **a/an 元音前**（跨技能复发，口语也常错）→ 记 W18 |
| comping → **camping** | 拼写 W13（进常错表）|
| now have occupied → **now occupy** | 时态（现状=现在时）|
| west of farmhouse → west of **the** farmhouse | 冠词（W5）|
| combining farm → **farming** | 词（farm=地/farming=活动）|
| 方位误：果蔬区在**西**非"南"；两停车场一北一东 | 位置精度（map 特有）|
| 漏：tracks → roads（土路升级）| 遗漏 |
| Beechwood Farm（图上无名）→ the farm | 别自造名字 |

**📄 纠正版**：
The maps illustrate the transformation of the farm from 1950 to the present day. Overall, the farm has shifted from an agricultural site to a commercialized area, combining farming with tourism and green energy, while the river and farmhouse remain unchanged. In the northern section, the large area designated for sheep has been completely replaced. A camping field and a set of solar panels now occupy this space. Additionally, two new parking areas have been constructed, one to the north of the main road and one to the east. Regarding the rest of the site, the agricultural areas have been partially repurposed. The fruit tree zone has been halved to accommodate a new farm shop. To make room for several newly built holiday cottages, the barn has been relocated south, positioning it west of the farmhouse. The tracks have also been upgraded to roads. Meanwhile, the original zones for soft fruits, vegetables, and chickens are retained.

## 🎯 7/8 大结论（题型全覆盖后的定论）
- **所有题型的方法她都一次就会**：数据图（bar/line/pie）+ Process + Map，overview/结构/专用语言 first-try 全对。
- **天花板永远是同一批表层错**：拼写（W13 头号）、a/an 元音前（W18）、冠词（W5）。**训练重心 = 磨表层**（拼写表 + a/an 规则 + 冠词），方法不用再教。

# 第四题 — 多图题 Little Chalfont 图书馆（饼+表+柱，她 cold 自写）≈7

## 数据
饼(会员年龄2016%)：Children 22 · YA 13-17 **15** · Adults 18-64 **51** · 65+ 12。
表(借阅品类2016%)：儿童 fiction **38** / non-fic 6 / DVD 1（儿童类合计 **45**）· Young adults **2** · 成人 fiction **38** / non-fic 13 / audio 2（成人类合计 **53**）。
柱(总借阅 2007→2016)：15,700 / 18,900 / 19,000 / 20,600 / 21,000 / 20,700 / **19,600** / 20,700 / 21,200 / 21,600。

## 她的原文
The charts illustrate how the total number of loans in Little Chalfont library changed from 2007 to 2016, as well as the breakdown of the age of library members and borrowing habits in 2016.
Overall, the number of loans experienced an upward growth through the entire period. By 2016, the majority of library members were adults aged 18–64, and fiction was by far the most popular category.
Regarding the total number of loans, the figure started at about 16,000 in 2007. It then steadily grew to around 21,000 in 2011 before dipping to 19,000 in 2013. From then on, the total number continued an upward trend, ending the period at approximately 22,000 in 2016.
In terms of the age of library members, adults aged 18–64 accounted for over half of the total at 51%. Children followed at 22%, while the groups of 13–17 age and over 65 made up the remaining at 15% and 12% respectively. Looking into borrowing habits, children's fiction and adult fiction were the most favored categories, equally making up 38% each, while the other categories only accounted for the remaining 24%.

## 判分 ≈ 7

**⭐ 多图题三条规矩首次全做到**：① overview 覆盖三张图 ② body 按「趋势 / 静态快照」分组 ③ 表格按大类合并不逐行报。
**⭐⭐ 两个头号老面孔双清**：**拼写 W13 零错** + **a/an W18 零错**（首次）。
其余绿：全过去时 · 静态 accounted for/made up vs 趋势 grew/dipping 分得清 · `the figure` 严谨主语 · respectively · W15 碎片仍干净（Regarding X, 逗号）。

**⚠️ 最大 gap — 漏跨图错配（W6 第 4 次）**：这题 "make comparisons" 最值分的一刀没砍——**青少年占会员 15% 却只借 2%**；**儿童占会员 22% 但儿童类借阅占 45%**。她三张图分别描述，没把饼图×表格并排比。（与折线图漏两线关系同族。）**多图题的分大头在跨图那一句。**

**小修（措辞/搭配为主）**：an upward growth→an upward trend（冗余）· **through**→**throughout**（7/7 复发）· the groups of 13–17 age→the 13–17 age group（语序）· made up the remaining at→accounted for…respectively · continued an upward trend→continued to rise · equally…each（冗余）→each making up 38% · Looking into→Turning to · library→**L**ibrary（专名）· 数据 2013 实为 ~19,600（写 19,000 偏低）；2007→2008 最陡一跳被 "steadily" 抹平。

**📄 纠正版**（补跨图句）：
The charts illustrate how the total number of loans at Little Chalfont Library changed from 2007 to 2016, as well as the breakdown of the age of library members and their borrowing habits in 2016. Overall, the number of loans followed an upward trend throughout the entire period. By 2016, the majority of library members were adults aged 18–64, and fiction was by far the most popular category. Regarding the total number of loans, the figure started at about 16,000 in 2007 and rose sharply to nearly 19,000 the following year. It then grew more gradually to around 21,000 in 2011 before dipping to about 19,500 in 2013. From then on, the total continued to rise, ending the period at approximately 22,000 in 2016. In terms of the age of library members, adults aged 18–64 accounted for over half of the total, at 51%.
