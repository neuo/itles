# Writing Session — 2026-05-03（W5 Day 2，第4篇）

## 题目

**类型**：Pie Chart × 3
**来源**：剑桥 14 Test 1 Task 1
**题目**：The charts below show the average percentages in typical meals of three types of nutrients, all of which may be unhealthy if eaten too much.

| | Breakfast | Lunch | Dinner | Snacks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sodium | 14% | 29% | 43% | 14% |
| Saturated fat | 16% | 26% | 37% | 21% |
| Added sugar | 16% | 19% | 23% | 42% |

---

## suzy 原文

The charts illustrate the breakdown of three different nutrients (sodium, saturated fat, and added sugar) across four types of meals in the USA.

Overall, dinner and lunch combined contribute the vast majority of all three nutrients. The only exception is added sugar, which is primarily consumed through snacks. Meanwhile, breakfast accounts for the smallest proportion across every category.

Regarding sodium intake, dinner is the primary source at 43%, followed by lunch at 29%. In contrast, snacks and breakfast contribute an equal 14% each. A similar pattern is mirrored in the distribution of saturated fat, though dinner's share is slightly lower (37%), whereas the figure for snacks is notably higher at 21%.

When it comes to added sugar, snacks emerge as the largest contributor (42%), a figure that almost matches the combined share of dinner (23%) and lunch (19%). The percentage of added sugar from breakfast remains the lowest, at 16%.

## 评级：Band 7

## 比 AI 范文更好的两处

1. **数据合成**：发现 snacks(42%) ≈ dinner+lunch combined for added sugar(23+19=42)。Band 7+ 标志：从数据中看出关系
2. **嵌套对比**：用 "A similar pattern is mirrored in..., though..." 一句话同时做"相似"+"差异"两件事

## 小问题

1. Overview 内部逻辑：第1句 "all three" 和第2句 "exception" 撞车 → 改为 "vast majority of sodium and saturated fat, while added sugar follows a different pattern"
2. "an equal 14% each" 冗余（equal/each 重复）→ "each contribute 14%"

## 关键能力解锁

- 模式相似性（mirrored）
