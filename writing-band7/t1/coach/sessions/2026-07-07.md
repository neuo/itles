## 📄 纠正版（161 词,G1/G2 已补,池内)

The graph illustrates how the number of shop closures and openings changed in one country over the eight-year period from 2011 to 2018.

Overall, both figures ended the period lower than they began, while closures fluctuated far more sharply. In most years, more shops closed than opened.

Regarding closures, the figure remained high for the first four years, fluctuating between about 5,900 and a peak of 7,200 in 2013. It then plummeted from 6,500 in 2014 to just 600 in 2015, the lowest point on the graph. The next year saw a strong recovery to about 5,200, and the figure remained stable at around 5,000 for the remaining years.

Openings followed a similar but more moderate pattern. After starting at 8,500, the highest point on the graph, the number of new shops dropped to approximately 4,000 in 2012 before recovering to just over 6,000 by 2014. However, the figure then fell back to about 4,000, levelled off until 2017, and finished at 3,000, its lowest level, in the final year.

> 每篇写完，对着这 7 条**逐条**查一遍再交。self-catch 目标 ~85%。

1. **碎片**：任何 `Regarding / Having / Starting / Followed by` 开头的句子——**是不是用句号断开了?** 是→改逗号接主句。（W15，已 3 次）
2. **specific**：全文有 `specific` 吗？→ 划掉换 `one / a particular`。（已 3 次）
3. **all of / both of + 名词**：→ 加 `the` 或去 `of`（all of appliances→all three appliances）。（已 2 次）
4. **冠词**：每个 the refrigerator / the washing machine / the figure——单数可数**前面有 the 吗?**
5. **动词**：① 升降只用 rose/climbed/fell（别 incline/decline 乱当动词）② 每个从句**有没有完整动词**（leveling→levelled）。
6. **时态**：有年份=**全篇过去时**；别 is/was 混（is echoed→was）。
7. **拼写 + 数字**：默读一遍拼写；**年份/数值对着图核**（2020→2019）。
