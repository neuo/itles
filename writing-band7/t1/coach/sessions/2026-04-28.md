# Writing Session — 2026-04-28（W4 Day 4）

## 题目

**类型**：Bar Chart（第 1 次限时练）
**题目**：The bar chart shows the percentage of households with internet access in five European countries in 2000, 2005, and 2010.

| 国家 | 2000 | 2005 | 2010 |
|------|------|------|------|
| Germany | 30% | 62% | 82% |
| France | 14% | 51% | 74% |
| Spain | 7% | 36% | 65% |
| Italy | 8% | 34% | 53% |
| Poland | 4% | 26% | 59% |

---

## suzy 原文

The char chart compares the household internet penetration across five different countries — Germany, France, Spain, Italy and Poland — in 2000, 2005, and 2010

Overall, all countries experienced a dramatically growth, with Poland showing the steepest percentage increase of five countries, but Germany maintained a consistent lead.

In 2000, Germany had the highest internet penetration at 30%, followed by France at 14%. Italy, Spain and Poland had significantly lower rate, at 8%, 7% and 4% respectively.

By 2020, Poland had achieved the most striking growth in percentage terms, while France recorded the most largest increase in absolute terms. Germany rose to 82%, leading all countries. Spain and Italy increased to 65% and 52% respectively

---

## 4 项自评

| 项目 | 结果 | 说明 |
|------|------|------|
| Overview | ✅ | 存在，方向正确（全体增长+Poland最大+Germany领先），但语言混入 body |
| 结构 | ⚠️ | intro+overview+body 框架有，**2005 数据整列缺失** |
| 比较句 | ⚠️ | 有 steepest/consistent lead，**无 gap/narrowed/widened/surpassed** |
| 语法 | ❌ | 4 处错误（见下） |

---

## 语法错误

| 原文 | 改法 | Pattern |
|------|------|---------|
| a dramatically growth | dramatic growth | W1（adverb修饰名词）+ W3（growth不可数） |
| lower rate | lower rates | W5（复数） |
| most largest | largest | W2（双重最高级） |
| By 2020 | By 2010 | W4（年份笔误） |

---

## 内容缺口

- **2005 整列未写**（W3）——body 只有 2000 和 2010 两列
- Body 2 混入了 overview 语言，结构不清晰（W7）

---

## Band 7 范文

The bar chart illustrates the proportion of households with internet access in five European countries — Germany, France, Spain, Italy, and Poland — in 2000, 2005, and 2010.

Overall, all five countries saw substantial growth over the decade. Germany remained the frontrunner throughout, while Poland, despite starting from the lowest base, recorded the greatest proportional rise and had overtaken Italy by 2010.

In 2000, internet penetration varied considerably across the five nations. Germany led at 30%, noticeably higher than the others. France followed at 14%, while Spain, Italy, and Poland all had single-digit rates of 7%, 8%, and 4% respectively.

By 2005, figures had roughly doubled in most countries. Germany climbed to 62% and France to 51%, both crossing the halfway mark. The remaining three nations rose to 36%, 34%, and 26% respectively, showing strong but still lagging growth.

The upward trend continued into 2010. Germany reached 82%, maintaining its lead, while France followed at 74%. Spain rose to 65%, and Poland surpassed Italy, reaching 59% compared to Italy's 53%. Notably, all five countries had by this point exceeded 50%, suggesting internet access had become mainstream across the region.

---

## 本次教的句型

1. `figures had roughly doubled in most countries by 2005` — 中间时间点过渡句（避免逐国列数据）
2. `Poland surpassed Italy, reaching 59% compared to Italy's 53%` — 顺序变化句（suzy之前没用过）
