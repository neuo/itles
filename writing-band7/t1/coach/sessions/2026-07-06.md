## suzy 的框架（原样）

3. **Body 1**：涨的 3 个（Spanish / Another / Two other），各从 X 涨到 Y。

## 📄 Claude 成稿（Band 7，170 词，examiner ✅ 7.0）

The two pie charts compare the percentage of British students at one English university who could speak a foreign language alongside English, over the ten-year period from 2000 to 2010.

Overall, students became more likely to speak an additional language over the decade, as the share speaking only English fell markedly. Spanish was the most popular foreign language in both years, and its figure rose further.

Turning to the categories that grew, the percentage of students who spoke Spanish only climbed from 30% to 35%, keeping it the largest single group. Over the same period, the figure for another language rose from 15% to 20%, while the share of students who could speak two other languages went up from 10% to 15%.

In contrast, several groups shrank or stayed the same. The proportion speaking no other language halved, from 20% to 10%, and the figure for French-only speakers fell from 15% to 10% as well. German was the only category to remain unchanged, at 10% in both years.

### 连接词/过渡地图（她要学的"框架→顺滑英文"）
- `Overall,` — 开 overview 的信号词
- `as` — 把两个相关事实（英语only↓ / 更多人会别的语言）连成**一句**因果/伴随，不是两句并列
- `Turning to the categories that grew,` — 开 body 1 并直接点出该段中心（涨的那组）
- `Over the same period,` / `while` — 段内串第 2、3 个数据，避免逗号堆砌
- `In contrast,` — body 1→body 2 转折枢纽（涨↔跌）
- `... as well` / `the only category to...` — 段内追加/收束

## 📄 Claude 成稿（Band 7，165 词，examiner ✅ 7.0 四维全 7.0，数据 12/12 对）

The three pie charts show how three potentially unhealthy nutrients — sodium, saturated fat and added sugar — are spread across four types of meal (breakfast, lunch, dinner and snacks) in a typical American diet.

Overall, dinner is the main source of both sodium and saturated fat, whereas snacks provide most of the added sugar. Breakfast makes one of the smallest contributions across all three.

The patterns for sodium and saturated fat are quite similar. Dinner accounts for the largest share of each — 43% and 37% respectively — followed by lunch, at 29% and 26%. The remaining meals contribute far less: breakfast provides 14% of sodium and 16% of saturated fat, while snacks add 14% and 21%.

Added sugar follows a different pattern. Here, snacks are by far the biggest source at 42%, well above the share of any other meal. Dinner comes next at 23%, ahead of lunch (19%) and breakfast (16%) — a far more even spread across the main meals than for the other two nutrients.

### Examiner 门 + 一个精度 slip（教学点）
- verdict：TA/CC/LR/GRA 全 7.0 → overall 7.0 ✅ PASS，数据 12/12 对。
- ⚠️ examiner 抓到我 overview 一处不严谨：原写 "Breakfast makes **the smallest** contribution to all three" 对 sodium 不成立（breakfast 14 与 snacks 14 **并列最小**）→ 改 "one of the smallest"。
- 🌟 **新规则（连 Claude 都踩，给 suzy）**：说 "the largest/smallest" 前先查 **tie（并列）**；有并列用 `one of the smallest / among the largest / tied with X`。

### 连接词地图（她要学的）
- **两句 topic 句替你分组**：`The patterns for sodium and saturated fat are broadly similar.`（body1 先说为何合）/ `Added sugar follows a different pattern.`（body2 预告"不一样"，把 odd-one-out 摆台前）
- `whereas`(overview 对比枢纽) · `respectively`(配对两组) · `followed by`(排名不另起句) · `The remaining… :`(冒号打包小项) · `while`(对比两小项) · `well above… / a far more even spread than…`(跨营养素对比=W6)

## Takeaways（整日）
- **两根不同柱**已在她自己写的语言题落地 ✅；但**快框架时又漏 odd-one-out（W11 第 2 次）** → 下次框架第一步先问"哪个和别人不一样"。
- **新精度规则**：① rise/fall 主语=量非人（已记）② 说 the largest/smallest 前查 tie（今日新增）③ 精确框定人群（if any）。
- **分组 topic 句**是 T1 高分手法：body 开头一句先声明分组逻辑（相似/不同），再上数字。
