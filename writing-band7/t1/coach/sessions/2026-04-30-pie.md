## 主要问题

1. 🔴 **数据错位**：South America 写成 77%/19%/10%，实际 71%/19%/10%（agriculture 数字错 + industrial/domestic 标反）
2. 🔴 **"agriculture becomes the primary source of water"**: becomes→is, source→use（W10新）
3. 🔴 **"A striking similar pattern"** → strikingly similar（W1复发：副词修饰形容词）
4. 🟡 主谓不一致："the figure...stay" → figures stay 或 figure stays
5. 🟡 拼写：countris/fallowed/remaing/char、大写：europe/Center Asia

## Band 7 范文

The pie charts show how water is allocated across three sectors — industrial, agricultural, and domestic — in six different regions of the world.

Overall, agriculture is the dominant water consumer in most regions, with the notable exceptions of North America and Europe, where industrial use takes the largest share. Domestic use accounts for the smallest portion in every region.

In North America, industry consumes 48% of water, followed by agriculture at 39% and domestic use at 13%. Europe shows a strikingly similar pattern, with industry taking the lion's share at 53%, agriculture accounting for 32%, and domestic use making up the remaining 15%.

The picture changes dramatically in the other four regions. In South America, agriculture dominates at 71%, with industrial use at 19% and domestic at just 10%. The pattern becomes even more pronounced in Africa, Central Asia, and South East Asia, where agriculture consumes 84%, 88%, and 81% of water respectively. In all three, industrial and domestic use combined account for less than 20%.

## 核心教训

1. **写完留 30 秒回去核数据** — 数据错是 T1 最致命的扣分项
2. **W1 副词 vs 形容词修饰** 已两次（dramatically growth / striking similar），重点监控
3. **becomes vs is**：静态对比用 is，becomes 暗示变化
