## 题目（P/S，新）
> In many countries, people are working longer hours than ever before and have very little time for rest or leisure. What problems does this cause, and what measures could be taken to solve them?

## 给的 4 段 TEEL stem 骨架（suzy 学习用，下次可自填）
```
【Intro】In recent years, an increasing number of people have ______.
        Although ______, this trend has led to ______. This essay will
        examine the main problems and propose some practical solutions.
【Body1-Problems】T: The most serious problem caused by ______ is ______.
        E: This is mainly because ______. Ex: For instance, ______.
        L: As a result, ______.
【Body2-Solutions】T: The most effective solution to this would be ______.
        E: By doing so, ______.  (原因方向用 by doing,非 making)
        Ex: A good example is ______, where ______. L: This shows that ______.
【Conclusion】In conclusion, although ______, these problems can be addressed if ______.
```

## 📄 填好的 Band 7 范文（examiner 校准 7.0，clean 全文，可朗读对照）

> **[Intro]** In recent years, an increasing number of people have found themselves working far longer hours than previous generations, leaving them with very little time to rest. Although these long hours may boost short-term productivity, the trend has created some worrying problems. This essay will examine the main drawbacks and suggest a few practical solutions.
>
> **[Body1]** The most serious problem caused by overwork is the damage it does to people's health. When someone is constantly under pressure and short of sleep, their body and mind simply do not get the chance to recover. For instance, doctors increasingly link long working hours to conditions such as anxiety, high blood pressure and burnout. As a result, employees who are pushed too hard often end up less productive, not more — the very opposite of what their companies intended.
>
> **[Body2]** The most effective solution to this would be for governments to set clear limits on working hours. By capping the working week and enforcing proper rest periods, the authorities can protect employees who might otherwise feel unable to refuse their bosses. A good example is France, where workers have a legal "right to disconnect" from work emails after hours. This shows that sensible regulation can give people back their personal time without harming the economy.
>
> **[Conclusion]** In conclusion, although working long hours may seem unavoidable in a competitive world, the problems it causes can be addressed if both governments and employers are willing to treat rest as a priority rather than a luxury.

|--------|----------|
| 主谓 attraction（W2-17 attraction 变体）| `the problems **it causes** can be addressed`——动词跟真正 head(problems / it=单数→causes),不被中间名词带偏 |

### 成文（4 段 · ~211 词）

> **[Intro]** In modern society, the trend towards overwork has grown rapidly, with people working far longer hours than ever, leaving them with little time to rest. This essay will examine the main problems this causes before suggesting practical solutions.
>
> **[Body1 — Problem]** The most serious problem caused by overwork is the damage it does to people's health, both mentally and physically. When someone is constantly under pressure and short of sleep, their body and mind do not get the chance to recover. For instance, doctors increasingly link long working hours to conditions such as anxiety, high blood pressure and burnout. Therefore, overwork can slowly build into a serious health problem.
>
> **[Body2 — Solution]** Solving the problem requires governments to set clear limits on working hours. By capping the working week and enforcing proper rest periods, the authorities can protect employees who might otherwise feel unable to refuse their bosses. For example, in France, workers have a legal "right to disconnect" from work emails after hours. Therefore, clear limits on working hours can give people back the time they need to rest and recover.
>
> **[Conclusion]** In conclusion, although working long hours may seem unavoidable in a competitive world, these problems can be addressed if both governments and employers are willing to treat rest as a necessity rather than a luxury.

**复用了哪几条核心壳**：
- opener（P/S）：`In modern society, the trend towards [X] has grown rapidly.`（#1）
- 预告句：`This essay will examine the main problems [this causes] before suggesting solutions.`（#2）
- P/S 问题段 T：`The most serious problem caused by [X] is ___.`（#3）
- P/S 方案段 T：`Solving the problem requires [主体] to ___.`（=would require sb to do，#7）
- Ex 句：`For instance, ___.`（#5）/ `For example, in [X], ___.`（#9）
- 🌟 L 句：`Therefore, [重述本段中心].`（#6 Body1 / #10 Body2，两段各一，全落地）
- Conclusion：`In conclusion, although [让步], these problems can be addressed if ___.`（#11）

**塞不进的 + 原因**：
- **长度 ~211 词，低于 250–280 目标**。原因：忠实**删掉教练范文两处 L-drift 句**（Body1 productivity 句 / Body2 economy 句）+ P/S 不硬加让步段 → 内容变少；铁律禁“引入清单外新句式 / 加肉”，故**不注水补长度**。study-model 篇以“结构完整（每段 T-E-Ex-L）+ 壳统一”为合格核心，长度让位于忠实。真 cold 自写时她可自然多铺 1–2 句到 250+。
- `These examples demonstrate that ___` L 壳没用上：两个 body 段各只 1 个例子，`Therefore + 重述` 更贴切（demonstrate + 复数 examples 语义对不上）。
