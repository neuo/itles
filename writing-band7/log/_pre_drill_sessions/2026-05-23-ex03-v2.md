## 题目（同 v1）

> Obesity is a growing problem worldwide, particularly among children. **What are the causes of this trend, and what can be done to address it?**

**题型**：C/S | 立场：不需要 | 结构：Body 1 causes / Body 2 solutions（2-Body 版）

## 我的 v2（原版，保留错误）

### Intro
"These days, obesity has become a serious problem in many countries, and particularly a lot of children are overweight. The essay will examine the main causes of the problem before suggesting possible solutions."

### Body 1
"Childhood obesity results primarily from a change in everyday diet and lifestyle. On the dietary side, although processed food is high in sugar and fat, modern busy families prefer them because they are much cheaper and more convenient than fresh produce, leading children to having too much unhealthy nutrition. On the activity side, children today spend many more hours on screens, which has reduced their opportunities to play outdoors. A case in point is the United Kingdom, where researches show that an average child eats far more sugar than recommended and walks less than 30 minutes a day. This shows that the problem is not caused by only one factor, but poor diet combined with low activity."

### Body 2
"Solving the problem requires a combination of government policies and school education. At the policy level, government could impose extra taxes on sugary drinks and require a clearer nutrition labels. For example, the European Union makes it mandatory for all food to clearly display their sugar and fat content. At the school level, daily exercise should be added to the timetable, and school lessons should teach students about food nutrition. For instance, Japan has long included school lunches and food education in its curriculum, and its childhood obesity is one of the lowest worldwide. These examples clearly demonstrate that real progress depends on improving the food environment around children and encouraging more active lifestyle."

### Conclusion
"In conclusion, the primary reason for childhood obesity is unhealthy diets and inactive lifestyles, and solving it requires action on both sides. By combining sensible policies with better education on exercise and nutrition, societies can give the next generation a lower obesity rate than today."

## 修复版（只改硬伤，保留 suzy 句式）

### Intro
These days, obesity has become a serious problem in many countries, with **a large number of children now overweight**. **This** essay will examine the main causes of the problem before suggesting possible solutions.

### Body 1
**[T]** Childhood obesity results primarily from a change in everyday diet and lifestyle.
**[E1]** On the dietary side, although **processed foods are** high in sugar and fat, modern busy families prefer them because they are much cheaper and more convenient than fresh produce, leading children **to consume** too much unhealthy food.
**[E2]** On the activity side, children today spend many more hours on screens, which has reduced their opportunities to play outdoors.
**[Ex]** A case in point is the United Kingdom, where **studies** show that **the** average child eats far more sugar than recommended and **exercises for** less than 30 minutes a day.
**[L]** This shows that the problem is not caused by only one factor, but **by** poor diet combined with low activity.

### Body 2
**[T]** Solving the problem requires a combination of government policies and school education.
**[E1]** At the policy level, **governments** could impose extra taxes on sugary drinks **and on foods harmful to children's health**, and require clearer nutrition labels.
**[Ex1]** For example, the European Union makes it mandatory for all food to clearly display **its** sugar and fat content.
**[E2]** At the school level, daily exercise should be added to the timetable, and school lessons should teach students about food nutrition.
**[Ex2]** For instance, Japan has long included school lunches and food education in its curriculum, and its childhood obesity **rate** is one of the lowest worldwide.
**[L]** These examples clearly demonstrate that real progress depends on improving the food environment around children and encouraging more active **lifestyles**.

### Conclusion
In conclusion, **the primary reasons for** childhood obesity **are** unhealthy diets and inactive lifestyles, and solving it requires action on both **fronts**. By combining sensible policies with better education on exercise and nutrition, societies can give the next generation a lower obesity rate than today.
