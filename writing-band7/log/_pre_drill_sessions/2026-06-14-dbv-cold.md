## 题目（剑 19 T1，DBV）
> Some people think that competition at work, at school and in daily life is a good thing. Others believe that we should try to cooperate more, rather than competing against each other. Discuss both these views and give your own opinion.

2. Body1(竞争,不完全认同那边):竞争有利进步,"做得比别人好"是天然催化剂;如国家间竞争带来科技/社会福利进步,大多数人受益 → 各层面竞争有好处

## 📄 v2（真实 level,untimed）逐字
> **Intro**: People have different opinions about whether competition or cooperation is better in our daily lives. While competition can motivate people to work harder, I believe that cooperation is more important for achieving long-term success.
> **Body1**: On the one hand, competition is a natural driver for progress. When people or countries try to do better than others, they will work harder and improve themselves. This can bring great benefits to the public. For example, on a global scale, competition between different countries in technology has brought many scientific achievements. This has improved social welfare and made most people's lives better. Therefore, competition has some clear advantages at all levels.
> **Body2**: On the other hand, pure competition has its limits, and cooperation can bring bigger progress. The power of a single person or group is limited. However, cooperation can bring different people together to reach a big goal. For example, in a company, if product managers and developers do not talk to each other and just work alone, they will achieve nothing and jeopardize the project. In contrast, team cooperation has proven that it can successfully push the team to reach its goals.
> **Conclusion**: In conclusion, although competition is good for progress, it cannot solve every problem. I believe that cooperation is the main way to reach our goals because working together is the only way to get sustainable success.

**Intro**: People have different opinions about whether competition or cooperation is better in our daily lives. While competition can motivate people to work harder, I believe that cooperation is more important for achieving long-term success.

**Body1**: On the one hand, competition is a natural driver for progress. When people or countries try to do better than others, they will work harder and improve themselves. This can bring great benefits to the public. For example, on a global scale, competition between different countries in technology has brought many scientific achievements. This has improved social welfare and made most people's lives better. Therefore, competition has some clear advantages at all levels.

**Body2**: On the other hand, pure competition has its limits, and cooperation can bring bigger progress. The power of a single person or group is limited. However, cooperation can bring different people together to reach a big goal. For example, in a company, if product managers and developers do not talk to each other and just work alone, they will achieve nothing and jeopardize the project. These examples demonstrate that team cooperation can successfully push the team to reach its goals.

**Conclusion**: In conclusion, although competition is good for progress, it cannot solve every problem. I believe that cooperation is the main way to reach our goals because working together is the only way to get sustainable success.

**复用了哪几条规范句**：
- ✅ opener（DBV）：`People have different opinions about whether [X].`
- ✅ 折叠式立场句：`While [对方观点], I believe that [我的立场].`
- ✅ Body 主开关：`On the one hand, ___.` → `On the other hand, ___.`
- ✅ Ex 句：`For example, ___.`（×2）
- ✅ L 句：`Therefore, [重述].`（Body1）+ `These examples demonstrate that ___.`（Body2）
- ✅ Conclusion：`In conclusion, although [让步], [重申立场].`

**改了哪几句**（原句 → 改后句）：
