## 题目（环境,A&D 观点型,新)
> Some people believe that individuals can do very little to protect the environment, and that real change can only come from governments and big companies. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

## suzy 原版（~280 词,保留错误）

**[Intro]** It is often argued that governments and big corporations should be responsible for environmental protection because individuals can do very little. While I understand why some people support this view, I largely disagree, since the power of a significant number of individual contributors **are** also huge.

**[Body1]** Admittedly, huge organizations can play a more crucial role compared to a single person. On the one hand, individuals **effort on** protecting **environment looks like** negligible. For instance, even if one person insists on taking public transport instead of driving, the reduction of carbon emission is actually very little. On the other hand, governments hold the power to legislate and big companies **achieve** the capacity to generate scalable impact. A case in point is the United Kingdom, where there is a law that puts higher taxes on environment-unfriendly companies. This shows that the view is not entirely unfounded**,** the power of the collective is indeed much stronger from a certain perspective.

**[Body2]** Nevertheless, individuals are vital components of the collective. The key point is that any tiny effect, when multiplied thousands of times, becomes absolutely impossible to ignore. For example, as a city with one of the highest rates of public transit usage in the world, Tokyo also boasts some of the lowest vehicle carbon emissions among global megacities. Furthermore, without the involvement of individuals, plenty of policies cannot be enforced normally. For instance, the process of garbage sorting and recycling **required** not only **polices** and infrastructure, **but more** individual compliance and execution. These examples clearly demonstrate that individuals can make huge contributions **to protect environment**.

**[Conclusion]** In Conclusion, huge organizations and individuals are not mutually exclusive; rather, both of them play an irreplaceable role **respectively**. Without the engagement of individuals, the goal of protecting the environment cannot be achieved.

### 成文
**[Intro]** It is often argued that governments and big corporations should be responsible for environmental protection because individuals can do very little. While I understand why some people support this view, I believe that the power of a significant number of individual contributors is also huge.

**[Body1]** Admittedly, huge organizations can play a more crucial role compared to a single person. On the one hand, an individual's efforts to protect the environment can look negligible. For instance, even if one person insists on taking public transport instead of driving, the reduction in carbon emissions is actually very little. On the other hand, governments hold the power to legislate, and big companies have the capacity to generate scalable impact. A prime example is the United Kingdom, where a law puts higher taxes on environmentally unfriendly companies. Therefore, the view is not entirely unfounded; the power of the collective is indeed much stronger from a certain perspective.

**[Body2]** However, these arguments are outweighed by individuals as vital components of the collective. The key point is that any tiny effect, when multiplied thousands of times, becomes absolutely impossible to ignore. For example, as a city with one of the highest rates of public transit usage in the world, Tokyo also boasts some of the lowest vehicle carbon emissions among global megacities. Furthermore, without the involvement of individuals, plenty of policies cannot be enforced normally. For instance, the process of garbage sorting and recycling requires not only policies and infrastructure, but also individual compliance and execution. These examples clearly demonstrate that individuals can make huge contributions to protecting the environment.

**[Conclusion]** In conclusion, while huge organizations play a more crucial role, they and individuals are not mutually exclusive, and both play an irreplaceable role. Without the engagement of individuals, the goal of protecting the environment cannot be achieved.

**复用了哪几条核心壳**：
- ✅ opener（A/D）：It is often argued that [X].（S1）
- ✅ 🌟 折叠式立场句：While [对方], I believe that [立场].（S2）
- ✅ 让步：Admittedly, ___.（S3）
- ✅ 🌟 Body 主开关：On the one hand ___ → On the other hand ___.（S4/S6，她放在 Body1 内对比个人 vs 组织）
- ✅ Ex 句：For instance ___ / For example ___ / A prime example is [X].（S5/S11/S13/S7）
- ✅ 🌟 称重 pivot：However, these [X] are outweighed by [Y].（S9，Body2 开关）
- ✅ 🌟 L 句 ×2：Therefore, [重述].（S8，Body1 尾）/ These examples demonstrate that ___.（S14，Body2 尾）—— 两个 body 段末尾都落 L 句 ✅
- ✅ 🌟 Conclusion：In conclusion, while [让步], [重申].（S15）

**换掉的「范文/工具集皮」（共 3 处）**：① S7「A case in point is X, where Y」→「A prime example is [X]」 ② S8「This shows that ___」→「Therefore, [重述]」 ③ S9「Nevertheless（立场型 pivot）」→「However, these [X] are outweighed by [Y]」。

**塞不进的 + 原因**：
- 枚举壳「The first major factor is ___ / Another critical reason is ___ / First…Second…」：Body2 两个子论点她用「The key point is that…」+「Furthermore…」自然加合推进，硬套编号会打乱她原有展开逻辑；「Furthermore」是合法加合连接、非清单皮，故保留不换。
- E 句「This is because ___ / As a result ___」：她的因果展开走「The key point is that…」，非显式因果壳，硬塞会改肉，保留。
