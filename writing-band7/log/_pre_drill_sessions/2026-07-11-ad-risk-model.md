## 题目（剑16T4 / AD-outweigh）
> It is important for people to take risks, both in their professional lives and their personal lives. Do you think the advantages of taking risks outweigh the disadvantages?

## suzy 中文构思（她的立场+论点+例子，一字保留）
> 1. opener，然后略微同意。
> 2. 现在社会很稳当，良好的经济、完善的教育体系以及社会保障，可以让人们不冒任何风险就获得一个还不错的生活。唯一需要做的就是付出努力去追求自己想要的（努力学习/工作）。比如北欧，一个普通人在没遇到意外的情况下，一般可获得还可以的学历或尚可的工作。这些是高度发展的社会保障。因此人们无需冒险即可获取还不错的生活和工作。
> 3. 但如果想获得超过平均水平的生活或职业发展，总要额外付出一些（一般是足够的努力）。但成功有不确定性，这就是需要承担风险的地方。比如做生意，良好预期+足够投入（资金+组织）是成功的必要非充分条件，市场和政策变化也可能导致失败。所以获得更大回报往往需要承担风险。
> 4. 所以现代社会我们无需冒险即可过上良好生活，但想获取更多往往仍需承担风险。但必须注意：承担风险不代表赌博，它是在对未来的充分预见和评估。

## 📄 范文 v1（273 词，examiner 7.0，但 ⚠️ **TR 偏题 + 直译腔 → 已被 v2 取代**）

> suzy 反馈：① Body1 讲"不需要冒险"＝偏题（AD 问的是利/弊，Body1 该讲**弊**）② 整体太直译不自然。→ 见下方 **v2（最终版）**。

Many people believe that taking risks is important in both our professional and personal lives. While it is true that a stable modern society allows people to live a decent life without taking any risks, I believe that the advantages of taking risks still outweigh the disadvantages, especially for those who want more than an ordinary life.

On the one hand, in a well-developed society, people can secure a reasonable life without taking any risks at all. This is because a strong economy, a complete education system and a solid social security net protect ordinary citizens from serious harm. As long as they are willing to work hard, whether by studying or by doing their job well, they can expect a fair outcome. For example, in Northern European countries, an average person who runs into no major accidents can usually obtain a decent qualification and a stable job, simply because the social support system there is so well developed. Therefore, for a basic and comfortable life, taking risks is not really necessary.

On the other hand, anyone who hopes to achieve more than an average life usually has to take on some risk. Working hard is often enough to reach a normal standard, but going beyond it requires something extra, and success at this level is never certain — this is exactly where risk comes in. For instance, when someone starts a business, good planning and sufficient investment, in both money and organisation, are necessary but not enough to guarantee success, because changes in the market or in government policy can still lead to failure. This possibility of losing what one has invested is the real disadvantage of taking risks. However, these risks are outweighed by the far greater rewards they can bring. Therefore, greater achievements in life usually require people to accept a certain amount of risk.

In conclusion, while people can now live comfortably without taking any risks, those who want to go further usually have to accept some risk along the way. In my view, its advantages clearly outweigh its disadvantages, as long as the risk is based on careful thinking and assessment rather than blind gambling.

## 📄 范文 v2（最终 · 262 词 · examiner ✅ 7.0 · suzy 反馈后重写）

> **两处大改**：① **Body1 换成"冒险的弊"**（不确定性→损失；跳槽融不进新公司→伤长期发展）＝真正切 AD 的"劣势"面。Body2 保留她的（创业例）。② **去翻译腔**：整体口语化，改了 6 处直译 tell（achieve more than an ordinary life→want more out of life / extra losses→unnecessary losses / 删冗余 already / fit into→fit in at / extreme success→great success / always→usually require）。
> 结构现在干净：**Body1=弊 / Body2=利 / 立场=利大于弊**。

Many people believe that taking risks is an important part of both professional and personal life. Although taking risks does have its dangers, I believe that its advantages outweigh its disadvantages, especially for those who want more out of life.

On the one hand, taking risks clearly has its dangers, and the biggest one is uncertainty. Because the outcome can never be guaranteed, a risk can easily lead to unnecessary losses. In today's world, most people enjoy a fairly comfortable life, so taking a risk may actually make things worse rather than better. For example, someone might change jobs for a higher salary, only to find that they cannot fit in at the new company. Having to start all over in an unfamiliar environment can slow down, or even damage, their long-term career. Therefore, taking risks can cost people the stable life they already have.

On the other hand, if a person wants a life that is better than the average, they usually have to take risks. Hard work is necessary, but it does not guarantee great success because the future is always uncertain. A good example is starting a business. Even if a business owner invests a lot of money and plans carefully, changes in the market or government policies can still lead to failure. However, if they are afraid of this danger and never try, they will never get huge financial rewards or career growth. Therefore, higher returns usually require taking risks.

In conclusion, while taking risks can bring losses and even set people back, those who want more from life usually have to accept a certain amount of risk. In my view, the advantages clearly outweigh the disadvantages, as long as the risk is based on careful judgement rather than blind gambling.

### 🌟 翻译腔清单（她自查用 · 从 v1→v2 提炼）
- **硬动词** secure / obtain / protect → 换简单：have / get / keep
- **直译名词块** extra losses / achieve more than an ordinary life → unnecessary losses / want more out of life
- **从句堆密**：一句塞 2-3 个从句 → 拆开、松散
- **冗余状语** can already enjoy → enjoy
- 口诀：**natural ≠ 难，是更简更顺。**
