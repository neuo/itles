## 题目（technology，C/E，新）
> These days, people are increasingly dependent on technology in their everyday lives. What are the causes of this growing dependence, and what effects does it have on individuals and society?

## ✅ Step 1.5 中文骨架（suzy cold 锁题型）— 🎉 没串 P/S
> 1. Intro:现在人们愈发依赖科技,本文讨论原因+影响
> 2. Body1(原因):可以轻易放大人们能力(做到以前难做到的,如 AI)+ 让生活更容易(如洗衣机)
> 3. Body2(影响):科技让人更幸福、社会发展更快,但也导致人之间隔离感更强
> 4. 结论:科技带来很多好处,但需注意潜在负面影响

**🎉 本次最大赢**:Body1 全是"为什么(原因)"、Body2 全是"结果(影响)",**主动避开 P/S↔C/E 串台**(她的 TR 复发风险,study_hub 点名)。Step 1.5 中文骨架先锁题型的机制**起效**。
> 小优化建议:Body1 两原因略重叠(都"科技让生活更好")→ 分清 capability(放大能力,AI) vs convenience(便利省事,洗衣机) 更清。

## 英文 stem 骨架（给了,suzy 要范文未自填）
Intro paraphrase + C/E 预告 / Body1 `The primary cause is…` + `Another key reason is…` / Body2 `This reliance has produced several noticeable effects` + `On the positive side…` + `However, it also has a downside…` / Conclusion `…stems mainly from…, and it has…`。

## 📄 范文（C/E Band 7,用 suzy 骨架+内容）— ✅ 考官 gate 7.0 PASS

> **[Intro]** These days, people are becoming increasingly dependent on technology in almost every part of their daily lives. This essay will examine the main causes of this trend and discuss the effects it has on both individuals and society.
>
> **[Body 1·原因]** The primary cause of this growing dependence is that technology greatly extends what an individual can do. Modern tools allow ordinary people to complete tasks that they could never have managed alone. For example, with AI, a single person can now write code, translate languages or analyse large amounts of data in minutes. Another key reason is simply convenience: many devices take the effort out of everyday chores, such as a washing machine that cleans our clothes while we do something else. Together, these factors explain why people come to rely on technology so quickly.
>
> **[Body 2·影响]** This reliance has produced several noticeable effects. On the positive side, it has made life more comfortable and helped society develop much faster. For instance, online platforms let people work, study and access services without leaving home, which saves a great deal of time. However, it also has a downside: as people spend more hours on their screens, they often feel more isolated from those around them. A family may sit together in the evening, yet each member is absorbed in a separate device. As a result, technology connects us to the wider world while quietly pushing us apart in person.
>
> **[Conclusion]** In conclusion, the growing dependence on technology stems mainly from its power to extend human ability and make life easier. While it brings clear benefits to individuals and society, we should also stay aware of its potential to weaken genuine human connection.

**结构**:Body1 全 causes / Body2 全 effects(正:社会发展+省时 / 负:隔离)——干净分离,没串 P/S。
**可偷 chunk**:take the effort out of X / a great deal of time / absorbed in a separate device / push us apart in person。
