"There are two questions to consider about the rise of solo living: why it has become so common, and whether the trend is beneficial to society as a whole. In my view, although living alone reflects greater personal freedom, its overall effects are mostly negative."

### Body 1
"The main causes of solo living are changes in the economy and attitudes towards marriage. In terms of the economy, rising incomes allow individuals to move out and establish their homes. Furthermore, model careers often require people to relocate to major cities for work, making it impractical to live with traditional extended family. Regarding attitudes towards marriage, an increasing number of people are pursuing independence in lifestyle and remain unmarried, which can reduce daily conflict over habits. Secondly, the popularity of single-person househoulds also leads to a weaker family bonds, which serve as the cornerstone of communities, exacerbating social isolation. For example, health research in the United Kingdom has founded that long-term living alone in older citizens is linked to higher rates of depression and memory problems. These examples demonstrate that personal gains from solo living come with environmental and social costs."

## 修复版

### Intro
There are two questions to consider about the rise of solo living: why it has become so common, and whether the trend is beneficial to society as a whole. In my view, although living alone reflects greater personal freedom, its overall effects are mostly negative.

### Body 1
The main causes of solo living are changes in the economy and attitudes towards marriage. In terms of the economy, rising incomes allow individuals to move out and establish their **own** homes. Furthermore, **modern** careers often require people to relocate to major cities for work, making it impractical to live with **a traditional extended family**. Regarding attitudes towards marriage, an increasing number of people **pursue** independence in lifestyle and remain unmarried, which can reduce daily **conflicts** over habits. A case in point is Japan, where delaying marriage and remaining single have led to cities where nearly half of households are single-person. Therefore, economic independence and the pursuit of individual freedom are driving the decline of the traditional extended family.

### Body 2
Although living alone brings some benefits for individuals, its wider effects on society are **mostly** negative. Firstly, the per **capita** energy and land used for single-person households **are** significantly higher, which puts extra pressure on the environment. Secondly, the popularity of single-person **households** also leads to **weaker family bonds**, which serve as the cornerstone of communities, exacerbating social isolation. For example, health research in the United Kingdom has **found** that long-term living alone in older citizens is linked to higher rates of depression and memory problems. These examples demonstrate that personal gains from solo living come with environmental and social costs.

### Conclusion
In conclusion, people have picked up new living habits because of economic independence and changing attitudes towards family. While solo living gives individuals greater personal freedom, its effects on society, mental health and **resource** use are mostly negative.

## 🆕 新错误模式

### W2-20 — 词形相近词辨义混淆 🆕🚨 重点
**首次浮现**：2026-05-26（Day 11 v2 共 5 处密集）
**出现次数**：5
**示例错句**：
- `**model** careers` → `**modern** careers`（model = 模型 / modern = 现代的）
- `are **almost** negative` → `are **mostly** negative`（almost = 几乎 / mostly = 大部分）
- `per **capital**` → `per **capita**`（capital = 资本/首都 / capita = 人均）
- `**househoulds**` → `**households**`（漏 o，纯拼写）
- `has **founded**` → `has **found**`（found = 创立过去式 / find→found 找到的过去分词）

**类别**：W2-1 拼写的进化形态（从"漏字母"升级为"词形相近词辨义"）+ W2-6 词义错的近亲
**状态**：🚨 重点（新瓶颈层，5/26 v2 集中暴露）
**修法**：
1. 写复杂词时**慢一拍**自问"这是 modern 还是 model"（同源高频混淆对）
2. 倒读时**双词专项扫**：mostly/most/almost / find/found / capita/capital / modern/model
3. 高频混淆词对清单（持续累加）：
    - mostly / most / almost
    - find / found（动词 find 还是动词 found）
    - capita / capital
    - modern / model
    - their / there / they're
    - its / it's
    - then / than
    - affect / effect
4. **拼写自检与修 gap 不能合并** —— 每篇都必须做倒读
**修复进展**：0 / 3
