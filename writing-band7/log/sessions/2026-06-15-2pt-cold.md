## 题目（剑 14 T4，2PT）
> Nowadays, many people choose to be self-employed, rather than to work for a company or organisation. Why might this be the case? What could be the disadvantages of being self-employed?
（Q1 为什么自雇 / Q2 自雇弊端）

## suzy 中文计划（2PT 结构对:分别答两问,不选边）
1. Intro:自雇成潮流,讨论原因 + 不利影响
2. Q1 原因:① 自由(自己当老板,决定目标/今天做什么;网约车有事可不出车)② 为自己目标(钱/兴趣),发挥能动性
3. Q2 弊端:缺乏稳定性(公司有固定工资,自雇收入不稳;烘焙师一天没卖出就没收入)
4. 结论:自雇给自我支配 + 满足兴趣/追求,但不稳定值得考虑
→ ✅ 没惯性"选边",规矩答两问。教练建议:Q2 加第 2 弊端(独自扛/没福利)+ Q1 两原因分清(时间灵活 vs 为自己目标)。

## 📄 suzy cold 逐字
> Nowadays, becoming self-employed has become a popular trend, with many people choosing to work for themselves rather than for a company. This essay will discuss the main reasons behind this choice and the potential disadvantages of this lifestyle.
> There are two primary reasons why many individuals prefer self-employment. The first major factor is freedom and flexibility. Self-employed individuals are their own bosses, meaning they can decide their own working hours and daily tasks. For instance, a ride-sharing driver can choose not to work if they have personal matters to attend to. Another critical reason is personal motivation. When people work for themselves, their efforts directly support their own goals, whether for money or personal interest. This alignment of work and passion allows them to take full initiative, making their career feel more rewarding than simply working to fulfill a company's targets.
> Despite these benefits, self-employment comes with significant drawbacks, particularly the lack of financial stability and security. Unlike company employees who receive a steady monthly salary, self-employed workers face unpredictable incomes. For example, if a self-employed baker does not sell any bread on a rainy day, they will earn zero income, whereas a hired baker still gets paid. Furthermore, self-employed individuals must handle all business responsibilities alone, including marketing and bookkeeping. They also miss out on corporate benefits such as paid sick leave, health insurance, and retirement pensions, which places a heavier emotional and financial burden on them.
> In conclusion, people choose self-employment because it offers great personal freedom and allows them to work for their own interests. However, the lack of stability and the absence of employee benefits are major disadvantages that self-employed individuals must carefully consider.

## 📌 统一复用版（2026-07-10 · 只换壳不换肉，句式全部取自 [[t2_reuse_kit.md]] v2）

> 目的：所有 cold 文章收敛到同一批句式，**减少背诵**。论点/例子/数据一字未动。
> **原文在上方，未作任何改动。**
> 核心动作：把 2PT 的两段身收敛到和 DBV 同一个 `On the one hand / On the other hand` 主开关（背一套身开关通吃），并给两段身补上规范 L 句（她原文两段都缺 L）。

**Intro**: Nowadays, becoming self-employed has become a popular trend, with many people choosing to work for themselves rather than for a company. This essay will discuss the main reasons behind this choice and the potential disadvantages of this lifestyle.

**Body1**: On the one hand, there are two primary reasons why many individuals prefer self-employment. The first major factor is freedom and flexibility. Self-employed individuals are their own bosses, meaning they can decide their own working hours and daily tasks. For instance, a ride-sharing driver can choose not to work if they have personal matters to attend to. Another critical reason is personal motivation. When people work for themselves, their efforts directly support their own goals, whether for money or personal interest. This alignment of work and passion allows them to take full initiative, making their career feel more rewarding than simply working to fulfill a company's targets. Therefore, both freedom and personal motivation make self-employment appealing.

**Body2**: On the other hand, self-employment comes with significant drawbacks, particularly the lack of financial stability and security. Unlike company employees who receive a steady monthly salary, self-employed workers face unpredictable incomes. For example, if a self-employed baker does not sell any bread on a rainy day, they will earn zero income, whereas a hired baker still gets paid. Furthermore, self-employed individuals must handle all business responsibilities alone, including marketing and bookkeeping. They also miss out on corporate benefits such as paid sick leave, health insurance, and retirement pensions, which places a heavier emotional and financial burden on them. These examples demonstrate that self-employed workers face financial insecurity and lack proper support.

**Conclusion**: In conclusion, although self-employment offers great personal freedom and allows people to work for their own interests, the lack of stability and the absence of employee benefits are major disadvantages that self-employed individuals must carefully consider.

**复用了哪几条规范句**：
- ✅ opener（trend-fact）：`Nowadays, [趋势事实].`
- ✅ 预告句（2PT 无立场）：`This essay will discuss [Q1] and [Q2].`
- ✅ Body 主开关：`On the one hand, ___.` → `On the other hand, ___.`（和 DBV 收敛到同一套）
- ✅ 枚举两理由：`The first major factor is ___.` → `Another critical reason is ___.`
- ✅ Ex 句：`For instance, ___.` + `For example, ___.`
- ✅ 分面：`Furthermore, ___.`
- ✅ L 句：`Therefore, [重述].`（Body1）+ `These examples demonstrate that ___.`（Body2）
- ✅ Conclusion：`In conclusion, although [让步], [重申].`

**改了哪几句**（原句 → 改后句）：
1. Body1 开头：`There are two primary reasons why many individuals prefer self-employment.` → `On the one hand, there are two primary reasons why many individuals prefer self-employment.`（前置主开关，和 DBV 统一）
2. Body1 补 L 句（新增壳，内容取自她自己的两条理由）：`+ Therefore, both freedom and personal motivation make self-employment appealing.`
3. Body2 开头：`Despite these benefits, self-employment comes with significant drawbacks, particularly...` → `On the other hand, self-employment comes with significant drawbacks, particularly...`（换连接词 → 主开关）
4. Body2 补 L 句（新增壳，内容取自她的 unpredictable income + 缺福利/独自扛）：`+ These examples demonstrate that self-employed workers face financial insecurity and lack proper support.`
5. Conclusion 两句合并进 While/Although 让步壳：`In conclusion, people choose self-employment because it offers great personal freedom and allows them to work for their own interests. However, the lack of stability and the absence of employee benefits are major disadvantages...` → `In conclusion, although self-employment offers great personal freedom and allows people to work for their own interests, the lack of stability and the absence of employee benefits are major disadvantages that self-employed individuals must carefully consider.`（them→people 是重组后消歧，非改肉）

**没能复用的地方 + 原因**：
- 折叠式立场句 `While…, I believe…`：2PT 不选边、无立场，用不上。
- 称重 pivot `these downsides are outweighed by X`：2PT 不称重（不比大小），用不上。
- `Admittedly` 让步 / 分面 Physically·Mentally / `A prime example is` —— 她这篇没有对应内容位，硬塞 = 注水或改肉，故不用。
- ⚠️ 保留了她原文的 `becoming self-employed has become`（重复）和 `incomes`：属于"肉"（内容措辞），只换壳不改肉，教练诊断照留原文。
