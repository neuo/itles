## 题目（剑 14 T4，2PT）
> Nowadays, many people choose to be self-employed, rather than to work for a company or organisation. Why might this be the case? What could be the disadvantages of being self-employed?
（Q1 为什么自雇 / Q2 自雇弊端）

## suzy 中文计划（2PT 结构对:分别答两问,不选边）
1. Intro:自雇成潮流,讨论原因 + 不利影响
2. Q1 原因:① 自由(自己当老板,决定目标/今天做什么;网约车有事可不出车)② 为自己目标(钱/兴趣),发挥能动性
3. Q2 弊端:缺乏稳定性(公司有固定工资,自雇收入不稳;烘焙师一天没卖出就没收入)
4. 结论:自雇给自我支配 + 满足兴趣/追求,但不稳定值得考虑
→ ✅ 没惯性"选边",规矩答两问。教练建议:Q2 加第 2 弊端(独自扛/没福利)+ Q1 两原因分清(时间灵活 vs 为自己目标)。

## 📄 suzy cold 逐字
> Nowadays, becoming self-employed has become a popular trend, with many people choosing to work for themselves rather than for a company. This essay will discuss the main reasons behind this choice and the potential disadvantages of this lifestyle.
> There are two primary reasons why many individuals prefer self-employment. The first major factor is freedom and flexibility. Self-employed individuals are their own bosses, meaning they can decide their own working hours and daily tasks. For instance, a ride-sharing driver can choose not to work if they have personal matters to attend to. Another critical reason is personal motivation. When people work for themselves, their efforts directly support their own goals, whether for money or personal interest. This alignment of work and passion allows them to take full initiative, making their career feel more rewarding than simply working to fulfill a company's targets.
> Despite these benefits, self-employment comes with significant drawbacks, particularly the lack of financial stability and security. Unlike company employees who receive a steady monthly salary, self-employed workers face unpredictable incomes. For example, if a self-employed baker does not sell any bread on a rainy day, they will earn zero income, whereas a hired baker still gets paid. Furthermore, self-employed individuals must handle all business responsibilities alone, including marketing and bookkeeping. They also miss out on corporate benefits such as paid sick leave, health insurance, and retirement pensions, which places a heavier emotional and financial burden on them.
> In conclusion, people choose self-employment because it offers great personal freedom and allows them to work for their own interests. However, the lack of stability and the absence of employee benefits are major disadvantages that self-employed individuals must carefully consider.
