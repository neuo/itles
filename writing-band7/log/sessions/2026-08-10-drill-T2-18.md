## Clean 版（最小改动，未升级词汇）
见对话记录 / 下次重写前先读一遍。关键 6 处：
1. `compared to the younger` → **the young**
2. `the challenge caused by` → **challenges**
3. `As for family level` → **At the family level**
4. `A case in point is China, students leave…` → **China, where students leave… — far earlier than most parents finish work**（合并，解决粘连+主语+比较级）
5. `the pension` → **pension payments**；`pressure` → **strain**；`At the same time` → **On the other hand**（补上未闭合的 On the one hand）
6. `makes sb have` ×2 → **allows sb to** / **leaves sb with no choice but to**

## 带走的一件事
**中文"让某人有/让某人做" → 不要用 `make sb have`。** 三个替代（都是她会的）：`allow sb to` / `enable sb to` / `leave sb with`。
