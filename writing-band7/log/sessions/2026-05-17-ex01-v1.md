## 题目

> Some people think that schools should teach children academic subjects that will be beneficial to their future careers. Others think it is more important for schools to teach a wide range of subjects, including arts and music. **Discuss both views and give your own opinion.**

**题型**：DBV
**立场**：largely agree with View B（broader curriculum）

## 修复版（fix 后 = Band 6.5 边缘）

### Intro
What schools should teach children is often debated. Some people believe that schools should focus on academic subjects that help students find jobs. Others argue that educators should also offer a broader curriculum, such as art and music. I largely agree with the second view.

### Body 1
**[T]** Those who support a job-related curriculum argue that it is the duty of schools to help students find good jobs.
**[E]** As the job market becomes increasingly competitive, students without practical skills in subjects like computing, science or maths may struggle to find stable work.
**[Ex]** For example, in countries such as Singapore, schools focus particularly on STEM subjects, and as a result, the majority of students go on to well-paid jobs in technology and finance.
**[L]** This is what supporters worry about — schools should not produce students who are well-rounded but cannot find work.

### Body 2
**[T]** However, a broader curriculum is more important because education is not only about preparing students for jobs, but also about developing them as whole people.
**[E]** Subjects like art and music teach students to express their feelings and develop creativity, both of which play an important role in life and work.
**[Ex]** For example, students who learn music tend to have better memory and concentration, while those who study art tend to be more imaginative when solving problems. These abilities are increasingly valued in creative industries and many academic careers.
**[L]** This shows that a wider curriculum is not just a luxury, but the foundation for both personal growth and long-term career success.

### Conclusion
In conclusion, while preparing students for work is important, focusing only on career-related subjects would mean missing the bigger purpose of education. A school system that combines academic subjects with arts and music is more likely to produce students who can succeed both at work and in their wider lives.

## 教过的 frameworks（5/17 Day 2 一次性教完）

| Framework | 内容 |
|-----------|------|
| **抽 → 重组**（4 槽公式）| WHO + ACTION + WHAT + WHY → 重新组合不同 frame |
| **Object frame vs META frame** | S1 是 META（"is debated"），S2-3 是 Object；不能混 |
| **DBV 防混 checklist** | 写之前先标 A vs B 的 1 个核心区别词 |
| **L 的 view holder 视角**（不是事实总结）| Band 7 L 必须从 view 持有者的 concern 角度回扣 |
| **"X may struggle to do Y"** 替代 L1 直译 "X is difficult to do Y" | 人作主语难做事，不能用 "X is difficult to do Y" |
| **E 因果链 3 环**（前提 + 后果 + 结论） | 不能漏"否则会怎样" |
| **Ex 中文详细 ≠ 英文好** | 英文砍到 1-2 个 outcome |
| **写完一句立刻回读问"我想说的就是这个吗"** | 防 W2-5 致命（逻辑反 / 词错）|
