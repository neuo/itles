## 📄 suzy cold 逐字
> Many people think that music is a good way to bring people of different cultures and ages together. I completely agree with this opinion. I believe that music is a universal language that can connect different people easily.
> First, music can bring different cultures together because people do not need to understand the words to enjoy it. Language can be a barrier, but melodies and rhythms are the same for everyone. For example, at big international music festivals, thousands of people from different countries listen to the same songs. Even if they do not understand the language, they can feel the same happiness or sadness. Therefore, music helps people from different countries find a common interest and become closer.
> Second, music can also connect people of different ages and reduce the generation gap. Usually, young people and old people like different types of music. However, some classic songs can bring them together. For example, during family parties like weddings or New Year celebrations, when a famous old song plays, grandparents, parents, and children often dance together. In this situation, the age difference is not important anymore, and the family feels very happy and united.
> In conclusion, I strongly believe that music is a great tool to unite people. It can cross language barriers between countries, and it can also bring family members of different ages together.
