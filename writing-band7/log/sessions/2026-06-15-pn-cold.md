## 题目（剑 19 T4，PN）
> Nowadays, consumers can go to a supermarket and buy food produced all over the world. Do you think this is a positive or a negative development?

## suzy 中文计划（立场 + 让步结构）
1. 立场:超市供各国食物,虽冲击本土,**好处>坏处**(positive)
2. 让步(negative):全球化冲击本地产业尤其初级农畜;南美牛肉加运费仍比中国便宜+质优 → 本土破产/失业
3. 主张(positive):多样选择利消费者(口味/价格,西部能吃美洲樱桃);竞争不全坏,带来进步(日本牛肉无地理优势仍享誉全球)
4. 结论:选择多 + 虽冲击但好处远大于坏处

## 📄 suzy cold 逐字
> Nowadays, supermarkets provide a wide range of food imports produced all over the world. Although this trend might bring some challenges to local industries, I believe that it is a largely positive development.
> Admittedly, the global sourcing of food can negatively affect local businesses, especially in agriculture. Imported food is sometimes cheaper and of higher quality than local products. For instance, beef from South America can be less expensive than domestic beef in China, even after adding transportation costs. If local farmers cannot compete with these low prices, they may face heavy financial losses, which could lead to business closures and unemployment.
> However, these downsides are outweighed by the significant benefits for consumers and market development. First of all, a greater variety of food choices directly improves people's quality of life. Consumers can choose food based on their personal taste or budget. For example, people living in western China can now enjoy fresh cherries from the Americas every year. Furthermore, foreign competition is not entirely a bad thing; it can drive local industries to improve. A prime example is Japanese beef. Despite a lack of geographic advantages, Japanese farmers focused on high quality and made their products world-famous. Therefore, global competition can motivate domestic markets to upgrade.
> In conclusion, while buying food from all over the world might create pressure for some local producers, the advantages are far greater. It not only offers consumers more diverse options but also encourages local industries to progress through healthy competition.
