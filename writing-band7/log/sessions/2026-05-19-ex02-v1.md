## 我的 v1（原版，保留错误）

"It is ofter argued that the heavy use of digital communication tools is damaging the social abilities of young people. While I understand the concern behind the view, I largely disagree, since digital tools have actually widened rather than narrowed how young people interact."

### Body 2（主反驳）
"Nevertheless, online communication is essentially a form of socialization, and it actually strengthened youth's ability to connect with others. Digit tools allow people to break physical barriers, enabling them to exchange information anytime and anywhere. Furthermore, it lowers the threshold for getting to know others, making socialization more accessible. For instance, friends who live in different cities can easily share their daily lives every week via WeChat. Similarly, strangers who do not know each other can comfortably discuss their cats' preferences in an online pet community. Digital tools break geographical limits and lower social barriers, making communication more convenient than ever. Therefore, online communication should be seen as a helpful extension of social life, rather than a threat to it."

## 修复版（fix 后 = Band 6.5-7）

### Intro
> It is often argued that the heavy use of digital communication tools is damaging the social abilities of young people. While I understand the concern behind this view, I largely disagree, since digital tools have actually widened rather than narrowed how young people interact.

### Body 1
**[T]** Admittedly, excessive screen time can weaken certain face-to-face habits.
**[E]** When young people put most of their attention on screens, the time they spend on real-life interaction naturally decreases. As a result, some abilities, such as reading body language or managing awkward silences, have few opportunities to develop.
**[Ex]** For example, many teenagers in China can express themselves fluently in online group chats, but appear noticeably awkward when speaking face-to-face.
**[L]** This shows that the concern is not entirely unfounded, but only when young people lack proper guidance on how to use these tools.

### Body 2
**[T]** Nevertheless, online communication is not only a form of socialization, but also something that has strengthened young people's ability to connect with others.
**[E]** Digital tools allow people to break physical barriers, enabling them to exchange information anytime and anywhere. Furthermore, they lower the threshold for getting to know others, making socialization more accessible.
**[Ex]** For instance, friends who live in different cities can easily share their daily lives every week via WeChat. Similarly, people who have never met in person can comfortably discuss tips for raising cats in an online pet community.
**[L]** These examples clearly demonstrate that online interaction and traditional socialization are not mutually exclusive; rather, online communication itself is a vital component of modern social life.

### Conclusion
In conclusion, although the heavy use of technology may reduce the opportunities for young people to practice real-life communication, its benefits cannot be ignored. Digital tools break geographical limits and lower social barriers, making communication more convenient than ever. Therefore, online communication should be seen as a helpful extension of social life, rather than a threat to it.
