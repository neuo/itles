# 5 篇仿写（ex01–05）统一复用版 — 2026-07-10

> **来源**：`log/sessions/` 里 ex01–ex05 的 **v2 二刷**（她的成熟版）。每篇三段式呈现：
> ① **她的原文**（v2 cold 原版，一字未改）
> ② **教练修复版**（当时 session 里的纠正版）
> ③ **📌 统一复用版**（2026-07-10 新做：**只换壳不换肉**，句式全部换成 [[../t2_reuse_kit.md]] v2 的规范句）
>
> **为什么要做这个**：7/10 全语料审计证明——**仿写期她写的很多是"范文的措辞"**（`This shows that` / `Those who support…` / `[X] is often debated` / `look at…and then`），这些在她 **纯 cold 时 0 次出现**。把 5 篇仿写也收敛到**她自己的壳**，则 **12 篇文章（7 cold + 5 仿写）共用同一批 6 条核心句**，背诵量降到最低。
>
> **铁律**：只换壳（opener / 立场句 / BodyT / pivot / 让步 / L 句 / Conclusion 起手 / 连接词）；**论点、例子、数据、话题词一字不动**；不引入池外新词。

---

## 要背死的 6 条核心壳（所有版本共用）

1. `While / Although [让步], I believe that [立场].` —— 零让步时换 `I strongly believe that ___.`
2. `On the one hand, ___.` → `On the other hand, ___.`（两边对立的 body；2-part 的 Q1 用枚举）
3. `these [X] are outweighed by [Y].`（称重 pivot，跨题型）
4. **`Therefore, [重述].`** ← **每个 body 段最后一句永远写它**（cold 时你的头号漏点）
   备选：`These examples demonstrate that ___.` / `This proves that ___.`
5. `For example, ___.` / `For instance, ___.` / `A prime example is [X].`
6. `In conclusion, while / although [让步], [重申].` —— 零让步时 `In conclusion, I strongly believe that ___.`

**opener（trend-fact 型，按题型挑）**：`Nowadays, [趋势].` / `In many parts of the world, [趋势].` / `People have different opinions about whether [X].`(DBV) / `Many people think that [X].`(AgD) / `In the future, it is expected that [趋势].`(AD) / `In modern society, the trend towards [X] has grown rapidly.`(P/S)
**预告句（无立场题）**：`This essay will discuss ___ and ___.`
**让步**：`Admittedly, ___.`（限量：certain / excessive / to some extent / not entirely）
**枚举**：`The first major factor is ___.` → `Another critical reason is ___.` / `First, ___. Second, ___.`
**分面**：`Physically, ___. Mentally, ___.` / `First of all, ___. Furthermore, ___.`
**E 句**：`This is because ___.` / `As a result, ___.`

## ex01 — DBV Education（原 v2 二刷，2026-05-18）

### 题目
> Some people think that schools should teach children academic subjects that will be beneficial to their future careers. Others think it is more important for schools to teach a wide range of subjects, including arts and music. **Discuss both views and give your own opinion.**

### ① 她的原文（v2，一字未改）
What is the goal of education is frequently debated. Some people argue that schools should focus on academic subjects to help students find jobs. Others believe teaching a variety of subjects, like arts and music, is also important. I largely agree with the second view.

Those who support a job-focused curriculum argue that it is the duty of schools to help students find good jobs. As the job market becomes increasingly competitive today, students without practical skills in subjects like computer or math may struggle to find stable jobs. For example, in some countries such as Singapore, schools particularly focus on STEM subjects, and as a result, the majority of students there get on to well-paid jobs in technology and finance. That is why the supporters worry about — schools should not produce students who are well-rounded but can't find jobs.

Nevertheless, I lean towards that a broader curriculum is more beneficial because education is not only about preparing students for jobs but also about fostering them as whole people. Subjects like art and music teach students to express their feelings and develop creativity, both of which also play an important role in their life and career. For instance, students who learn music tend to have better memory and concentration, while those who study art tend to show more imaginative when solving problems. These abilities are more valued in creative industries and many academic careers. This demonstrates that a wider curriculum is not just a luxury, but the foundation for both personal growth and long-term career development.

In conclusion, while preparing students for jobs is important, focusing on a job-related subject would mean missing the bigger purpose of education. A education system that combines academic subjects with art and music is more likely to produce students who would success both at work and in their wilder lives.

- 枚举 `The first major factor is ___.` —— 每个 body 单一主论点，非多因枚举。

## ex02 — A/D Technology（原 v2 二刷，2026-05-20）

### 题目
> Some people believe that the increasing use of computers and mobile phones for communication has had a negative effect on young people's social skills. **To what extent do you agree or disagree?**

### ① 她的原文（v2，一字未改）

Admittedly, excessive screen time may, to some extend, weaken certain face-to-face skills. When teenagers put the majority of their attention on screens, the time spent on real-life interaction naturally decreases. In real life, however, a large part of social interaction involves reading body language and listening to voice tones. For example, many adolescents in China can express themselves fluently in online group chats, but show noticeably social awkward when speaking face-to-face because they don't know how to handle a live conversation. Digital devices allow people to break physical barriers, enabling them to share information anytime and anywhere. Furthermore, they lower the threshold for getting to know others, making socialization more accessible. For instance, friends who live in different cities can easily share their daily lives every week via WeChat. Similarly, people who have never met in person can comfortably discuss their cats' preferences in an online pet community. These examples clearly demonstrate that online interaction and traditional socialization are not mutually exclusive; rather, online communication itself is a vital component of modern social life.

In conclusion, although the heavy use of technology may reduce the opportunities for young people to practice real-life social skills, its benefits cannot be ignored. Digital tools break geographic limits and lower social barriers, making communication more convenient than ever. Therefore, online communication should be seen as an extension of social life, rather than a threat to it.

## ex03 — C/S Childhood Obesity（原 v2 二刷，2026-05-23）

### 题目
> Obesity is a growing problem worldwide, particularly among children. **What are the causes of this trend, and what can be done to address it?**

### ① 她的原文（v2，一字未改）
These days, obesity has become a serious problem in many countries, and particularly a lot of children are overweight. The essay will examine the main causes of the problem before suggesting possible solutions.

Childhood obesity results primarily from a change in everyday diet and lifestyle. On the dietary side, although processed food is high in sugar and fat, modern busy families prefer them because they are much cheaper and more convenient than fresh produce, leading children to having too much unhealthy nutrition. On the activity side, children today spend many more hours on screens, which has reduced their opportunities to play outdoors. A case in point is the United Kingdom, where researches show that an average child eats far more sugar than recommended and walks less than 30 minutes a day. This shows that the problem is not caused by only one factor, but poor diet combined with low activity.

Solving the problem requires a combination of government policies and school education. At the policy level, government could impose extra taxes on sugary drinks and require a clearer nutrition labels. For example, the European Union makes it mandatory for all food to clearly display their sugar and fat content. At the school level, daily exercise should be added to the timetable, and school lessons should teach students about food nutrition. For instance, Japan has long included school lunches and food education in its curriculum, and its childhood obesity is one of the lowest worldwide. These examples clearly demonstrate that real progress depends on improving the food environment around children and encouraging more active lifestyle.

In conclusion, the primary reason for childhood obesity is unhealthy diets and inactive lifestyles, and solving it requires action on both sides. By combining sensible policies with better education on exercise and nutrition, societies can give the next generation a lower obesity rate than today.

## ex04 — C/E Online Shopping（原 v2 二刷，2026-05-24 · 🎉 她的最佳篇 14/14 Band 7.0）

### 题目
> In recent years, more and more people are buying clothes, shoes, and other items online instead of in shops. **What are the reasons for this trend, and what are the effects on traditional retail stores?**

### ① 她的原文（v2，一字未改）
These days, the trend towards purchasing clothes, shoes, and other goods online has grown rapidly, transforming the way people shop. This essay will examine the main reasons for this shift and its effects on traditional retailers.

The popularity of online shopping results primarily from the combination of convenience and price. Regarding convenience, online platforms allow consumers to break geographic barriers, enabling them to browse thousands of goods and place orders through digital devices at any time, from anywhere. As for price, online retailers often offer competitive pricing because they have lower running costs, especially through saving on rent. A case in point is the growth of Amazon, which has built large consumer bases through low-priced goods and their convenient delivery service. Therefore, the practical advantages, which are hard for physical shops to match, make online shopping an irresistible choice for modern consumers.

However, the digital shift has brought significant challenges, most of which are negative, for traditional retailers. The most direct impact is a dramatic decline in foot traffic and sales. As more consumers prefer shopping online, a large number of physical shops struggle to cover their fixed costs. For example, many traditional appliance stores located in city centers in China have had to close down over the last decade. On the other hand, the pressure also forces surviving shops to adapt by upgrading their in-store services, such as personal styling, cafes and interactive shopping experiences that cannot be replicated online. For instance, many shopping malls in China have moved towards "experience-led" formats, where shoppers come for the events as much as the goods. These examples demonstrate that the growth of online shopping not only threatens the survival of physical shops, but also drives the remaining ones to reinvent themselves.

In conclusion, modern consumers have picked up new shopping habits because online shopping is more convenient and cheaper. While this has undoubtedly put immense pressure on traditional shops and caused some of them to close down, it also forces the retail industry to evolve and find new ways to attract consumers.

### ① 她的原文（v2，一字未改）

For example, health research in the United Kingdom has founded that long-term living alone in older citizens is linked to higher rates of depression and memory problems. These examples demonstrate that personal gains from solo living come with environmental and social costs.

In conclusion, people have picked up new living habits because of economic independence and changing attitudes towards family. While solo living gives individuals greater personal freedom, its effects on society, mental health and resources use are mostly negative.

## 🔴 三个实战发现（收敛过程挖出来的）

1. **cold 时你第一个丢的是 L 句。** 仿写 12 个 body 段**零漏 L**；纯 cold **5/7 篇**有 body 段没写 L（共 7 处）。
   → **每个 body 段最后一句永远写 `Therefore, [重述本段].`** 这是最便宜的 CC/TR 分。

2. **一边倒题不能硬折立场句。** AgD 音乐篇你完全同意、零让步，硬套 `While [让步], I believe…` 会**凭空造一个不存在的让步**。
   → 用 `I completely agree with this opinion.` + `In conclusion, I strongly believe that ___.`

3. **肉太密会超字数。** ex04 复用版 **335 词**、ex05 **291 词**（例子/数据都是你的，不许删）。
   → 考场 40 分钟，**同一个 body 段两个例子只留一个**。

## 壳不适用的地方（别硬套）
- **2-part / 2PT 的 Q1 原因段** 不用主开关，用枚举 `The first major factor is… / Another critical reason is…`
- **C/E 客观题**没有让步段，`Admittedly` 塞不进
- **C/S 没有影响段**，`This trend has produced several noticeable effects.` 塞不进
- ⚠️ ex05 的 pivot `these benefits are outweighed by…` 指代回 intro 的"personal freedom"，**跨段指代略远**——同款 pivot 在 DBV 老龄化/AD 无人驾驶里指代距离短，更自然。
