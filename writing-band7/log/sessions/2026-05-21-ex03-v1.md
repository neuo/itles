## 修复版

### Intro
These days, obesity has become a serious problem in many countries, and a large number of children are now overweight. This essay will examine the causes before suggesting possible solutions.

### Body 1
**[T]** The main cause of rising childhood obesity is a change in everyday diet and lifestyle.
**[E1]** Although processed foods are high in sugar and fat, modern busy families prefer them because they are often cheaper and more convenient than fresh produce, leading children to consume too much unhealthy food.
**[E2]** In addition, children today spend many hours on screens, which greatly reduces their opportunities to play outdoors.
**[Ex]** For example, in the United Kingdom, studies show that the average child eats far more sugar than recommended and exercises for less than 30 minutes a day.
**[L]** This shows that childhood obesity is not caused by one factor, but by poor diet combined with low physical activity.

### Body 2
**[T]** Reducing this trend requires a combination of government policies and school education.
**[E1]** At the policy level, governments could put higher taxes on sugary drinks and require clearer nutrition labels, which would push companies to offer healthier options.
**[Ex1]** For example, the European Union requires all packaged food to display its sugar and fat content.
**[E2]** At the school level, daily exercise should be added to the timetable, and cooking lessons should teach children healthy eating habits.
**[Ex2]** Japan has long included school lunches and food education in its curriculum, and its childhood obesity rate is one of the lowest in the world.
**[L]** These examples demonstrate that real progress depends on both improving the food environment around children and encouraging more active lifestyles.

### Conclusion
In conclusion, childhood obesity is mainly the result of an unhealthy diet and an inactive lifestyle, and solving it requires action on both fronts. By combining sensible policies with better education on exercise and nutrition, societies can give the next generation a lower obesity rate than today.
