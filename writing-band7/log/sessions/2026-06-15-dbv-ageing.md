> In many countries, people are now living longer than ever before. Some people say an ageing population causes problems for governments. Others think there are benefits if society has more elderly people. Discuss both these views and give your own opinion.

## 📄 suzy cold 逐字
> In many parts of the world, people are living longer than ever before. While having more elderly people can bring some benefits to society, I believe that an ageing population poses much greater economic and social challenges for governments.
>
> On the one hand, an increasing number of senior citizens can contribute positively to society. The elderly possess valuable life experience and professional knowledge. In the workplace, they can act as mentors to guide younger generations, which helps maintain high work standards. Furthermore, on a family level, many grandparents provide free childcare for their grandchildren. This support allows young parents to return to work full-time without worrying about expensive daycare costs. Therefore, older people can provide both intellectual and practical support to communities.
>
> However, these benefits are outweighed by the massive problems caused by an unbalanced population structure. As the number of retired people grows, governments face huge financial pressure regarding pensions and healthcare. Since elderly individuals require more medical attention, public healthcare systems can easily become overloaded. This financial burden falls heavily on the shrinking younger workforce. For instance, countries like China have already introduced policies to delay the retirement age. This proves that having fewer young workers support a larger elderly population is unsustainable and will eventually jeopardize the country's long-term economic growth.
>
> In conclusion, although an ageing society can benefit from the wisdom and family support of older individuals, it creates severe structural problems. The heavy burden on public finance and younger generations means that the negative impacts of an ageing population far outweigh the benefits.
