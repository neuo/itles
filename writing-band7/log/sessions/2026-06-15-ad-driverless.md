## 题目（剑 16 T4，AD-outweigh，交通）
> In the future, all cars, buses and trucks will be driverless. The only people travelling inside these vehicles will be passengers. Do you think the advantages of driverless vehicles outweigh the disadvantages?

## suzy 中文计划（AD 结构对 + 教练提示连接接缝）
- 裁决:advantages outweigh
- Body1 缺点(轻,承认):安全性(算力依赖→断电/黑客→交通瘫痪)+ 事故责任难判(两无人车追尾,乘客/厂商/服务商?无定论)
- Body2 优点(重):解放人类出行轻松 + 比人类安全(机器不分心,Tesla 每百万公里事故率低于人类)
- 结论:虽有未解问题,但无人驾驶是更美好交通未来的必需技术
- ⚠️ 教练提示:"安全"在两边都出现(Body1 不安全/Body2 更安全)→ 会矛盾 → 用过渡分层(极端风险 vs 日常安全)

## 📄 suzy cold 逐字
> In the future, it is expected that all vehicles will become driverless and people will only travel as passengers. Although this development brings some potential problems, I believe that the advantages of autonomous vehicles far outweigh the disadvantages.
> On the one hand, driverless vehicles have some serious downsides, especially regarding extreme technical failures and legal issues. Since these vehicles rely fully on computers, a large-scale power cut or a hacker attack could cause the entire transport system to stop working completely. Furthermore, it is very difficult to choose who is responsible when an accident happens. For example, if two driverless cars hit each other from behind, it is hard to decide whether the blame belongs to the passengers, the car manufacturer, or the software provider. This legal confusion is still a big problem today.
> On the other hand, these disadvantages are outweighed by the massive benefits in daily life. Although driverless systems carry risks in extreme cases like hacking, in everyday driving they are actually far safer than human drivers. This is because machines never get tired or lose attention like humans do. According to recent data, the accident rate of Tesla's self-driving system per million kilometers is already much lower than that of human drivers. Additionally, automated driving completely frees people from the stress of driving, making daily travel much easier and more relaxing.
> In conclusion, although driverless cars still have some unsolved problems like cyber-attacks and legal responsibility, they are a great technology. Their ability to reduce daily traffic accidents and free passengers makes them a better choice for our future.
