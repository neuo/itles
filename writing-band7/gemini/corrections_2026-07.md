## 1. T2 — Taking risks

**题目**

> It is important for people to take risks, both in their professional lives and their personal lives.
>
> Do you think the advantages of taking risks outweigh the disadvantages?
>
> Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience.

**我的原文**

> These days, many people believe that taking risks is import part of both professional and personal life. Although taking risks does have its dangers, I believe that its advantages outweigh its disadvantages, especially for those who want to achieve great success.
>
> Admittedly, taking risks clearly has its dangers, and the major one is uncertainty. Because the outcome can never be guaranteed, a risk can easily lead to uneccessary losses. In modern society, most people enjoy a fairly convenient life, so taking a risk actually make things worse than better. For example, someone change jobs for a higher salary, but it is possible for them to struggle to fit in a new environment, which will slow down, even damage, their long-term career. Therefore, taking risks can cost people the stable life they already have.
>
> Nonetheless, the advantage of taking a risk is that it opens the door to extraordinary achievements. If a person wants a life that is better than the average, they usually have to take risks. Hark work is neccessary, but it does not guarantee success because the future is always uncertain. A good example is starting a business. Even if a bussiness owner invests a lot of money and plans carefully, changes in goverment policies can still cause failure. But if they are afraid of this danger and never try, they will never get huge financial rewards or career growth. Thus, higher returns require taking risks.
>
> In conclusion, while taking risks can bring losses and even set people back, those who want more from life usually have to accept a certain amount of risk. In my view, the advantages clearly outweigh the disadvantages, as long as the risk is based on careful judgement rather than blind gambling.

**Gemini 逐条修改**

第一段：
- `import part` → 漏了形容词后缀，应为 **important part**。

第二段：
- `uneccessary` → 拼写错误，应为 **unnecessary**（双写 n）。
- `taking a risk actually make things` → 主语是单数动名词 taking a risk，动词应该用单三：**makes things**。
- `someone change jobs` → 同上，主语是 someone，动词应该用单三：**changes jobs**（或者用过去时 changed / 过去分词 changing，这里用 changes 最顺）。

第三段：
- `Hark work` → 笔误，应为 **Hard work**。
- `neccessary` → 拼写错误，应为 **necessary**（一个 c，两个 s）。
- `bussiness` → 拼写错误，应为 **business**。
- `goverment` → 拼写错误，应为 **government**（漏了 n）。

其他语感微调（未单列但已改入终稿）：`taking a risk actually make things worse than better` → `can actually make things worse`；`someone change jobs` → `someone might change jobs`；`fit in a new environment` → `fit into the new environment`；`slow down, even damage` → `slow down, or even damage`；`judgement` → `judgment`。

**最终修改结果全文（291 词）**

> These days, many people believe that taking risks is an important part of both professional and personal life. Although taking risks does have its dangers, I believe that its advantages outweigh its disadvantages, especially for those who want to achieve great success.
>
> Admittedly, taking risks clearly has its dangers, and the major one is uncertainty. Because the outcome can never be guaranteed, a risk can easily lead to unnecessary losses. In modern society, most people enjoy a fairly convenient life, so taking a risk can actually make things worse. For example, someone might change jobs for a higher salary, but it is possible for them to struggle to fit into the new environment, which will slow down, or even damage, their long-term career. Therefore, taking risks can cost people the stable life they already have.
>
> Nonetheless, the advantage of taking a risk is that it opens the door to extraordinary achievements. If a person wants a life that is better than the average, they usually have to take risks. Hard work is necessary, but it does not guarantee success because the future is always uncertain. A good example is starting a business. Even if a business owner invests a lot of money and plans carefully, changes in government policies can still cause failure. But if they are afraid of this danger and never try, they will never get huge financial rewards or career growth. Thus, higher returns require taking risks.
>
> In conclusion, while taking risks can bring losses and even set people back, those who want more from life usually have to accept a certain amount of risk. In my view, the advantages clearly outweigh the disadvantages, as long as the risk is based on careful judgment rather than blind gambling.

**Gemini 总评**：结构极其完整，达到 7 分（甚至 7.5）水平；能熟练用 Although / Admittedly / Nonetheless / Therefore，全篇逻辑浑然一体。考场上放慢一点打字速度、避开这些拼写小错，凭这个逻辑和表达稳拿 7 分。

## 2. T2 — Professionals working in the country where they trained

**题目**

> Some people believe that professionals, such as doctors and engineers, should be required to work in the country where they did their training. Others believe they should be free to work in another country if they wish.
>
> Discuss both these views and give your own opinion.
>
> Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience.

**我的说明**：时间不太够，小错误应该挺多的，逻辑也没写顺，结尾匆匆 close。

**我的原文**

> These days, people have different opinions about whether professionals should be work in the country where they got trained or they can work at wherever they want. While I understand why some people have the former view, I strongly believe that people are free to choose where to work.
>
> Admittedly, training a professional, such as a doctor or a engineer, is not a easy thing, which requires tons of resources on both physical and mental sides. Physically, a large number of facilities like various education building and devices are neccessary, which would cost substantial money and impose significant financial burden on government. Mentally, the proper training teachers or professors are more valueable. The number of them and their attention are both limit. A case in point is China, where there was rarely any teacher who was able to use computer in 1990s. Therefore, the requirement for professionals working in the country where they are trained is just for reward.
>
> Nevertherness, I largely believe that no reasons should completely restrict individuals' freedom, including the right to choose where to work. Even if some profressionals get government's support, like money or opportunities, they should be only limited base on contracts, which may require them working at somewhere for certain time. For instance, universities in China used to recruit some students with low scores or performance, but those students must server at a specific place or deparment for a centain peroid of time.
>
> In conclusion, while limiting professionals' workplace is reasonable from some pespective, securing them having right to choose where they work is more important.

**Gemini 逐条修改**

**1. 逻辑与切题度（最大问题）**
- Body 1：花大量篇幅写"培养医生和工程师需要巨额资金、设备和稀缺老师"，很好，但因果链在最后断了——结论 `Therefore, the requirement ... is just for reward`。考官的疑惑：为什么国家付出了这些，他们就应该被强制留下？
  - **理顺方法**：把"reward（报答）"升华成"社会责任 / 纳税人的回报"——培养资源来自本国纳税人，所以他们有义务回馈当地社会，缓解当地医疗或工程人才短缺。
- Body 2：论点是"人身自由不该被限制"，却用了中国大学"定向生/委培生"的例子。
  - **逻辑冲突**：定向生恰恰是"限制自由"的代表（必须在特定地方工作几年），用它支持"自由不该被限制"，逻辑反了。
  - **理顺方法**：例子很好，但要这样解释——即使要强制留下，也必须通过**自愿的合同协议**（如定向生减免学费作为交换），而不是"一刀切"的法律强制。

**2. 语法与词汇小错误**
- `should be work` → **should work**（情态动词后直接加动词原形）。
- `work at wherever they want` → **work wherever they want**（wherever 本身可作副词，不用 at）。
- `a engineer` → **an engineer**；`a easy thing` → **an easy thing**。
- `education building` → **educational buildings**。
- `limit`（形容词应为 **limited**）；`valueable` → **valuable**。
- `Nevertherness` → **Nonetheless** 或 **Nevertheless**（拼写错误）。

**最终修改结果全文（逻辑理顺 + 词汇降温）**

> These days, people have different opinions about whether professionals, like doctors and engineers, should be forced to work in the country where they were trained. While I understand the reasons behind this requirement, I strongly believe that individuals should be free to choose where they want to work.
>
> Admittedly, training a professional requires a massive amount of public resources. Physically, governments must invest substantial money into expensive facilities, such as laboratories and medical equipment. Mentally, experienced professors and teachers are highly valuable and limited. Since these resources are paid for by local taxpayers, many people believe that these professionals have a social responsibility to stay. From this perspective, requiring them to work locally for a few years is a fair way to return the investment and serve the local community.
>
> Nevertheless, I believe that no government should completely restrict a person's freedom to travel and work. Even if some professionals receive financial support from the government during their training, this should be managed by voluntary contracts rather than a total ban on leaving. For example, some universities in China recruit students with lower test scores under a special agreement: these students receive free education but must work in a specific local area for a fixed period. This contract-based method is much better because it respects personal choice while still securing the necessary workforce.
>
> In conclusion, it is reasonable for a country to want to keep the talent it has paid to train. However, protecting individual freedom is more important. The best solution is to use fair employment contracts with financial incentives, rather than forcing professionals to stay against their will.

**复盘建议**
- **结尾匆匆**：Discuss both views 的结尾最稳公式 = 总结 View A 的合理性 + 总结 View B 的合理性 + 重申立场。范文只用了两句话，1 分钟也能收尾。
- **举例逻辑**：考场上发现例子和论点矛盾时，加转折过渡把例子掰回来（"支持不等于强制，应该用合同来管理，比如中国的定向生……"）。

**后续追问（开头 rephrase）**：我问"第一句 about whether 后面需要 rephrase 下吗"，Gemini 说原句够了（考官能懂、语法没硬伤），但两个小瑕疵是 `should be forced to work` 被动略生硬 + `where they were trained` 属原词重现。给了 3 个改写：
1. （推荐）`...about whether qualified professionals should remain in the country that educated them, or if they should have the freedom to move abroad.`
2. （最精炼）`...about whether newly trained experts should be required to work locally or allowed to pursue careers abroad.`
3. （改动最小）`...about whether specialists like doctors and engineers should serve the country of their education, or enjoy the liberty to work anywhere.`

## 3. T2 — Alternative medicines and treatments

**题目**

> Nowadays, a growing number of people with health problems are trying alternative medicines and treatments instead of visiting their usual doctor.
>
> Do you think this is a positive or a negative development?
>
> Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience.

**我的说明**：这道题感觉有点难，只能大概想了下内容，但发现很多句子翻译不出来。这是我 40min 写的，字数也不太够。

**我的中文思路（原样）**：立场不赞同。Body1 让步——有时候换疗法或药的想法主要来自患者较长时间没有得到明显好转，心理上难以接受，想着新的疗法是否会更好是比较自然的想法，特别是长期慢性病或绝症患者，确实会去冒险最极小概率甚至不可能的方法，所以能理解这个 concern。Body2——但一般来说相信专业人士是更稳妥的方法，因为医生的专业知识和信息面优于患者，如果有更好的方法，一般医生也会给出指引和尝试。总结：尽管在面临困境时积极选择方法可以理解，但要尽可能参考专业人士的建议，这样才能更好地治疗疾病。

**我的原文**

> These days, a increasing number of patients are looking for alternative medicines and treatments, rather than believing their usual doctor. Whlie I understand why this trend is growing now, I largely disagree with it.
>
> The tread towards diverse treatments mainly results from dramatic urge for health. Patients suffer physical and mental problems, and current medicines and treatments don't work pretty well. As a result, it is natural for people to wonder whether they should try a new method or change a doctor. Especially regarding cancer patients, some of them definitely would take risks to try treatments which barely success for surviving. Therefore, the popularity of this tread is understandable.
>
> Nevertheless, trusting professionals is much better and more stable way for most patients. The knowledge and information from doctors are more accurate compared to the general public. If there are better medicines and treatments, doctors will provide guidance and give a try if neccessary. A case in point is hierarchical diagnosis and treatment, current doctors would recommend patients to advanced hospitals or other specialist focusing on special diseases if they cannot handle. Therefore, patients should believe doctors and follow the advices from professionals, rather than blindly gambling to try new medicines or treatments.
>
> In conclusion, while I understand the urge that patients want to be healthy, they shouldn't try alternative medicines without prescription.

**Gemini 逐条修改**

先表扬的地道表达：
- `dramatic urge for health`（渴望健康的迫切心情）用得非常好。
- `trusting professionals is a much better and more stable way` 逻辑对比很清晰。
- `hierarchical diagnosis and treatment`（分级诊疗）非常高级且准确的专有名词。

必须修正的语法与搭配：
- `a increasing number` → **an increasing number**。
- `rather than believing their usual doctor` → 题目是 visiting（看医生），用 **consulting**（咨询）或 **seeing** 更妥。
- `The tread towards...` → 拼写错误，是 **trend**（趋势），不是 tread（践踏）。全篇错写了好几次。
- `treatments which barely success` → success 是名词，应改为动词 **succeed**。
- `follow the advices` → **advice** 是不可数名词，不能加 s。
- `without prescription` → 替代疗法（如针灸、草药）一般不需要处方，用 **without professional guidance**（没有专业指导）更切题。

**最终修改结果全文（268 词）**

> These days, an increasing number of patients are looking for alternative medicines and treatments rather than seeing their usual doctors. While I understand why this trend is growing, I believe it is a negative development.
>
> The trend towards alternative treatments mainly results from a strong urge to get well. When patients suffer from serious physical problems and modern medicine does not work quickly, it is natural for them to wonder if other methods might be better. This is especially true for patients with long-term illnesses or terminal cancers. Many of them are willing to take any risk, even trying unproven methods with a very low chance of success, just to survive. Therefore, the popularity of this choice is psychologically understandable.
>
> Nevertheless, trusting professionals is a much safer and more effective way for most patients. The knowledge and medical information that doctors have are far better than those of the general public. If a better treatment exists, qualified doctors will provide guidance. A case in point is the hierarchical diagnosis system. If a usual doctor cannot cure a disease, they do not leave the patient helpless; instead, they refer them to advanced specialists or experts in that specific field. Therefore, relying on this professional network is much wiser than blindly gambling on alternative therapies.
>
> In conclusion, while the desire of sick people to find a cure is fully understandable, switching to alternative medicines without expert advice is dangerous. To ensure the best recovery, patients should always rely on the guidance of trained medical professionals.

**考场心态复盘**
- "很多句子翻译不出来"：头脑风暴时想得越复杂，翻译时越痛苦。范文把复杂心理拆成"当现代医学见效慢时，人们自然会想有没有更好的办法"（`When modern medicine does not work quickly, it is natural for them to wonder...`），极简的词反而干净利落。
- 字数：原文其实 4 句话就快到 250 字。主体段每段 4–5 句（1 论点 + 2–3 解释/举例 + 1 总结）字数稳过 250，不需要刻意拉长句子。

**我的追问 + Gemini 的答复（词汇痛点）**：我发现自己经常想不到 safe / strong / dangerous / wise / qualified / trained 这类不难的形容词，还有 desire 这类名词；另外 `If a better treatment exists` 比 there is 高级。Gemini 给了三招：
1. **把"人"和"物"具体化**（多用小形容词）：stable life / wise decision / unfamiliar environment。
2. **把"抽象想法"变成"人类本能"**：`the desire for success`、`the urge to get well`（名词 + for/to 结构，比 Many people want to be successful 成熟得多）。
3. **反义词平替法**：卡文时退回 safe vs dangerous —— `This method is dangerous and risky.` / `Trusting doctors is a safer and wiser choice.`
- 另外：`If there are problems` → `If problems arise`；`If there is an opportunity` → `If an opportunity presents itself`。
- 下一篇的两个硬目标：**绝不用 There is/are 开头**；**核心名词至少戴一顶"简单形容词帽子"**。

## 4. T1 — 四个亚洲国家城市人口比例折线图（1970–2040）

**题目**

> The graph below gives information about the percentage of the population in four Asian countries living in cities from 1970 to 2020, with predictions for 2030 and 2040.
>
> Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

**我的原文**

> The chart illustrates how the percentages of the citizens changed in four specific Asian countries over the 50-year period from 1970 to 2020, as well as the predictions of them for the next two decades.
>
> Overall, the all countries experienced and will see a growth through the entire period. Meanwhile, Malaysia and Indonesia showed a more rapid increase, and the trend is going to remain in the future.
>
> Regarding Malaysia and Indonesia, starting at 30% and about 14% in 1970, both of two countries witnessed a surge, reaching at approximately 75% and 53% repectively to rank the top 2 in 2020. From then on, the dominance will be continue, hitting a peak of about 80% and 60% by 2040.
>
> A similar but more moderate pattern is seen in the Philippines and Thailand. The Philippines was the first runner in 1970. The figure sharply growed to just below 50% in 1990 before a dip to about 42% in 2010, with a recovery for the rest of the period. Meanwhile, Thailand experienced a gradual growth from 1970 to 2020, and is expected to increase to about 44% in 2040.

**Gemini 逐条修改**

**1. 数据描述失误（Task Achievement，最可惜）**
- 马来西亚 2040：原文写 `hitting a peak of about 80% and 60% by 2040`，但图中马来西亚 2040 已超过 80%（约 83%），印尼也超过 60%（接近 64%）。终点数据"缩水"会被扣分，建议 **over 80%** 或 **around 83%**。
- 泰国 2040：原文 `about 44%`，但图中泰国 2040 已顶到 **50%**，44% 大概是它 2030 年的位置。

**2. 时态切换（线图常考陷阱）**
- 2020 年之前一律一般过去时（做到了）；2030 年之后一律将来时/预测表达。
- `the dominance will be continue` 语病明显 → **will continue**（continue 是动词，前面不能加 be）。

**3. 小词固定搭配**
- `the all countries` → **all countries** 或 **all the countries**。
- `reaching at approximately 75%` → reach 是及物动词，直接加数字 → **reaching approximately 75%**。
- `growed` → 极其致命的低级拼写错误，grow 的过去式是 **grew**。
- `both of two countries` → 啰嗦，直接 **both countries**。
- 另：`citizens` 换题目里的 population 不妥（citizens 指公民，涉国籍法律身份），稳妥写法是 **the proportion of people**。

**最终修改结果全文**

> The chart illustrates how the percentages of the population living in cities changed in four specific Asian countries over a 50-year period from 1970 to 2020, as well as predictions for the next two decades.
>
> Overall, all four countries experienced and are projected to see continuous growth in urbanization. Meanwhile, Malaysia and Indonesia showed a much more rapid increase, and this leading trend is expected to remain in the future.
>
> Regarding Malaysia and Indonesia, starting at 30% and about 14% respectively in 1970, both countries witnessed a surge. By 2020, their figures reached approximately 75% and 53%, making them the top two. From then on, their dominance will continue, with the percentages expected to hit around 83% for Malaysia and 64% for Indonesia by 2040.
>
> A similar but more moderate pattern is seen in the Philippines and Thailand. The Philippines was the frontrunner in 1970 at over 30%. The figure grew sharply to just below 50% in 1990 before experiencing a dip to about 42% in 2010, followed by a gradual recovery. Meanwhile, Thailand experienced a steady growth throughout the period and is expected to reach the 50% mark by 2040.

**追加修改：最后一段加入排名（我要求菲律宾第三、泰国第四）**

> A similar but more moderate pattern is seen in the Philippines and Thailand. The Philippines was the frontrunner in 1970 at over 30%. The figure grew sharply to just below 50% in 1990 before experiencing a dip to about 42% in 2010, followed by a gradual recovery to occupy third place at around 56% by the end of the period. Meanwhile, Thailand experienced a steady growth throughout the period and is expected to reach the 50% mark by 2040, ranking as the tail-ender among the four countries.

**排名的三个"小钩子"**
1. **现在分词作状语**：`occupying / holding the lowest position`、`ranking the last`。
   - `Meanwhile, Thailand experienced a steady growth throughout the period, occupying the lowest position to reach the 50% mark by 2040.`
2. **with 结构把排名挂句尾**：`Thailand is expected to reach 50% by 2040, with this being the lowest figure among the four nations.`
3. **名词代称**：第一名 = `the frontrunner / the leader`；最后一名 = `the tail-ender / the last runner`。
   - `Thailand, as the tail-ender in 1970, experienced a steady growth...`

**主语悬挂（我自己发现的点）**：原句 `the dominance will be continue, hitting a peak of...` 中 hitting 的逻辑主语会指向 the dominance（优势不能达到 83%），属典型主语悬挂。改成 `with the percentages expected to hit...` 就给了 expected to hit 一个明确主语。**with + n. + done/doing** 是小作文"数据并列/补充说明"的 7 分+ 核心句型。
- 套用公式：`The industrial output of both countries dropped sharply in 2010, with the figures decreasing to 20% for the UK and 15% for France.`

## 16. T2 — Free water supply

**题目**

> Access to clean water is a basic human right. Therefore every home should have a water supply that is provided free of charge. Do you agree or disagree?

**我的原文**

> These days, some people believe that the water supply should be free because having clean water is a basic human right. While I acknowledge that governments should secure water safty, I largely disagree this opinion.
>
> Admittedly, water is basic and necessary for everyone. It is governments' duties to provide clean and sufficient water to local residents. To achieve the goal, governments should build the infrastucture and facilities, like water cleaning factory and the pipes transporting water. If households cannot have a water supply, it is definitely a faulty of the local government. This demonstrates every home has right to require a water supply.
>
> Nevertheless, having right to require water does not mean that water should be provided free of charge. On the financial side, all facilities require a large amount of money and resources. If water is free for residents, the maintenance is impossible for governments. On the social side, providing water free is unfair for househoulds which use water far less than others. A case in point is North Korea, where people can get water free of charge but they actually pay for it in another yet more expensive way. Therefore, charging money for the water supply is the most sustainable and fair way for all households.
>
> In conclusion, while securing the water supply is the government's job, the benefits are far outweighted by the drawbacks caused by providing water free. Every home should push government to keep the water clean and supplied smoothly, rather than to get it free of charge.

**预估得分：6.5 ~ 7.0**（TR 7.5 / CC 7.5 / LR 6.0 / GRA 6.5）

**Gemini 逐段"原地微调"**

**引言段**
- 拼写：`safty` → **safety**。
- `disagree this opinion` → disagree 是不及物动词 → **disagree with this opinion**。

**Body 1**
- `It is governments' duties` → **It is the government's duty**（单数更地道）。
- `build the infrastucture` → **build infrastructure**（拼写 + 去掉多余定冠词）。
- `water cleaning factory` → 标准表达 **water treatment plants**；`the pipes transporting water` → **water pipes**（简洁地道）。
- `cannot have a water supply` → **cannot access a water supply**。
- `a faulty of the local government` → faulty 是形容词（损坏的），名词"过错"是 **the fault of**。
- `has right to require` → **has the right to demand**（加定冠词 the；"要求"用 demand/request 比 require 自然）。
- `This demonstrates every home...` → 加 **that**。

**Body 2**
- `having right to require water` → **having the right to access water**。
- `the maintenance is impossible for governments` → 略抽象 → 动名词作主语 **maintaining these systems is impossible for governments**。
- `providing water free` → **providing water for free**；`househoulds which` → **households that**（限制性定语从句先行词是物/家庭时 that 更地道）。
- `in another yet more expensive way` → 拗口 → **in another, even more expensive way**。

**结论段**
- `outweighted` → **outweighed**（没有 t）。
- `push government` → **push the government**；`supplied smoothly` → **consistently supplied**（或 safely supplied，更贴合供水语境）。
- `rather than to get it free of charge` → **rather than expect to receive it free of charge**（与 push the government 构成更有力的对比）。

**最终修改结果全文**

> These days, some people believe that the water supply should be free because having clean water is a basic human right. While I acknowledge that governments should secure water safety, I largely disagree with this opinion.
>
> Admittedly, water is basic and necessary for everyone. It is the government's duty to provide clean and sufficient water to local residents. To achieve this goal, governments should build infrastructure and facilities, like water treatment plants and water pipes. If households cannot access a water supply, it is definitely the fault of the local government. This demonstrates that every home has the right to demand a water supply.
>
> Nevertheless, having the right to access water does not mean that water should be provided free of charge. On the financial side, all facilities require a large amount of money and resources. If water is free for residents, maintaining these systems is impossible for governments. On the social side, providing water for free is unfair for households that use water far less than others. A case in point is North Korea, where people can get water free of charge, but they actually pay for it in another, even more expensive way. Therefore, charging money for the water supply is the most sustainable and fair way for all households.
>
> In conclusion, while securing the water supply is the government's job, the benefits are far outweighed by the drawbacks caused by providing water for free. Every home should push the government to keep the water clean and consistently supplied, rather than expect to receive it free of charge.

**三类小漏洞**：① 动词固定搭配的介词（disagree **with**）；② 抽象名词 vs 形容词（safety/safe、fault/faulty）；③ 专业词汇（water treatment plant）。

**后续讨论（不会专业词怎么办）**：我说"water treatment plant 这个词组不会也没办法呀"。Gemini：想不起来完全不扣分，**强行瞎编（water cleaning factory）反而扣 LR**。三个极简替代公式：
1. **用动作描述代替专有名词**：`build facilities to clean and purify water` / `build facilities to make water safe for drinking`。
2. **用万能词 facility / system / station**：water cleaning facilities、water processing stations；recycling plant → recycling facilities；power plant → power generation facilities。
3. 心态：想不起大词时，用最朴素的词把逻辑说通，就是最高级的考场策略。
