# Writing Task 2 题库

> 抽题练 T2 用。**A 区 = 剑桥真题（逐字，权威）**；**B 区 = 网上机经（考生回忆，非官方，仅参考方向）**。
> 题型标签对应 `examples/` 标杆范文：DBV=discuss both views / AD=advantages-disadvantages / AgD=agree-disagree / PN=positive-negative / 2PT=two-part(direct questions) / PS=problem-solution。
> **练法**：高脚手架渐进，不直接 cold（见 [[output_gap]] / `05_path.md`）。一次选 1 题，cold 骨架 → 填充 → 范文对照。

## A. 剑桥真题 16–20（Academic，逐字）

### 剑 16
- **T1 · 2PT** — In some countries, more and more people are becoming interested in finding out about the history of the house or building they live in. What are the reasons for this? How can people research this?
- **T2 · PN** — In their advertising, businesses nowadays usually emphasise that their products are new in some way. Why is this? Do you think it is a positive or negative development?
- **T3 · AgD** — Many manufactured food and drink products contain high levels of sugar, which causes many health problems. Sugary products should be made more expensive to encourage people to consume less sugar. Do you agree or disagree?
- **T4 · AD** — In the future all cars, buses and trucks will be driverless. The only people travelling inside these vehicles will be passengers. Do you think the advantages of driverless vehicles outweigh the disadvantages?

### 剑 17
- **T1 · AD** — It is important for people to take risks, both in their professional lives and their personal lives. Do you think the advantages of taking risks outweigh the disadvantages?
- **T2 · PN** — Some children spend hours every day on their smartphones. Why is this the case? Do you think this is a positive or a negative development?
- **T3 · DBV** — Some people believe that professionals, such as doctors and engineers, should be required to work in the country where they did their training. Others believe they should be free to work in another country if they wish. Discuss both these views and give your own opinion.
- **T4 · PN** — Nowadays, a growing number of people with health problems are trying alternative medicines and treatments instead of visiting their usual doctor. Do you think this is a positive or a negative development?

### 剑 18
- **T1 · AgD** — The most important aim of science should be to improve people's lives. To what extent do you agree or disagree with this statement?
- **T2 · DBV** — Some university students want to learn about other subjects in addition to their main subjects. Others believe it is more important to give all their time and attention to studying for a qualification. Discuss both these views and give your own opinion.
- **T3 · PN** — In many countries around the world, rural people are moving to cities, so the population in the countryside is decreasing. Do you think this is a positive or a negative development?
- **T4 · DBV** — In many countries, people are now living longer than ever before. Some people say an ageing population creates problems for governments. Other people think there are benefits if society has more elderly people. Discuss both these views and give your own opinion.

### 剑 19
- **T1 · DBV** — Some people think that competition at work, at school and in daily life is a good thing. Others believe that we should try to cooperate more, rather than competing against each other. Discuss both these views and give your own opinion.
- **T2 · AgD** — The working week should be shorter and workers should have a longer weekend. To what extent do you agree or disagree?
- **T3 · AgD** — It is important for everyone, including young people, to save money for their future. To what extent do you agree or disagree with this statement?
- **T4 · PN** — In many countries nowadays, consumers can go to a supermarket and buy food produced all over the world. Do you think this is a positive or negative development?

### 剑 20
- **T1 · AgD** — Access to clean water is a basic human right. Therefore, every home should have a water supply that is provided free of charge. Do you agree or disagree?
- **T2 · 2PT** — In many countries, primary and secondary schools close for two months or more in the summer holidays. What is the value of long school holidays? What are the arguments in favour of shorter school holidays?
- **T3 · AD** — Some people have decided to reduce the number of times they fly every year or to stop flying altogether. Do you think the environmental benefits of this development outweigh the disadvantages for individuals and businesses?
- **T4 · 2PT/PN** — Many aspects of the way people dress today are influenced by global fashion trends. How has global fashion become such a strong influence on people's lives? Do you think this is a positive or a negative development?

## B. 网上机经（2025末–2026，考生回忆，仅参考方向，非逐字权威）

> 这些是网上分享的"近期考到"题，措辞可能与真卷有出入；练方向 / 押题用，别当官方真题。

- **AgD** — The government should protect culture. Therefore, some people believe that new buildings should be built in traditional styles. To what extent do you agree or disagree? *(报告 2026-01-24)*
- **PN** — Nowadays, some consumers are less influenced by advertising than in the past. What are the reasons? Is it a positive or negative development? *(报告 2026-01-10)*
- **DBV** — To be successful in sport, some people believe it is essential to have a good coach. Others say that an athlete's natural ability is more important. Discuss both views and give your own opinion. *(报告 2025-12-20)*
- **DBV** — Some people think that historical sites should be completely closed to the public. Others believe that it is important for the public to visit these sites. Discuss both views and give your opinion. *(报告 2026-03-21)*
- **PS** — In every country, there are driving laws to ensure road safety. Some people still break these laws, such as by speeding or using mobile phones while driving. What are the reasons for this? What measures could help solve this problem? *(报告 2026-02-28)*
- **2PT** — Some parents give their children everything that their children ask for or allow them to do whatever they want. Is this good for children? What could be the consequences for these children when they grow up? *(报告 2026-01-31)*
- **DBV** — Some people prefer staying in hotels when travelling, while others prefer renting holiday homes for a short period of time. Discuss both views and give your own opinion. *(报告 2026)*
- **AD** — Many countries are facing problems with transport and accommodation; therefore, many governments are encouraging businesses to move from big cities to rural areas. Do you think the advantages outweigh the disadvantages? *(报告 2026)*
