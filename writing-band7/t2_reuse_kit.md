## Part 1 — 第一段（她的真实打法：**折叠，不列两边**）

### 🌟 头号句型：**折叠式立场句**（cold 4/7，跨题跨段跨 cold 全稳）

```
While / Although [对方观点 or 让步],  I believe that [我的立场].
```
> **她从不单独写「列两边」那一句。** 两边永远压进这一句里。v1 把「列两边 S2 + 立场 S3」拆成两句是错的。

### ⚠️ 备选：**一边倒（零让步）时不要硬折**（7/10 收敛压力测试挖出）

题目若让你**完全同意 / 强烈反对**、你心里根本没有让步，**硬套折叠式会凭空造一个不存在的让步**。这时用直接表态：
```
I completely agree with this opinion.        →  I believe that [立场延伸].
I strongly believe that [立场], because ___.
```
Conclusion 同理不加让步：`In conclusion, I strongly believe that ___.`
> 实证：AgD 音乐篇（cold ~7）全篇零让步，她写 `I completely agree with this opinion.` + `In conclusion, I strongly believe that music is a great tool to unite people.` —— **这是对的，别改。**
> 实证：`While competition can motivate people to work harder, I believe that cooperation is more important…`（DBV竞争）· `While having more elderly people can bring some benefits, I believe that an ageing population poses much greater challenges…`（DBV老龄）· `Although this development brings some potential problems, I believe that the advantages … far outweigh the disadvantages.`（AD无人驾驶）· `Although this trend might bring some challenges to local industries, I believe that it is a largely positive development.`（PN全球化）

### 第一段两个引擎

**引擎 A · 有立场题**（AgD / DBV / AD / PN / 2-Pt-Q2）
```
S1  trend-fact opener（陈述现象，别装深沉）
S2  【折叠式立场句】  ← 上面那一句
```

**引擎 B · 无立场题**（P/S / C/E）
```
S1  trend-fact opener
S2  【预告句】This essay will discuss ___ and ___.
```

## Part 2 — Body（她的真实骨架）

### ① 🌟 主开关（DBV / AD / PN 的 Body T，cold 3/7）
```
On the one hand, ___.        （对方 / 缺点）
On the other hand, ___.      （自己 / 优点）
```
> v1 的 `Those who support [View A] argue that…`（cold 0/2）和 `However, I think the case for [B] is stronger`（cold 0/2、仿写也 0/2）**已删**。
> ⚠️ **主开关只用于"两边对立"的 body**（DBV / AD / PN）。**2-part / 2PT 的 Q1 原因段不用它**——那里用下面④的枚举（`The first major factor is… / Another critical reason is…`）。

### ② 🌟 称重 pivot（**跨题型**，cold 3/7 — v1 错锁成 AD 专用）
```
However / On the other hand,  these [downsides / benefits]  are outweighed by  [X].
```
> 实证：DBV老龄 `However, these benefits are outweighed by the massive problems…` · AD无人驾驶 `On the other hand, these disadvantages are outweighed by the massive benefits…` · PN全球化 `However, these downsides are outweighed by the significant benefits…`

### ③ 让步（cold 1/7 + 仿写高频）
`Admittedly, ___.`
> **让步要限量**（W2-10 教训）：加 `certain` / `excessive` / `to some extent` / `not entirely` + 限定条件，**别承认对方全部主张**。
> 实证：`Admittedly, excessive screen time may, to some extent, weaken certain face-to-face skills.`

### ④ 两理由枚举（原因段，cold 2/7）
`The first major factor is ___.` → `Another critical reason is ___.`
`First, ___ because ___.` → `Second, ___.`

### ⑤ 分面信号（影响/理由段，cold 2/7）
`Physically, ___.` → `Mentally, ___.`
`First of all, ___.` → `Furthermore, ___.`

### ⑥ 题型专用 T 句（**教练真教过、v1 全漏**）

| 场景 | 句子 | 出处 |
|---|---|---|
| **P/S 问题段** | `The most serious problem caused by ___ is ___.` | 教练 06-04 stem，suzy 套用 |
| **P/S 方案段** | `The most effective solution to this would be for [主体] to [do].` | 教练 06-04 stem+范文（session 自标"§2 之外的新句式"）|
| **C/E 原因段** | `The primary cause is that ___.` → `Another key reason is ___.` | 教练 06-11 stem+范文 |
| **C/E 影响段** | `This trend has produced several noticeable effects.` | 教练 06-11 stem+范文 |
| **原因（她自己的）** | `The main cause of ___ is ___.`（**从不填 "the combination of A and B"**）| suzy 3/10 |
| **方案（她自己的）** | `Solving the problem requires [主体] to ___.` | suzy 06-04 cold 顶掉 stem |

### ⑦ E 句（因果）
`This is because ___.` · `As a result, ___.`

### ⑧ Ex 句（举例）— **v1 唯一做对的一条，cold 7/7 ✅**
`For example, ___.` （换花样一次：`For instance, ___.`）
她的变体：`A prime example is [X].`
> 注：`A case in point is X, where Y` 只活在**仿写期**（照着范文抄），**纯 cold 里 0 次**。别背。

### ⑨ 🌟 L 句（回扣）—— **换掉 v1 的 `This shows that`（cold 0/7）**
| 她的真 L（背这些）| 频次 |
|---|---|
| **`These examples demonstrate that ___.`** | 仿写 8+ 处、cold 常用（她的首选）|
| **`Therefore, [重述].`** | 仿写 6/10 + cold 3/7 |
| `This proves that ___.` | cold 1/7 |

### ⑩ 双面壳（**并列段内部**，非万能）
`On the dietary side, ___. On the activity side, ___.`
同壳换皮：`In terms of [X], ___.` · `At the [policy] level, ___.` · `Regarding [X], ___. As for [Y], ___.`
> **实为 3/5 范文在用**（03/04/05），不是 v1 说的 4/5。**用于 P/S·C/E·2-Pt 的并列 body**；DBV/AD 的 body 用上面①的主开关。

## Part 4 — 两个题型补丁（v1 严重出错处）

### 🔴 AD-outweigh（**按她 6/15 driverless 实产重写；v1 那套凭一个 demo 造，且极性混乱**）

```
opener   In the future, it is expected that [趋势].
立场     Although this development brings some potential problems, I believe that
         the advantages of [X] far outweigh the disadvantages.
Body1    On the one hand, [X] have some serious downsides, especially [A] and [B].
Body2    On the other hand, these disadvantages are outweighed by the massive benefits of [Y].
结论     In conclusion, although [X] still have some problems like [A] and [B],
         they are [正面裁决]. Their ability to [好处] makes them a better choice.
```
- ❌ **已删** `Supporters point to A and B, while critics warn about C and D.`（**我凭空造的，0 印证**）
- ❌ **已删** `Admittedly, [X] offers real advantages.` 作 AD Body1（她写 `On the one hand, [X] have some serious downsides`）
- ⚠️ **弊大于利版**：她只 cold 写过"利大于弊"。弊大于利的形状去看下面 PN（同构）。

### 🆕 PN（positive / negative development）—— **第 7 个题型，v1 完全没有**

```
opener   Nowadays, [趋势事实].
立场     Although [让步], I believe that it is a largely positive / negative development.
Body1    Admittedly, [缺点]. （+ Ex + 因果）
Body2    However, these downsides are outweighed by [优点]. （+ First of all… Furthermore…）
结论     In conclusion, while [让步], the advantages are far greater.
```
> 全部 verbatim 来自她 6/15 PN 食物全球化 cold（判分 ~7.5）。

## Part 5 — floor 词汇（从她 **cold** 作文里提，不是范文里提）

| 类 | 词 |
|---|---|
| **名词** | benefit · drawback · downside · effect · impact · trend · factor · reason · problem · pressure · burden |
| **动词** | believe · argue · outweigh · improve · reduce · weaken · struggle to · require · rely on · face（问题）· contribute to |
| **形容词** | serious · clear · massive · significant · wider · broader · mostly · far（greater / safer）|
| **高频短语** | `a large number of` · `as a result` · `in terms of` · `rather than` · `far outweigh` · `not only … but also …` · `not X, but Y` |
| **PN/AD 专用** | `a largely positive / negative development` · `these downsides are outweighed by` · `the advantages are far greater` |

⚠️ **一篇最多 1 次**：`considerable` · `substantial` · `crucial` · `mitigate` · `utilise`
→ 默认降档：`use` > utilise · `reduce` > mitigate · `huge/big` 不必升成 `considerable`
> （审计发现：06-01 教练把她的 `huge` 升成 `considerable`/`substantial` —— 那篇**没过 examiner 门**。**别为了"显得高级"升档。**）

## Part 6 — 硬规则（已修 v1 的 4 个 bug）

1. **立场不摇摆**；禁 `balanced view`。
2. 🔴 **每段必有 L 句**（`Therefore, [重述].` 或 `These examples demonstrate that ___.`）——**这是你 cold 时的头号漏点**。
   > **7/10 收敛实测**：**仿写**时 12 个 body 段**零漏 L**；**纯 cold** 时 **5/7 篇**有 body 段没写 L（2PT ×2 · 儿童手机 ×2 · AD ×1 · AgD ×1 · PN ×1，共 7 处）。
   > → **压力一上来，你第一个丢的就是 L 句。** 修法极便宜：**每个 body 段最后一句，永远写 `Therefore, [重述本段].`**
   > ~~v1 bug（已删）：写"漏 L 是你 W2-1 家族的老毛病"——W2-1 其实是拼写。~~
3. **一段最多 1 个显性衔接词**；同一篇不重复用同一个。
4. 🔴 **collocation > vocabulary**（`conduct research` 不是 `do a research`）—— **W2-9 是她的 LR 头号瓶颈**。
5. **机械错必扫**（占她错误 75%）：拼写 · 单复数 · 不可数（information/advice/equipment）· comma splice · 冠词。
6. **不用 Band 8+ 结构**：cleft · inversion · mixed conditional · reduced relative · passive reporting。
   > ⚠️ **豁免**：固定题型 opener（如 `It is often argued that`）不算——examples/02 用它 examiner PASS 7.0。规则针对**正文里生产性**的 `It is widely believed that…`。
7. **`particularly` 一篇别连出 3 次**（换一处 `especially`）。
8. 🆕 **任何"我给你写的英文"必须过 examiner 门**。审计实证：**所有漂移都出在没过门的那一篇**（06-01），过了门的 06-04 / 06-11 反而干净。**缺门 = 漂移入口。**

## Part 7 — 背诵优先级（第 1 批就够上场）

**第 1 批（背死这 6 条，考场直接用）**
1. 折叠式立场 `While/Although [让步], I believe that [立场].`
2. 主开关 `On the one hand, ___. On the other hand, ___.`
3. 称重 pivot `these [X] are outweighed by [Y].`
4. L 句 `Therefore, [重述].` + `These examples demonstrate that ___.`
5. Ex 句 `For example, ___.` / `For instance, ___.`
6. Conclusion `In conclusion, while/although [让步], [重申].`

**第 2 批**：trend-fact opener（6 型）· 预告句 · `Admittedly` 限量让步 · 两理由枚举 · 分面信号
**第 3 批**：题型专用 T 句（P/S 问题/方案 · C/E 原因/影响）· floor 词 · 硬规则
