# Example 03 — Health C/S（Band 7 范文 + 详细标注）

> **第三篇标杆范文**，演示 C/S（Causes + Solutions，P/S 变体）题型的"原因段 + 方案段"结构。
>
> **目标水平**：Band 7（不是 Band 8、不是 Band 5.5）。仿写时**只能学这个水平**，不要学得太花。
>
> 关键技术点：每段压 **2 个 mini-point**，但每段只有 **1 个段末 L 句**——不要每个 mini-point 都收尾，那样会写散。

---

## 题目

> Obesity is a growing problem worldwide, particularly among children.
>
> **What are the causes of this trend, and what can be done to address it?**

题型：**C/S**（P/S 变体）| 结构：Body 1 = 2 个原因压一段 / Body 2 = 2 个对应方案压一段

---

## 范文（275 词）

### Intro

Childhood obesity is a serious problem in many countries, and a large number of children worldwide are now overweight. This essay will look at the main causes and then suggest ways to deal with them.

### Body 1 — 原因段（2 个原因压一段）

**[T]** The main reason for rising childhood obesity is a change in everyday diet and lifestyle.
**[E]** On the dietary side, processed foods that are high in sugar and fat have become cheaper than fresh produce, so busy families often rely on them. On the activity side, children today spend many more hours on screens, which has cut the time they spend on outdoor play.
**[Ex]** A case in point is the United Kingdom, where studies show that the average child eats far more sugar than recommended and walks less than thirty minutes a day.
**[L]** This shows that the problem is caused not by one factor, but by poor diet combined with low activity.

### Body 2 — 方案段（2 个对应方案压一段）

**[T]** A range of measures can help reduce this trend, ideally combining government policy with school education.
**[E]** At the policy level, governments could put higher taxes on sugary drinks and require clearer nutrition labels, which would push companies to offer healthier options. At the school level, daily exercise should be added to the timetable, and cooking lessons should teach children how to prepare balanced meals.
**[Ex]** For instance, Japan has long included school lunches and food education in its curriculum, and its childhood obesity rate is still one of the lowest in the world.
**[L]** This shows that real progress depends on changing both the food environment around children and the habits they form.

### Conclusion

In conclusion, childhood obesity is mainly the result of unhealthy diets and inactive lifestyles, and solving it requires action on both sides. With sensible rules and better nutrition education, the trend could be reversed in the next generation.

## 4 维度评分检查（examiner verified Band 7.0）

### TR（Task Response）= 7.0

| 检查点 | 怎么做到 |
|--------|---------|
| Addresses both parts (causes + solutions) | Body 1 给原因 + Body 2 给方案；Intro 第 2 句已经预告 |
| Causes / solutions clearly mapped | Body 1 = diet + activity 两个原因 → Body 2 = 对应的 policy (diet) + school PE/cooking (activity) → 一一对应 |
| Main ideas extended + supported | 每段都有完整 TEEL；例子具体（UK 数据 / Japan 校餐）|
| Position consistent | 全文一致认为 obesity 是 lifestyle + diet 双因素，解决要 policy + education 双管齐下 |

**注意**：没讨论方案的潜在阻力（如"sugar tax 会被 industry 反对"）—— 没到 7.5 well-developed。

### CC（Coherence and Cohesion）= 7.0

| 检查点 | 怎么做到 |
|--------|---------|
| 4 段结构 + clear progression | Intro 框架 → Body 1 原因 → Body 2 方案 → Conclusion 综合 |
| Range of cohesive devices | 用了 **6 个不同功能**：The main reason is / On the dietary side...On the activity side / At the policy level...At the school level / A case in point is / For instance / In conclusion |
| 每段一个明确主题 | Body 1 T = "change in everyday diet and lifestyle"；Body 2 T = "measures...combining government policy with school education" |
| 段内 mini-point 衔接清楚 | Body 1 用 "On the dietary side / On the activity side"；Body 2 用 "At the policy level / At the school level" → 段内 2 点对仗 |

### LR（Lexical Resource）= 7.0

**Mid-level 词汇（用对的 collocation）**：
- "rising childhood obesity" / "everyday diet and lifestyle" / "processed foods"
- "fresh produce" / "outdoor play" / "poor diet" / "low activity"
- "sugary drinks" / "nutrition labels" / "balanced meals"
- "school lunches" / "food education" / "food environment"
- "sensible rules" / "reversed"

**没用的 Band 8 高阶**：
- ❌ "increasingly pressing issue in recent years"（改用 "serious problem in many countries"）
- ❌ "significant number"（改用 "large number"）
- ❌ "examine the main causes before suggesting measures"（改用 "look at the main causes and then suggest ways"）
- ❌ "primary reason"（改用 "main reason"）
- ❌ "demonstrates"（改用 "shows"）
- ❌ "mitigate this trend"（改用 "help reduce this trend"）
- ❌ "impose stricter taxes"（改用 "put higher taxes"）
- ❌ "lasting progress" / "tackling"（改用 "real progress" / "solving"）

### GRA（Grammatical Range and Accuracy）= 7.0

**复杂句类型（4 种 = variety）**：

| 类型 | 例 |
|------|---|
| 状语从句（so）| `..., **so** busy families often rely on them...` |
| 关系从句 | `processed foods **that** are high in sugar and fat...` / `the United Kingdom, **where** studies show...` / `the time **they** spend on outdoor play` |
| 名词性从句 | `studies show **that** the average child eats far more sugar...` / `This shows **that** the problem is caused...` |
| 模态条件链 | `governments **could** put higher taxes..., **which would push** companies to offer healthier options` |

**无 Band 8+ 雷区**：
- ❌ 无 cleft（无 "It is X that Y"）
- ❌ 无 inversion（无 "Not only / Never has"）
- ❌ 无 mixed conditional
- ❌ 无 reduced relative clause（用全 relative：`foods that are high in...` 不是 `foods high in...`）
- ❌ 无 passive reporting clause（无 "It is widely believed that..."）

**5.5 级硬伤清点**：0 个 ✅
- 冠词都对（the main reason / the dietary side / the time / the United Kingdom / the food environment）
- 可数不可数都对（progress 不可数 / measures 复数 / education 不可数 / lifestyles 复数）
- 主谓一致都对（foods...have become / children spend / Japan has included）
- 时态稳定（present perfect 表"长期形成的趋势" + present simple 描述常态 + would/could 用于建议）

## 工具集对应（§4.toolkit）

### 衔接词使用（用了 6 个，全在 §4.1 ✅ 列）

| §4.1 类别 | 用的衔接词 | 出现位置 |
|----------|----------|---------|
| 引入第 1 论点 | The main reason is that... | Body 1 T |
| 段内并列对仗 | On the dietary side / On the activity side | Body 1 E（mini-T）|
| 段内并列对仗 | At the policy level / At the school level | Body 2 E（mini-T）|
| 举例 | A case in point is / For instance | Body 1 Ex / Body 2 Ex |
| L 句回扣 | This shows that | Body 1 L / Body 2 L |
| 结论 | In conclusion | Conclusion |

### 句式使用（用了 §4.2 的 5 个核心句式）

| §4.2 句式 | 文章里对应 |
|----------|----------|
| 句式 4（The main reason for ... is ...）| Body 1 T |
| 句式 6（A case in point is X, where Y）| Body 1 Ex |
| 句式 7（This shows that...）| Body 1 L / Body 2 L |
| 句式 8（In conclusion, ...）| Conclusion |
| 句式 5 变体（cause + result chain）| Body 2 E "could put higher taxes..., which would push..." |

### Opener 使用

P/S opener：`[Topic] is a serious problem in many countries.` ✅ 用了（§4.3 校准后的新版；旧版 "has become an increasingly pressing issue in recent years" 是 Band 8）

## 这篇为什么是 Band 7（不是 6.5 也不是 7.5）

| 维度 | 离 6.5 的差距 | 离 7.5 的差距 |
|------|-------------|-------------|
| TR | 因果 + 方案一一对应（diet→policy / activity→school PE）→ **不是 6.5 的 "causes 和 solutions 各说各的"** | 没讨论方案的潜在阻力 → 没到 7.5 的 "fully developed" |
| CC | 段内用 "On the X side / At the X level" 对仗 → **不是 6.5 的 "mini-point 之间没标记"** | 衔接还是 "appropriate" 不到 "seamless" → 没到 7.5 |
| LR | mid-level 词 + topic-specific collocation（"food environment" / "balanced meals" / "school lunches"）→ **不是 6.5 的 "limited"** | 没用 academic intensifier / 没精巧 word formation → 没到 7.5 |
| GRA | 4 种复杂句 + would/could 用得当 + 0 硬伤 → **不是 6.5 的 "some errors"** | 没用 inversion / cleft / 高级条件 → 没到 7.5 |

**这就是 C/S 题型 Band 7 该长的样子——原因和方案一一对应 + 段内 mini-point 标记清楚 + 不在每个 mini-point 后强行收尾**。

## 怎么用这份材料

### Phase 1 — 看 3 遍画结构（15 min）

#### 第 1 遍（2-3 min）— 读懂
一句话总结："作者认为儿童肥胖根因是 diet（廉价加工食品）+ lifestyle（屏幕时间挤掉运动）两块；解决靠 government 政策（糖税 + 营养标签）+ 学校教育（每天体育 + 烹饪课），日本是好例子。"

#### 第 2 遍（5-8 min）— 画结构

```
【Intro】
  Sentence 1 paraphrase: ___（话题严重性）
  Sentence 2 路线图: ___（这篇要讲 causes + solutions）

【Body 1 = 原因段】
  T = ___（一句话概括所有原因的共性）
  E = ___
    mini 1 (饮食侧): ___
    mini 2 (运动侧): ___
  Ex = ___（具体国家/数据）
  L = ___（段末收尾——只 1 个 L 句，不在每个 mini 后单独收）

【Body 2 = 方案段】
  T = ___（一句话概括所有方案的共性）
  E = ___
    mini 1 (政策侧 → 对应饮食): ___
    mini 2 (学校侧 → 对应运动): ___
  Ex = ___
  L = ___

【Conclusion】
  概括原因：___
  概括方案：___
```

#### 第 3 遍（2-3 min）— 合上原文复述
口头说出骨架（30-45 秒）。

### Phase 2 — 仿写同题（25 min）

**仿写时刻意控制**：
- 原因和方案必须**一一对应**（不能 Body 1 讲 A+B 原因，Body 2 给 C+D 方案）
- 每段只在**段末**写 1 个 L 句，不要 mini 1 后写一个、mini 2 后再写一个
- 段内 mini-point 必须用对仗短语标记（"On the X side / On the Y side" 或 "At the X level / At the Y level"）

### Phase 3 — 对比 + 反馈（10 min）

按本文的"4 维度评分检查"清单逐项打勾。
