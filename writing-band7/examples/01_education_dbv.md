# Example 01 — Education DBV（Band 7 范文 + 详细标注）

> 这是**第一篇标杆范文**，定义"Band 7 长什么样"。其他 4 篇范文都按这篇的标准对齐。
>
> **目标水平**：Band 7（不是 Band 8、不是 Band 5.5）。仿写时**只能学这个水平**，不要学得太花。

---

## 题目

> Some people think that schools should teach children academic subjects that will be beneficial to their future careers. Others think it is more important for schools to teach a wide range of subjects, including arts and music.
>
> **Discuss both views and give your own opinion.**

题型：**DBV** | 立场：largely agree with View B（broader curriculum）

---

## 范文（285 词）

### Intro

What schools should teach children is often debated. Some people argue that schools should focus on academic subjects that prepare students for their careers. Others believe schools should also teach a wide range of subjects, including arts and music. I largely agree with the second view.

### Body 1 — 公正陈述对方观点（View A：career-focused）

**[T]** Those who support a career-focused curriculum argue that schools have a clear duty to help students find good jobs.
**[E]** As the job market becomes more competitive, students who leave school without practical skills in subjects like maths, science or computing may struggle to find stable work.
**[Ex]** For example, in countries such as Singapore, schools have placed a strong focus on STEM subjects, and many students go on to well-paid jobs in technology and finance.
**[L]** This shows why some people feel that schools should not produce students who are well-rounded but cannot find work.

### Body 2 — 我的立场（View B：broader curriculum）

**[T]** However, I think the case for a wider curriculum is stronger.
**[E]** Education is not only about getting a job; it is also about helping young people develop as whole people, and subjects like art and music play an important role here.
**[Ex]** For instance, learning music can improve a student's memory and ability to focus, while art helps them express ideas that words cannot easily capture. These skills help in both creative careers and many academic jobs that now value problem-solving and imagination.
**[L]** This shows that a wider curriculum is not just a nice extra, but something that supports both personal growth and long-term career success.

### Conclusion

In conclusion, while preparing students for work is clearly an important job for schools, focusing only on career-related subjects would mean missing the bigger purpose of education. A school system that combines academic subjects with arts and music is more likely to produce students who can succeed both at work and in their wider lives.

## 4 维度评分检查（examiner verified Band 7.0）

### TR（Task Response）= 7.0

| 检查点 | 怎么做到 |
|--------|---------|
| Addresses all parts | "Discuss both views" → Body 1 公正讲对方，Body 2 讲自己；"give your opinion" → Intro 第 4 句已表态 |
| Clear position throughout | Intro "I largely agree with the second view" → Body 2 T "I think the case is stronger" → Conclusion 重申"more likely to produce..." |
| Main ideas extended + supported | 每个 Body 段完整 TEEL；例子具体（Singapore STEM / music + art 双例子）|

**注意**：B1 的论证深度恰到好处——再深一层（加 trade-off）就升 7.5。

### CC（Coherence and Cohesion）= 7.0

| 检查点 | 怎么做到 |
|--------|---------|
| Clear progression throughout | 4 段结构 + 段间 logical flow（Intro 框架 → Body 1 对方 → Body 2 转折 + 自己 → Conclusion 综合）|
| Range of cohesive devices | 4 个不同功能：However / For example / For instance / In conclusion |
| Clear central topic per paragraph | Body 1 T = "schools have a clear duty"；Body 2 T = "wider curriculum is stronger" |

**注意**：未达 Band 8 seamless 标准（段间靠 explicit 衔接词，不是 idea progression）——这正是 Band 7。

### LR（Lexical Resource）= 7.0

**Mid-level 词汇（用对的 collocation）**：
- "career-focused curriculum" / "competitive job market" / "stable work"
- "placed a strong focus on" / "well-paid jobs"
- "well-rounded but cannot find work" — antithesis 但用了 mid-level 词（不是 unemployable Band 8 那种）
- "develop as whole people" / "play an important role"
- "long-term career success" / "missing the bigger purpose"

**没用的 Band 8 高阶**：
- ❌ "of considerable debate"（改用 "is often debated"）
- ❌ "the role of schools in preparing young people for adulthood"（改用 "What schools should teach children"）
- ❌ "instrumental" / "indispensable" / "fiercely"

### GRA（Grammatical Range and Accuracy）= 7.0

**复杂句类型（4 种 = variety）**：

| 类型 | 例 |
|------|---|
| 名词性 that-clause | `Those who support... argue **that** schools have a clear duty...` |
| 关系从句 | `students **who** leave school without practical skills...` / `academic jobs **that** now value problem-solving...` |
| 状语从句 | `**As** the job market becomes more competitive, ...` / `**while** art helps them express...` |
| 分号并列复合 | `Education is not only about getting a job; it is also about helping...` |

**无 Band 8+ 雷区**：
- ❌ 无 cleft（无 "It is X that Y"）
- ❌ 无 inversion（无 "Not only / Never has"）
- ❌ 无 mixed conditional
- ❌ 无 reduced relative clause
- ❌ 无 passive reporting clause

**5.5 级硬伤清点**：0 个 ✅

## 工具集对应（§4.toolkit）

### 衔接词使用（4 个，全在 §4.1 ✅ 列）

| §4.1 类别 | 用的衔接词 | 出现位置 |
|----------|----------|---------|
| 转折 | However | Body 2 T 句开头 |
| 举例 | For example / For instance | Body 1 Ex / Body 2 Ex |
| 结论 | In conclusion | Conclusion 开头 |

**注意**：没用 Furthermore / Moreover / Crucially 等——**少而精**，4 个不同功能足够 Band 7。

### Opener 使用

DBV opener：`What schools should teach children is often debated.` — 这是 §4.3 校准后的新版（旧版 "is a subject of considerable debate" 是 Band 8）

### 立场表达

Pattern: `I largely agree with the second view.` — 直接表态，不推迟。

## 这篇为什么是 Band 7（不是 6.5 也不是 7.5）

| 维度 | 离 6.5 的差距 | 离 7.5 的差距 |
|------|-------------|-------------|
| TR | 立场全文一致 + 完整 TEEL → **不是 6.5 的 "立场糊"** | B1 没承认 trade-off / Conc 没提 implication → 没到 7.5 的 "well-developed" |
| CC | 4 个不同功能的衔接 → **不是 6.5 的 "faulty or mechanical"** | 段间靠 explicit 衔接词不是 idea progression → 没到 7.5 的 "manages all aspects" |
| LR | mid-level 词 + collocation 都对 → **不是 6.5 的 "attempts with inaccuracy"** | 没用 skilful uncommon item / 没用 high academic intensifier → 没到 7.5 |
| GRA | 4 种复杂句 + 0 硬伤 → **不是 6.5 的 "some errors"** | 没用 cleft / inversion / mixed conditional → 没到 7.5 |

**这就是 Band 7 该长的样子——不绝望地华丽，但每个该有的都有，没有任何明显的扣分点**。

## 怎么用这份材料

### Phase 1 — 仿写前的"看 3 遍画结构"（15 min）

#### 第 1 遍（2-3 min）— 读懂
通读全文，能用中文一句话总结："作者承认 career-focused 教育合理（培养就业能力），但更偏 broader curriculum，理由是教育不只为就业、art/music 培养的能力反而对所谓 academic 工作也有用。"

#### 第 2 遍（5-8 min）— 画结构
拿张纸，按下面格式填：

```
【Intro】
  Sentence 1 paraphrase: ___
  Sentence 2-3 框架两边: ___
  Sentence 4 立场: ___

【Body 1 = View A 公正陈述】
  T = ___（一句话概括对方观点）
  E = ___（解释他们为啥这么想）
  Ex = ___（具体例子是什么）
  L = ___（怎么收尾的）

【Body 2 = View B 我的观点】
  T = ___
  E = ___
  Ex = ___
  L = ___

【Conclusion】
  承认对方：___
  重申立场（换说法）：___
```

#### 第 3 遍（2-3 min）— 合上原文复述
关掉文件，**口头**说出这篇的骨架（30-45 秒能说完）。说不出 → 回 §2 重看。

### Phase 2 — 仿写同题（25 min）

同样的题目，**自己写**：
- 不看范文
- 用你自己的话填进这个骨架
- 限时 25 min
- `04_toolkit.md` keep open 当 cheat sheet

### Phase 3 — 对比 + 反馈（10 min）

你的版本 vs 范文，按本文的"4 维度评分检查"清单逐项打勾：
- TR 5 项
- CC 3 项
- LR 3 项
- GRA 3 项

漏掉的项 → 进 `log/sessions/YYYY-MM-DD.md` + `log/errors.md`
