# Example 02 — Technology A/D（Band 7 范文 + 详细标注）

> **第二篇标杆范文**，演示 A/D 题型的"让步 + 主反驳"结构。注意 Intro 第 2 句的 explicit position statement——A/D 题最容易丢分的地方就是立场糊。
>
> **目标水平**：Band 7（不是 Band 8、不是 Band 5.5）。仿写时**只能学这个水平**，不要学得太花。

---

## 题目

> Some people believe that the increasing use of computers and mobile phones for communication has had a negative effect on young people's social skills.
>
> **To what extent do you agree or disagree?**

题型：**A/D** | 立场：largely disagree（数字沟通整体利大于弊）

---

## 范文（280 词）

### Intro

It is often argued that the growing use of computers and mobile phones is harming young people's social skills. While I understand this concern, I largely disagree, since digital tools have widened rather than narrowed the way young people interact.

### Body 1 — 让步段（承认对方部分合理）

**[T]** Admittedly, spending too much time on screens can weaken some face-to-face habits.
**[E]** When teenagers spend hours messaging friends or scrolling through social apps, they have fewer chances to read body language, manage awkward pauses, or hold long spoken conversations. As a result, some young users do appear less confident when they need to speak in person.
**[Ex]** A case in point is South Korea, where teachers have reported that students often struggle to give classroom presentations despite being highly fluent online.
**[L]** This shows that the concern is not entirely wrong, particularly when device use replaces offline contact rather than adding to it.

### Body 2 — 主反驳（我的立场）

**[T]** However, the wider picture suggests that digital communication has improved young people's social abilities far more than it has damaged them.
**[E]** Messaging apps and video calls let teenagers keep in touch with friends across cities, join groups based on shared interests, and practise foreign languages with real speakers. Importantly, these platforms also give shy individuals a low-pressure space to express their views in writing first.
**[Ex]** For instance, many young people who joined online study groups during the pandemic later said they felt more comfortable speaking up in physical classrooms.
**[L]** This shows that, if used in a balanced way, technology expands rather than replaces traditional social skills.

### Conclusion

In conclusion, while heavy use of screens can affect certain parts of face-to-face interaction, the wider effect of digital communication on young people has been positive. If they are used wisely, computers and phones add to social life rather than weaken it.

## 4 维度评分检查（examiner verified Band 7.0）

### TR（Task Response）= 7.0

| 检查点 | 怎么做到 |
|--------|---------|
| Clear position from Intro | Intro 第 2 句 "I largely disagree" → 直接表态，不糊 |
| Position consistent throughout | Intro disagree → Body 1 让步但 L 句限定 → Body 2 主反驳 → Conclusion 重申 "wider effect...positive" |
| Both sides addressed (A/D 不是只讲一边) | Body 1 给对方让步 + 具体例（South Korea）→ Body 2 再反驳 |
| Main ideas extended + supported | 每段完整 TEEL；例子具体（South Korea 老师反馈 / online study groups 后续在 classroom 说话变自在）|

**注意**：没把对立面"reframe to nuance"——再深一层就升 7.5。

### CC（Coherence and Cohesion）= 7.0

| 检查点 | 怎么做到 |
|--------|---------|
| Clear progression throughout | 4 段结构 + 段间 logical flow（Intro 表态 → Body 1 让步 → Body 2 反驳 → Conclusion 综合）|
| Range of cohesive devices | 用了 **6 个不同功能**：While（让步）/ Admittedly / As a result / However / Importantly / In conclusion |
| Clear central topic per paragraph | Body 1 T = "spending too much time on screens can weaken..."；Body 2 T = "improved...far more than damaged" |

**注意**：段间靠 explicit 衔接词（However）不是 idea progression——正是 Band 7 标志。

### LR（Lexical Resource）= 7.0

**Mid-level 词汇（用对的 collocation）**：
- "growing use" / "face-to-face habits" / "awkward pauses"
- "speak in person" / "give classroom presentations" / "highly fluent online"
- "low-pressure space" / "shy individuals" / "shared interests"
- "wider picture" / "used in a balanced way"

**没用的 Band 8 高阶**：
- ❌ "eroding the social skills of young people"（改用 "harming young people's social skills"）
- ❌ "Owing to this shift in daily routine"（改用 "As a result"）
- ❌ "Crucially"（改用 "Importantly"）
- ❌ "maintain friendships across cities"（改用 "keep in touch with friends across cities"）
- ❌ "interest-based communities"（改用 "groups based on shared interests"）
- ❌ "enrich social life"（改用 "add to social life"）

### GRA（Grammatical Range and Accuracy）= 7.0

**复杂句类型（4 种 = variety）**：

| 类型 | 例 |
|------|---|
| 名词性 that-clause | `the wider picture suggests **that** digital communication has improved...` / `This shows **that**...` |
| 关系从句 | `South Korea, **where** teachers have reported that students often struggle...` / `many young people **who** joined online study groups...` |
| 状语从句 | `**When** teenagers spend hours messaging..., they have fewer chances...` / `**since** digital tools have widened...` / `**despite** being highly fluent online` / `**if** used in a balanced way` |
| 复合并列 | `messaging friends **or** scrolling through social apps` / `read body language, manage awkward pauses, **or** hold long spoken conversations` |

**无 Band 8+ 雷区**：
- ❌ 无 cleft（无 "It is X that Y"）
- ❌ 无 inversion（无 "Not only / Never has"）
- ❌ 无 mixed conditional
- ❌ 无 reduced relative clause（用全 relative：`young people who joined` 不是 `young people joining`）
- ❌ 无 passive reporting clause（无 "It is widely believed that..."）

**5.5 级硬伤清点**：0 个 ✅
- 冠词都对（the growing use / the way / the concern / the wider picture / the wider effect）
- 可数不可数都对（screen time 不可数 / chances 可数 / contact 不可数 / confidence 不可数）
- 主谓一致都对（technology has / messaging apps...let / platforms...give）
- 时态稳定（present perfect 表"已造成的影响" + present simple 描述常态）

## 工具集对应（§4.toolkit）

### 衔接词使用（用了 6 个，全在 §4.1 ✅ 列）

| §4.1 类别 | 用的衔接词 | 出现位置 |
|----------|----------|---------|
| 让步 | While I understand / Admittedly | Intro 第 2 句 / Body 1 T |
| 因果 | As a result | Body 1 E |
| 转折 | However | Body 2 T 句开头 |
| 强调 | Importantly | Body 2 E |
| 举例 | A case in point is / For instance | Body 1 Ex / Body 2 Ex |
| 结论 | In conclusion | Conclusion 开头 |

### 句式使用（用了 §4.2 的 4 个核心句式）

| §4.2 句式 | 文章里对应 |
|----------|----------|
| 句式 1（It is often argued that...）| Intro 第 1 句 |
| 句式 2（While I understand X, I Y because...）| Intro 第 2 句 |
| 句式 6（A case in point is X, where Y）| Body 1 Ex 句 |
| 句式 7（This shows that...）| Body 1 L / Body 2 L |
| 句式 8（In conclusion, while...）| Conclusion 第 1 句 |

### Opener 使用

A/D opener：`It is often argued that ___.` ✅ 用了（这是 §4.3 校准后保留的 Band 7 版本）

### 立场表达

Pattern: `I largely disagree, since...` — 直接表态加一句简短理由。

## 这篇为什么是 Band 7（不是 6.5 也不是 7.5）

| 维度 | 离 6.5 的差距 | 离 7.5 的差距 |
|------|-------------|-------------|
| TR | 立场从 Intro 第 2 句就明确，让步段也没倒戈 → **不是 6.5 的 "position unclear"** | 没量化反驳（没有"X% 青少年..."）/ 没 reframe to nuance → 没到 7.5 的 "well-developed" |
| CC | 6 个不同功能衔接 + 段内段间节奏自然 → **不是 6.5 的 "mechanical"** | 段间靠 explicit 衔接词（However）不是 idea progression → 没到 7.5 的 "manages all aspects" |
| LR | mid-level 词 + collocation 都对 → **不是 6.5 的 "attempts with inaccuracy"** | 没用 skilful uncommon item / 没用 high academic intensifier → 没到 7.5 |
| GRA | 4 种复杂句 + 0 硬伤 → **不是 6.5 的 "some errors"** | 没用 cleft / inversion / mixed conditional → 没到 7.5 |

**这就是 A/D 题型 Band 7 该长的样子——立场清楚 + 让步段不变心 + 主反驳有具体支持，没有任何明显扣分点**。

## 怎么用这份材料

### Phase 1 — 仿写前的"看 3 遍画结构"（15 min）

#### 第 1 遍（2-3 min）— 读懂
一句话总结："作者承认数字沟通确实削弱了部分面对面能力（如 South Korea 学生做 presentation），但整体上 messaging / video call 反而让 friendships 跨地理延伸 + 给 shy 学生表达空间，所以 largely disagree。"

#### 第 2 遍（5-8 min）— 画结构

```
【Intro】
  Sentence 1 paraphrase: ___（重写题目主张）
  Sentence 2 立场: ___（一句话表态 + 简短理由）

【Body 1 = 让步段】
  T = ___（承认对方哪一点合理）
  E = ___（为什么这个让步成立）
  Ex = ___（具体例子）
  L = ___（收尾时把让步限定住，不让步太多）

【Body 2 = 主反驳】
  T = ___（我的立场是什么）
  E = ___（数字沟通的具体好处）
  Ex = ___（具体例子）
  L = ___（回扣立场）

【Conclusion】
  承认对方一小步：___
  重申自己立场（换说法）：___
```

#### 第 3 遍（2-3 min）— 合上原文复述
口头说出骨架（30-45 秒）。说不出 → 回去重看。

### Phase 2 — 仿写同题（25 min）

同样的题目，**自己写**：
- 不看范文
- 用自己的话填进骨架
- 限时 25 min

**仿写时刻意控制**：
- Intro 第 2 句必须有显性立场词（agree / disagree / partly agree）
- Body 1 让步段的 L 句必须有"限定"语气（"not entirely wrong / particularly when / used in a balanced way"）
- Body 2 必须出现明确转折词（However / Nevertheless）

### Phase 3 — 对比 + 反馈（10 min）

按本文的"4 维度评分检查"清单逐项打勾。漏掉的项 → 进 `log/sessions/YYYY-MM-DD.md` + `log/errors.md`。
