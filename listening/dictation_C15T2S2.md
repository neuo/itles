## 段落 [3-4]（Q11 答案句）

**原文：**
> It's been a feature of the city for well over a century, and has been the responsibility of the city council for most of that time. What perhaps isn't so well known is the origin of the park: unlike many public parks that started in private ownership, as the garden of a large house, for instance, Minster was some waste land, which people living nearby started planting with flowers in 1892.

**听写：**
> It's a bit xx several centuries, and be Responsibility of our city council for most of that time. What's not well known is the origin of the park. Unlike many public parks, it started at private ownership. As the garden of a large house, minster was some waster land. Rich people living nearly started to plant flowers in 1892

**逐句对照：**

| 标记 | 问题 |
|------|------|
| 🔴 | "been a feature of the city for well over a century" → "a bit xx several centuries"（核心结构丢失） |
| 🔴 | **"unlike many public parks that started in private ownership"** → 整个关系从句漏听，"that started in" 是 Q11 答案对比信号的关键逻辑链 |
| 🟡 | "has been the responsibility" → "be Responsibility"（时态+大小写） |
| 🟡 | "waste land" → "waster land"（词形） |
| 🔴 | "people living nearby" → "Rich people living nearly"（幻听 rich；nearby→nearly 近形混淆） |
| 🟡 | "started planting with flowers" → "started to plant flowers"（介词 with 漏） |

**Paraphrase 评估（Q11）：**
对比结构核心：unlike many parks [that did X], Minster [did Y]
- X = started in private ownership（私家花园）
- Y = was waste land（荒地，公共起源）
→ 答案 C：it was originally public land
suzy 在听写中把 unlike 后的从句 "that started in private ownership" 归到了 Minster 身上，语义反转。

## 段落 [10]（Q13 答案句）

**原文：**
> Soon after this the First World War broke out, in 1914, and most of the park was dug up and planted with vegetables, which were sold locally. At one stage the army considered taking it over for troop exercises and got as far as contacting the city council, then decided the park was too small to be of use.

**听写：**
> Soon after this, the first World War broke out. In 1914, and most of the park was used to plant vegetables, which was sold locally. One day, the army took over of it for troop exercise, xxx. decided that the park was too small to be used

**逐句对照：**

| 标记 | 问题 |
|------|------|
| 🔴 | **"considered taking it over"** → "took over"（意义完全反转：considered = 想了但没做；took over = 做了）→ 这是 Q13 答错的根因 |
| 🟡 | "dug up and planted" → "used to plant"（丢失 dug up 动作，结构不同） |
| 🟡 | "which were sold" → "which was sold"（vegetables 复数，需 were） |
| 🔴 | "got as far as contacting the city council" 整句丢失 |

**Paraphrase 评估（Q13）：**
关键双动词信号：`considered V-ing... then decided` = 考虑了但最终否决
→ 答案 A：The army decided not to use the park
→ "considered" 听成 "took over"，决策链完全断裂
