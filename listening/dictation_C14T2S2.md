### Part A：伊丽莎白一世访问段（Q11-12 附近）

**原文**: However, when **Queen Elizabeth I** announced that she was going to visit the castle in **1576**, it was beginning to look a bit **run down**, and it was decided **that** rather than repair the guest **rooms**, **they'd** make a new house **for her** out of wood next to the main hall.

**你写**: However, when xxx first announced that she was going to visit the castle in xxx, It was beginning to look a bit xx down. and it was decided rather than repair the guest room, xx make a new house out of wood next to the main hall

- 🔴 `Queen Elizabeth I` → xxx — **伊丽莎白一世**读作 Queen Elizabeth **the First**，你捕捉到 first，但 "Queen Elizabeth the" 整块丢了。人名/王室称号要整体识别
- 🔴 `1576` → xxx — 年份还是没抓到（fifteen seventy-six）
- 🔴 `run down` → xx down — **生词/短语**：run down = 破败、失修、年久失修
- 🟡 `that` 漏 — "it was decided **that** rather than..."，弱读
- 🟡 `rooms` → room — 复数漏
- 🔴 `they'd` → xx — they would 缩写，/ðeɪd/，表"将要（做）"
- 🔴 `for her` 漏 — 整个介词短语没听到。新房子是**给她**建的

**原文**: She stayed there for four nights and apparently it was very **luxurious**, but unfortunately it was destroyed a few years later by fire.

**你写**: She stayed there for four nights, and apparently it was very xxx, but unfortunately it was destroyed a few years later by a fire

### Part B：地图题开始（Q16 起点 + Q17 Bow & arrow）

**原文**: The **starting point's** quite near the main entrance - walk straight down the path **until** you get **to** the south gate, and it's just there.

**你写**: The start point is quite near the main entrance. Walk straight down the path and you get the South Gate, and it's just there.

- 🟡 `starting point` → start point — ing 词尾没听出
- 🔴 `until` → and — 连接词听错，**until**（直到）= 一直走直到...
- 🔴 `to` 漏 — get **to** the south gate

---

**原文**: Don't go **on to** the north gate - there's **no** way **up** from there.

**你写**: Don't go x the north gate, there is not way from there

- 🔴 `on to` → x — 连读 /ˈɒntu/，"上到、继续到"
- 🟡 `no way` → not way — no 和 not 发音混
- 🔴 `up` 漏 — "no way **up**"（没有上去的路），这个 up 指上城墙

---

**原文**: There'll shortly be a show in which you can see archers **displaying** **their** skill with **a** bow and **arrow**.

**你写**: There'll shortly be a show, in which you can see archers display the skill with bow and **narrow** ❗❗

- 🟡 `displaying` → display — -ing 分词漏
- 🔴 `their` → the — 所有格弱读被替换
- 🟡 `a` 漏 — a bow and arrow
- ❗❗❗ **`arrow` → `narrow`** — **致命幻听**！
  - **arrow** /ˈærəʊ/ = 箭（名词，配 bow 弓箭）
  - **narrow** /ˈnærəʊ/ = 狭窄的（形容词）
  - 只差一个 n，但意思完全不同
  - **bow and arrow** 是固定搭配 = 弓箭。记住这个词组，arrow 不会再听成 narrow

### Part B 续：Q17 Bow & arrow 位置描述 ❗地图题核心

**原文**: The quickest way to get there is to take the first left after the main entrance and follow the path **past** the bridge, then you'll see it in front of you **at** the end.

**你写**: The quickest way to get there is to take the first left after the main entrance and follow the path **pass** the bridge. Then you'll see it in front of you xx the end

- ❗ **`past` → `pass`** — **致命**！你做题时就错了，精听还是听成 pass
  - **past** /pɑːst/ = 经过（介词）
  - **pass** /pɑːs/ = 通过（动词）
  - 两者发音几乎一样，但 past 后面直接跟名词（past the bridge = 经过桥），pass 是动词要配合句子结构
  - 看到 "follow the path ___ the bridge" 这种结构，99% 是 past
- 🔴 `at` 漏 — "in front of you **at** the end"

### Part B 续：Q18 Hunting birds ❗方向介词重灾区

**原文**: If you go **from** the main entrance in the direction of the south gate, **but** turn right before you **get there** instead of **going through it**, you'll see it on your right **past** the first tower.

**你写**: If you go **for** the main entrance in the direction of the South Gate, **and** turn right before you **getting** instead of xxx. you'll see it on your right **pass** the first tower

- ❗ **`from` → `for`** — **致命**！方向完全反了
  - **from the main entrance** = 从主入口出发
  - **for the main entrance** = 为了/朝向主入口（语意不通）
  - /frəm/ vs /fɔː/，弱读时易混，但语境下只能是 from
- 🔴 `but` → and — 连接词听错。**but turn right** = 但是（到之前就）右转，暗示不是直走穿过
- 🟡 `before you get there` → before you getting — 语法错（before 后接谓语动词 get）+ 漏 there
- 🔴 `going through it` 整块漏 — "instead of **going through it**"（而不是穿过它）。through = 穿过
- ❗ `past` → pass — 又一次！

## 致命错误汇总（最优先复习）

| # | 错误 | 正确 | 为什么致命 |
|---|------|------|-----------|
| 1 | narrow | **arrow** | bow and arrow 是固定搭配（弓箭），narrow 在这句根本不通 |
| 2 | pass | **past** | 地图题方向词，past = 经过（位置参照），pass 是动词，题干看到 "path ___ the bridge" 就是 past |
| 3 | for | **from** | 方向完全反！from X = 从 X 出发，for X 语意不通 |
| 4 | 漏 first | **the first left** | 地图题转第几个路口是关键信息，漏 first 就转错路口 |
| 5 | 漏 up | no way **up** | up = 上去（指上城墙），漏了会理解成"没有路" |

## 生词 / 短语（加入 app 精听卡点词）

- **run down**（短语）= 破败、年久失修 — "a bit run down"
- **luxurious** /lʌɡˈʒʊəriəs/ = 豪华的 — "it was very luxurious"
- **arrow** /ˈærəʊ/ = 箭 — "a bow and arrow"（对比 narrow）
- **past**（介词）= 经过 — "past the bridge / past the first tower"

## 听力卡点（只练听辨，不需要拼写）

- Queen Elizabeth **I** → 发音是 "Queen Elizabeth the First"（王室称号 I/II/III 要读 the first/second/third）
- **1576** → fifteen seventy-six（四位数年份分两半读）
- **they'd** /ðeɪd/ → they would 缩写
- **on to** /ˈɒntu/ → 连读，"继续到"
- **from** /frəm/ vs **for** /fɔː/ → 弱读时几乎一样，靠语境判断

## 语法点（口语练习也要注意）

- **before you get there**（before 后接一般现在时，不是 -ing）
- **displaying their skill**（-ing 分词 + 所有格 their）
- **traditional** dancing（形容词修饰名词，不是 tradition）
- **on the stage**（固定搭配）
