### Q15 Habib 段

**原文**: The songs take the listeners back to **their** youth, and for a little while **they can forget the difficulties that they face now**.

**你写**: The songs take the listeners back to **the** youth, and for a little while, they can forget the difficulties they face now

- 🟡 `their youth` → the youth — 所有格 their 弱读漏，写成定冠词 the。"take sb back to **their** youth" = 带他们回到**自己的**青春
- 🟡 `difficulties that they face` → difficulties they face — 关系代词 that 省略（语义不变，可接受）
- ✅ 其余全对，这句是全篇质量最高的一句 ✨

### Q16 Consuela 段（⚠️ 失分最严重的段落）

**原文**: Our volunteer **Consuela** is an amazing woman. She has difficulty walking herself, but she doesn't let **that** stop her. She **helps** a couple of people **with** similar difficulties, **who had almost stopped walking altogether**.

**你写**: **Out** volunteer X is an amazing woman. She has difficulty walking herself, but she doesn't let **it** stop her. She **help** a couple of people **having** similar difficulties. **They almost stop walking all together**

- 🟡 `Our` → Out — /aʊə/ vs /aʊt/ 弱读时混，看语境应是 Our
- 🔴 `Consuela` — 人名漏（正常，S2 匹配题人名不是考点）
- 🟡 `let that stop her` → let it stop her — that/it 混淆（不影响语义）
- 🟡 `She helps` → She help — **第三人称单数 -s 漏听**（老问题，本段出现3次）
- 🟡 `with similar difficulties` → having similar difficulties — 介词 with 听成 having
- ❗❗ `who had almost stopped walking altogether` → They almost stop walking all together — **致命 3 连错**：
  1. 🔴 `who had` 整块没听到 — 关系从句 who + 过去完成时 had。"They" 是脑补的主语
  2. 🟡 `stopped` → stop — 过去式词尾 -ped 漏
  3. ❗❗ `altogether` → all together — **发音几乎一样，意思完全不同**：
     - **altogether** /ˌɔːltəˈɡeðə/（副词）= 完全地、彻底地。"stopped walking altogether" = 彻底不走路了
     - **all together** /ɔːl təˈɡeðə/（短语）= 一起、同时。"stopped walking all together" = 一起停止走路（语意不通）
     - 这是 S3 听力里反复考的陷阱词，记住：**altogether = 一个词 = 副词 = 完全地**

**原文**: **By using herself as an example, Consuela encourages them to walk more and more.**

**你写**: By using xx as an example xxx walk more and more

- 🔴 `herself` 漏 — 反身代词弱读
- 🔴 `Consuela encourages them to` 整段漏 — 核心动词 **encourages**（鼓励）丢失。这就是 Q16 答案 A（overcome physical difficulties）的**关键信号词**：encourage to walk more = 鼓励克服困难，对应"overcome"
- 💡 **匹配题救援思路**：即使没听到 encourage，只要听到前面那句的 "difficulty walking herself" + "walk more and more"，就能推出 A（overcome physical difficulties）

### Q17 Minh 段

**原文**: Minh **visits a young man who lives alone and can't leave his home on his own, so he hardly ever saw anyone**.

**你写**: XX is a young man, who live alone. And XXX won So he hardy ever x anyone

- ❗ `Minh visits a young man` → XX is a young man — **致命听错动词结构**：
  - 原文：Minh **visits** 一个 young man（Minh 是主语，young man 是宾语 = 被帮助者）
  - 你理解：Minh **is** 一个 young man（Minh 就是 young man）
  - 整段匹配题语境就会错：志愿者 Minh 不是被帮助者，他访问的人才是
  - **visit** /ˈvɪzɪt/（及物动词）在人物介绍句里是关键结构词
- 🟡 `who lives` → who live — 又是第三人称 -s
- 🔴 `can't leave his home on his own` → won — **整个短语全丢**。"on his own" = 独自（= by himself），"can't leave ... on his own" = 无法独自出门。这句是 Q17 答案 E（escape isolation）的核心信号
- 🟡 `hardy` → **hardly** — 拼写错：hardly（几乎不）是 hard + ly 去 e 加 ly 的副词。没有 "hardy" 这个拼法（hardy 是"耐寒的"，无关）
- 🔴 `ever saw` → ever x — saw（过去式）漏

---

**原文**: But together they go out **to the** cinema, or **to** see friends the young man **hadn't been able to visit** for a long time.

**你写**: But together they go out to cinema, or see friends the young man **hasn't visited** for a long time

- 🟡 `to the cinema` → to cinema — 冠词 the 漏（go to **the** cinema 固定搭配）
- 🟡 `or to see friends` → or see friends — 不定式 to 漏
- ❗ `hadn't been able to visit` → hasn't visited — **时态 + 情态结构全错**：
  - 原文：had**n't** been able to visit（过去完成 + be able to = 那时一直都没能去探望）
  - 你听：has**n't** visited（现在完成 = 一直都没去探望）
  - "been able to" 表**能力**（想去但去不了），visited 只表"没去"。这里强调的是"因为 can't leave home 所以没能力去"，hadn't been able to 是关键
  - 5-6 个音节连读一个词都没抓到，这是本段最长的薄弱点

### Q19 Alexei 段

**原文**: **Alexei is a volunteer** with a family that faces a number of difficulties.

**你写**: X is volunteer with a family that faces a number of difficulties

- 🔴 `Alexei` — 人名漏（OK）
- 🟡 `is a volunteer` → is volunteer — 冠词 a 漏
- ✅ `faces a number of difficulties` — 对了！

**原文**: By **calmly talking over** possible solutions with family members, **he's helping** them to realise that they aren't helpless, and that they **can do something themselves to improve their situation**. **This has been great for their self-esteem**.

**你写**: By xx possible solutions With family members, **he helps** them to realise that they aren't helpless and they **can do some themself to improve the situations**. **It's been great to build self-esteem**

- 🔴 `calmly talking over` 整块漏 — **calmly**（冷静地，副词）+ **talking over**（讨论，短语动词）连续两个弱读词+短语动词。"talk over" = 讨论、商议，不是单纯的 talk
- 🟡 `he's helping` → he helps — 缩写 he's (he is) 漏，-ing 进行时漏
- ❗ `can do something themselves` → can do some themself — **双重错误**：
  - **something** → some（漏 -thing 词尾）
  - **themselves** → themself（复数反身代词拼错，themself 不是标准词）
  - 这里 "**themselves**" 是 Q19 答案 D（solve problems **independently**）的最强信号词。漏了 themselves，就丢了"独立"这个抽象概念的来源
- 🟡 `to improve their situation` → to improve the situations — their → the（所有格漏）+ situation → situations（单复数错，应为单数）
- 🔴 `This has been great for their self-esteem` → It's been great to build self-esteem — **主语+介词全错**：
  - 原文：This has been great **for their self-esteem**（对他们的自尊很好）
  - 你写：It's been great **to build self-esteem**（用来建立自尊）
  - for + their（介词+所有格）漏 → 脑补成 to build（不定式+动词）。**for 介词结构**是 S2 匹配题的高频盲区

### Q20 Juba 段

**原文**: And the last volunteer I'll mention, **though there are plenty more**, is Juba. She volunteers with a **teenage** girl with learning **difficulties**, **who** wasn't very good at **talking to** other people.

**你写**: And the last volunteer I'll mention xxx plenty more is XX, she volunteers with XX girl with learning difficulty, **it** wasn't good at **talk** XX people

- 🔴 `though there are` 漏 — 插入语 "though there are plenty more"（虽然还有更多）。though 是让步连词，提示"这里只是举例"
- 🔴 `teenage` 漏 — "a **teenage** girl"（一个青少年女孩）。teenage 是人物关键定语
- 🟡 `learning difficulty` → learning **difficulties** — 单复数：learning difficulties 是固定表达（学习障碍），永远用复数
- ❗ `who wasn't` → it wasn't — **关系代词 who 听成 it**，致命：
  - who wasn't = 指代前面的 girl（这个女孩不善于...）
  - it wasn't = 指代某个事物（这件事不...，语意不通）
  - 关系从句的 who 在人物描述里出现必是指人，语境决定
- 🟡 `good at talking to` → good at talk — 介词后动名词 -ing 漏 + to 漏

## 致命错误汇总（最优先复习）

| # | 错误 | 正确 | 为什么致命 |
|---|------|------|-----------|
| 1 | all together | **altogether** | 副词"完全地" vs 短语"一起"，只差空格但语义反转 |
| 2 | X is a young man | **Minh visits a young man** | visits → is，主谓结构完全听错，整段匹配语境就错 |
| 3 | hasn't visited | **hadn't been able to visit** | 时态+情态结构漏，"能力受限"的语义消失 |
| 4 | some themself | **something themselves** | 漏 themselves 就丢了 Q19 答案 "independently" 的最强信号 |
| 5 | it wasn't | **who wasn't** | 关系代词听错，指代对象从"人"变"事" |
| 6 | to build self-esteem | **for their self-esteem** | for 介词结构漏，脑补成不定式 |

## 生词 / 结构（加入 app 精听卡点词）

- **altogether** /ˌɔːltəˈɡeðə/（副词）= 完全地、彻底地 — "stopped walking altogether"（对比 all together = 一起）
- **hardly** /ˈhɑːdli/（副词）= 几乎不 — "hardly ever saw anyone"。拼写：hard + ly（不是 hardy）
- **talk over** /tɔːk ˈəʊvə/（短语）= 讨论、商议 — "calmly talking over possible solutions"
- **themselves** /ðəmˈselvz/（反身代词）= 他们自己 — 复数形式是 themselves，不是 themself
- **teenage** /ˈtiːneɪdʒ/（形容词）= 青少年的 — "a teenage girl"
- **encourage** /ɪnˈkʌrɪdʒ/（动词）= 鼓励 — "encourages them to walk more"
- **calmly** /ˈkɑːmli/（副词）= 冷静地 — l 不发音

## 听力结构卡点（只练听辨）

- **who had** + 过去分词 → 关系从句+过去完成时，弱读 /hu həd/
- **hadn't been able to V** → 过去完成+能力动词，5 音节连读
- **on his own** → = by himself，独自
- **'s helping**（he's helping）→ 's 缩写 + ing 进行时
- **there are**（though **there are** plenty more）→ 弱读 /ðərə/
- **teenage** → 两音节重读在 teen，要和 teen（n.）区分
