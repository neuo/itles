# C14 T3 S2 — Volunteering（志愿者组织介绍）

> 题型：多选 Q11-12 + 多选 Q13-14 + 匹配题 Q15-20
> 场景：Lucy Crittenden（Director of Operations）介绍志愿者能做什么、志愿者自己能获益什么、6 个具体志愿者案例

---

## Part A：开场 + 定义（Q11-Q14 相关）

「1」Good morning. My name's Lucy Crittenden, and I'm the Director of Operations for an organisation that arranges volunteering in this part of the country. I'm hoping I can persuade one or two of you to become volunteers yourselves.

「2」Let me start by briefly explaining what we mean by volunteering.

「3」Volunteers are teenagers and adults who choose to spend some time, unpaid, helping other people in some way. Most volunteers devote two or three hours to this every week, while a few do much more.

「4」The people they help may have physical or behavioural difficulties, for example.

「5」Volunteers can do all sorts of things, depending on their own abilities and interests. If they're supporting a family that's struggling, for example, they may be able to give them tips on cooking, or recommend how to plan their budget or how to shop sensibly on their income.

「6」**They might even do some painting or wallpapering**, perhaps alongside any members of the family who are able to do it. **Or even do some babysitting so that parents can go out for a while.** ← Q11-12 答案 A (decorating = painting/wallpapering) + E (childcare = babysitting)

「7」The benefit from volunteering isn't only for the people being helped. Volunteers also gain from it: they're using their skills to cope with somebody's mental or physical ill health, and **volunteering may be a valuable element of their CV when they're applying for jobs: employers usually look favourably on someone who's given up time to help others.** ← Q13-14 答案 E (boosting employment prospects)

「8」Significantly, **most volunteers feel that what they're doing gives them a purpose in their lives.** And in my opinion, they're lucky in that respect, as many people don't have that feeling. ← Q13-14 答案 B (having a sense of purpose)

## Part B：6 个具体案例（Q15-Q20 匹配题）

「9」Now I'd like to tell you what some of our volunteers have said about what they do, to give you an idea of the range of ways in which they can help people.

「10」Habib supports an elderly lady who's beginning to show signs of dementia. Once a week they, along with other elderly people, go to the local community centre, where a group of people come in and sing.

「11」**The songs take the listeners back to their youth, and for a little while they can forget the difficulties that they face now.** ← Q15 Habib 答案 **F** (remember past times = take back to their youth)

「12」Our volunteer Consuela is an amazing woman. **She has difficulty walking herself**, but she doesn't let that stop her. She helps a couple of people with similar difficulties, who had almost stopped walking altogether.

「13」**By using herself as an example, Consuela encourages them to walk more and more.** ← Q16 Consuela 答案 **A** (overcome physical difficulties = difficulty walking → walk more)

「14」Minh visits a young man who lives alone and can't leave his home on his own, **so he hardly ever saw anyone.**

「15」**But together they go out to the cinema, or to see friends the young man hadn't been able to visit for a long time.** ← Q17 Minh 答案 **E** (escape isolation = hardly saw anyone → go out to see friends)

「16」Tanya visits an elderly woman once a week. When the woman found out that Tanya is a professional dressmaker, she got interested.

「17」Tanya showed her some soft toys she'd made, and the woman decided to try it herself.

「18」**And now she really enjoys it, and spends hours making toys.**

「19」They're not perhaps up to Tanya's standard yet, but she gains a lot of pleasure from doing it. ← Q18 Tanya 答案 **G** (start a new hobby = decided to try it herself + spends hours making toys)

「20」Alexei is a volunteer with a family that faces a number of difficulties.

「21」By calmly talking over possible solutions with family members, he's helping them to realise **that they aren't helpless, and that they can do something themselves to improve their situation.** This has been great for their self-esteem. ← Q19 Alexei 答案 **D** (solve problems independently = do something themselves to improve)

「22」And the last volunteer I'll mention, though there are plenty more, is Juba. She volunteers with a teenage girl with learning difficulties, **who wasn't very good at talking to other people**.

「23」Juba's worked very patiently with her, and **now the girl is far better at expressing herself, and understanding other people.** ← Q20 Juba 答案 **C** (improve communication skills = better at talking → expressing herself + understanding others)

## 核心同义替换（S2 匹配题必背）

| 原文具体描述 | = 选项抽象概念 |
|-------------|---------------|
| painting or wallpapering | **decorating**（Q11）|
| babysitting | **childcare**（Q12）|
| valuable element of CV / look favourably on | **boost employment prospects**（Q14）|
| feel what they're doing gives them a purpose | **having a sense of purpose**（Q13）|
| take back to their youth / forget difficulties now | **remember past times**（Q15）|
| difficulty walking → encourages to walk more | **overcome physical difficulties**（Q16）|
| lives alone / hardly ever saw anyone → go out | **escape isolation**（Q17）|
| decided to try it herself / spends hours making | **start a new hobby**（Q18）|
| do something themselves to improve situation | **solve problems independently**（Q19）|
| wasn't good at talking → better at expressing/understanding | **improve communication skills**（Q20）|

---

## 干扰项陷阱

- **Q16 Consuela 被选成 D**（solve problems independently）：因为 "she encourages them" 被误读成 "她让他们自己..."。实际 D 是 Q19 Alexei 的答案（"themselves" + "improve their situation"）
- **Q20 Juba 被选成 B**（rediscover skills not used for a long time）：B 是纯干扰项，没对应任何人。Tanya 的案例看似"重拾技能"，但"decided to try it herself"明确说是新尝试
- **选项 B（rediscover skills）不对应任何人名**——这是 6→7 匹配题的典型手法，至少 1 个选项是纯 distractor
