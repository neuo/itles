# C14 T2 S2 — Branley Castle 城堡导游词

> 题型：单选 Q11-15 + 地图题 Q16-20
> 场景：导游在巴士上介绍 Branley Castle，先讲历史和参观内容，再讲地图位置

---

## Part A：历史介绍 + 参观说明（Q11-Q15）

「1」We'll be arriving at Branley Castle in about five minutes, but before we get there I'll give you a little information about the castle and what our visit will include.

「2」So in fact there's been a castle on this site for over eleven hundred years.

「3」The first building was a fort constructed in 914 AD for defence against Danish invaders by King Alfred the Great's daughter, who ruled England at the time.

「4」In the following century, after the Normans conquered England, the land was given to a nobleman called Richard de Vere, and he built a castle there that stayed in the de Vere family for over four hundred years.

「5」However, when Queen Elizabeth I announced that she was going to visit the castle in 1576, it was beginning to look a bit run down, and **it was decided that rather than repair the guest rooms, they'd make a new house for her out of wood next to the main hall.** ← Q11 答案 B

「6」She stayed there for four nights and apparently it was very luxurious, but unfortunately it was destroyed a few years later by fire.

「7」In the seventeenth century the castle belonged to the wealthy Fenys family, who enlarged it and made it more comfortable.

「8」However, by 1982 the Fenys family could no longer afford to maintain the castle, even though they received government support, and they put it on the market.

「9」**It was eventually taken over by a company who owned a number of amusement parks**, but when we get there I think you'll see that they've managed to retain the original atmosphere of the castle. ← Q12 答案 C（amusement parks = entertainment company）

「10」When you go inside, **you'll find that in the state rooms there are life-like moving wax models dressed in costumes of different periods in the past, which even carry on conversations together.** ← Q13 答案 C（wax models moving and talking）

「11」As well as that, in every room there are booklets giving information about what the room was used for and the history of the objects and furniture it contains.

「12」The castle parks quite extensive. At one time sheep were kept there, and in the nineteenth century the owners had a little zoo with animals like rabbits and even a baby elephant.

「13」**Nowadays the old zoo buildings are used for public displays of paintings and sculpture.** ← Q14 答案 B（art exhibition）

「14」The park also has some beautiful trees, though the oldest of all, which dated back 800 years, was sadly blown down in 1987.

「15」Now, you're free to wander around on your own until 4:30, but then at the end of our visit we'll all meet together at the bottom of the Great Staircase.

「16」We'll then go on to the long gallery, where there's a wonderful collection of photographs showing the family who owned the castle a hundred years ago having tea and cakes in the conservatory - **and we'll then take you to the same place where afternoon tea will be served to you.** ← Q15 答案 A（afternoon tea in conservatory）

## Part B：地图描述（Q16-Q20）

「17」Now if you can take a look at your plans you'll see Branley Castle has four towers, joined together by a high wall, with the river on two sides.

「18」Don't miss seeing the Great Hall. That's near the river in the main tower, the biggest one, which was extended and redesigned in the eighteenth century.

「19」If you want to get a good view of the whole castle, you can walk around the walls.

「20」**The starting point's quite near the main entrance - walk straight down the path until you get to the south gate, and it's just there.** ← Q16 起点 Walking the walls

「21」Don't go on to the north gate - there's no way up from there.

「22」There'll shortly be a show in which you can see archers displaying their skill with a bow and arrow.

「23」**The quickest way to get there is to take the first left after the main entrance and follow the path past the bridge, then you'll see it in front of you at the end.** ← Q17 Bow & arrow（"past the bridge" = 走过桥旁边，不是过桥）

「24」If you like animals there's also a display of hunting birds - falcons and eagles and so on.

「25」**If you go from the main entrance in the direction of the south gate, but turn right before you get there instead of going through it, you'll see it on your right past the first tower.** ← Q18 Hunting birds（朝南门方向，到之前右转，过第一个塔在右边）

「26」At 3 pm there's a short performance of traditional dancing on the outdoor stage.

「27」**That's right at the other side of the castle from the entrance, and over the bridge.** ← Q19 Dancing（在城堡对面 + 过桥，"over the bridge" = 真的要过桥）

「28」It's about ten minutes' walk or so.

「29」And finally the shop. **It's actually inside one of the towers but the way in is from the outside.**

「30」**Just take the first left after the main entrance, go down the path and take the first right. It's got some lovely gifts and souvenirs.** ← Q20 Shop

## 核心卡点：方向介词区分（地图题命脉）

| 短语 | 含义 | 地图含义 |
|------|------|----------|
| **past the bridge** | 走过桥旁边/路过桥 | 位置参照，**不一定过桥** |
| **over the bridge** / **across the bridge** | 真的过桥到对面 | 位置改变 |
| **before the bridge** | 还没到桥 | 位置在桥之前 |
| **after the bridge** | 过了桥之后 | 位置在桥之后 |
| **first left** | 第一个左转 | 转向 |
| **turn right before X** | 到 X 之前右转 | 转向 + 位置 |
| **in front of you at the end** | 路尽头正前方 | 终点 |
| **on your right past X** | 过 X 后在你右边 | 终点 |
| **the other side from X** | X 的对面 | 位置 |
