# C15 T1 S2 — Matthews Island Holidays（马恩岛度假推介）

> 题型：Q11-14 多选（ABC）+ Q15-20 表格填空
> 场景：Erica Matthews 介绍家族旅行公司的 6 天 Isle of Man 套餐

---

「1」Good morning, my name is Erica Matthews, and I'm the owner of Matthews Island Holidays, a company set up by my parents.

「2」Thank you for coming to this presentation, in which I hope to interest you in what we have to offer.

「3」We're a small family-run company, and we believe in the importance of the personal touch, so **we don't aim to compete with other companies on the number of customers**. ← Q11 C 干扰（不是 more customers）

「4」**What we do is build on our many years' experience—more than almost any other rail holiday company**—to ensure we provide perfect holidays in a small number of destinations, which we've got to know extremely well. ← Q11 A 答案（在业最久）

「5」I'll start with our six-day Isle of Man Holiday.

「6」This is a fascinating island in the Irish Sea, with Wales to the south, England to the east, Scotland to the north and Northern Ireland to the west.

「7」**Our holiday starts in Heysham, where your tour manager will meet you**, ← Q12 B 答案 Heysham

「8」then you'll travel by ferry to the Isle of Man. **Some people prefer to fly from Luton instead, and another popular option is to go by train to Liverpool and take a ferry from there.** ← Q12 A/C 干扰（Luton 是飞 / Liverpool 是另一种方式）

「9」You have five nights in the hotel, and the price covers five breakfasts and dinners, **and lunch on the three days when there are organized trips**: day four is free, and most people have lunch in the cafe or restaurant in Douglass. ← Q13 three

「10」The price of the holiday includes the ferry to the Isle of Man, all travel on the island, the hotel and the meals I've mentioned.

「11」Incidentally, we try to make booking our holidays as simple and fair as possible, so unlike with many companies, **the price is the same whether you book six months in advance or at the last minute**, and **there's no supplement for single rooms in hotels**. ← Q14 A/B 干扰（预定时间和单间不加钱）

「12」**If you make a booking then need to change the start date, for example because of illness, you're welcome to change to an alternative date or a different tour, for a small administrative fee.** ← Q14 C 答案 transferring to another date 要收费

「13」OK, so what does the holiday consist of?

「14」Well, on Day 1, you'll arrive in time for a short introduction by your tour manager, followed by dinner in the hotel.

「15」**The dining room looks out at the river**, close to where it flows into the harbour, and there's usually plenty of activity going on. ← Q15 river

「16」On Day 2, you'll take the coach to the small town of Peel, on the way calling in at the Tynwald Exhibition.

「17」The Isle of Man isn't part of the United Kingdom, and it has its own parliament, called Tynwald.

「18」**It's claimed that this is the world's oldest parliament that's still functioning, and that it dates back to 979**. ← Q16 979 干扰年份

「19」**However, the earliest surviving reference to it is from 1422, so perhaps it isn't quite as old as it claims!** ← Q16 1422 答案 ⚠️ "not 979" 信号 + However 转折

「20」Day 3 we have a trip to the mountain Snaefell. This begins with a leisurely ride along the promenade in Douglass in a horse-drawn tram.

「21」Then you board an electric train which takes you to the fishing village of Laxey.

「22」**From there, it's an 8-kilometre ride in the Snaefell Mountain Railway to the top**. ← Q17 top

「23」Lunch will be in the cafe, giving you spectacular views of the island.

「24」Day 4 is free for you to explore, **using the pass which we'll give you, so you won't have to pay for travel on local transport, or for entrance to the island's heritage sites**. ← Q18 pass

「25」Or you might just want to take it easy in Douglass and perhaps do a little light shopping. ← Q18 shopping 干扰

「26」The last full day, Day 5, **is for some people the highlight of the holiday with the ride on a steam railway, from Douglas to Port Erin**. ← Q19 steam

「27」After some time to explore, a coach will take you to the headland that overlooks the Calf of Man, a small island just off the coast.

「28」From there you continue to Castletown, **which used to be the capital of the Isle of Man**, and its medieval castle. ← Q20 capital

「29」And on Day 6, it's back to the ferry—or the airport, if you flew to the island—and time to go home.

「30」Now I'd like to tell you ...

## 答案速查

| Q | 答案 | 陷阱类型 |
|---|------|----------|
| 11 | A longer in business | 否定干扰（don't compete on customers）+ 正向信号（more than any other company） |
| 12 | B Heysham | **"tour manager meet" 信号直接定位**；Luton/Liverpool 是"另一种方式"干扰 |
| 13 | A three | 直给 |
| 14 | C transferring to another date | 排除法：A/B 都不加钱，C 有 administrative fee |
| 15 | river | has view of = looks out at；景观名词 |
| 16 | 1422 | ⚠️ "not 979" 强信号 + However 转折；年份分两半读 fifteen-twenty-two |
| 17 | top | train to the ___ of Snaefell = 位置名词 |
| 18 | pass | **第一信息句**定位；shopping 是 Or 引导的干扰 |
| 19 | steam | railway 修饰词；别写 electric（Day 3 用的是 electric） |
| 20 | capital | former ___ = 城市角色名词 |
