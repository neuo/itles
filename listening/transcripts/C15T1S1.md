# C15 T1 S1 — Bankside Recruitment Agency（推荐中介）

> 题型：Notes 笔记填空
> 场景：William 向 Amber 介绍 Bankside 招聘中介（临时文员工作）

---

「1」Hello, William! This is Amber. You said to phone if I wanted to get more information about the job agency you mentioned? Is now a good time?

「2」Hi, Amber. Yes, fine. The agency I was talking about is called Bankside. They're based in Dockland. I can tell you the address now. **497 Eastside.**

「3」Ok thanks. So is there anyone in particular I should speak to there?

「4」The agent I always deal with is called **Becky Jamieson**. ← Q1

「5」Let me write that down. Becky......

「6」Jamieson. J-A-M-I-E-S-O-N.

「7」Do you have her direct line?

「8」Yes, it's in my contact somewhere. Right. Here we are....**07866 510333**. I wouldn't call her until the **afternoon** if I were you. She's always really busy in the morning, trying to fill last-minute vacancies. She's really helpful and friendly, so I'm sure it will be worth getting in touch with her for an informal chat. ← Q2 afternoon

「9」It's mainly clerical and admin jobs they deal with, isn't it?

「10」That's right. I know you're hoping to find a full-time job in the media eventually. But Becky mostly recruits temporary staff for the finance sector, which will look good on your CV and generally pays better, too.

「11」Yeah......I'm just a bit worried because I don't have much office experience.

「12」I wouldn't worry. They'll probably start you as a receptionist or something like that. So what's important for that kind of job **isn't so much having business skills** or knowing lots of different computer systems. **It's communication that really matters.** So you'd be fine there. And you'll pick up office skills really quickly on the job. It's not that complicated. ← Q3 ⚠️ 否定转折陷阱：isn't so much = 不是这个；communication 才是答案

「13」Okay, good. So how long do people generally need temporary staff for? **It would be great if I could get something lasting at least a month.**

「14」That shouldn't be too difficult. But **you're more likely to be offered something for a week at first**, which might get extended. It's unusual to be sent somewhere for just a day or two. ← Q4 ⚠️ 主客观陷阱：Amber 希望 month（愿望）≠ William 说 week（事实）

「15」Right. I've heard the pay isn't too bad, better than working in a shop or a restaurant.

「16」Oh, yes. Definitely. **The hourly rate is about 10 pounds**, 11 if you are lucky. ← Q5 £10

「17」That's pretty good. I was only expecting to get eight or nine pounds an hour.

「18」Do you want me to tell you anything about the registration process?

「19」Yes, please. I know you have to have an interview.

「20」The interview usually takes about an hour and you should arrange that about a week in advance.

「21」**I suppose I should dress smartly if it's for office work. I can probably borrow a suit from Mum.** ← Q6 ⚠️ 审题语法：wear a ___，a 后面要可数名词 → suit（smart 是形容词不合语法）

「22」Good idea! It's better to look too smart than too casual.

「23」Will I need to bring copies of my exam certificates or anything like that?

「24」No. They don't need to see those, I don't think......

「25」What about my passport?

「26」Oh yes, they will ask to see that. ← Q7 passport

「27」Okay...

「28」I wouldn't get stressed about the interview though, it's just a chance for them to build relationship with you so they can try and match you to a job which you'll like. So **there are questions about personality** that they always ask candidates, fairly basic ones and they probably won't ask anything too difficult, like what your plans are for the future. ← Q8 personality

「29」(Chuckle.....) Hope not.

「30」Anyway there are lots of benefits to using an agency. For example, the interview will be useful because **they'll give you feedback on your performance** so you can improve next time. ← Q9 feedback

「31」And I'll have access to jobs which aren't advertised.

「32」Exactly. Most temporary jobs aren't advertised.

「33」And I expect finding a temporary job this way **takes a lot less time**. It's much easier than ringing up individual companies. ← Q10 time

「34」Yes, indeed. Well, I think I covered...
