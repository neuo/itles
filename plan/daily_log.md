### 口语

#### 第一步：通读 v6 第一二章

6种开篇类型 + 对应题型：

| 类型 | 适用题型 | 核心思路 |
|------|---------|---------|
| 1. 直答事实 | What/Where/When/Who/How | 不废话，直接给答案 |
| 2. 频率程度 | Do you often / How often | 先给频率判断，再解释 |
| 3. 偏好态度 | Do you like / prefer / enjoy | 表达程度，不只Yes/No |
| 4. 经历回应 | Have you ever / Did you | 回答Yes/No后立即进场景 |
| 5. 变化对比 | difference / change / compared to | 先给判断强度，再展开 |
| 6. 观点意见 | Do you think / Should / Is it important | 表达观点但保持口语化 |

#### 第二步：手抄 + 录音的句子

我抄的版本（含录音用的完整句）：

**类型1 直答事实：**
- I'd say my favourite would be ___. / That would be ___, actually.

**类型2 频率程度：**
- Yeah, fairly often. / Almost always, actually.
- Not as often as I'd like to.
- Hardly ever. / Almost never.

**类型3 偏好态度：**
- ___, without question / without a doubt.
- Yeah, quite a lot / quite into ___.
- I enjoy ___, but ___.

**类型4 经历回应：**
- No, never.
- Once or twice.

**类型5 变化对比：**
- Huge differences.
- The biggest difference is ___.
- Oh, completely [different].

**类型6 观点意见：**
- I'd say so.
- Depends on ___.
- I think ___.

#### 录音时发现的问题

- **actually** 拼成了 actualy（少了一个 l）
- **quite** 拼成了 quit（少了结尾 e）—— quit 是"辞职"，quite 是"相当"，意思完全不同
- 第三遍不看原文时非常卡顿，一个一个词往外吐 → 这是正常的，因为句子还没变成"整块"存进记忆。目前处于"逐词回忆"阶段，需要通过重复变成"骨架记忆"

**骨架记忆技巧**：不要记12个单词，记1个固定块 + 1个随便发挥的尾巴。比如 "Not as often as I'd like to" 是固定块，后面接什么都行。

#### 第三步：自测10题结果

| # | 问题 | 我的回答 | 结果 | 反应速度 | 备注 |
|---|------|---------|------|---------|------|
| 1 | What's your favourite type of food? | home-cooked stir-fries | ✅ | 秒答 | 类型1 |
| 2 | Do you often go to the cinema? | Not as often as I'd like to | ✅ | 快 | 类型2 |
| 3 | Do you think children should learn to cook? | Depends on age and personal interest | ✅ | 慢，想了很久 | 类型6。一开始想说"不"但找不到否定模板 |
| 4 | Have you ever been to a museum? | Just recently, actually | ✅ | 快 | 类型4 |
| 5 | Do you prefer shopping online or in stores? | Online for electronic things... but physical store for clothes | ⚠️ | 卡住 | 类型3分场景句式。想说"快消品/标准化产品"但词汇量不够卡住了 |
| 6 | Has your hometown changed much? | Yeah, huge differences | ✅ | 秒答 | 类型5 |

| 10 | How often do you use public transport? | Yeah, almost every day, actually | ✅ | 自然 | 类型2 |

**总结：10题中7题秒答，3题有问题**

#### 口语今日3个教训（碎片时间想这些）

1. **类型选择看时态**：过去时（did/were/was）→ 优先类型4经历回应，不要跳到 "it depends"
2. **词汇卡壳时用 "things like + 举例" 救场**：想不起"标准化产品"就说 "things like phones, books, that kind of stuff"——简单词说完比高级词卡住强一百倍
3. **观点题的否定不需要专门模板**：用 "Depends on..." 或 "I'd say so, but..." 就能表达保留意见

#### 录音纠正

我说的：I'd say my **favorite rate** would be the home cooked stir fries.
应该是：I'd say my **favourite** would be home-cooked stir-fries.
- "rate" 是多出来的，嘴巴带出来的
- 断句技巧：I'd say / my favourite / would be / home-cooked stir-fries（四段微停）

#### S2 裸测：剑14 T1 S2（6/10）

场景：HR经理给新学徒做入职介绍 —— 第一周建议 + 导师职责 + 公司政策

**逐题分析：**

| 题号 | 正确答案 | 我的答案 | 对错 | 错因分析 |
|------|---------|---------|------|---------|
| Q11 | A (get to know colleagues) | A | ✅ | 音频 "make an effort to talk to as many people as possible" = get to know colleagues |
| Q12 | C (ask lots of questions) | E (enjoy new challenges) | ❌ | **同义替换**。音频说 "check with someone if you're not sure what to do" = ask lots of questions。我选E是因为听到 "the first week may be quite challenging" 就联想到了 enjoy new challenges —— 这是**干扰项** |
| Q13 | B (making career plans) | D (making a weekly timetable) | ❌ | **同义替换+张冠李戴**。音频说 "discuss your more long-term ambitions" = making career plans。我选D是因为听到 "meet with them on a weekly basis"，但那是见面频率，不是帮你做周计划 |
| Q14 | E (reviewing progress) | E | ✅ | 音频 "asking you about what goals you've achieved so far" = reviewing progress |
| Q15 | B (some restrictions) | B | ✅ | 音频 "you'll have unlimited access... but please don't use it for personal use" |
| Q16 | B (some restrictions) | B | ✅ | 音频 "some of you will be eligible... but it will depend on..." |
| Q17 | C (against the rules) | B (some restrictions) | ❌ | **程度判断错误**。音频说 "we ask that you **don't book any** holidays until after..."。"don't book any" = 完全禁止 = against the rules，不是 "some restrictions" |
| Q18 | A (encouraged) | A | ✅ | 音频 "it can be a valuable experience - so we advise you to take it up" |
| Q19 | A (encouraged) | C (against the rules) | ❌ | **信息错位**。音频说 "the only restriction for shoes is on **high heels**" + "Comfortable shoes like **trainers are preferable**"。被限制的是高跟鞋，trainers 是被鼓励的。我把两者搞反了 |
| Q20 | C (against the rules) | C | ✅ | 音频 "we've decided to introduce a no packed lunch policy" |

**S2 关键教训：**

- **同义替换是S2最大杀手**：Q12和Q13都是因为没反应过来替换词
- **"张冠李戴"陷阱**：音频提到某个词不代表它就是答案——要看它在句子里的角色。"weekly basis"修饰的是见面频率，不是导师帮你做的事
- **程度判断要精确**：don't book any（禁止）≠ some restrictions（有限制）。听到 "don't" + "any" 连用，基本就是 against the rules
- **信息错位要注意主语**：被限制的是什么？被鼓励的是什么？不能搞混

### 口语：P1 自由练习（6题语法纠正）

#### Q1: What do you usually do in your free time?
我说的：Before going bed, I always browse articles...
纠正：Before going **to** bed / **read** articles（browse 是随便翻网页）

#### Q2: Do you think children spend too much time on screens?
我说的：Smartphones has occupied... which make them disconnect
纠正：Smartphones **have**（复数）/ which **makes**（指代整件事，单数）

#### Q3: Do you prefer to travel alone or with others?
我说的：there were so much laughter / the moment my son grow
纠正：there **was** so much（laughter 不可数）/ my son **grows**（第三人称 s）
技巧："it's regrettable" 太书面 → **I'd hate to miss him growing up**

#### Q4: Has the way people shop changed much?
我说的：people do online shop significantly more frequently
纠正：people **shop online** way more（online shop 是名词"网店"，不是动词）

#### Q5: Do you enjoy your job?
我说的：If i'm be honest / a bit of tired / I'v not quitted for making a living rather than for career development
纠正：**to be honest** / **a bit tired**（不加 of）/ **I haven't quit because I need to make a living, not because I enjoy it.**
更口语版：**I stay for the paycheck, not the passion.**
