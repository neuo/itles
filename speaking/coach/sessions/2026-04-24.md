## Q1 (Q31): Do you walk a lot?

**suzy 原句：**
not during the week. On weekdays, i'm constantly stuck at a desk all day. But on weekends, quite a bit, I usually take my son to explore playgrounds or wander around chengdu neighborhoods. he likes to stop and look at everything, bugs, flowers, and construction sites. so walks are always longer than planned. I'm not sporty man, so walk is my main exercise.

**诊断：**
1. 🔴 "I'm not sporty man" → **"I'm not a sporty person"** — 冠词漏，每次 "I'm not a ___" 必须有 a
2. 🔴 "walk is my main exercise" → **"walking is my main exercise"** — 主语名词需用动名词形式
3. 🟡 结尾无收尾 — `I wouldn't trade it` 未产出（count 1，完美场合）

**亮点：**
- ✅ "wander around Chengdu neighbourhoods" — 精准动词，有画面感
- ✅ "bugs, flowers, and construction sites" — 具体细节列举，有 texture
- ✅ "longer than planned" — 自然压缩

**精修版：**
> Not during the week — I'm constantly stuck at a desk. But on weekends, quite a bit. I usually take my son to explore playgrounds or wander around Chengdu neighbourhoods. He likes to stop and look at everything: bugs, flowers, construction sites. So walks are always longer than planned. I'm not a sporty person, so walking is my main exercise. I wouldn't trade it, though.

**复产：**
> "I'm not a sporty man, so walk is my main exercise. I wouldn't trade it, though"
- "walk" 仍未改成 walking
- ✅ `I wouldn't trade it` — count **1→2** 🎓

## Q2 (Q14): Did you have any hobbies when you were a child?

**suzy 原句：**
yeah, football. back in primary school, i always played football after school every day. I even played goalkeeper once and sprained my wrist. as result I had got a ban from football for several months. good time, though. I'm stucked in a desk all day now

**诊断：**
1. 🔴 "I'm stucked in a desk" → **"I'm stuck behind a desk"** — 双重错：stucked 不存在（stuck 已是过去分词）+ in → behind
2. 🔴 "good time" → **"Good times"** — 固定复数表达
3. 🟡 "I had got a ban" → **"I got banned for months"** — 过于书面/笨重，直接说 got banned

**亮点：**
- ✅ "sprained my wrist" — 精准词汇
- ✅ 尝试用 inventory 表达（stuck behind a desk）——方向对，介词滑了

**精修版：**
> Yeah, football. Back in primary school I played after school almost every day. I even played goalkeeper once and sprained my wrist — got banned for months as a result. Good times, though. I'm stuck behind a desk all day now.

**复产：**
> "I'm stuck behind a desk all day now. not really a sports guy anymore"
- ✅ `stuck behind a desk` — count **0→1** 🎓

## Q4 (Q34): Where would you like to take a long walk if you had the chance?

3. 🟡 "it gave a peaceful feeling" → **"there's something peaceful about it"** — Pattern 3 静态描述

**亮点：**
- ✅ "wander around the Kyoto neighbourhoods" — 精准动词再次出现

**精修版：**
> Kyoto, definitely. I've been three times, and every time there's something peaceful about it — the way classic architecture sits alongside modern life. I'd love to just wander for a whole day, in and out of temples and shrines, with no particular destination.
