## Q1 (Q55): Have you seen an unforgettable and beautiful view or scenery?

**suzy 原句：**
last time i went to Mountain emei. the sun rising was spectacular. I stood on the top of the mountain, watching the valley in mist, frog peeling away as the sun rose, colours shifting from grey to gold and to green. I stood still for 20 minute. it was really one time where i could completely unwind

**诊断：**
1. 🔴 "frog peeling away" → "fog lifting" — "frog"是青蛙，应为 fog（雾）。搭配：雾是 lift/clear，不是 peel
2. 🟡 "the sun rising was spectacular" → "the sunrise was spectacular" — sunrise 整词做主语更自然
3. 🟡 "20 minute" → "20 minutes" — 复数漏 -s；"last time i" → "The last time I"（冠词+大写）

**亮点：**
- ✅ `unwind` — count **0→1** 🎓 第一次冷启动产出，自然嵌入
- ✅ "colours shifting from grey to gold and to green" — Band 7 画面感，participial phrase 运用自然
- ✅ "watching the valley in mist" — 好意象
- ✅ 结构完整：去了→看到了什么→如何感受→反应

**精修版：**
> The last time I went to Mount Emei, the sunrise was spectacular. I stood at the top of the mountain, watching the valley in mist, the fog lifting as the sun rose, colours shifting from grey to gold to green. I stood still for about 20 minutes. It was really one of those moments where I could completely unwind.

**范文（Band 7）：**
> The one that stands out is Mount Emei — I went a couple of years ago. I made it to the summit just before dawn, and when the sun came up, the whole valley was still blanketed in mist. Then the fog started lifting, and the colours just shifted — grey to gold to green — all within about twenty minutes. I didn't move. Just stood there. Probably the one time in recent memory I've completely unwound.

**今日表达：`blanketed in`**
- suzy 首次尝试："it's a winter morning blanketed in the rain" ❌（rain 不搭，雨是落的不是覆盖的）
- 修正后："it's a winter morning blanketed in the mist" ✅ — count **0→1**
- 正确搭配：blanketed in snow / mist / fog / darkness / silence

## Q2 (Q140): Have you ever forgotten something important?

**suzy 原句：**
once or twice, occasionally. The time I was most impacted is that I forgot a client meeting time. When the meeting began, I was focusing on coding, completely locked in. I didn't realise what happend util my colleage called me to ask where I was. I scrawled to the meeting room 10 minutes later. it was really embarrassing. After that, I put everything into digital reminder APP

**诊断：**
1. 🔴 "scrawled to the meeting room" → "scrambled to the meeting room" — scrawl=潦草书写；scramble=慌乱赶去。词汇混淆
2. 🔴 "The time I was most impacted" → "The worst one was when..." — Pattern 9 书面商务语气，口语不说 "most impacted"
3. 🟡 "put everything into digital reminder APP" → "started putting everything in a calendar app" — 漏冠词 a，APP全大写

**亮点：**
- ✅ "completely locked in" — 自然有画面感
- ✅ "my colleague called to ask where I was" — 好的具体细节
- ✅ 故事结构清晰：频率→案例→经过→后果→改变

**精修版：**
> Once or twice, yeah. The worst was when I forgot a client meeting. It had already started, and I was completely locked in to coding — I didn't realise what had happened until my colleague called to ask where I was. I scrambled to the meeting room ten minutes late. Genuinely embarrassing. After that I started putting everything into a calendar app.

**范文（Band 7）：**
> Once or twice. The worst one was when I missed a client meeting entirely. I was so locked in to coding that I completely lost track of time — it only hit me when my colleague called to ask where I was. I scrambled over ten minutes late. It still makes me cringe. After that, I started putting everything in a calendar app — deadlines, reminders, meetings, you name it.

**今日表达：`you name it`**
- Drill："drawing, playing dinosaur, watching cartoons, you name it" ✅ — count **2→3** 🎓 **毕业！**
- 范文中自然嵌入 Q2 结尾："deadlines, reminders, meetings, you name it"

## Q3 (Q16): Do you have the same hobbies as your family members?

**suzy 原句：**
Not really. My wife is a big fan of TV series, while I'm into reading the sci-fi novels. as for my son, he likes everything funny, drawing, playing dinosaur toys, watching cartoons, you name it. We have totally different hobbies, but we still have a happy life as a family.

**诊断：**
1. 🔴 "we still have a happy life as a family" → "we still have a great time together" — Pattern 13：过着幸福的家庭生活直接翻译，中式结尾
2. 🟡 "reading the sci-fi novels" → "reading sci-fi" — the 多余，指的是类型不是特定的书
3. 🟡 "he likes everything funny" → "he's into anything fun" — funny（搞笑）vs fun（好玩）

**亮点：**
- ✅ "while I'm into..." — 对比结构自然
- ✅ "as for my son" — 好的话题转换信号词
- ✅ `you name it` — 已毕业后再次自然产出
- ✅ 三人分开说，结构清晰，接近 Band 7

**精修版：**
> Not really. My wife is a big fan of TV series, while I'm more into sci-fi novels. As for my son, he's into anything fun — drawing, playing with his dinosaur toys, watching cartoons, you name it. Totally different hobbies, the three of us — but we still have a great time together.

**范文（Band 7）：**
> Not really. My wife could binge dramas all weekend and be perfectly happy. I'd rather curl up with a sci-fi novel. My son's just into whatever's fun at the time — drawing, dinosaur toys, cartoons, you name it. Completely different, the three of us. But it works — we still have a good time together.

**今日表达：`could [verb] all [time period]`**
- 框架：[人] could [动词] all [时间段] — 表示非常沉迷，能一直做
- suzy drill："I could binge coding all day" — 方向对，"binge coding" 偏（binge 接可消费内容）
- 修正：**"I could code all day"** ✅ 或 "I could spend all day coding and be perfectly happy"
