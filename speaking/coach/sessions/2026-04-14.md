## Q1: Do you prefer cooking at home or eating out?

**suzy 原句：**
It depends. For weekdays, I have to eat outside because it is almost 9pm when i get home. For weekends or whenever I have free time, I prioritize cooking a meal at home, which is more healthy and delicious.

**诊断：**
1. 🔴 "eat outside" → **eat out**（eat outside = 在户外吃，eat out = 去餐馆吃）— L1 直译
2. 🟡 "prioritize cooking" → **I'd rather cook** — 书面→口语
3. 🟡 Functional words absent — 没有 just/though

**范文（Band 7）：**
> It depends. On weekdays I just eat out — I don't get home till like 9, so there's no time to cook. But on weekends I'd rather cook at home. It's healthier and honestly tastes better too.

**教的表达：** eat out, just（轻描淡写）, I'd rather

## Q2: Is your city a good place for young people to live?

**suzy 原句：**
I'd say so. I now live in Chengdu, which is famous for its leisure lifestyle. Unlike other large cities in China, people living in chengdu work much less hours, and young people could have more freedom to pursue something look like funny.

**诊断：**
1. 🔴 "much less hours" → **far fewer hours**（less 不可数 / fewer 可数）
2. 🔴 "pursue something look like funny" → **pursue what they actually enjoy** — L1 直译崩了
3. 🟡 "could have more freedom" → **get to** have more freedom — could 太弱，get to 更口语

**范文（Band 7）：**
> I'd say so. I live in Chengdu, which is known for its laid-back lifestyle. Unlike other big cities in China, people here just work far fewer hours, so young people actually get to pursue what they enjoy — not just grind all the time.

**教的表达：** get to（有机会做某事）, far fewer, laid-back

## Q3: Do you think children should learn to cook?

**suzy 原句：**
Absolutely not. I think the kitchen would be dangerous for kids. While children could attend a bake workshop to experience how to cook as a practical or interesting class, cooking a meal is not their responsibilities.

**诊断：**
1. 🔴 "Absolutely not" → **Not really** — 太绝对，口语需要缓冲
2. 🟡 "attend a bake workshop" → **take a baking class** — attend 太正式 + bake→baking 词形
3. 🟡 "not their responsibilities" → **not really their responsibility** — 不可数 + 口语化

**范文（Band 7）：**
> Not really. I think the kitchen's actually pretty dangerous for kids — sharp knives, hot oil, that kind of thing. I mean, they could take a baking class just for fun, but I don't think they should be expected to cook proper meals. That's not really their job.

**教的表达：** that kind of thing（列举收尾）, not really their job

## Session Summary — Round 1

### 错误模式统计

| 维度 | Q1 | Q2 | Q3 |
|------|----|----|-----|
| 书面表达太重 | prioritize | — | attend, responsibilities |
| L1 直译 | eat outside | look like funny | — |
| 可数/不可数 | — | less→fewer | responsibilities→responsibility |
| Functional words 缺失 | ✅ 零 | ✅ 零 | ✅ 零 |

### 做得好的地方
- "It depends" / "I'd say so" 自然使用（W1 学的句式已内化）
- 内容具体有画面感（9pm 下班、成都、厨房刀和油）

### 新增表达（加入 inventory）
- get to（有机会做）
- that kind of thing（列举收尾）
- eat out（去餐馆吃）
- though（句尾转折）

## Round 2 — Pets / Morning / Gifts

## Q4: What's your favourite animal? Why?

**suzy 原句：**
Cat, without question. Cats are so cute, and they don't need people paying much attention to. When I was child, I always imaged to have a cat as a pet. But my grandmother did't allow. I have the freedom to choose not but not the energy with a full-time job and a five-year-old.

**诊断：**
1. 🔴 "imaged to have" → **imagined having** — image 是名词，imagine + doing 才是"想象做某事"
2. 🔴 "don't need people paying much attention to" → **don't need much attention** — L1 直译，中文"不需要人太关注"逐词翻出来的
3. 🟡 "I have the freedom to choose not but not the energy" → **Now I'm free to get one, but I just don't have the energy** — free to 更口语，加 just 缓冲

**范文（Band 7）：**
> Cats, without a doubt. They're cute and they don't need much attention — pretty low-maintenance compared to dogs. When I was a kid, I always imagined having one, but my grandmother wouldn't allow it. Now I'm free to get one, but with a full-time job and a five-year-old, I just don't have the energy.

**教的表达：** low-maintenance（不费心照顾的）

## Q5: Do you like getting up early in the morning?

**suzy 原句：**
Not really. I am not a morning person. When I was younger before work, I wouldn't get up util the alarm had alerted three times. At present I have to wake up early to grab breakfast and do kindergarten run for my son.

**诊断：**
1. 🔴 "the alarm had alerted three times" → **the alarm had gone off three times** — 闹钟响用 go off，alert 不对
2. 🟡 "do kindergarten run" → **do the kindergarten run** — 固定搭配需要 the
3. 🟡 "before work" 位置夹在句子中间造成断裂 → **Back in college** 或直接去掉

**范文（Band 7）：**
> Not really — I've never been a morning person. Back in college, I wouldn't get up until the alarm had gone off three times. But now I have to be up early to grab breakfast and do the kindergarten run for my son. It's just part of being a working dad, though.

**教的表达：** go off（闹钟响）

## Q6: What do you consider when choosing a gift?

**suzy 原句：**
Whether it's practical. Every time I choose a gift, I always wonder how people will use it. I'd rather give something functional than something flashy that ends up in drawer.

**诊断：**
1. 🔴 "ends up in drawer" → **ends up in a drawer** — 漏冠词，drawer 是可数单数名词
2. 🟡 "Every time...I always" — 重复，去掉一个
3. 🟡 答案太短（2句），缺延伸收尾

**范文（Band 7）：**
> Mainly whether they'll actually use it. Every time I choose a gift, I wonder if it'll just end up in a drawer somewhere. I'd rather give something functional than something flashy. My wife is way better at this than I am — she always knows exactly what someone would like.

**教的表达：** way better at this than I am（口语加强比较）
