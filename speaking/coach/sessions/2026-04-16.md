## Q1 (Q57): What do you think is the most important thing in your life at the moment?

**suzy 原句：**
make a happy living with my wife and son. I know what matters. In my age, nearly 40 years old, there are lots of pressure, mortgage, career developing, and that kind of things. as long as i have a happy family, everything else will follow

**诊断：**
1. 🔴 "In my age" → **At my age** — 介词错（at + 具体年龄）
2. 🔴 "career developing" → **career development** — 需要名词不是动名词（与 mortgage/pressure 并列）
3. 🟡 "make a happy living" → L1 直译「过好日子」。make a living = 谋生赚钱 → **build a good life** / **keep my family happy**
4. 🟡 "that kind of things" → **that kind of thing**（固定搭配单数）

**精修版：**
> Making a good life with my wife and son. I know what matters. At my age, nearly 40, there's a lot of pressure — mortgage, career development, that kind of thing. But as long as I have a happy family, everything else will follow.

**范文（Band 7）：**
> I'd say just keeping my family happy — my wife and my son. At my age, nearly 40, there's no shortage of pressure — mortgage, career, all of that. But I've kind of figured out what actually matters. As long as we're doing well as a family, everything else tends to fall into place. My son's only five, and these years go by fast, so I'd rather be present than chase something that doesn't really matter.

**教的表达：** fall into place（事情自然就顺了）
- 练习：once you understand the question, the rest will fall in place ❌ → **fall into place** ✅（into 不能漏）
- 第二次：once you understand the question, the rest will fall into place ✅

**亮点：** that kind of thing 用了（inventory count +1，虽然多加了 s，但结构内化）

## Q2 (Q62): Do you keep plants at home?

**suzy 原句：**
my wife has dozens of plants on the bacolny, but I hardly ever deal with them. I am actually not a planty person. i have been banned from gardening by overwatering her jasmine when she was away on a business trip

**诊断：**
1. 🔴 "banned from gardening **by** overwatering" → **for overwatering** — by 表方式，for 表原因
2. 🟡 "a planty person" → **a plant person** — planty 非标准词
3. 🟡 bacolny → balcony（拼写）

**精修版：**
> My wife has dozens of plants on the balcony, but I hardly ever deal with them. I'm actually not really a plant person. I've been banned from gardening for overwatering her jasmine when she was away on a business trip.

**范文（Band 7）：**
> My wife does — she's got dozens on the balcony. I hardly ever touch them, though. I'm not really a plant person. I actually got banned from gardening after I overwatered her jasmine while she was away on a business trip. She came back and it was basically dead. I haven't been allowed near them since.

**教的表达：** haven't been allowed to... since（从那以后再没被允许…）
- 练习：i haven't been allowed to play football for months since i sprained my wrist once
- 问题：for months + since 冲突（since 已含"一直到现在"）；once 句尾歧义 → **I haven't been allowed to play football since I sprained my wrist.**

**亮点：**
- hardly ever ✅ 自然
- actually ✅ 功能正确（inventory count +1）

## Q3 (Q80): Do you have an experience when you did something well?

**suzy 原句：**
A couple of weeks ago. I got a good feedback from the department manager and colleagues. At that time, I had been undertaking a urgent task which a costumer required a new feature in our app in short time, and I completed it in high quality on time. I suppose it was what I chased in career

**诊断：**
1. 🔴 "a urgent" → **an urgent**（元音开头用 an）
2. 🔴 "costumer" → **customer**（costumer = 戏服师）
3. 🔴 "a good feedback" → **good feedback**（feedback 不可数）
4. 🟡 "undertaking a urgent task which a costumer required" → 书面+结构绕（Pattern 9 复发）→ 口语: **A customer needed a new feature built fast**
5. 🟡 "in high quality" → **to a high standard** / **without cutting corners**
6. 🟡 "what I chased in career" → **what I'm after in my career** — chase 太直译「追求」

**精修版：**
> A couple of weeks ago. I got good feedback from my department manager and colleagues. I'd been working on an urgent task — a customer needed a new feature built in a short time, and I delivered it on time to a high standard. I suppose that's what I'm after in my career.

**范文（Band 7）：**
> Yeah, just a couple of weeks ago, actually. A customer needed a new feature built on a really tight deadline, and I managed to deliver it on time without cutting corners. My manager gave me a shoutout in front of the team, which felt really good. That kind of moment is what keeps me going, I guess — knowing I can handle the pressure and still do quality work.

**教的表达：** without cutting corners（没偷工减料）

## Session Summary

### 错误模式统计

| 维度 | Q1 Life stages | Q2 Plants | Q3 Did well |
|------|---------------|-----------|-------------|
| 介词错误 | In my age→At my age | banned by→banned for | — |
| 冠词/单复数 | that kind of things→thing | — | a urgent→an urgent, a good feedback→good feedback |
| 词形/词类 | career developing→development | planty→plant person | costumer→customer |
| L1 直译 | make a happy living, chase→after | — | in high quality, undertaking(书面), chased in career |
| 功能词 | — | actually ✅ hardly ever ✅ | — |

### 核心问题
- **介词错误 2 次**（In my age, banned by）→ 新 pattern 入 error log
- **L1 直译 4 次**（make a happy living, chased in career, in high quality, costumer 幻听词）→ Pattern 9 复发显著

### 进步
- that kind of thing 第 2 次出现（虽然带 s，结构内化中）→ inventory count +1
- actually 第 3 次自然冒出 → **即将毕业**
- hardly ever 新出现且自然
- fall into place 当场掌握（第二次就对）

### 新增表达
- fall into place（事情自然顺了）
- haven't been allowed to... since（从那以后再没被允许）
- without cutting corners（没偷工减料）

### 下次重点
1. **介词**（at my age / for doing sth / on sth）— 新 pattern 需要刻意注意
2. **L1 直译持续高频** — 说之前问自己「这话翻回中文是不是太生硬」
3. 冠词 a/an（元音开头用 an）+ 不可数名词（feedback, advice, information）
4. fall into place / without cutting corners 等待复现
