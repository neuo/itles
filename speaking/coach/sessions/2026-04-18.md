## Q1 (Q9): Have you ever been part of a sports team?

**suzy 原句：**
When I was young, Yes. I played football with classmates almost every day back in primary school. I even played goalkeeper once and spained my wrist, which making my grandma pretty furious. Good time, though. I'm just stuck in working at a dest right now. It's actually a working dad lifestyle, I guess.

**诊断：**
1. 🔴 "which making my grandma pretty furious" → **which made** — Pattern 11 复发（past participle/tense 形态在压力下丢失）。叙事在过去时，关系从句需要 `made`，-ing 省掉了形态转换那一步。
2. 🟡 "Good time, though" → **Good times, though** — 库存里的固定块，复数 `s` 漏了。结构找对了（inventory 里放进去就是冲着毕业去的），但形态没落地。
3. 🟡 "It's actually a working dad lifestyle"
   - "actually" 是 Pattern 5 L1 filler（其实），无对比不需要
   - "a working dad lifestyle" 名词堆叠 → **the working-dad life** / **dad life**
4. 🔴 拼写：spained → sprained / dest → desk
5. 🟡 "stuck in working at a dest" → **stuck behind a desk** — 介词 + 更自然

**精修版：**
> When I was young, yes. I played football with classmates almost every day back in primary school. I even played goalkeeper once and sprained my wrist, which made my grandma pretty furious. Good times, though. Now I'm just stuck behind a desk. It's just the working-dad life, I guess.

**范文（Band 7）：**
> Yeah, back in primary school. Me and my classmates played football pretty much every day after class. I actually tried goalkeeper once and ended up spraining my wrist — my grandma was furious. Good times, though. These days I'm just stuck behind a desk all day. Not really a sports guy anymore — that's just dad life, I guess.

**教的表达：** stuck behind a desk（困在办公桌后）/ 强化 "Good times, though" 的复数形态

## Pattern Drill — "which + past tense verb"（Pattern 11 矫正）

Context 1: My son dropped my phone on the floor, which ___ the screen.
→ suzy: **destroyed** ✅ 时态+精准动词

Context 2: We took a wrong turn on the hike, which ___ us an extra hour.
→ suzy: **wasted** ✅ 时态+精准动词（比 took/cost 好）

Context 3: I forgot to save the document, which ___ two hours of work.
→ suzy: **wasted** ✅

Context 4: My son refused to nap yesterday, which ___ the whole afternoon difficult.
→ suzy: **made** ✅

**判定**：Pattern 11 在 "which + V-ed" 结构上 4/4 稳定掌握。下次换一个触发点（如 `-ed` 作形容词：surrounded / blanketed）继续验证是否全域迁移。

## Q2 (Q60): Do you enjoy being the age you are now?

**suzy 原句：**
yeah. at nearly 40 ages, I know I'm better than twenties and what matters for family. Although there are pretty much pressure, mortgage, career development, that kind of thing, i really enjoy the life with my wife and son, which is peaceful and happy

**诊断：**
1. 🔴 "at nearly 40 ages" → **at nearly 40** — L1 transfer from 快40岁了，岁 被翻出来变成 ages。数字本身就含 age，不加 ages/years old age。
2. 🔴 "I know I'm better than twenties and what matters for family" — **Pattern 3 骨架句压缩失败**。两个想法（「我比20多岁时更了解自己」+「我知道什么重要，即家庭」）硬塞一个从句里，语法撑不住。修成两个 clause：**"I know myself way better than I did in my twenties, and I know what actually matters — my family."**
3. 🔴 "pretty much pressure" → **quite a lot of / a lot of pressure**。pretty much = almost/basically（副词修饰整句）；pretty + adj = quite（pretty tired）。两者不能融合成 pretty much + 名词。
4. 🟡 "Although ... , I really enjoy" → 句首 Although 偏书面（Pattern 9），口语用 "There's a lot of pressure..., but..."

**精修版：**
> Yeah. At nearly 40, I know myself better than I did in my twenties, and I know what actually matters — family. There's a lot of pressure — mortgage, career development, that kind of thing — but I really enjoy my life with my wife and son. It's peaceful and happy.

**范文（Band 7）：**
> Yeah, I'd say I do. I'm nearly 40 now, and honestly, I feel like I know myself way better than I did in my twenties. I've figured out what actually matters — mostly just family. There's still plenty on my plate — mortgage, career, that kind of thing — but I'd much rather be where I am now. Life with my wife and son is pretty quiet, and I'm good with that.

**教的表达：** know [X] way better than I did in [past time]（Band 7 比较框架）

**亮点：**
- **"that kind of thing" 这次带对了（无 s）** → inventory count 1→2 ✓
- 4/16 session 的 "mortgage / career development / that kind of thing" 结构跨 session 复用
- "Not particularly" 式开头技巧延用

### Pattern Drill — "I [verb] way better than I did in [past time]"

**第一轮（理解错了）：**
- "I know myself way better than I did in housework" ❌
- "I feel like myself way better than I did job" ❌

诊断：把右边的槽当成 topic 而不是 past time。左边是**技能**，右边是**过去某个时间**。

**重讲 + 第二轮：**
- "I code way better than I did in college" ✅
- "I do housework way better than I did when I was just married" ✅（小改：when I first got married 更自然；houseword 拼写）

**判定**：Structure 重讲后理解到位。注意 past time slot ≠ topic slot。

## Q3 (Q58): Do you have any plans for the next five years?

**suzy 原句：**
Not Particularly. With a full-time job and a five-year-old, I actually made quite a lot of plans for me and family, but the plans all have been abandoned. The only expectation is that we live together with safety, health and happiness, nothing else.

**诊断：**
1. 🔴 "for me and family" → **for me and my family** — 所有格漏（L1: 我和家人，不带"的"）。高频盲点，口语里几乎听不见。
2. 🔴 "The only expectation is that we live together with safety, health and happiness" — **Pattern 9 + 13 双重复发**。expectation 是 hope 的书面版；live together with safety/health/happiness 是 "过上有安全健康快乐的生活" 直译，英语里这个 frame 不存在。改成：**"All I really want is for us to stay healthy and happy."**
3. 🔴 "the plans all have been abandoned" → **they've all fallen through**。语序乱（the plans all have been），abandoned 太戏剧化（书面语）。plans fall through / don't pan out / get dropped 才是口语默认。

**亮点：**
- "actually" 这次是**功能用法正确**（与 "Not particularly" 形成对比）✅
- "With a full-time job and a five-year-old" 自然压缩开头 ✅
- "Not particularly" 式 hedged 开头延用 ✅

**精修版：**
> Not particularly. With a full-time job and a five-year-old, I've actually made quite a lot of plans for me and my family, but they've all fallen through. All I really hope for is that we stay healthy, safe, and happy together — that's it, really.

**范文（Band 7）：**
> Honestly, not really. With a full-time job and a five-year-old at home, I've made plenty of plans over the years — trips, hobbies, that kind of thing — but they pretty much all fell through. So I've kind of stopped planning that far ahead. The only thing I really want is for us to stay healthy and happy as a family. That's honestly enough.

**教的表达：** fall through（计划泡汤）

### Pattern Drill — "fall through / fell through"

- "We had a trip to Japan planned last year, but they fell through eventually" → pronoun 错（a trip 单数 → **it fell through**）；结构✅
- "The house deal fell through at the last minute because the seller regretted" → regret 需要宾语；应用 **backed out**（退出交易）；结构✅

**判定**：fall through 结构使用 2/2 正确。副错：代词一致 + regret 及物。新词 **back out of sth**（退出交易/承诺）记一下。
