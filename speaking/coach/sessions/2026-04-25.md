## Q1 (Q36): Do you prefer typing or handwriting?

**suzy 原句：**
typing definitely. After 14 years of a programmer, typing is actually my second nature. I just do typing for everything, coding, documenting, you name it. otherwise, my handwriting is pretty terrible. To be honest, I often forget how to write a character with holding a pen

**诊断：**
1. 🔴 "after 14 years **of** a programmer" → **"as a programmer"** — Pattern 12 介词，描述身份/角色用 `as`
2. 🔴 "forget how to write a character **with holding** a pen" → **"my hands forget how to hold a pen"** — L1 直译"拿着笔写字"，`with holding` 不存在
3. 🟡 "I just **do typing**" → **"I just type"** — 动名词绕路，直接用动词
4. 🟡 "typing is **actually** my second nature" — `actually` 无对比功能，接近 Pattern 5 填充，可删

**亮点：**
- ✅ `you name it` — 冷启动首次成功 count **0→1** 🎓（drill 追加 count **1→2**）
- ✅ "pretty terrible" — 自然口语
- ✅ 答案结构：正面（typing）→ 反面（handwriting）对比清晰

**精修版：**
> Typing, definitely. After 14 years as a programmer, typing is second nature. I just type for everything — coding, documenting, you name it. My handwriting, on the other hand, is pretty terrible. To be honest, I often forget how to hold a pen.

**范文（Band 7）：**
> Typing, no question. After 14 years as a programmer, my fingers just know what to do — I barely think about it. I type for literally everything: code, docs, messages, you name it. My handwriting, though, is embarrassingly bad. I genuinely forget how to hold a pen sometimes.

**教的表达：** `you name it` — 列举收尾，count 0→2

## Q2 (Q17): Do you like getting up early in the morning?

**suzy 原句：**
not really, i'm not literally a morning person. back in college, I couldn't drag me out of bed, even with three alarms. good times, though. these day mornings are non-stop, kindergarten drop-off by 8, then straight to work. It's just dad life, i guess. On weekends, thought, i guard my sleep-in like it's sacred

**诊断：**
1. 🔴 "I'm not **literally** a morning person" → **"not really"** — `literally` 修饰比喻性描述时无意义（"morning person" 本身是比喻）。换 `really` 或直接去掉
2. 🔴 "couldn't drag **me** out of bed" → **"drag myself out of bed"** — 反身代词。`drag oneself out of bed` 在词库里，方向对，介词载体用错
3. 🟡 "**these day**" → **"these days"** — 固定复数短语，等同于 nowadays

**亮点：**
- ✅ `Good times, though` — 自然产出，count **2→3** 🎓🎓🎓 **毕业！**
- ✅ "non-stop, kindergarten drop-off by 8, then straight to work" — 节奏好，有压缩感
- ✅ "guard my sleep-in like it's sacred" — 生动，画面感强

**错过的机会：** "It's just dad life, I guess" 加 `I wouldn't trade it` 完全自然，还差 1 次毕业

**精修版：**
> Not really — I'm not a morning person. Back in college, I couldn't drag myself out of bed, even with three alarms. Good times, though. These days mornings are non-stop — kindergarten drop-off by 8, then straight to work. It's just dad life, I guess. On weekends, though, I guard my sleep-in like it's sacred.

## Q3 (Q19): What did you do in the morning when you were little?

**suzy 原句：**
when i was little, I usually got up quite late and had congee or steamed buns for breakfest, my grandmother made. then ran off to school with my cousins, not rush, no schedule. My grandfather wound be the first to get up, tending his garden. sometimes I helped him water the plants. It was really a relaxed routine.

**诊断：**
1. 🔴 "My grandfather **wound** be the first" → **"would be"** — `wound` 是 `wind` 的过去式。描述过去习惯用 `would + 动词原形`
2. 🔴 "**not** rush, no schedule" → **"no rush, no schedule"** — 名词短语用 `no` 否定，不是 `not`（`not` 否定动词/形容词）
3. 🟡 "buns for breakfast, my grandmother made" → 关系从句断裂，需要 → **"buns that my grandmother made"**

**亮点：**
- ✅ "ran off to school with my cousins" — 动词精准，有动感
- ✅ "tending his garden" — 自然分词短语
- ✅ 细节具体（congee, steamed buns, watering plants），有故事感

**错过的机会：** "It was really a relaxed routine" → 完美加 `I wouldn't trade those mornings`，已连续两题错过

**精修版：**
> When I was little, I usually got up quite late and had congee or steamed buns that my grandmother made. Then ran off to school with my cousins — no rush, no schedule. My grandfather would be the first up, tending his garden. Sometimes I helped him water the plants. Really relaxed mornings. I wouldn't trade those, honestly.

### 核心发现

- **`literally` 新问题首现**：用作强调语气，但修饰比喻性表达时语义矛盾。和 Pattern 5（`actually`）同类，都是把语气助词从 L1 直接搬运过来
- **反身代词盲点**：`myself/himself/herself` 在 L1 中无对应形态，production 时被 `me` 替代。`drag oneself out of bed` 方向感对，但载体没切换
- **`I wouldn't trade it` 连续两题错过**：两题都是完美场合（"dad life" / "relaxed childhood routine"），说明这个表达还没到自动产出阶段，下次需要主动意识触发

### 冷启动更新

| 表达 | 今日 | 累计 |
|------|------|------|
| `you name it` | ✓✓ count 0→2 | 2 ✓✓ |
| `Good times, though` | ✓ count 2→3 | **毕业 🎓** |
| `drag oneself out of bed` | 方向对但用 "me" | 0（待重练） |
| `I wouldn't trade it` | 两题均未产出 | 仍 2 |

### 下次重点

1. **`I wouldn't trade it`** 还差 1 次，主动在回忆/反思类题目结尾用
2. **反身代词**：`myself / yourself / themselves` — myself 对自己，yourself 对听者。需要在下次 drill 里专门触发
3. **`literally` 使用原则**：只能修饰字面意思可以成立的描述（"I literally ran 10km"），不能修饰比喻（"a morning person"）
